# G-01 — Store (المتجر)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: Draft (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/screens-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 1–7; `/spec/screens-map.md` §4 decisions 1–16.
Conventions set by this file (reused by every later screen): Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label from store settings, written after the amount in Arabic ("45.00 ر.س") and before it in English ("SAR 45.00"); amounts always with two decimals; every tap target at least 44 × 44 px; the five order-status labels in §5.2 are the canonical ones.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** G-01
- **Name:** المتجر / Store
- **Type:** Full guest screen. It is the landing screen of the whole product: the URL encoded in the hotel-wide QR code opens G-01 directly (map §4 decision 1 — no splash, no welcome screen).
- **Language:** Arabic (RTL) on first open regardless of browser language; the header toggle switches to English (LTR) in place. The choice is saved on the device (locked decision 7; map §4 decision 12).

---

## 2. Purpose

Let the guest see everything the hotel sells, find a category fast, and add products to the cart from the card itself, without leaving this screen.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 Entry (how the guest arrives)

| From | Trigger | What G-01 shows on arrival |
|---|---|---|
| QR scan (first open) | Browser opens the store URL | Loading state (§6.1), then the success state scrolled to the top; Arabic unless a saved language choice exists on the device |
| QR scan (later open, same device) | Browser opens the store URL | Same as above, in the saved language; the saved cart is restored, so cards show steppers and the floating cart bar shows immediately after load |
| G-08 Store unavailable | "Retry" succeeds | Success state scrolled to the top |
| G-02 Product details | Back arrow / browser back | Same scroll position the guest left; card steppers reflect any change made on G-02 |
| G-03 Cart | Back arrow / browser back (when G-03 was opened from G-01), or "Continue shopping" in the empty-cart state | Same scroll position the guest left (back), or top of the list ("Continue shopping"); card steppers reflect any change made on G-03 |
| G-05 Order submitted | "Back to store" or browser back | Top of the list; cart is empty, so every card shows "Add" and the floating cart bar is hidden; the active-order banner shows the order just sent |
| G-06 Order tracking | Back arrow (when opened from G-01 or G-05) or "Back to store" | Top of the list; the active-order banner reflects the status G-06 just fetched (hidden if that status is Delivered or Cancelled) |
| G-07 My orders | Back arrow / browser back, or "Browse the store" in the empty state | Top of the list |

Browser back from G-01 leaves the store (there is no earlier screen in the store's history). G-01 has no back arrow (map §2.3 rule 1).

### 3.2 Exit (where each action leads)

| Action on G-01 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap a product card (anywhere except its buttons) | G-02 Product details for that product |
| Tap the floating cart bar | G-03 Cart |
| Tap the "My orders" button | G-07 My orders |
| Tap the active-order banner | G-06 Order tracking for the order shown in the banner |
| Tap a category chip | Stays on G-01; the list scrolls to that category's section |
| Tap the language toggle | Stays on G-01; the whole screen re-renders in the other language, same scroll position, same cart |
| Tap "Add", "+", "−" | Stays on G-01; the cart is updated in place, the card and the floating cart bar update |
| Tap "Refresh" (empty state only) | Stays on G-01; the catalog is fetched again (loading state, then success or empty or G-08) |
| Catalog fails to load (first load, Refresh, or browser reload) | G-08 Store unavailable replaces G-01 entirely |

## 5. Content

### 5.1 Header (S01)

Layout, in visual order for Arabic (RTL): [B01 language toggle] … [C01 hotel name over C02 pay-on-delivery line] … [B02 My orders]. In English the two buttons swap edges automatically with the direction; the hotel name stays centered.

- C01 hotel name: the value for the current language from store settings. If the English value is missing, the Arabic value is shown in the English interface too (never a blank).
- C02: fixed copy, "الدفع عند الاستلام في غرفتك" / "Pay on delivery to your room". This is the only place that tells the guest how payment works before checkout (map §4 decision 1).
- B01: shows "English" while the interface is Arabic, "العربية" while the interface is English. No flag icon.
- B02: receipt-outline icon 24 px with the label "طلباتي" / "My orders" beneath it in 10 px. No count badge on the icon in version 1; the active-order banner is the only indicator of an ongoing order.

### 5.2 Active-order banner (C03)

Shown only while the device holds at least one order whose last known status is New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way.

- Line 1 (14 px, bold): "طلبك رقم {order number} — {status label}" / "Your order {order number} — {status label}". The order number is printed exactly as it appears on G-05 and G-06 (example: 1042).
- Line 2 (12 px): "متابعة الطلب ›" / "Track order ›". The chevron points in the reading direction (left-pointing in Arabic, right-pointing in English).
- Status labels (canonical; G-06 and G-07 reuse these verbatim):

| Status (locked decision 5) | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| New | جديد | New |
| Accepted & preparing | تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير | Accepted & preparing |
| On the way | في الطريق | On the way |
| Delivered | تم التوصيل | Delivered |
| Cancelled | ملغى | Cancelled |

- Source of the status: when G-01 loads (first open, browser reload, Refresh), one request fetches the live status of every order stored on the device whose last known status is New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way, and updates the saved copies. G-01 does **not** poll afterwards; live polling belongs to G-06. If that status request fails, the banner shows the last known status from the device without any error text.
- When two or more orders are active, the banner shows the most recently submitted one. Tapping opens G-06 for that order only.
- The banner is never shown for Delivered or Cancelled orders.

### 5.3 Category bar (S02) and category sections (S04)

- Chips and sections appear in the order defined by the catalog (the hotel decides the order; the guest interface never re-sorts).
- Every non-empty category has exactly one chip and exactly one section. Empty categories have neither.
- Category names come from the catalog in both languages. If the English name is missing, the Arabic name is shown in the English interface too.
- Active chip rule ("the active chip follows the scroll position"): the active chip is the one whose section title is the lowest title that is at or above the bottom edge of the sticky category bar. On first render the first chip is active. When the guest taps a chip, that chip becomes active immediately and stays active until the scroll animation ends; after that the scroll-position rule applies again. If the last section is too short to reach the top of the list area, the list scrolls as far as it can and the tapped chip stays active until the guest scrolls manually.
- Whenever the active chip changes, the chip row scrolls horizontally (animated, 200 ms) so the active chip is fully visible with at least 16 px of the next chip peeking in the scroll direction.
- Section title C04 is the category name; no product count, no icon.

### 5.4 Product card (C05)

Each card contains, top to bottom:

1. **Image** — square (1:1), full card width (171 px), 8 px rounded corners, product photo from the catalog scaled to fill. If the image fails to load or the product has none, a gray box of the same size with a centered 32 px generic product icon is shown; no text.
2. **Name** — product name in the current language, 14 px, maximum 2 lines, then truncated with an ellipsis ("…"). If the English name is missing, the Arabic name is shown in the English interface too. The full name is always visible on G-02.
3. **Price** — unit price, 14 px, bold: Arabic "12.00 ر.س", English "SAR 12.00". Two decimals always; currency label from store settings (examples use SAR). No strikethrough, no discount, no "per unit" text in version 1.
4. **Action row** (40 px tall), exactly one of:
   - B04 "أضف / Add" — when the product is available and its cart quantity is 0.
   - Stepper [B05 −] [C06 quantity] [B06 +] — when the product is available and its cart quantity is 1–10. C08 "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10" appears under the stepper when the quantity is 10.
   - C07 "غير متوفر / Out of stock" badge — when the catalog marks the product as out of stock. The image is shown at 50 % opacity. No stepper is shown even if the cart already contains this product; that cart line is handled on G-03 (map §4 decision 9), and the floating cart bar still counts it.

Products within a section are laid out in a 2-column grid, in the order defined by the catalog. A section with an odd number of products leaves the last cell empty. The whole catalog is loaded in one request; there is no pagination and no infinite scroll in version 1.

Tapping anywhere on the card except B04, B05, or B06 opens G-02. A tap on B04, B05, or B06 acts on the cart and does not open G-02.

### 5.5 Floating cart bar (S05 / B07)

- Visible only when the cart has at least one line (any product with quantity ≥ 1, including out-of-stock lines still in the cart).
- Start side (right in Arabic, left in English): item count per §7.3, 14 px.
- Center: "عرض السلة" / "View cart", 16 px, bold.
- End side: total per §7.2, 16 px, bold.
- Updates within 100 ms of any tap on B04, B05, or B06 with no animation other than the text changing.
- The bar does not scroll with the list; it stays fixed above the bottom edge of the viewport.

### 5.6 What is deliberately not on this screen

- No search field (map §4 decision 2).
- No toast. The only transient toast in version 1 is on G-03 after Reorder (map §1.3). Feedback for "Add" is the stepper appearing on the card and the cart bar appearing or updating.
- No pull-to-refresh. The catalog is fetched on load, on browser reload, and on "Refresh" in the empty state. Stock is re-checked on G-03 and again at submission (M-04).
- No store-hours notice (map §4 decision 16).
- No back arrow (map §2.3 rule 1).

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

Triggered by: first open, browser reload, "Retry" on G-08, "Refresh" (B08).

On screen:
- S01 header with B01 and B02 fully functional; C01 replaced by a gray placeholder bar 140 × 18 px; C02 hidden.
- S02 with 4 gray placeholder chips 80 × 32 px (C10).
- List area with 6 gray placeholder cards in 3 rows of 2, each the size of a real card (C10). Placeholders pulse in opacity between 100 % and 60 % every 1.5 seconds.
- C03 banner hidden. S05 cart bar hidden.
- No text of any kind in the list area.

Rules:
- The catalog request and the active-order status request are sent at the same time. The screen leaves the loading state as soon as the catalog response arrives; the banner is added when the status response arrives (or shows the last known status if that request fails or arrives after the catalog).
- If the catalog response has not arrived after **10 seconds**, or arrives as a connection or server error, the screen is replaced by G-08 (connection/server variant). If the URL is not a valid store link, G-08 (invalid-link variant) is shown instead.
- The loading state never lasts longer than 10 seconds.

### 6.2 Empty

Triggered by: the catalog loads successfully but contains zero products (every category empty, or no categories).

On screen:
- S01 header complete (C01 hotel name, C02 line, B01, B02).
- C03 banner shown if an active order exists (an active order can exist even when the catalog is empty).
- S02 category bar hidden. S05 cart bar hidden even if the saved cart has lines (nothing on screen can be added; the guest reaches the cart again when the catalog returns).
- C09, centered in the list area: 
  - Line 1 (18 px, bold): "لا توجد منتجات حاليًا" / "No products right now"
  - Line 2 (14 px): "يرجى المحاولة لاحقًا أو التواصل مع الاستقبال" / "Please try again later or contact reception"
- B08 "تحديث / Refresh" 24 px under the text.

Tapping B08 shows the loading state (§6.1) and then the success, empty, or G-08 outcome.

### 6.3 Error

There are three error situations. None of them shows a toast.

1. **Catalog cannot be loaded** (no connection, server error, timeout, invalid link): G-01 is not shown at all; G-08 replaces it (map §1.3, §4 decision 13). All copy for that case lives in the G-08 file.
2. **Active-order status request fails** while the catalog succeeds: the banner shows the last known status from the device. No error text anywhere. The next load tries again.
3. **A product image fails to load**: the image area shows the gray placeholder box with the generic product icon (§5.4 item 1). No error text. The card stays fully functional.

There is no typed input on G-01, so there are no field error messages on this screen.

### 6.4 Success

The catalog loaded and has at least one product.

On screen:
- S01 header complete.
- C03 banner if an active order exists.
- S02 with one chip per non-empty category; the first chip active.
- S03 with one S04 section per non-empty category; list scrolled to the top (or to the remembered position when arriving by back from G-02 or G-03).
- Each card in the state matching the saved cart and the catalog stock (§5.4 item 4).
- S05 cart bar shown if the saved cart has at least one line, with the correct count and total.

Add/stepper success feedback: after a tap on B04, B05, or B06 the card's action row and the cart bar update within 100 ms. Nothing else moves, nothing scrolls, no toast.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on this screen. The rules below govern the bounded values G-01 displays or changes.

### 7.1 Quantity per product (changed with B05 / B06)

- Type: whole number.
- Minimum in the cart: 1. Tapping "−" at 1 removes the line (the card returns to "Add"). There is no "remove" confirmation.
- Maximum per product line: **10**. At 10 the "+" button is disabled (50 % opacity, tap does nothing) and C08 "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10" is shown under the stepper. Nothing else happens at the maximum: no toast, no modal.
- No limit on the number of different products in the cart in version 1.
- The quantity shown on the card is always the quantity stored in the device cart for that product, so it matches what G-02 and G-03 show.
- Adding is a device-side action only; G-01 does not call the server on "Add", "+", or "−". Stock is re-checked on G-03 and at submission (M-04).

### 7.2 Prices and total

- Unit price: from the catalog, shown with exactly two decimals.
- Cart total on the bar: sum over all cart lines of (quantity × unit price as currently loaded in the catalog), rounded to two decimals, half up. Lines for out-of-stock products are included until the guest removes them on G-03.
- Currency label: from store settings. Arabic: after the amount with a space ("45.00 ر.س"). English: before the amount with a space ("SAR 45.00").
- Digits: Western digits 0–9 in both languages.

### 7.3 Item count on the cart bar

The count is the **sum of quantities** across all cart lines (3 bottles of water = 3 items). Exact wording:

| Count | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | منتج واحد | 1 item |
| 2 | منتجان | 2 items |
| 3–10 | {N} منتجات | {N} items |
| 11 and above | {N} منتجًا | {N} items |

### 7.4 Language

- On first open with no saved choice: Arabic, RTL.
- The saved choice ("ar" or "en") is read on every open and applied before the first render, so the guest never sees a flash of the wrong language.
- The toggle text is always the name of the other language, written in that language: "English" when the interface is Arabic, "العربية" when the interface is English.
- Switching language re-renders in place: no reload, scroll position kept, cart unchanged, active chip unchanged.

### 7.5 Text limits on cards

- Product name: 2 lines maximum on the card, ellipsis after that. Catalog side there is no limit imposed by this screen; the full name is shown on G-02.
- Category name on a chip: one line, no wrapping, no truncation — a chip grows to fit its text; a chip wider than the bar still fits because the row scrolls horizontally.

### 7.6 Sizes and touch targets

- Every tappable element has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px (chips, "Add", "+", "−", language toggle, "My orders", banner, cart bar, cards).
- Minimum text size on the screen: 10 px (the "My orders" label). All other copy is 11 px or larger; product name and price are 14 px; hotel name and cart-bar label are 16–18 px.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Opening the store URL on a device with no saved language shows G-01 in Arabic with right-to-left layout, regardless of the browser language.
2. Opening the store URL on a device where English was saved shows G-01 in English with left-to-right layout on the first painted frame (no Arabic flash).
3. The header (S01) and the category bar (S02) stay fixed at the top while the product list scrolls; the header is 56 px tall and the bar 48 px tall.
4. The hotel name (C01) and the line "الدفع عند الاستلام في غرفتك / Pay on delivery to your room" (C02) are visible in the header in the success state.
5. Tapping B01 while in Arabic switches every visible text to English, flips the layout to LTR, keeps the same scroll position and the same cart, and changes B01's text to "العربية". Tapping again reverses all of this. No page reload occurs.
6. After a language switch, closing the tab and reopening the store URL shows the last chosen language.
7. Tapping B02 opens G-07, whether or not the device has any stored order.
8. When the device holds an order with last known status New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way, C03 is visible under the header, shows that order's number and its status label from §5.2, and tapping it opens G-06 for that order.
9. When the device holds no order in those three statuses, C03 is not rendered (the category bar sits directly under the header).
10. When two orders are active, C03 shows the most recently submitted one.
11. When the status request fails but the catalog loads, C03 shows the last known status and no error text appears.
12. S02 shows exactly one chip per category that contains at least one product, in the catalog's order, and no "All" chip.
13. A category with zero products has no chip and no section.
14. Tapping a chip scrolls the list in an animation of about 300 ms so that the tapped section's title sits directly below the category bar, and the tapped chip becomes active immediately.
15. Scrolling the list by hand changes the active chip according to the rule in §5.3 (lowest section title at or above the bar's bottom edge), and the chip row scrolls so the active chip is fully visible.
16. Products are laid out two per row within each section, in the catalog's order.
17. Every card shows an image (or the gray placeholder with the icon), the product name (max 2 lines, ellipsis), and the price with two decimals and the currency label in the position defined in §7.2.

# G-03 — Cart (السلة)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/screens-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 1–7; `/spec/screens-map.md` §4 decisions 1–16 (decisions 7 and 9 apply directly to this screen).
Conventions reused verbatim from G-01 (not re-decided here): Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label from store settings, after the amount in Arabic ("45.00 ر.س") and before it in English ("SAR 45.00"); amounts always with two decimals, rounded half up; every tappable element has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px even when its visible shape is smaller (G-01 §7.6); maximum quantity 10 per line with the helper "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10" (G-01 §7.1); a line's stepper ignores taps for 300 ms after any tap while it re-renders (G-01 §7.1); the session catalog copy and its out-of-stock marks (G-01 §5.7) — G-03 is one of the two screens that **writes** marks into it; the item-count wording in G-01 §7.3; body copy never smaller than 14 px (the only smaller text on this screen is the 11 px "Max 10" helper, kept at G-01's size so the two screens match).

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** G-03
- **Name:** السلة / Cart
- **Type:** Full guest screen with its own URL and history entry. It is the only screen where a line can be removed while the product is out of stock, and the first of the two stock gates (map §4 decision 9; the second gate is M-04 at submission).
- **Language:** the current language chosen on G-01 (map §2.3 rule 6). G-03 has no language toggle.

---

## 2. Purpose

Let the guest check what is in the cart, fix quantities, clear anything that is no longer available, see the total, and go to checkout.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

## 5. Content

### 5.1 Header (S01)

Layout, in visual order for Arabic (RTL): [B01 back chevron] … [C01 "السلة"] … (nothing at the end edge). In English the chevron sits at the left edge and points left; the title stays centered.

- No language toggle (map §2.3 rule 6). No "My orders" button (it lives on G-01 only). No line-count badge on the title.

### 5.2 What the device cart holds (input to this screen)

Each cart line stored on the device carries: product identifier, product name in both languages, unit price, image address, quantity (1–10), and the moment it was added. Name, price, and image are a snapshot taken when the line was created on G-01 or G-02 (or by the Reorder merge, §5.7).

When G-03 renders a line it uses, in this order of preference:

1. The session catalog copy (G-01 §5.7) for the product's name, unit price, and image, if the product is present there — this is always the fresher source; the snapshot on the line is updated to match.
2. The line's own snapshot when the product is absent from the session catalog copy (the hotel removed it from the catalog since the line was added). Such a line is treated as out of stock (§5.4). No "price changed" or "product removed" notice exists in version 1.

### 5.3 Cart line (C02) — available product

Layout, in visual order for Arabic (RTL), start edge to end edge:

1. **Image** — 64 × 64 px, 8 px rounded corners, at the start edge; the product photo scaled to fill. If the image fails to load or the product has none: a gray box of the same size with a centered 20 px generic product icon; no text.
2. **Text column** (fills the middle): 
   - Name — product name in the current language, 14 px, maximum 2 lines, then truncated with an ellipsis ("…"). If the English name is missing, the Arabic name is shown in the English interface too.
   - Unit price — 14 px, regular weight: Arabic "12.00 ر.س", English "SAR 12.00". No "per unit" text, no strikethrough.
3. **End column** (at the end edge, 120 px wide, contents aligned to the end edge):
   - C05 line total — 14 px bold, on top: quantity × unit price, two decimals.
   - Stepper beneath it: [B02 −] [C03 quantity] [B03 +], 40 px tall. B02 is on the left and B03 on the right in both languages (same as G-01, so the two screens' steppers look identical).
   - C04 "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10" under the stepper when the quantity is 10.

Lines are ordered by the moment they were added to the cart, oldest first. Changing a quantity never reorders a line. Lines added by a Reorder merge are appended at the end in the previous order's own line order. Removing a line closes the gap with no animation.

**Maximum number of lines.** Each catalog product appears at most once as a line, and G-01 §7.1 sets no limit on the number of different products; therefore the maximum number of lines equals the number of products in the catalog. The list scrolls; there is no pagination and no "show more".

### 5.4 Cart line (C02) — out-of-stock product

A line is out of stock when its product carries an out-of-stock mark in the session catalog copy (from the loaded catalog, from this screen's availability check, or from M-04), **or** when the product is absent from the session catalog copy (§5.2 item 2).

Differences from the available form:

- The image is shown at 50 % opacity.
- C06 badge "غير متوفر / Out of stock" is shown under the unit price.
- The stepper (B02, C03, B03) and the C04 helper are **hidden**; B04 "إزالة / Remove" takes the stepper's place at the end edge.
- C05 line total is still shown, because the total in C07 still includes this line until it is removed (§7.2, matching G-01 §7.2 so the two screens never disagree about the amount).
- The quantity the guest had chosen is no longer shown anywhere on the line (there is nothing to adjust); it remains stored in the device cart so that G-01's badge suffix "— في سلتك {N} / — {N} in your cart" (G-01-C07) stays correct until the line is removed here.

The only way to clear an out-of-stock line is B04 "Remove". There is no "remove all unavailable" control on G-03 in version 1; with several such lines the guest taps "Remove" on each. (M-04, at submission time, is the place that removes several at once.)

### 5.5 Bottom bar (S03): totals block (C07), helper (C08), Checkout (B05)

Fixed to the bottom edge, 140 px tall in every case: 12 px padding + row 1 (20 px) + row 2 (28 px) + helper row (20 px, always reserved) + B05 (48 px) + 12 px padding.

- **C07 row 1** — label "المنتجات / Items" at the start edge, value at the end edge: the **sum of quantities** across all lines, including out-of-stock lines, as a plain number (example: 3). This is the same number the G-01 cart bar counts (G-01 §7.3).
- **C07 row 2** — label "الإجمالي / Total" at the start edge, value at the end edge: the total per §7.2 (example: "45.00 ر.س" / "SAR 45.00"), 18 px bold.
- **Decision — no other lines.** The totals block has exactly these two rows. There is **no delivery fee, no service charge, no tax line, no discount, and no promo code** in version 1. The catalog unit price is the amount the guest pays per unit, and the Total is the amount staff collect at the door. The pay-on-delivery statement stays on G-01's header only (G-01 §5.1 declares it the only place before checkout), so it is not repeated here.
- **C08 helper** — the only place that explains why "Checkout" is disabled. Exact texts:

| Condition | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| Availability check running (§5.6) | جارٍ التحقق من التوفر… | Checking availability… |
| Exactly 1 line out of stock | أزل المنتج غير المتوفر للمتابعة | Remove the out-of-stock item to continue |
| 2 or more lines out of stock | أزل المنتجات غير المتوفرة للمتابعة | Remove the out-of-stock items to continue |
| Check failed or timed out, no line out of stock | تعذّر التحقق من التوفر — سيتم التحقق عند إرسال الطلب | Could not check availability — it will be checked when you send the order |
| Check succeeded, no line out of stock | *(blank, row reserved)* | *(blank, row reserved)* |

  Precedence when several conditions hold: out-of-stock text first, then "checking", then the failure text.

- **B05 Checkout** — enabled: dark filled, 100 % opacity, label "إتمام الطلب / Checkout". Disabled: the same button at 50 % opacity, same label, no change of text; a tap does nothing (no shake, no toast, no tooltip); the reason is the C08 line above it. The Total is **not** printed inside the button; C07 is the one place for it.

### 5.6 Availability check (the stock re-check)

**When it runs.** Exactly once on **every** entry to G-03 that has at least one line (from G-01, G-02, G-04, M-04, G-06 Reorder, browser reload, direct URL). It does **not** run on a "Checkout" tap, on any stepper or Remove tap, or while the guest stays on the screen. On an entry with zero lines it does not run. Re-entering G-03 (for example G-03 → G-04 → back → G-03) runs it again.

**What the request stands for.** One request carrying the product identifiers of every cart line (and, on a Reorder entry, of every line of the previous order as well). It carries no quantities, reserves nothing, and changes nothing on the server. The response states, for each identifier, one of: available, out of stock, not found. Stock in version 1 is yes/no per product; there is no "only 2 left".

**Timeout.** 5 seconds. A response arriving later is treated as a failure and ignored, except that its out-of-stock marks (if it does arrive) are still written into the session catalog copy for the benefit of the next screen.

**What the guest sees while it runs** (not on a Reorder entry — see §6.1 for that case): the lines are on screen and fully usable (steppers and Remove work); C08 reads "جارٍ التحقق من التوفر… / Checking availability…"; B05 is disabled at 50 % opacity. Nothing else is dimmed and there is no spinner.

**Result handling.**

| Response for a product | Effect |
|---|---|
| available | No change. An existing out-of-stock mark in the session catalog copy is **not** cleared (G-01 §5.7: marks are cleared only by the next catalog fetch). A line already marked therefore stays out of stock until the guest reloads the store; this is accepted for version 1 |
| out of stock, or not found | An out-of-stock mark for the product is written into the session catalog copy (G-01 §5.7). The line switches to its out-of-stock form (§5.4) within 100 ms. If the guest removed the line during the check, the mark is still written and nothing else happens |
| Whole request fails (no connection, server error, 5 s timeout) | No mark is written. C08 shows the failure text. B05 becomes enabled if no line is out of stock. The guest can go to G-04; M-04 at submission is the safety net |

After the response: C08 shows the out-of-stock text if any line is out of stock (B05 disabled), otherwise blank (B05 enabled). If every line was marked, B05 stays disabled until each is removed; when the last line is removed the screen switches to the empty state.

### 5.7 Reorder merge (entry from G-06) and the toast (C10)

G-06 hands G-03 the previous order's lines (product identifier, quantity) and **does not** touch the device cart itself; the merge is owned by G-03 so that availability is decided by one request and one rule. Steps, in order:

1. Show the loading state (§6.1). Send one availability check (§5.6) covering the current cart's products and the previous order's products.
2. When the response arrives (or, on failure or timeout, using the session catalog copy alone as the fallback: a product is "skipped" if it is absent from the copy or carries an out-of-stock mark): for each line of the previous order, in its order —
   - Product available and not marked in the session catalog copy → **add** its quantity to the existing line for that product, or create a new line appended at the end. The result is capped at **10**; any excess is dropped silently, and the line then shows "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10". No toast for the cap.
   - Product out of stock, not found, or absent from the session catalog copy → **skip** it (nothing is added) and count it. Marks are written for out-of-stock / not-found responses as in §5.6.
   - A product in the current cart (not in the previous order) that the response marks out of stock → not skipped (it is already a line); it switches to its out-of-stock form.
3. Write the merged cart to the device once, then show the success state (or the empty state if the cart ended with zero lines) with C08 in the state the check produced.
4. If the count of skipped products is at least 1, show the toast C10 once.

If the guest taps the back arrow or browser back during step 1, the merge is abandoned and the device cart is left exactly as it was before "Reorder"; the response, if it arrives later, only writes marks.

**Toast copy (C10).** N counts **distinct products** skipped, not units.

| N | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | لم تتم إضافة منتج واحد لأنه لم يعد متوفرًا | 1 item was not added because it is no longer available |
| 2 | لم تتم إضافة منتجين لأنهما لم يعودا متوفرين | 2 items were not added because they are no longer available |
| 3–10 | لم تتم إضافة {N} منتجات لأنها لم تعد متوفرة | {N} items were not added because they are no longer available |
| 11 and above | لم تتم إضافة {N} منتجًا لأنها لم تعد متوفرة | {N} items were not added because they are no longer available |

**Toast behavior.** Appears within 100 ms of the success or empty state being shown; stays for **5 seconds**, then fades out over 200 ms; a tap anywhere on it dismisses it at once; it has no button and no icon; it does not block any tap on the screen beneath it; it is shown once per "Reorder" tap and never again on re-entry, back, or reload; it disappears immediately if the guest leaves G-03. Position and size per the C10 row of §4.

### 5.8 Decisions this file settles (not previously settled by CLAUDE.md, the map, or G-01)

1. **Empty state hides the whole bottom bar.** Map §2.2 says "Checkout is disabled while the cart is empty"; G-03 implements this in its strictest form: S03 (and with it B05) is not rendered at all, so the empty state has exactly one control, "Continue shopping". An empty cart never shows a greyed "Checkout" next to "Continue shopping".
2. **Only "−" removes an available line.** No trash icon, no swipe-to-delete, no "Clear cart", no long-press. This keeps the removal gesture identical on G-01, G-02, and G-03. Out-of-stock lines, which have no stepper, get the one dedicated "إزالة / Remove" button.
3. **No confirmation and no undo on removal** (matches G-01 §7.1).
4. **The availability check runs on every entry, never on the Checkout tap**, and "Checkout" waits for it (≤ 5 s). A check failure never blocks checkout; M-04 is the safety net.
5. **G-03 never clears an out-of-stock mark** (consequence of G-01 §5.7).
6. **Out-of-stock lines stay inside Items and Total until removed** (consequence of G-01 §7.2).
7. **Two-row totals block; no fee, service charge, tax, or discount line.**
8. **The Reorder merge is executed by G-03**, with a single availability check before merging, a loading state while it waits, and a cap of 10 per line without a message.
9. **The toast counts distinct products, lasts 5 seconds, sits above the bottom bar, and is dismissed by a tap.**
10. **Snapshot fallback.** A line whose product is missing from the session catalog copy is rendered from the line's own snapshot and treated as out of stock.
11. **Lines are never tappable as a whole**; there is no path from G-03 to G-02.
12. **Line order is add order**, Reorder appends, quantity changes never reorder.
13. **History: G-04 is always popped on return to G-03**, so back from G-03 always goes to the original opener; "Continue shopping" resets the history to G-01 alone.

### 5.9 What is deliberately not on this screen

- No language toggle (map §2.3 rule 6), no "My orders" button, no active-order banner (G-01 only). G-03 never mentions an in-flight order.
- No room-number field, no order notes, no payment choice — all on G-04 (map §4 decision 4; locked decisions 1–3).
- No search, no product recommendations, no "save for later", no promo code, no fee/tax lines.
- No "Clear cart", no swipe-to-delete, no trash icon on available lines, no undo.
- No quantity typing, no "only N left" stock counts, no price-change notice.
- No toast other than C10 after Reorder; adding, removing, and quantity changes give feedback only by the line and the totals block changing.
- No total inside the Checkout button.
- No pull-to-refresh; the availability check runs on entry only.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

Triggered by: (a) browser reload on G-03 or a direct open of the G-03 URL (the session catalog copy does not exist yet); (b) entry from G-06 "Reorder" (§5.7 step 1).

On screen:
- S01 header with B01 working and C01 "السلة / Cart" shown.
- C11: 3 gray line placeholders pulsing between 100 % and 60 % opacity every 1.5 seconds. No text in the list area.
- S03 bottom bar hidden. C10 toast hidden.

Rules:
- Case (a): the catalog request (same as G-01's) has a 10-second limit; on failure or timeout G-08 (connection/server variant) replaces G-03. On success the lines render (success state, or empty state if the cart has no lines) and the availability check starts as in §5.6, with C08 "Checking availability…" and B05 disabled.
- Case (b): the loading state lasts until the availability check responds or its 5-second timeout elapses; on timeout the fallback merge runs (§5.7 step 2). The loading state in case (b) never lasts longer than 5 seconds. If the device holds no session catalog copy (Reorder tapped right after a reload landed on G-06), case (a) runs first, then case (b).
- Back during either case: leaves G-03 (case (b): merge abandoned, cart unchanged).

### 6.2 Empty

Triggered by: entry with zero lines in the device cart; removing the last line by "−" or "Remove"; a Reorder merge that produced zero lines; M-04 "Remove them and continue" that emptied the cart.

On screen:
- S01 header complete (B01 goes to the opener).
- S02, S03 hidden. No availability check.
- C09, centered in the list area: 48 px gray cart outline icon, then
  - Line 1 (18 px, bold): "سلتك فارغة" / "Your cart is empty"
  - Line 2 (14 px): "أضف منتجات من المتجر لبدء طلبك" / "Add products from the store to start your order"
- B06 "متابعة التسوق / Continue shopping" 24 px under the text.
- C10 toast shown on top of this state only when it was triggered by a Reorder merge that skipped at least one item (§5.7), positioned 16 px above the viewport's bottom edge.

Re-entry after the cart was emptied elsewhere (M-04 removal, successful submission followed by any route that reaches G-03 again, or removal in another browser tab on the same device): the empty state is shown exactly as above; no leftover lines, no toast, no error text.

### 6.3 Error

None of these shows a toast, and there is no typed input, so there are no field error messages.

1. **Availability check fails or times out** (5 s): C08 shows "تعذّر التحقق من التوفر — سيتم التحقق عند إرسال الطلب / Could not check availability — it will be checked when you send the order"; B05 is enabled unless a line is already out of stock; no marks are written; lines keep their current form. The next entry to G-03 tries again; there is no Retry control on this screen.
2. **Catalog fetch fails during loading case (a)**: G-08 replaces G-03. All copy for that case lives in the G-08 file.
3. **Reorder merge with a failed check**: the fallback merge from the session catalog copy runs (§5.7); C08 shows the failure text; C10 is shown if anything was skipped.
4. **A line image fails to load**: gray 64 × 64 px placeholder with the generic icon; the line stays fully functional.

### 6.4 Success

At least one line exists and the screen is not loading.

On screen:
- S01 header complete.
- S02 with one C02 per cart line in add order; available lines with their stepper, out-of-stock lines in the form of §5.4; list scrolled to the top on every entry.
- S03 with C07 (Items and Total correct per §7.2 and §7.3), C08 in the state the availability check produced, B05 enabled or disabled per its row in §4.
- C10 toast only after a Reorder merge that skipped at least one item.

Feedback after any tap on B02, B03, or B04: the line (or its removal), C05, and C07 update within 100 ms; nothing scrolls, nothing animates apart from the text changing and the lines beneath a removed line moving up. If the tap made the last line disappear, the screen switches to the empty state in place, also within 100 ms.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on this screen. The rules below govern the bounded values G-03 displays or changes.

### 7.1 Quantity per line (changed with B02 / B03)

- Type: whole number. Minimum 1; "−" at 1 removes the line (no confirmation). Maximum **10**: at 10 "+" is disabled (50 % opacity, tap does nothing) and C04 "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10" is shown. No toast, no modal at the maximum (same as G-01 §7.1).
- The quantity shown is always the quantity stored in the device cart for that product; G-01 and G-02 show the same number.
- Stepper taps are device-side only: no request is sent on "+", "−", or "Remove".
- **300 ms lock per line:** after any tap on B02, B03, or B04, that line ignores further taps for 300 ms while it re-renders. A rapid double-tap on "+" changes the quantity by exactly 1; a rapid double-tap on "−" at quantity 2 leaves 1, never removes the line. Buttons on other lines and B05 are not affected by the lock.
- Reorder merge cap: existing quantity + previous order's quantity, capped at 10; the excess is dropped silently.
- Out-of-stock lines have no stepper; their stored quantity is unchanged until the line is removed.

### 7.2 Prices, line totals, and the total

- Unit price: from the session catalog copy when the product is present there, otherwise the line's snapshot (§5.2). Two decimals always.
- Line total (C05) = quantity × unit price, rounded to two decimals, half up.
- Total (C07 row 2) = sum of all line totals, including out-of-stock lines until they are removed (same rule as G-01 §7.2, so the G-01 cart bar and G-03 always show the same amount).
- Currency label from store settings: Arabic after the amount with a space ("45.00 ر.س"); English before the amount with a space ("SAR 45.00"). Western digits 0–9 in both languages.

### 7.3 Items count (C07 row 1)

Sum of quantities across all lines, including out-of-stock lines, shown as a plain number with no unit word (example: "3"). It equals the number the G-01 cart bar would describe with the wording in G-01 §7.3.

### 7.4 Availability check bounds

- Runs once per entry with at least one line; never on Checkout tap; timeout 5 seconds; results per §5.6.
- On a Reorder entry it also covers the previous order's products; the loading state waits for it, at most 5 seconds.

### 7.5 Toast bounds

- Shown only when a Reorder merge skipped at least 1 product; N = distinct products skipped; copy per §5.7; 5 seconds visible, 200 ms fade, tap to dismiss; at most one toast at a time (a second Reorder while a toast is visible replaces it).

### 7.6 Text limits

- Product name on a line: 2 lines maximum, ellipsis after that.
- Toast text: 2 lines maximum at 14 px; the longest string ("11 items were not added because they are no longer available" / its Arabic form) fits in 2 lines at 358 px width.
- C08 helper: one line at 14 px; the longest string (the failure text) fits in one line at 358 px width in both languages; if the browser's text scaling makes it wrap, the bar grows and S02's bottom padding grows with it.

### 7.7 Sizes and touch targets

- Every tappable element (B01–B06, the toast) has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px. Visible shapes smaller than that (B02/B03 40 × 40 px, B04 40 px tall) have the tap area extended around the visible shape, as in G-01 §7.6; B01, B05, and B06 are already 44 px or taller in their visible shape.
- Minimum text size: 11 px (C04 "Max 10", kept identical to G-01). All other copy is 14 px or larger: names, unit prices, line totals, badge, helper, Items row, toast, empty-state line 2 at 14 px; quantity and Checkout label 16 px; title, Total row, and empty-state line 1 at 18 px.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled (same rule as G-01 §7.6).

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Tapping the floating cart bar on G-01 (or G-02) opens G-03 with the header (chevron B01 at the start edge, title "السلة / Cart" centered), one C02 line per cart line in the order the lines were added, and the bottom bar S03 showing "المنتجات / Items", "الإجمالي / Total", and the "إتمام الطلب / Checkout" button.

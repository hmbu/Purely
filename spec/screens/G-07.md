# G-07 — My orders (طلباتي)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/screens-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decision 4 (this screen is that decision made visible) and decisions 1, 5, 7; `/spec/screens-map.md` §1.3 (no count badge; the banner on G-01 is the only in-flight indicator), §2.2 (G-07 row), §2.3 rules 1, 5 and 6, §4 decisions 8 and 15; `/spec/screens/M-01.md` §7.4 step 1 (the shape of the record this screen reads); `/spec/screens/G-01.md` §5.2 (canonical status labels and the once-per-load status fetch rule).
Conventions reused verbatim from G-01 (not re-decided here): Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label from store settings, after the amount in Arabic ("45.00 ر.س") and before it in English ("SAR 45.00"), two decimals always; item-count wording of G-01 §7.3; every tappable element has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px; body copy never smaller than 14 px; browser zoom and text scaling never disabled; no toast; the five status labels of G-01 §5.2.

There is no guest login (locked decision 4), so the hotel cannot show a guest "your orders" from the server. The only copy of the guest's order history is the record M-01 writes on the device at the moment of a successful submission. G-07 is the one place where those records are gathered, and everything on it follows from that: it reads the device, needs no catalog, never shows anything the device does not hold, and is honest in its empty state about what that means.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** G-07
- **Name:** طلباتي / My orders
- **Type:** Full guest screen with its own URL and history entry. Reached from G-01's "My orders" button and by back from G-06 when G-06 was opened from here.
- **Language:** the language currently saved on the device (locked decision 7; map §4 decision 12). G-07 has no language toggle; the toggle lives on G-01 and G-08 only.

---

## 2. Purpose

List every order this device has sent, newest first, with its status at a glance, and open any of them for tracking.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 Entry (how the guest arrives)

Every entry renders the list from the **device order list** (the records written by M-01 §7.4 step 1, with statuses updated since by G-01 §5.2, G-06, and this screen's own refresh in §5.5). Reading the device list is synchronous and takes less than 100 ms, so the list is drawn on the first frame; there is no skeleton (§6.1). No catalog request is ever sent from G-07: the records carry the product names in both languages, so the catalog is not needed, and G-07 therefore never leads to G-08.

| From | Trigger | What G-07 shows on arrival |
|---|---|---|
| G-01 Store | Tap G-01-B02 "طلباتي / My orders" | The list (§6.4) scrolled to the top, or the empty state (§6.2) when the device holds no readable record. G-07 is pushed onto the history above G-01. The status refresh (§5.5) starts at once |
| G-06 Order tracking | Back arrow or browser back, when G-06 was opened from G-07 (map §2.2 G-06 row) | G-06 is popped; G-07 is re-rendered from the device list at the **same scroll position** the guest left. Because G-06 always writes the live status it fetched into the record (map §2.3 rule 5), a row whose order was just cancelled on G-06 shows "ملغى / Cancelled" immediately. The status refresh starts again (one request; the rule in §5.5 is "on every entry", with no exception) |
| Browser reload on G-07, or the G-07 URL opened directly | Browser load | Same as arrival from G-01: list or empty state, scrolled to the top, status refresh started. There is no G-01 beneath in the history; the back arrow then behaves per §3.2 |
| Any of the above with zero readable records on the device | — | Empty state (§6.2). No status refresh (there is nothing to refresh) |

**Trim on entry (§5.4).** Before the list is drawn, on every entry, the device order list is trimmed to the cap of 20 records. This is the only moment anything is ever deleted from the device order list, and it happens before the first frame, so the guest never sees a row disappear.

### 3.2 Exit (where each action leads)

| Action on G-07 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap an order row (C02) | G-06 Order tracking for that order, pushed above G-07. G-06 is told that G-07 opened it, so its back arrow returns here (map §2.2 G-06 row). The tap opens G-06 once; further taps within 300 ms on any row are ignored (§7.4) |
| Tap the back arrow (B01) or browser back | G-01, list scrolled to the top (G-01 §3.1). When G-01 is beneath G-07 in the history it is popped to; after a reload or direct URL open (no G-01 beneath), G-01 is loaded afresh and the history is reset so G-01 is the only store entry, exactly as G-03 "Continue shopping" does (G-03 §3.2) |
| Tap "تصفح المتجر / Browse the store" (B02, empty state only) | G-01, list scrolled to the top; the history is reset so that G-01 is the only store entry (browser back from G-01 then leaves the store, as G-01 §3.1 states) |
| Status refresh response arrives (§5.5) | Stays on G-07; the affected pills (C04) update in place. No navigation, no scroll change |

G-07 never leads to G-02, G-03, G-04, G-05, or G-08, and never opens a modal. There is no "Reorder" here (map §4 decision 8); Reorder lives on G-06 only.

## 5. Content

### 5.1 Header (S01)

Layout, in visual order for Arabic (RTL): [B01 back arrow] … [C01 "طلباتي"] … [empty]. In English the arrow moves to the left edge with the direction; the title stays centered. The end edge is deliberately empty: no language toggle (map §2.3 rule 6), no "clear history", no filter, no count.

### 5.2 Order row (C02) — what a row shows and why

Each row has exactly three lines. Every value comes from the device record written by M-01 §7.4 step 1; nothing on the row is fetched.

| Line | Start side | End side |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | C03 order number — "طلب رقم 1042" / "Order 1042", 16 px bold | C04 status pill, 24 px tall, 14 px label |
| 2 | C05 date and time — "اليوم 14:05" / "Today 14:05" or "21/09/2026 09:12", 14 px | C06 total — "36.00 ر.س" / "SAR 36.00", 14 px bold |
| 3 | C07 item count · room — "منتجان · الغرفة 0305" / "2 items · Room 0305", 14 px | (empty) |

Vertical spacing inside the row: 16 px padding top and bottom, 8 px between lines; with 16 px and 14 px text this gives a row of about 100 px, which is the minimum height. A row never grows beyond one line per slot: the order number, the date, the total, the count, and the room number are all bounded values (§7) and the status pill never wraps.

Each value, decided with one reason:

1. **Order number — shown.** It is the only identifier the guest and the hotel share: G-05 shows it once, staff see it on their side, and reception can look it up if the guest phones. A list of orders without their numbers cannot be talked about.
2. **Status — shown, as a pill.** Status is the question a guest opens this list to answer ("is it coming?"); making them tap into every order to find out would make the list a table of contents, not a status list.
3. **Date and time — shown.** A stay lasts several days and the same phone may be used on a later stay; the time is what tells two "2 items, SAR 36.00" orders apart. "اليوم / Today" replaces the date for the current day because at a glance "today 14:05" is faster to read than a full date (§7.2).
4. **Total — shown.** The guest pays at the door; the amount is what they check against what staff ask for, and it is a one-number summary of the order.
5. **Item count — shown, names not shown.** The count fits on one line for any order; product names would need truncation rules and a taller row, and the full line list is one tap away on G-06.
6. **Room number — shown.** It is the one value that decides where staff go. A guest who typed the wrong room (tester persona 4) sees it here without opening the order, and a guest whose phone still holds orders from an earlier stay (or a different room on this stay) sees which room each order went to. It is shown exactly as typed, leading zeros kept (G-04 §7.1).
7. **Payment method and cash amount — not shown.** They do not help identify an order in a list; G-06 shows them.
8. **Product images — not shown.** No image is stored in the record (M-01 §7.4 step 1), and fetching the catalog just to decorate a list would give G-07 a way to fail that it otherwise does not have.

The row has **no chevron, no arrow, no "View" button**. The whole row is the target (minimum 100 × 358 px) and the pressed-state background is the affordance. Reason: the end edge of lines 1 and 2 is already occupied by the pill and the total; a chevron would crowd the pill on the long "تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير / Accepted & preparing" label.

### 5.3 What a row looks like in each of the five statuses

The layout and every value are identical in all five; only the pill (C04) changes. Nothing is signalled by color alone: an active status is a filled pill, a finished status an outlined one, and the label itself is always present.

| Last known status | Pill fill | Pill text | Anything else on the row |
|---|---|---|---|
| New — جديد | Dark filled | Light | Nothing else changes |
| Accepted & preparing — تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير | Dark filled | Light | Nothing else changes |
| On the way — في الطريق | Dark filled | Light | Nothing else changes |
| Delivered — تم التوصيل | Outlined (1 px dark border), no fill | Dark | Nothing else changes |
| Cancelled — ملغى | Outlined (1 px gray border), no fill | Gray (at least 4.5:1 against white) | Nothing else changes: the total is **not** struck through, the row is **not** dimmed, and the row stays tappable. The staff's optional cancellation reason (map §4 decision 15) is not shown on the row; G-06 shows it |

The three active statuses share one look and the two finished statuses share another, so a guest scanning the list sees at once which rows are still moving without reading every label.

### 5.4 Sort order, cap, and deletion — decision

**Sort.** One list, no sections. Rows are sorted by the record's order time (M-01 §7.4 step 1: the server's timestamp, else the device time at receipt), **newest first**. Two records with the same time (possible only when both fell back to device time within the same second) are ordered by order number, higher first. The sort never changes because of a status change: a refreshed status updates the pill in place and the row stays where it is.

Active orders are **not** split into a separate section. Reason: a device holds few orders (20 at most, and in practice a handful per stay), the active ones are visually distinct by their filled pill (§5.3), and the most recent active order is already surfaced on G-01's banner; two sections would add two headings, two empty cases, and a rule for a row that moves between them while the guest is looking.

**Cap: 20 records.** The device order list holds at most **20** records. The number is chosen so that a full stay — several orders a day for a week — fits without loss, while the list stays short enough to scroll in one or two swipes.

**Trim rule** (applied by G-07 on every entry, before the first frame, §3.1):

1. While the device holds more than 20 records: delete the **oldest record (by order time) whose status is Delivered or Cancelled**.
2. If more than 20 records remain and none of them is Delivered or Cancelled: delete the oldest record regardless of status. (Twenty simultaneously active orders on one phone is not a real situation, but the rule must have no gap; an "active" record that old is a record whose status was never updated.)
3. Repeat until 20 or fewer remain.

Deleted records are gone from the device; they cannot be recovered on the device, and the guest is not asked. This is the **only** deletion of order records in version 1:

- G-07 offers no "delete", "clear history", swipe-to-delete, or long-press.
- No record is deleted because of its age; a 6-month-old order from an earlier stay stays on the list until the cap pushes it out. (Its room number on the row tells the guest which stay it belonged to.)
- No screen other than G-07 trims the list. Between two visits to G-07 the device may briefly hold more than 20 records (M-01 writes one per success and never trims); G-01's banner and status fetch are unaffected by that, and the next entry to G-07 trims.
- C08 tells the guest the cap exists, and only when it can have had an effect (exactly 20 rows shown).

### 5.5 Status refresh on entry — decision

G-07 shows the **last known** status of every order from the device record, on the first frame, and then refreshes it **once per entry** with a single request, the same request G-01 §5.2 sends on load:

- **When:** on every entry per §3.1 (from G-01, back from G-06, reload, direct URL), started as soon as the list is drawn. Skipped when the device holds no order whose last known status is New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way (nothing can change for Delivered and Cancelled; they are final under locked decision 5).
- **What it asks:** the live status of every order on the device whose last known status is one of the three active ones.
- **On response:** each returned status is written into the record (map §2.3 rule 5) and the row's pill (C04) is updated in place within 100 ms. Rows do not move (§5.4). No sound, no highlight, no toast. If a status changed from active to Delivered or Cancelled, the pill changes from filled to outlined, nothing else.
- **On failure** (no connection, server error, unreadable response, or no response within **10 seconds** of sending — the same limit G-01 uses for the catalog): the rows keep their last known status and **no error text appears anywhere**, exactly as G-01 §6.3 case 2. A response that arrives after the 10 seconds, or after the guest has left G-07, is discarded for the screen; it is still written into the records, since it is the device's own data.
- **An order the server reports as unknown** (not found for its number) keeps its last known status on the row; G-07 does not delete it and shows no message. G-06 owns that case ("Order not found", map §2.2 G-06 row) when the guest taps the row.
- **No polling** while the guest stays on G-07, and no pull-to-refresh. Live polling belongs to G-06 (map §2.3 rule 5). A guest who wants the freshest status of one order taps its row.
- **No "updating…" indicator** and no "last updated" time (the latter is already deferred in `/spec/backlog.md` G-01-L4). The pill is either the last known or the live value; the guest cannot tell which, and the label "last known" is never rendered.

This keeps G-07 consistent with G-01 §5.2: one fetch per load, silent fallback to the saved copy, no polling.

### 5.6 Date and time on the row (C05)

- Source: the record's order time (M-01 §7.4 step 1). M-01 guarantees the value exists, so no fallback is rendered.
- Displayed in the **device's local time zone**, 24-hour clock, Western digits, in both languages: "14:05", never "2:05 PM" and never "٢:٠٥ م". One clock for both languages avoids a localized AM/PM marker and matches how hotel receipts print time.
- Date: "DD/MM/YYYY" with Western digits in both languages ("21/09/2026"). Numeric in both languages so no month name has to be localized for Arabic regional variants. The date and time string is laid out left-to-right in both languages (a digit string has no reading direction to flip, as M-01 §1 states for the room number).
- When the record's date (in the device's local time zone) equals the device's current date, the date is replaced by "اليوم / Today": "اليوم 14:05" / "Today 14:05". No "أمس / Yesterday" and no relative phrases ("2 hours ago"); one word for today is enough to separate the current day from the rest of the stay, and a relative time would have to be re-rendered every minute.
- The "Today" decision is evaluated at render time; a list left open across midnight is not re-rendered until the next entry.

### 5.7 The honest limitation of locked decision 4 — what the guest sees and what G-07 does about it

The device order list is stored in the browser's persistent storage for the store's site on this device. It is not on the server under any name, phone number, or room; nothing on the server links an order to a person. Consequences, each with what G-07 does:

| Situation | What G-07 shows | What G-07 does about it |
|---|---|---|
| The guest opens the store on a **different device** (a companion's phone, a tablet, a laptop) | The empty state, or only the orders that other device sent | Nothing can be merged: G-07 has no account, no recovery code, no "find my order by room number", and none is added (locked decision 4). The empty state's lines 2 and 3 say where orders appear and how to ask about one from another device |
| The guest **clears the browser's site data** (or the browser does it under storage pressure) | The empty state; the orders are gone from this device and do not come back | Same as above. The cart is stored in the same place and is gone too (map §2.3 rule 4); G-01 then shows every card with "Add". Any order still in flight is unaffected on the hotel's side and is still delivered; the guest simply cannot track it from this device any more, and the G-01 banner no longer shows it |
| The guest uses a **private / incognito tab** | Orders appear while the tab is open; after the private session ends the list is empty | Nothing; the browser owns this behavior. G-07 does not detect private mode and shows no warning |
| The guest uses a **different browser on the same phone** (for example the QR scanner opened an in-app browser and the guest later opens the store in the main browser) | Each browser has its own list; the second one is empty | Nothing. The empty-state copy covers it; no explanation of in-app browsers is written, because the guest's fix (reception, or the same browser) is the same either way |
| The same phone on a **later stay** | The old orders are still listed (up to the cap), each with the room number it went to | This is why C07 carries the room number (§5.2 item 6). No "new stay" reset exists |

What the guest can always do without the list: the order number was shown on G-05 at submission, and the hotel has the order under that number and the room number; reception can answer from either. The empty state's third line points to the room phone for exactly this reason. G-07 never claims that orders are "synced", "saved to your account", or "linked to your room", because none of that is true.

### 5.8 What is deliberately not on this screen

- No "Reorder" on rows (map §4 decision 8): it lives on G-06 only, so the merge, the availability check, and the toast are specified once.
- No "Cancel" on rows: cancellation needs the live status check and the confirmation modal M-02, both on G-06 (locked decision 6).
- No language toggle, no "My orders" button, no active-order banner, no floating cart bar, no hotel name. G-07 does not mention the cart at all.
- No search, filter, date grouping, section headings, "active / previous" split, or count of orders in the header.
- No delete, clear-history, swipe, or long-press action; no age-based deletion (§5.4).
- No account, sign-in, recovery code, "find my order", or "send my orders to another device" (locked decision 4; §5.7).
- No pull-to-refresh, no "updating…" spinner, no "last updated" time (§5.5).
- No product names or images on rows (§5.2 items 5 and 8).
- No payment method or cash amount on rows (§5.2 item 7).
- No toast (G-01 convention). No modal opens from G-07.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

**Not rendered, by rule.** The list is read from the device synchronously and drawn on the first frame of every entry; there is no network request to wait for before drawing, so there is no skeleton and no spinner. The status refresh (§5.5) runs after the list is drawn and is invisible while it runs: the rows show their last known status and update in place when the response arrives. If a build ever needs more than 100 ms to read the device list, it must still draw the header first and then the rows, never a blank screen with a spinner.

### 6.2 Empty

Triggered by: entry when the device holds **zero readable records** (never ordered from this browser on this device; site data cleared; a different device or browser; a private session that ended — §5.7). Never triggered by the trim (the trim stops at 20, never at 0) and never by a status change.

On screen:
- S01 header complete (B01 goes to G-01, C01 "طلباتي / My orders").
- S02 hidden. No status refresh.
- C09, centered in the area under the header: 48 px gray receipt-outline icon, then
  - Line 1 (18 px, bold): "لا توجد طلبات على هذا الجهاز" / "No orders on this device"
  - Line 2 (14 px): "تظهر هنا الطلبات المرسلة من هذا الجهاز فقط" / "Only orders sent from this device appear here"
  - Line 3 (14 px): "للسؤال عن طلب أرسلته من جهاز آخر، اتصل بالاستقبال من هاتف الغرفة" / "To ask about an order sent from another device, call reception from your room phone"
  - 8 px between lines; lines 2 and 3 may wrap to two lines each within the 358 px content width.
- B02 "تصفح المتجر / Browse the store", 24 px under line 3.

The copy is written for both guests who land here: the one who has never ordered (line 1 and the button are all they need) and the one whose orders are on another device or were cleared (lines 2 and 3 tell them why and what to do). The screen cannot tell the two apart and does not try.

### 6.3 Error

There are three error situations. None of them renders error text on G-07, and none leads to G-08.

1. **Status refresh fails or times out** (§5.5): rows keep their last known status; no text, no icon, no toast. The next entry tries again. Identical to G-01 §6.3 case 2.
2. **A record cannot be read** (missing order number, missing order time, missing total, unreadable lines): that record is **skipped** — not shown, not counted toward the cap, and not deleted (G-07 deletes only by the trim rule). If every record is unreadable the empty state is shown. Records are written only by M-01, which guarantees the shape, so this case exists as a rule for the technical team, not as a state the guest is expected to meet.
3. **The device storage itself cannot be read** (browser storage disabled or failing): treated exactly as zero records → the empty state. No "storage is disabled" message in version 1; the empty state's copy already points to reception.

Nothing is typed on G-07, so there are no field error messages.

### 6.4 Success

The device holds at least one readable record.

On screen:
- S01 header complete.
- S02 with one C02 row per record after the trim, sorted per §5.4, each showing C03–C07 with the pill per §5.3 from the record's last known status; scrolled to the top (or to the remembered position when returning from G-06).
- C08 under the last row only when exactly 20 rows are listed.
- The status refresh in flight (invisible) if any row is active; pills update in place on response.

Row-tap feedback: the pressed background appears on touch-down and G-06 opens within 100 ms of touch-up. Nothing else moves, no toast.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on this screen. The rules below govern the bounded values G-07 displays and the taps it accepts.

### 7.1 Values shown on a row — source and format

| Value | Source (record field, M-01 §7.4 step 1) | Format on the row |
|---|---|---|
| Order number | Server order number, as stored | Printed exactly as stored, Western digits, no "#", no thousands separator; identical to G-05 and G-06. Bounded at 12 characters by G-05 (if G-05 sets a different bound, that bound applies here too; G-07 never truncates the number) |
| Status | Last known status (New / Accepted & preparing / On the way / Delivered / Cancelled) | The canonical label of G-01 §5.2 in the current language. A record with any other status value is treated as unreadable (§6.3 case 2) |
| Order time | Order time (server timestamp, else device time at receipt) | Per §5.6 and §7.2 |
| Total | Total, as stored at submission | Two decimals, currency label from store settings, position per G-01 §7.2 ("36.00 ر.س" / "SAR 36.00"). The total is **never recomputed** from the lines and never re-priced against the current catalog; it is the amount the guest agreed to pay |
| Item count | Sum of the quantities of the record's lines | Wording of G-01 §7.3 in the current language ("منتج واحد / 1 item", "منتجان / 2 items", "3 منتجات / 3 items", "11 منتجًا / 11 items") |
| Room number | Room number string, as sent | Exactly as stored, leading zeros kept ("0305" stays "0305", G-04 §7.1); Western digits; laid out left-to-right in both languages; prefixed by "الغرفة " / "Room " |

Language switch: every label on the row (order prefix, count wording, currency position, "Today", status label) follows the language saved on the device at render time. Nothing on a row is a product name, so the record's bilingual names are not used on G-07 (they exist for G-06).

### 7.2 Date and time format

- Time: `HH:MM`, 24-hour, two digits each, Western digits, device local time zone. Examples: "09:12", "14:05", "00:40".
- Date: `DD/MM/YYYY`, Western digits, device local time zone. Example: "21/09/2026".
- Today rule: when the record's local date equals the device's current local date, the date part is replaced by "اليوم" / "Today". Otherwise the full date is shown; no "Yesterday", no weekday name, no month name, no relative phrase.
- Composition: "{اليوم | DD/MM/YYYY} {HH:MM}" with one space between the two parts, in both languages; the whole string is laid out left-to-right.
- If the device clock is wrong, the "Today" comparison is wrong in the same way; G-07 does not correct for it and does not use the server clock for the comparison.

### 7.3 Cap and sort (restated as rules)

- Maximum records kept: 20. Trim per §5.4 on every entry, before the first frame.
- Sort: order time descending; tie-break by order number descending.
- C08 is rendered when and only when the row count is exactly 20.

### 7.4 Tap handling

- A tap anywhere inside a row (including on the pill, the total, or the separators inside the row) opens G-06 for that order. There is no non-tappable region inside a row.
- After a tap on any row, all rows ignore further taps for **300 ms** while G-06 opens, so a double-tap opens G-06 once. (Same debounce figure as G-01 §7.1.)
- A scroll gesture that starts on a row does not count as a tap (the pressed state is shown only if the finger does not move more than 10 px before release).
- B01 and B02 respond to a single tap; there is no long-press action anywhere on G-07.

### 7.5 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: C01 18 px bold; C03 16 px bold; C04 label 14 px; C05 14 px; C06 14 px bold; C07 14 px; C08 14 px; C09 line 1 18 px bold, lines 2–3 14 px; B02 label 16 px bold. **Minimum text size on G-07 is 14 px** (G-02 §5.8 item 17 convention). Nothing on this screen is smaller.
- Row: minimum 100 px tall, full content width (358 px at 390 px viewport); the tap area is the whole row. B01 44 × 44 px; B02 240 × 48 px.
- The status pill's label never wraps and never truncates; the pill grows to fit its label (the longest, "تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير", is about 170 px at 14 px, leaving at least 150 px for the order number on the same line at 358 px width). If a language's label and the order number cannot share the line at an enlarged text size, the pill moves to its own line under the order number and the row grows; nothing is cut.
- Contrast: all text at least 4.5:1 against its background, including the gray pill text of the Cancelled state and the gray C08. Nothing is signalled by color alone (§5.3). The screen must read correctly in grayscale.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled (G-01 §7.6). With enlarged text, rows grow in height and lines wrap; nothing is truncated or hidden.
- Screen reader: on arrival the title "طلباتي / My orders" is announced, then the list with its row count ("{N} orders"); each row is one focusable item whose accessible name is, in order: the order number, the status label, the date and time, the total, the item count, the room number — so a guest listening hears the status second, not last. The room number is read digit by digit (M-01 §5.2 convention). When a pill updates from the status refresh, the change is announced once ("Order 1042: On the way" / "طلب رقم 1042: في الطريق"). In the empty state, line 1 is announced first, then lines 2–3, then the button.
- Motion: no animation on G-07 other than the pressed-state background; nothing to reduce.

### 7.6 Storage rules (for the technical team; no backend)

- The device order list lives in the browser's persistent storage for the store's site, the same place as the cart and the language choice (map §2.3 rules 4–6). G-07 reads it on every entry and writes to it only (a) statuses returned by its refresh (§5.5) and (b) deletions by the trim (§5.4).
- G-07 never writes a new record, never edits a record's order number, time, room number, lines, notes, payment, amount, or total, and never changes a Delivered or Cancelled status.
- G-07 sends no request other than the status refresh. It sends nothing that identifies the guest (locked decision 4): the request carries order numbers only.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Tapping G-01-B02 opens G-07 with the header (B01, C01 "طلباتي / My orders") and, if the device holds at least one readable order record, the list S02 scrolled to the top on the first painted frame; no skeleton and no spinner are shown at any time.
2. G-07 sends no catalog request on any entry (from G-01, back from G-06, reload, direct URL) and never shows G-08.
3. Rows are ordered by order time, newest first; two rows with the same time are ordered by order number, higher first; a status change never reorders rows.
4. Every row shows, in three lines: "طلب رقم {n} / Order {n}" at the start of line 1 and the status pill at its end; the date-time at the start of line 2 and the total at its end; "{count} · الغرفة {room} / {count} · Room {room}" on line 3.
5. The order number on a row is character-for-character the number shown on G-05 and G-06 for that order.
6. The status pill shows exactly one of the five labels of G-01 §5.2 in the current language; New, Accepted & preparing, and On the way are rendered as dark filled pills with light text; Delivered as an outlined pill with dark text; Cancelled as an outlined pill with gray text; no other visual difference exists between rows of different statuses (no strikethrough, no dimming).
7. The total on a row equals the total stored in the record at submission, with two decimals and the currency label positioned per G-01 §7.2, and is unchanged by later catalog price changes.
8. The item count on a row equals the sum of the record's line quantities, worded per G-01 §7.3 in the current language.
9. The room number on a row is exactly the string sent with the order, including leading zeros ("0305" is shown as "0305"), prefixed by "الغرفة " / "Room ".
10. A record whose order time falls on the device's current local date shows "اليوم {HH:MM} / Today {HH:MM}"; any other record shows "{DD/MM/YYYY} {HH:MM}"; times are 24-hour with two digits, dates are numeric, all digits Western, the string laid out left-to-right in both languages.
11. Tapping anywhere inside a row (including on the pill or the total) shows the pressed background on touch-down and opens G-06 for that order within 100 ms of touch-up; back from that G-06 returns to G-07 at the same scroll position.
12. Two taps on rows within 300 ms open G-06 exactly once.
13. A row whose status is Delivered or Cancelled is tappable and opens G-06 exactly like an active row.
14. No row contains a chevron, an icon, a "Reorder" control, a "Cancel" control, or any tappable element other than the row itself.
15. Tapping B01 or using the browser back gesture goes to G-01 at the top of the list; after a reload or direct URL open of G-07, the same action loads G-01 afresh and leaves G-01 as the only store entry in the history.
16. On every entry, when at least one record's last known status is New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way, exactly one status request is sent, covering those orders only; when no record is active, no request is sent.
17. When the status response arrives within 10 seconds, each returned status is written into its record and the corresponding pill changes in place within 100 ms, with no reorder, no scroll change, no toast, and no highlight.
18. When the status request fails, is unreadable, or has not answered within 10 seconds, every pill keeps its last known label and no error text, icon, or toast appears anywhere on G-07.
19. While the guest remains on G-07, no further status request is sent (no polling); there is no pull-to-refresh.
20. Returning to G-07 from a G-06 on which the guest cancelled the order shows that row with the "ملغى / Cancelled" outlined pill on the first frame, before any refresh response.
21. When the device holds more than 20 records on entry, G-07 deletes, before the first frame, the oldest Delivered or Cancelled records until 20 remain (and, only if none of those exist, the oldest records of any status), and then shows exactly 20 rows.
22. When exactly 20 rows are shown, C08 "يحتفظ هذا الجهاز بآخر 20 طلبًا فقط / This device keeps only your last 20 orders" is rendered under the last row; with 19 or fewer rows it is not rendered.
23. No control on G-07 deletes, hides, or archives a record; records are never deleted because of their age; no screen other than G-07 deletes records.
24. When the device holds zero readable records, G-07 shows the header, C09 with the three lines of §6.2 verbatim, and B02 "تصفح المتجر / Browse the store"; S02 is not rendered and no status request is sent.
25. Tapping B02 opens G-01 at the top of the list and resets the history so that browser back from G-01 leaves the store.
26. After the browser's site data for the store is cleared, opening G-07 shows the empty state; after the store is opened on a second device that never ordered, G-07 on that device shows the empty state; in both cases no account, code, or recovery control is offered.
27. A record missing its order number, order time, total, or lines, or carrying a status outside the five canonical values, is not shown, is not counted toward the cap, and is not deleted; if all records are unreadable, or the storage cannot be read, the empty state is shown.
28. Switching the language on G-01 and returning to G-07 re-renders every label on every row (order prefix, status label, "Today", count wording, currency position, "Room") in the new language; the record values themselves are unchanged.
29. G-07 shows no language toggle, no "My orders" button, no active-order banner, no floating cart bar, no product names, no product images, no payment method, and no cash amount.
30. Every tappable element has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px; each row is at least 100 px tall and spans the full content width.
31. No text on G-07 is rendered smaller than 14 px; all text has a contrast of at least 4.5:1; pinch-zoom and browser text scaling work, and with enlarged text rows grow and wrap instead of truncating.
32. All numbers on the screen (order numbers, dates, times, totals, counts, room numbers) use Western digits 0–9 in both languages.
33. A screen reader announces the title on arrival, reads each row as one item in the order: order number, status, date-time, total, item count, room number (room digits one at a time), and announces a pill change from the refresh once.
34. No toast, modal, or error message is ever displayed on G-07.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen (not previously settled by CLAUDE.md, the map, G-01, or M-01)

1. **Row contents are fixed at six values**: order number, status pill, date-time, total, item count, room number — with the reasons in §5.2. Payment method, cash amount, product names, and images are not on rows.
2. **One list, no "active / previous" split**, newest first by order time, tie-break by order number; status changes never reorder (§5.4).
3. **Cap of 20 records, trimmed by G-07 on every entry before the first frame**; the oldest Delivered/Cancelled records go first, then (only if none exist) the oldest of any status. No age-based deletion, no manual deletion, no deletion by any other screen (§5.4).
4. **C08 cap note is shown only when exactly 20 rows are listed** — the only moment the trim can have removed anything.
5. **Status is refreshed once per entry with G-01's request, active orders only, 10-second limit, silent fallback to the last known status, no polling, no indicator** (§5.5). Back from G-06 counts as an entry; the rule has no exception.
6. **An order the server reports as unknown keeps its last known status on the row**; G-06 owns the "Order not found" case.
7. **The three active statuses share one pill look (dark filled) and the two finished statuses another (outlined)**; Cancelled uses gray text; nothing else on the row changes by status, and a Cancelled total is not struck through (§5.3).
8. **Date-time format: "اليوم / Today HH:MM" for the current local day, otherwise "DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM"; 24-hour clock and numeric date in both languages; device time zone; laid out left-to-right** (§5.6, §7.2). No "Yesterday", no relative phrases.
9. **G-07 needs no catalog and never leads to G-08**; its only request is the status refresh, and a failed refresh is silent.
10. **G-07 has no loading state, by rule** (§6.1): the device list is drawn on the first frame.
11. **Empty state copy tells the truth of locked decision 4 in two lines** (device-only, and how to ask about an order from another device — the room phone), with "تصفح المتجر / Browse the store" as its only control (§6.2, §5.7). No account, recovery code, room-number lookup, or sync is offered or implied.
12. **Unreadable records are skipped, never deleted, and never counted** (§6.3 case 2); unreadable storage equals zero records.
13. **Rows are tappable as a whole with no chevron**, pressed-state background as the affordance, 300 ms debounce across all rows after a tap (§5.2, §7.4).
14. **Back from G-07 always goes to G-01 at the top of the list**; after a reload/direct URL, back and "Browse the store" both reset the history to G-01 alone (§3.2).
15. **Minimum text size on G-07 is 14 px**; rows are at least 100 px tall.
16. **G-07 never mentions the cart** and has no floating cart bar; the cart is reached from G-01 or G-02 only.

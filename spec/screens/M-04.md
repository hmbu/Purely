# M-04 — Items no longer available (منتجات غير متوفرة)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/screens-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 1–7; `/spec/screens-map.md` §4 decisions 1–16 (decision 9, the two stock gates, and decision 14, backdrop dismissal, apply directly); `/spec/screens/G-01.md` §5.7 (session catalog copy — M-04 is one of the two writers of out-of-stock marks); `/spec/screens/G-03.md` §5.4–§5.6 (out-of-stock lines and the availability check); `/spec/screens/G-04.md` §3.1 (guard and return after removal), §7.5 rule 8 (amount re-validation), §7.7 (client order key).

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** M-04
- **Name:** منتجات غير متوفرة / Items no longer available (the map's name; the title shown on the sheet varies by case, §5.2)
- **Type:** Modal (bottom sheet) on top of G-04 Checkout. No URL, no history entry. It is the **second and final stock gate** (map §4 decision 9): G-03's availability check is the first; M-04 catches what changed between that check and the moment the server processed the submission.
- **Language:** the language currently saved on the device. No language toggle.

---

## 2. Purpose

Show the guest exactly which items the hotel could not fulfil, and let them either drop those items and send the rest, or go back to the cart.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 How the modal opens

M-04 opens only on a **stock rejection response** to a submission attempt: the server refused to create the order and returned the list of products that are out of stock. The submission attempt is the request M-01 sends on "Confirm and send" or the identical request M-03 resends on "Retry".

| From | Trigger | What M-04 shows on opening |
|---|---|---|
| M-01 Confirm room number | "Confirm and send" was rejected for stock | The M-01 sheet is replaced by the M-04 sheet; the backdrop stays. Copy variant per §5.2; the list per §5.3; the new total per §5.4 |
| M-03 Order not sent | "Retry" was rejected for stock | The M-03 sheet is replaced by the M-04 sheet; the backdrop stays. Same content rules |

**On opening, before any tap, M-04 writes an out-of-stock mark into the session catalog copy for every product in the rejection list** (G-01 §5.7). This happens on open, not on a button tap, because the marks record a fact the server stated, not a decision the guest has made; every exit from M-04 therefore shows those products as out of stock on G-01, G-02, and G-03 until the next catalog fetch. A product in the rejection list that is not in the device cart gets its mark too, but is not listed on the sheet (§5.3). A rejection whose list is empty or unreadable is not a stock rejection: it opens M-03 in its server variant (M-03 §5.4), and M-04 is not shown.

M-04 is never opened from any other screen, never on page load, and never by a guest action other than the two above. A browser reload while M-04 is open does not restore it: the page reloads to G-04, whose render guard (G-04 §3.1) sees the marks written on open and replaces G-04 with G-03, where the lines show as out of stock; the guest removes them there. Nothing is lost.

### 3.2 Where each action leads

| Action on M-04 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap the primary button (B01: "إزالتها والمتابعة / Remove them and continue" or its variants, §5.5) | The listed cart lines are deleted from the device cart in one write (§5.6). Then, if the cart still has at least one line → M-04 and the backdrop close, **G-04** is re-rendered scrolled to the top with the summary rebuilt, every form value kept, and the amount re-validated (G-04 §3.1, §7.5 rule 8); the guest taps "Submit order" again and passes through M-01 again. If the cart became empty → M-04 and the backdrop close, G-04 is popped from the history, and **G-03** is shown in its empty state (G-03 §3.1, §6.2) |
| Tap "العودة إلى السلة / Back to cart" (B02) | M-04 and the backdrop close, G-04 is popped, **G-03** is shown with the listed lines in their out-of-stock form (G-03 §5.4) and its availability check running as on any entry (G-03 §5.6). The cart is unchanged; the guest removes the lines there with "إزالة / Remove" |
| Tap the backdrop (S01) | Identical to B02 (map §4 decision 14) |
| Browser back gesture/button | Identical to B02 (map §2.3 rule 2) |

No action on M-04 sends a request. The removal is device-side; the next submission is a new attempt made from M-01.

## 5. Content

### 5.1 Sheet layout (S02)

Top to bottom: C01 title → C02 body → S03 list box → C04 new total (some cases only) → B01 → B02. Nothing else: no image, no icon, no per-row remove control, no "keep this item anyway" option, no "notify me", no link to G-02.

Height at 390 px width: with 2 rows and C04 shown, about 420 px; at the 4½-row maximum with C04, about 520 px — under the 85 % ceiling on every phone viewport of 640 px or taller. Under browser text scaling the sheet scrolls inside per the M-03 conventions.

### 5.2 The three copy cases (C01, C02) — decision

The case is decided by comparing the rejection list with the device cart:

| Case | Condition | Why its own copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Some, N = 1** | Exactly one cart line is rejected, and the cart has at least one other line | Arabic and English both need the singular; "some items" over a single item reads as a mistake |
| **Some, N ≥ 2** | Two or more cart lines are rejected, and the cart has at least one other line | The plural, as the map's name |
| **All** | Every cart line is rejected (N = number of cart lines, N ≥ 1) | "Some items" would be false, and "send the rest of your order" would promise something that does not exist; the guest must be told plainly that nothing remains and be sent to the store, not back to an empty checkout |

The "All" copy is the same for N = 1 and N ≥ 2: "نفد كل ما في طلبك / Everything in your order has sold out" is grammatical for one item and for many. Only the pronoun in B01 differs (§5.5).

### 5.3 The list (S03 / C03) — what each row shows and why

Each row shows **"{qty} × {product name}"** and nothing else, in the same format as the G-04 summary line the guest was just looking at (G-04 §5.2), so the row is recognizably the same line.

- **Name** — identifies the item; the guest's one decision ("drop these and send the rest, or go back") needs nothing more.
- **Quantity** — tells the guest how much of the order is affected ("3 × water" is a different decision from "1 × water") and matches the summary format at no cost.
- **No image** — the 64 px image on G-03 is one tap away via "Back to cart"; on a sheet whose job is a yes/no decision, images add height and no information the name does not carry.
- **No price** — the price of an item that will not be delivered is not a number the guest acts on; the number they act on is the new total, which C04 gives once.
- **No per-row control** — one row removed while others stay is the G-03 flow ("Back to cart" then "Remove"); offering it here would make the sheet a second cart.

Rules:
- Rows are the cart lines whose product identifier appears in the rejection list, in **cart order** (the order the lines were added, G-03 §5.3). Products in the rejection list that are not in the cart are marked (§3.1) but not listed.
- Name in the current language from the session catalog copy; if the English name is missing, the Arabic name is shown in the English interface too (G-01 §5.4). If the product is absent from the session catalog copy, the cart line's own snapshot name is used (G-03 §5.2).
- Quantity is the cart line's stored quantity (1–10, G-01 §7.1). The "×" is U+00D7 and the digits are Western.
- One line per row, ellipsis "…" when too long, 44 px row height, so N rows are exactly 44 × N px plus separators.
- **Visible before scrolling: 4 rows. Maximum list height: 198 px (4½ rows).** With 5 or more rows the box scrolls inside; the half-visible fifth row is the cue that more rows exist. There is no "show more", no arrow, and no count line ("and 3 more"), because the cue is visible without reading. Four rows were chosen so that title, body, list, new total, and both buttons fit on a 640 px viewport without scrolling the sheet.

### 5.4 The new total (C04) — decision

**Shown, inside the modal, directly under the list**, in the "some" cases only: "الإجمالي بعد الإزالة / Total after removal" with the amount the remaining lines add up to (sum of remaining line totals, two decimals, rounded half up, same rule as G-04 §7.5).

Why here: the guest decides between B01 and B02 on this sheet, and what remains to pay is part of that decision (a 60.00 order that drops to 12.00 may not be worth a delivery to the guest). G-04's own total updates on return anyway (G-04 §3.1), so this line is not the only place the number appears — it is the place it appears **before** the decision.

Hidden in the "all" case: the total would be 0.00 and the copy already says nothing remains.

### 5.5 The primary button label (B01) — decision

The label changes with the case because "continue" is a lie when nothing remains:

| Case | Arabic | English | Goes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Some, N = 1 | إزالته والمتابعة | Remove it and continue | G-04 (summary rebuilt) |
| Some, N ≥ 2 | إزالتها والمتابعة | Remove them and continue | G-04 (summary rebuilt) |
| All, N = 1 | إزالته من السلة | Remove it from the cart | G-03 empty state |
| All, N ≥ 2 | إزالتها من السلة | Remove them from the cart | G-03 empty state |

Both "All" labels still lead to G-03's empty state, as the map states; from there "متابعة التسوق / Continue shopping" opens G-01. The "All" case keeps B02 "Back to cart" as well: it leads to G-03 with the lines still present in their out-of-stock form, which is the path for a guest who wants to see the items again before letting them go. This is the only difference between the two buttons in that case, and it is what map §2.2 defines, so both stay.

### 5.6 What removal does — the exact sequence on B01

1. Delete every listed cart line from the device cart in **one write** (never one line at a time, so a reload mid-way cannot leave a half-removed cart).
2. Leave the out-of-stock marks written on open (§3.1) in place — marks are cleared only by the next catalog fetch (G-01 §5.7). On G-01 those products now show the badge "غير متوفر / Out of stock" **without** the "— في سلتك {N} / — {N} in your cart" suffix, because the cart no longer holds them (G-01 §5.4 item 4).
3. Replace the client order key (§5.7) and reset M-03's attempt count to 0.
4. Close the modal and navigate per §3.2. The G-04 render that follows rebuilds the summary from the cart, keeps every form value, and re-validates the amount against the new total (G-04 §7.5 rule 8) — so a guest who typed "Amount you will pay with" 100 for a total that dropped to 30 sees no error, and a guest whose amount is now lower than the new total sees G-04-C12 immediately.

### 5.7 The client order key after removal — decision

**Removal replaces the client order key with a new one.** This follows G-04 §7.7 (the key is replaced whenever a cart line changes) and is the safe direction for both failure modes named in the map:

- **No duplicate is possible.** A duplicate can arise only if the *old* key's request created an order and the new request is not recognized as the same order. A stock rejection is a **definite** server response stating that no order was created for that key — unlike a lost response, there is no ambiguity. So the old key never corresponds to an existing order, and a fresh key cannot duplicate anything.
- **No legitimate order is deduplicated away.** If the old key were reused with the new, smaller payload, a server that remembers the old key as "rejected" could answer the resent key with the cached rejection, or a server that keys on payload could treat the two as one and discard the second — either way the guest's real order is lost. A new key makes the reduced order unmistakably a new request.
- The same reasoning applies whichever attempt produced the rejection (the first from M-01 or a Retry from M-03): the rejection is definite in both cases.

"Back to cart" (B02) does **not** touch the key; the cart is unchanged at that moment. When the guest then removes the lines on G-03, that cart change replaces the key under G-04 §7.7 — the same end result by the other path. There is no way to reach a submission from M-04 without the key being replaced, because G-04's render guard refuses to draw the checkout while any line carries a mark (G-04 §3.1), and clearing a mark requires a catalog fetch, which is a reload.

### 5.8 A second rejection for a different item — decision

After B01 leads to G-04, the guest taps "Submit order" → M-01 → "Confirm and send" as a **new attempt with the new key**. If the server rejects it again because a *different* item sold out in the meantime, **M-04 opens again with the new rejection list only**, exactly as the first time: no special copy, no "again" wording, no counter, no shortcut. Reasons: the second rejection is a different fact about different items; the earlier items are already gone from the cart, so listing them would be wrong; and every pass through B01 removes at least one line, so the loop is bounded by the number of lines and ends at the latest in G-03's empty state. The marks accumulate in the session catalog copy across passes, so G-01 and G-03 show every product marked in this session.

### 5.9 What is deliberately not on this modal

- No images, prices, or steppers in the rows; no per-row remove or keep; no link to G-02.
- No "keep the item and notify me when it is back", no substitute suggestions, no "similar products".
- No request of any kind: no re-check of the remaining items (the next submission is the check), no reservation of stock.
- No count of rows, no "and N more", no "show all".
- No attempt counter or "this happened again" copy.
- No language toggle, no toast, no room number.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading — not reachable

M-04 sends no request and reads only the rejection list it was opened with plus device data (cart, session catalog copy). It is drawn complete on its first frame; the removal on B01 is a single device-side write that finishes before the modal closes. There is no spinner, skeleton, or in-flight button state.

### 6.2 Empty — not reachable

A rejection with zero listed products opens M-03 (server variant), not M-04 (§3.1), so N is always at least 1 and S03 always has at least one row. A rejection whose products are all absent from the cart (possible only if the cart changed in another tab on the same device between M-01 and the response) is also treated as a server failure and opens M-03; M-04 is never drawn with an empty list.

### 6.3 Error — not reachable

Nothing on M-04 can fail: no request is sent, nothing is typed, and the device write on B01 is the same kind of write G-03's "Remove" performs (G-03 §7.1). There is no field error and no toast. The situation M-04 reports is the *server's* refusal; M-04 itself is a decision sheet, not an error state.

### 6.4 Success — the guest chose a way out

Both buttons (and the backdrop / browser back, which equal B02) are successful exits; there is no failure exit.

- After **B01, cart not empty:** G-04 is on screen within 100 ms, scrolled to the top, summary rebuilt without the removed lines, C04 and B05 on G-04 showing the new total, every form value kept, amount re-validated. No toast; the shorter summary and the new total are the feedback (G-04 §3.1).
- After **B01, cart empty:** G-03's empty state is on screen within 100 ms ("سلتك فارغة / Your cart is empty" with "متابعة التسوق / Continue shopping"); G-04 is no longer in the history; no toast (G-03 §6.2).
- After **B02 / backdrop / browser back:** G-03 is on screen within 100 ms with the listed lines in their out-of-stock form (image at 50 % opacity, badge "غير متوفر / Out of stock", "إزالة / Remove" instead of the stepper), G-03-C08 reading the out-of-stock helper, "Checkout" disabled, and the availability check running; G-04 is no longer in the history.

In every exit, the out-of-stock marks written on open remain until the next catalog fetch.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on this modal. Concretely: no `M-04-F` element exists, no keyboard opens while M-04 is shown, no value is validated, and no field error message exists. The bounded values M-04 displays or changes are:

### 7.1 Rows (C03)

- N = number of cart lines whose product identifier is in the rejection list; N ≥ 1 (§6.2); no upper bound other than the number of cart lines.
- Quantity: the cart line's stored whole number, 1–10 (G-01 §7.1). Product name: current language, one line with ellipsis; source order per §5.3.

### 7.2 New total (C04)

- Sum over the cart lines **not** listed of quantity × unit price as held in the session catalog copy (or the line's snapshot when absent, G-03 §5.2), rounded to two decimals half up; identical to the total G-04 will show after B01. Shown only when at least one line remains.

### 7.3 Cart write (B01)

- One write deleting exactly the listed lines; all other lines, their quantities, and their order are untouched. The checkout draft's four form values are untouched. The client order key is replaced; the attempt count is reset.

### 7.4 Session catalog marks

- Written on open for every product in the rejection list (in the cart or not); never cleared by M-04; cleared only by the next catalog fetch (G-01 §5.7).

### 7.5 List bounds

- 44 px per row; 4 rows visible; maximum box height 198 px; scrolls inside beyond that.

### 7.6 Sizes and touch targets

- B01 and B02: full sheet width (342 px at 390 px viewport) × 48 px; the backdrop is the whole remaining screen. Every tappable element exceeds 44 × 44 px. Rows are not tappable and have no tap target.
- Text: C01 18 px bold; C02 16 px; C03 16 px; C04 16 px bold; button labels 16 px bold. Nothing below 16 px on this modal.
- Contrast is carried by fill and outline: primary dark filled, secondary outlined, list box outlined. No color carries meaning.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled; under scaling the sheet grows up to 85 % of the viewport height, then scrolls inside, with the list box keeping its own 198 px maximum.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. When "Confirm and send" on M-01 (or "Retry" on M-03) is answered by a stock rejection listing at least one product that is in the cart, the previous sheet is replaced by the M-04 sheet without the backdrop disappearing, and G-04 beneath is unchanged.
2. On opening, before any tap, every product in the rejection list carries an out-of-stock mark in the session catalog copy; after any exit, those products show G-01-C07 on G-01 and the out-of-stock form on G-03 until the next catalog fetch.
3. With exactly one rejected line and at least one other line in the cart, C01 reads "منتج لم يعد متوفرًا / One item is no longer available", C02 reads "نفد هذا المنتج قبل إرسال طلبك. أزله ثم أرسل باقي الطلب. / This item sold out before your order was sent. Remove it, then send the rest of your order.", and B01 reads "إزالته والمتابعة / Remove it and continue".
4. With two or more rejected lines and at least one other line in the cart, C01 reads "بعض المنتجات لم تعد متوفرة / Some items are no longer available", C02 reads the "Some, N ≥ 2" text in §4, and B01 reads "إزالتها والمتابعة / Remove them and continue".
5. When every cart line is rejected, C01 reads "نفد كل ما في طلبك / Everything in your order has sold out", C02 reads the "All" text in §4, C04 is not rendered, and B01 reads "إزالته من السلة / Remove it from the cart" for one line or "إزالتها من السلة / Remove them from the cart" for two or more; the words "some" / "بعض" and "the rest" / "باقي" appear nowhere on the sheet.
6. S03 shows one C03 row per rejected cart line, in cart order, each reading "{qty} × {name}" at 16 px on one line with an ellipsis when too long, and no image, price, or control in any row.
7. A product in the rejection list that is not in the cart is not listed but is marked in the session catalog copy.
8. With 1 to 4 rejected lines, the list box shows all rows without scrolling; with 5 or more, the box is 198 px tall, the fifth row is half visible, and the box scrolls vertically inside; no "show more" or count text exists.
9. In both "some" cases, C04 reads "الإجمالي بعد الإزالة / Total after removal" with the sum of the remaining lines (two decimals, currency per convention), and that amount equals the total G-04 shows after B01.
10. Tapping B01 when at least one line remains deletes exactly the listed lines from the device cart in one write, closes M-04 and the backdrop, and shows G-04 within 100 ms scrolled to the top with the summary rebuilt, the new total in G-04-C04 and G-04-B05, the room number, notes, payment selection, and amount unchanged, and no toast.
11. After B01, if Cash is selected and the typed amount is now lower than the new total, G-04-C12 shows the "lower than total" message with the new total immediately; if the amount is not lower, no error is shown.
12. Tapping B01 when every line is listed deletes all lines, closes M-04, and shows G-03's empty state within 100 ms with "سلتك فارغة / Your cart is empty" and "متابعة التسوق / Continue shopping"; browser back from that G-03 does not reach G-04.
13. Tapping B02, tapping the backdrop, or using browser back closes M-04 without changing the cart or the client order key, pops G-04 from the history, and shows G-03 within 100 ms with the listed lines in their out-of-stock form, "Checkout" disabled, G-03-C08 showing the out-of-stock helper, and the availability check running; the next back from G-03 goes to G-03's original opener.
14. After B01, the next submission attempt carries a client order key different from the one the server rejected, and M-03's attempt count starts again at 1 on a later failure.
15. After B02 and removal of the lines on G-03, the next submission attempt likewise carries a new client order key (replaced by the cart change under G-04 §7.7).
16. If the submission after B01 is rejected again for a different item, M-04 opens again listing only the newly rejected lines, with the same copy rules and no additional text; the earlier removed items are not listed.
17. A stock rejection with an empty or unreadable product list, or one whose products are all absent from the cart, opens M-03 (server variant), and M-04 is never shown with zero rows.

# M-03 — Order not sent (لم يتم إرسال الطلب)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/screens-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 1–7; `/spec/screens-map.md` §4 decisions 1–16 (decision 10, duplicate-safe retry, and decision 14, backdrop dismissal, apply directly); `/spec/screens/G-04.md` §7.7 (checkout draft and client order key) and §7.8 (the payload M-01 sends).
Conventions reused verbatim from G-01 (not re-decided here): Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label from store settings, after the amount in Arabic ("45.00 ر.س") and before it in English ("SAR 45.00"), two decimals always; every tappable element has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px (G-01 §7.6); body copy never smaller than 14 px; the session catalog copy rule (G-01 §5.7); browser zoom and text scaling never disabled.

**Modal conventions set by this file** (M-04 reuses them by reference; M-01 and M-02, when written, reuse them unless their own file states a difference): a modal is a **bottom sheet** — full viewport width, anchored to the bottom edge, top corners rounded 16 px, light filled, 24 px padding on the sides and top, 16 px padding at the bottom plus the device's bottom safe-area inset; it slides up over 200 ms on open and is removed instantly on close (no exit animation). Behind it a **backdrop** covers the whole screen beneath, including that screen's fixed bottom bar, in black at 50 % opacity. The sheet is at most 85 % of the viewport height; if its content is taller (possible only under browser text scaling), the sheet's content scrolls inside it. Buttons are stacked vertically at the bottom of the sheet, full sheet width, 48 px tall, label 16 px bold, 8 px gap between them; the primary button is dark filled with light text, the secondary is outlined with dark text. Title 18 px bold at the top of the sheet, body copy 16 px, secondary copy 14 px, all start-aligned in the reading direction. When one sheet replaces another (M-01 → M-03, M-03 → M-04), the backdrop stays in place and only the sheet content changes, with no second slide-up. When a modal opens, keyboard focus moves to its title so a screen reader announces it, and the modal is announced as a dialog. The **submission request timeout is 15 seconds** for every submission attempt (the first from M-01 and every Retry from M-03); M-01 must use this same value.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** M-03
- **Name:** لم يتم إرسال الطلب / Order not sent
- **Type:** Modal (bottom sheet) on top of G-04 Checkout. No URL, no history entry (map §2.3 rule 2). It is the only place in the product where a failed submission is shown; G-04 itself never shows failure copy (G-04 §6.3).
- **Language:** the language currently saved on the device. No language toggle.

---

## 2. Purpose

Tell the guest that no confirmation of the order was received and let them resend the identical order safely, without losing anything they typed and without ever creating a second order.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 How the modal opens

The modal opens as the result of a **submission attempt that did not end in success or in a stock rejection**. The submission attempt is the request M-01 sends on "Confirm and send" (payload per G-04 §7.8) or the identical request M-03 resends on "Retry".

| From | Trigger | What M-03 shows on opening |
|---|---|---|
| M-01 Confirm room number | "Confirm and send" was tapped and the attempt failed (§5.4 defines failure) | The M-01 sheet is replaced by the M-03 sheet; the backdrop stays. Attempt count = 1. Variant per §5.4 (connection or server). G-04 beneath is unchanged: same values, same scroll position |
| M-03 itself | "Retry" was tapped and the attempt failed again | The same sheet re-renders in place (no slide-up): attempt count + 1, variant per the **latest** failure, B01 back to its rest label, B02 re-enabled. C04 appears when the count reaches 3 (§5.3) |

M-03 is never opened from any other screen, never on page load, and never by a guest action other than the two above. A browser reload while M-03 is open does not restore it: the page reloads to G-04 (modals are not history entries), with the checkout draft and the client order key intact (G-04 §7.7); the guest then taps "Submit order" → M-01 → "Confirm and send", which reuses the same key because nothing changed (G-04 acceptance criterion 42).

### 3.2 Where each action leads

| Action on M-03 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" (B01) | Stays on M-03 in the in-flight state (§6.1) while the identical request is resent with the same client order key. Then, by outcome: **success** → M-03 closes, G-05 Order submitted opens (the cart is emptied, the checkout draft and the key are discarded, the order is saved on the device, G-04 and M-01 are dropped from the history — all as G-04 §7.7 and map §2.3 rule 3 state); **stock rejection** → the M-03 sheet is replaced by M-04 (backdrop stays); **failure** → M-03 re-renders per the second row of §3.1 |
| Tap "إغلاق / Close" (B02) | M-03 closes. G-04 is shown exactly as it was: every field value, the payment selection, the amount, the scroll position, and the client order key are kept (G-04 §3.1). No field is focused, no error text is added, nothing is written to the device's order list |
| Tap the backdrop (S01) | Identical to B02 (map §4 decision 14). **Not available while a retry is in flight** (§6.1) |
| Browser back gesture/button | Identical to B02 (map §2.3 rule 2). **Ignored while a retry is in flight** (§6.1) |

There is no path from M-03 to G-03, G-01, or any other screen: the guest who wants to change the order taps "Close" and uses G-04's "Edit cart".

## 5. Content

### 5.1 Sheet layout (S02)

Top to bottom in the sheet: C01 title row → C02 status line → C03 guidance line → (rule + C04 reception line, from the 3rd failure) → B01 Retry → B02 Close. Nothing else: no order summary, no room number in large type (that is M-01's job and was already done), no countdown, no attempt counter, no "contact reception" button, no link.

The sheet's height is the sum of its content; it is about 320 px at 390 px width with C04 hidden and about 400 px with C04 shown, well under the 85 % ceiling.

### 5.2 The two variants (C03) and why there are two

The device can always tell the two situations apart without any extra request, and the guest's fix is different in each, so one generic message would send an offline guest into pointless retries:

| Variant | The device observed | What the guest should do | C03 copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Connection** | The request could not be sent (browser offline, network error), the connection dropped before a response arrived, or no response arrived within the 15-second timeout | Reconnect (Wi-Fi / mobile data), then Retry | "تحقّق من اتصالك بالإنترنت ثم أعد المحاولة. إعادة المحاولة آمنة ولن تُرسل طلبك مرتين." / "Check your internet connection, then try again. Retrying is safe and will never send your order twice." |
| **Server** | A response arrived, but it was an error response (any status the device does not treat as success or as a stock rejection), or a response the device could not read | Nothing to fix on their side; wait a moment, then Retry | "حدث خطأ من جهتنا. أعد المحاولة بعد لحظات. إعادة المحاولة آمنة ولن تُرسل طلبك مرتين." / "Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a moment. Retrying is safe and will never send your order twice." |

Everything else — title, C02, C04, both buttons — is identical in the two variants. The variant follows the latest attempt: a connection failure followed by a server failure shows the server copy on the second render.

### 5.3 What the guest is told about "did it go through?" — decision

**M-03 does not say "your order may have gone through".** It says the one thing the device knows to be true — *no confirmation was received* (C02) — and the one thing that resolves the doubt — *Retry is safe and never sends the order twice* (C03).

Reason: the doubt is real only on the connection variant (a response can be lost after the server created the order). Under map §4 decision 10 the Retry resends the same client order key, so the server answers a resent request for an order it already created with **success for that same order** — the guest lands on G-05 with the order number, and no second order exists. Retry is therefore the honest answer to "did it go through?", not a sentence. Writing "it may have gone through" would push the guest toward the two wrong actions: closing and waiting for an order that may never come, or phoning reception and creating a duplicate by voice.

The reception line (C04) is the fallback for the case Retry cannot resolve — the connection stays down — and it is honest about what reception can do: check whether the order arrived, or take it by phone.

**Threshold for C04 — decision:** shown from the **3rd consecutive failed attempt** with the same client order key (the first attempt from M-01 plus two failed retries), and from then on for that key. One-line justification: two retries are enough to get past a momentary Wi-Fi drop, and a third failure means the problem is not momentary, so the guest needs the one path that does not depend on the connection — the room phone — without that path competing with Retry on the first failure, where it would only invite duplicate phone orders. The count is stored with the checkout draft (G-04 §7.7), so it survives a page reload; it resets to 0 whenever the client order key is replaced (the order changed) and is discarded on success.

**Nothing else changes with repeated failures — decision:** no attempt counter is displayed ("attempt 2 of 3" gives the guest nothing to act on), no cap on retries (locking the guest out has no benefit; the key keeps every retry safe), no automatic retry (a spinner that runs by itself for a minute at 2 AM is worse than a button the guest controls), no change of title or buttons.

### 5.4 What counts as a failure, and what does not

| Outcome of a submission attempt | Result |
|---|---|
| Success response (order number returned) | Never M-03: G-05 |
| Stock rejection response (the server lists products that are out of stock) | Never M-03: M-04. A stock rejection whose list of products is empty or unreadable is treated as a **server** failure and opens M-03 |
| Error response of any other kind, or an unreadable response | M-03, **server** variant |
| No response within 15 seconds, connection dropped, request could not be sent | M-03, **connection** variant |
| A response that arrives **after** the 15-second timeout | Ignored: the modal does not change by itself, and the outcome is never applied — with one exception: if it is a stock rejection, its out-of-stock marks are still written into the session catalog copy (same rule as G-03 §5.6), without opening M-04 |

The device never guesses; it classifies only by what it observed.

### 5.5 What Retry sends — the duplicate-safety rules (map §4 decision 10, G-04 §7.7)

1. Retry resends the **byte-identical payload** of the last attempt (G-04 §7.8 item 2) with the **same client order key**. Nothing is recomputed: not the prices, not the total, not the language field.
2. The key **cannot** change while M-03 is open, because every value that would replace it lives on G-04 or G-03, and both are behind the backdrop.
3. After "Close", the key is kept with the draft. If the guest changes nothing and goes "Submit order" → M-01 → "Confirm and send", the same key is sent (G-04 acceptance criterion 42), so that path is as duplicate-safe as Retry. If the guest changes any order value first, G-04 replaces the key (G-04 §7.7) and resets the attempt count.
4. **Accepted limitation, stated so it is not mistaken for an oversight:** if a response was lost after the server created the order, and the guest then closes M-03, changes the order, and submits the new version, the server receives a **new key** and creates a second order. Version 1 accepts this: the guest was told no confirmation was received and that Retry is safe; the hotel receives both orders with the same room number and staff resolve it at the door. No device-side detection of this case exists in version 1.
5. On failure, **nothing is saved to the device's order list**: no order number exists, so G-07 and the G-01 banner show nothing for this attempt.

### 5.6 What is deliberately not on this modal

- No order summary, no room number in large type (already confirmed on M-01), no total.
- No "Edit order" button; editing is "Close" then G-04's own controls.
- No countdown, no automatic retry, no attempt counter, no retry cap.
- No reception phone number or extension (hotel room phones reach reception with a labeled key; adding an extension would require a store setting that does not exist in version 1).
- No language toggle.
- No toast, before, during, or after.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading — reachable: the retry in flight

Triggered by: a tap on B01.

On screen, for the whole in-flight period:
- B01 at 100 % opacity, its label replaced by the 20 px spinner and "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; inert.
- B02 at 50 % opacity, inert.
- S01 backdrop inert; browser back ignored.
- C01–C04 unchanged from the moment before the tap (the variant text does not switch to a neutral "sending" text; the guest keeps reading what they were reading).
- No other spinner, progress bar, or dimming.

Rules:
- The in-flight period ends when a response arrives or at the 15-second timeout, whichever comes first; it never lasts longer than 15 seconds.
- Making the modal non-dismissable during flight is a decision: if the guest could close it mid-flight, a success arriving a second later would have nowhere to go, and the device would hold no record of a submitted order.
- A second tap on B01 during flight does nothing (the button is inert), so one tap equals exactly one request.

### 6.2 Empty — not reachable

M-03 has no list and no data that could be absent. The only variable content is `{room}` in C04, which is always present because M-01 could not have opened without a valid room number (G-04 §7.1, §7.8).

### 6.3 Error — the modal is the error; its own error is "failed again"

M-03 exists to show a submission error, so there is no separate error state to draw. When a Retry fails, the same sheet re-renders per §3.1 row 2: attempt count + 1, C03 variant per the latest failure, C04 added from the 3rd failed attempt, buttons back to rest. There is no field error (nothing is typed) and no toast.

### 6.4 Success — the retry succeeded

Triggered by: a success response to the Retry within 15 seconds.

Exactly: M-03 and the backdrop are removed at once (no exit animation); G-05 Order submitted opens with the order number the server returned; the cart is emptied; the checkout draft, the client order key, and the attempt count are discarded; the order is saved on the device; G-04 and M-01 are dropped from the history (map §2.3 rule 3). If the server had already created the order on an earlier attempt whose response was lost, this response carries **that** order's number, and G-05 shows it — the guest never learns that two requests were made, because only one order exists.

A stock rejection to the Retry is neither success nor failure for M-03: the sheet is replaced by M-04 (§3.2).

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on this modal. Concretely: no `M-03-F` element exists, no keyboard opens while M-03 is shown, no value is validated, and no field error message exists. The only bounded values on the sheet are:

### 7.1 `{room}` in C04

- The trimmed room number M-01 confirmed: 2–5 Western digits, leading zeros kept (G-04 §7.1). Read-only; M-03 never changes it.

### 7.2 Attempt count

- Whole number starting at 1 when M-03 first opens for a client order key; + 1 per failed attempt; C04 shown when ≥ 3; no upper bound; stored with the checkout draft; reset to 0 when the key is replaced; discarded on success. Never displayed.

### 7.3 Timeout

- 15 seconds per attempt, measured from the moment the request is sent.

### 7.4 Sizes and touch targets

- B01 and B02: full sheet width (342 px at 390 px viewport) × 48 px; the backdrop is the whole remaining screen. Every tappable element therefore exceeds 44 × 44 px.
- Text: C01 18 px bold; C02 and C03 16 px; C04 14 px; button labels 16 px bold. Nothing below 14 px.
- Contrast is carried by fill and outline, not color: the primary button is dark filled, the secondary outlined, the disabled secondary at 50 % opacity, the "!" icon outlined dark on the light sheet.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled; under scaling the sheet grows up to 85 % of the viewport height, then scrolls inside.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. When "Confirm and send" on M-01 ends in a connection failure (offline, dropped, or no response in 15 s), the M-01 sheet is replaced by the M-03 sheet without the backdrop disappearing, showing the "!" icon, the title "لم يتم إرسال الطلب / Order not sent", C02 "لم نتلقَّ تأكيدًا بوصول طلبك إلى الفندق. / We have not received confirmation that your order reached the hotel.", the connection-variant C03, and the two buttons "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" (dark filled) above "إغلاق / Close" (outlined).
2. When the attempt ends in an error response or an unreadable response, the same sheet is shown with the server-variant C03 "حدث خطأ من جهتنا. أعد المحاولة بعد لحظات. إعادة المحاولة آمنة ولن تُرسل طلبك مرتين. / Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a moment. Retrying is safe and will never send your order twice."
3. A stock rejection never opens M-03; a stock rejection with an empty or unreadable product list opens M-03 in the server variant.
4. On the first and second failed attempts for a client order key, C04 is not rendered; on the third failed attempt and every later one for the same key, C04 is rendered with the exact copy in §4 and the confirmed room number with its leading zeros (e.g. "0305").
5. G-04 beneath the backdrop keeps its scroll position, its room number, notes, payment selection, and amount, and shows no new error text while M-03 is open and after it closes.
6. Tapping "Retry" sends exactly one request whose payload and client order key are byte-identical to the previous attempt's, and switches B01 to the spinner plus "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…" at 100 % opacity within 100 ms.
7. While the retry is in flight, a tap on B01, a tap on B02, a tap on the backdrop, and the browser back gesture all do nothing, and B02 is shown at 50 % opacity.
8. The in-flight state ends within 15 seconds in every case; if no response arrived, M-03 re-renders in the connection variant with B01 back to "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" and B02 enabled.
9. A response arriving after the 15-second timeout changes nothing on screen; if it is a stock rejection, its out-of-stock marks are written into the session catalog copy and M-04 does not open.
10. When the retry succeeds, M-03 and the backdrop disappear at once and G-05 is shown with the returned order number; the cart is empty, the draft and key are gone, and browser back from G-05 goes to G-01, not to G-04 or M-01.
11. When the server had already created the order on a previous attempt whose response was lost, the retry ends on G-05 with that existing order's number, and the device holds exactly one saved order for it.
12. When the retry is rejected for stock, the M-03 sheet is replaced by M-04 without the backdrop disappearing.
13. Tapping "Close" or the backdrop, or using browser back, while no retry is in flight closes M-03 and shows G-04 unchanged, with no field focused and the client order key still stored; a subsequent "Submit order" → M-01 → "Confirm and send" with nothing changed sends the same key.
14. After "Close", changing any order value (cart line, room number, notes, payment method, amount) and submitting again sends a new client order key, and the attempt count for M-03 starts again at 1 on the next failure.
15. After a failed attempt, no order appears on G-07 and no active-order banner appears on G-01.
16. Reloading the browser while M-03 is open lands on G-04 with the draft values restored and no modal; M-03 is not restored.
17. No attempt counter, countdown, automatic retry, or retry cap is visible or active: after ten consecutive failures B01 is still "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" and tappable.
18. Every string on M-03 is rendered in the device's current language; switching language on G-01 earlier changes every string, including C04.
19. B01 and B02 are each 48 px tall and span the sheet width; C01 is 18 px bold, C02 and C03 are 16 px, C04 is 14 px; the "!" icon is 32 px; nothing is smaller than 14 px.
20. The room number in C04 is plain text: tapping it does nothing and the browser does not render it as a phone link.
21. No toast is shown at any point while M-03 opens, re-renders, or closes.
22. Pinch-zoom and browser text scaling work while M-03 is open; with large text the sheet stops growing at 85 % of the viewport height and scrolls inside, with both buttons reachable.

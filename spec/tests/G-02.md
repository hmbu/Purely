# Tester report — G-02 Product details (تفاصيل المنتج)

Author: Tester (Agent 2)
Screen under test: `/spec/screens/G-02.md` (Draft)
Reference for consistency checks: `/spec/screens/G-01.md` (Approved), `/spec/screens-map.md`

Findings are tagged **[Critical]**, **[Medium]**, or **[Minor]** and numbered F1–F16 for the summary table. Findings only — no proposed fixes.

---

## Persona 1 — Guest in a hurry at 2 AM who wants one product fast

**Steps:** Scans QR → G-01 loads → taps a product card body (not the "Add" button) to see it bigger before deciding → lands on G-02 within 100 ms, no skeleton, no network request → sees image, name, price, "أضف / Add" already visible with no scrolling → taps "Add" → stepper shows 1, cart bar appears → taps the cart bar → G-03.

**Where they get stuck:** They don't get stuck. The no-request, no-skeleton entry from G-01 (§3.1) and the fact that name/price/action row all fit above the fold (§5.3) mean this persona's fast path works as intended.

**Findings:**
- **F12 [Minor]** — A tap anywhere on the card except B04/B05/B06 opens G-02 (G-01 §4). A hurried guest who misses the small "Add" hit-target and instead taps the card body is routed into G-02 first, then has to tap "Add" there and tap back (or the cart bar) — an extra screen-and-back round trip versus adding directly on G-01. The G-02 side of this is fast (instant render), but the detour itself is friction for this persona. This is really a G-01 hit-target issue surfacing here.
- **F13 [Minor]** — No rule exists for a product that has no name in either language (only the "one language missing → show the other" fallback is defined, §5.3/§7.7). Unlikely, but undefined for this screen.

No Critical or Medium finding for this persona.

## Persona 2 — A foreign guest who cannot read Arabic

**Steps:** Opens the QR link for the first time on a device with no saved language choice → G-01 renders in Arabic by default (locked decision 7) → taps a product card, arriving at G-02 → G-02 has no language control of its own (§5.1, §5.6: "no language toggle... the guest goes back to G-01 for both") → guest must tap the back chevron (icon only, accessible name "رجوع"/"Back" but no visible text) → return to G-01 → find B01 language toggle → switch to English → tap the same product again to re-enter G-02, now in English.

**Where they get stuck:** Momentarily, right after arriving at G-02 for the first time: the whole product page (fixed title, name, price, description, badge/helper copy) is in Arabic, and nothing on this screen tells them a language switch exists or where it is. They must correctly guess that the back arrow leads somewhere with a fix, rather than assume it just abandons the product they were looking at.

**Findings:**
- **F1 [Medium]** — G-02 has no language toggle at all (by design, map §2.3 rule 6). A non-Arabic-reading guest who lands here — especially on first open with no saved preference — has no way to change language without leaving the product page they came to read. The journey is not blocked (back → G-01 → toggle → back into G-02 works), but it is an avoidable detour for exactly the persona most affected by it.
- **F2 [Medium]** — Language fallback (§5.3 item 1, §5.4, §7.7): if the English name or description is missing, the Arabic value is shown in the English interface "too," with no visual marker that this particular piece of text did not switch. A guest who successfully switched to English and then sees a stray line of Arabic (e.g., the description) has no way to tell whether the toggle "half-worked" or whether this is expected fallback behavior — the spec gives the guest no signal either way.
- **F3 [Minor]** — The image is not tappable and has no zoom (§5.2, §6 "no zoom, no full-screen view"). A guest who cannot read the name/description might want to zoom into the product photo to read on-package text themselves; only whole-page pinch-zoom is available (§7.5), which is not signposted anywhere on the screen.

## Persona 3 — An elderly guest with poor eyesight

**Steps:** Taps a product card on G-01 → arrives at G-02 → reads the large 20 px name and 18 px price without difficulty → reads further down into the description (14 px) → wants to check the quantity cap or the "remove from cart" instruction while zoomed or scrolled.

**Where they get stuck:**
1. The two most functionally important helper lines on the whole screen — "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10" (C06) and "لإزالته من السلة افتح السلة / To remove it, open the cart" (C08) — are set at 12 px, the smallest text size used anywhere on G-02 (§7.5 confirms "minimum text size on this screen: 12 px"). These are exactly the kind of short, easy-to-miss instructional strings that are hardest to read at low vision, and they are the smallest text on the page rather than, say, a caption or fine-print element.
2. If the description is long, scrolling down to read it carries both the name (C03) and the action row (S03) off the top of the screen (the action row is explicitly not sticky, §5.3 "the action row scrolls away when the guest reads a long description; there is no second, sticky copy of it"). The header above stays fixed but only shows the generic title "تفاصيل المنتج / Product details" (C01, §4: "it is never the product name"). So a guest engrossed in a long description, especially one who scrolled slowly due to poor eyesight and pinch-zoom, has nothing on screen — not even the header — confirming which product they are reading about, and must scroll all the way back up to both re-confirm identity and reach "Add"/the stepper.
3. The description itself is capped at 500 characters with "…" and no "Read more" (§5.4, §7.4). Any real product information beyond that length (size variants, contents, usage) is permanently unreachable on this screen for a guest who specifically needs to read carefully before deciding.

**Findings:**
- **F4 [Medium]** — Smallest text on the screen (12 px) is assigned to functionally important helper copy (max-quantity and remove-from-cart instructions), not to decorative text.
- **F5 [Medium]** — No rule is given for how the one-line badge/helper strings behave if the guest has increased their browser/OS text size (an accessibility path the spec itself protects, §7.5: "enlarging the browser's text size enlarges the screen's text"). The spec only guarantees the out-of-stock badge "must fit on one line... it does" at the stated default 16 px within 358 px (§7.4); no wrap, truncation, or reflow rule is defined for larger sizes.
- **F6 [Medium]** — Fixed generic header title + non-sticky action row together mean a guest who has scrolled into a long description has no on-screen way to reconfirm which product this is, or to reach "Add"/the stepper, without scrolling back to the top.
- **F7 [Medium]** — 500-character hard cutoff with no "Read more" permanently hides any description content beyond that length; this is the same underlying gap as F6's information-loss but from length rather than position. (Flagging separately since it is a distinct explicit design choice, §5.4/§7.4/§5.6.)

## Persona 4 — A guest who typed the wrong room number

G-02 has no relationship to the room number: there is no room-number field, display, or reference anywhere on this screen (room number is entered on G-04 and confirmed on M-01). Walking through G-02 as this persona produces no steps that touch the problem at all — they browse a product, add it, and leave, exactly as any other guest would.

**Finding:** None. This persona's entire problem belongs to G-04 (room-number field) and M-01 (confirmation modal), not to G-02. Nothing on G-02 needs to change for or because of this persona.

## Persona 5 — A guest who added a product that went out of stock before submitting

**Steps:** On G-02, adds 3 units of a product (stepper shows 3) → navigates to G-03 (cart) via the floating cart bar → G-03's stock re-check marks the product out of stock during this session → guest taps back arrow on G-03, returning to G-02 (§3.1 row 2) → G-02 re-renders from the updated session catalog copy and shows C07 "غير متوفر — في سلتك 3 / Out of stock — 3 in your cart" with the image at 50 % opacity and helper C08 "لإزالته من السلة افتح السلة / To remove it, open the cart" underneath (§5.3 item 3, §5.7).

**Where they get stuck:** Right after reading C08. The guest is looking straight at the problem (their own out-of-stock line, right here on the page) and the only offered action is textual guidance to go somewhere else — there is no remove control on G-02 itself (§4 G-02-C07: "not tappable"; §5.6: "no stock re-check request"). They must tap the floating cart bar and go to G-03 to actually remove the line, then optionally come back.

**Findings:**
- **F8 [Medium]** — The out-of-stock badge and its helper text are purely informational; there is no way to act on the problem from the screen that is displaying it. The guest must leave the product page to resolve exactly what that page just told them about.
- **F9 [Minor]** — G-02 cannot detect a product going out of stock while the guest is actively on the screen (stock marks are written only by G-03/M-04 and only read again on the next render, §5.7: "G-02 reads them, it never writes them"; §5.6: "G-02 never calls the server on its own"). A guest could keep raising the quantity of an item that has, in reality, already sold out, with no indication on G-02 until they separately visit G-03 or submit and hit M-04. This does not reach staff as an operational error, since G-03's disabled "Checkout" and M-04's submission-time rejection remain as later gates — but on G-02 itself, the guest has no live signal.

## Persona 6 — A guest with a weak connection that drops during submission

The core of this persona's problem — a dropped connection while G-04 sends the order — is entirely a G-04/M-03 matter; G-02 plays no part in an actual submission. What G-02 itself does under a weak connection:

**Steps (path A — normal entry):** Guest with a weak connection taps a product card on G-01 → G-02 renders instantly from the session catalog copy already held on the device; no network request is made at all (§5.7, §6.1: "Never triggered by arriving from G-01"). A weak connection has zero effect on this path.

**Steps (path B — reload/direct URL):** Guest's page seems frozen (a plausible symptom of a bad connection) and they pull to reload, or open a bookmarked/shared G-02 link → G-02 now sends the same catalog fetch G-01 sends on first open, with the same 10-second limit (§6.1) → on a weak connection this either succeeds late, times out into G-08, or fails outright into G-08 (§6.1, §6.3 case 2) — the same handling as G-01, and consistent with it.

**Where this differs from the "normal" case:** When G-02 was reached by reload/direct URL, the back arrow (or browser back) returns to G-01 "at the top of the list" rather than to a remembered scroll position (§3.1 row 3, §3.2 row 1, acceptance criterion 5) — a harsher outcome than the G-01→G-02→back round trip, which restores the exact scroll position.

**Findings:**
- No finding on G-02 for the persona's actual problem (submission dropping mid-request); that belongs to G-04/M-03 and is out of scope for this screen.
- **F10 [Medium]** — A guest who reloads G-02 (e.g., because a weak connection made the page appear stuck) permanently loses their G-01 scroll context: back afterward always lands at the top of the list, never at the position they had before. This is a real, if secondary, consequence of the reload path this persona is likely to trigger.

## Persona 7 — A guest who wants to change the order after submitting it

G-02 offers no order-editing capability of any kind (no per-product notes, no link to a submitted order, §5.6), which is consistent with the map's decision that changes after submission are handled by cancel-while-New (M-02) and reorder (G-06 → G-03), not by editing screens. Most of this persona's problem is therefore out of scope for G-02.

**Steps:** Guest has already submitted an order (status New). They return to G-01, where the active-order banner (G-01 C03) is visible and explicitly states "أي منتج تضيفه الآن سيُرسل كطلب جديد منفصل / Anything you add now is sent as a new, separate order." Wanting one more item, they tap a product card → arrive at G-02 → tap "أضف / Add" → the product is added to the (new, second) cart exactly as G-02's §4/§5.3 describe.

**Where they get stuck — without realizing it:** Nowhere on G-02 does the "new, separate order" statement reappear. That warning lives only on G-01's banner (G-01 §5.2); G-02 has no active-order banner at all (§5.6: "No active-order banner (it lives on G-01 only)"). But the actual "Add" action — the one this whole warning is about — happens on G-02, reached one tap after the guest last saw that banner on G-01. A guest who scrolled past the banner, tapped a card, and is now looking only at G-02 has no on-screen reminder that this "Add" tap starts a second order rather than adding to the one already in flight.

**Findings:**
- **F11 [Critical]** — A guest with an active order (status New / Accepted & preparing / On the way) who taps "Add"/"+" on G-02 receives no repetition, on G-02 itself, of G-01's "this creates a new, separate order" notice. Since the Add action itself is performed on G-02 (not on G-01, where the only warning lives), a guest can plausibly believe they are adding to their existing order and unknowingly submit a second, unwanted order — an operational error that reaches staff as an unplanned duplicate order/delivery to the same room.

## Structural / cross-cutting checklist (explicit items requested)

- **Header never shows the product name, only the fixed title "تفاصيل المنتج / Product details" (G-02-C01):** confirmed as specified (§4, §5.1). See **F6** — combined with the non-sticky action row, this can leave a guest scrolled into a long description with no way to confirm which product they are on.
- **No "Available" label at all, availability shown only by which action-row shape appears:** confirmed (§5.3: "There is no separate 'متوفر / Available' label"). Functionally the Add button/stepper reads as an implicit "yes," so this was not severity-worthy for any persona on its own; noted as **F14 [Minor]** — availability is only ever inferred, never stated in words, which is a small extra interpretive step for any guest.
- **Action row inline under the price, not sticky, scrolls away with a long description:** confirmed (§5.3, explicit rationale given). See **F6**.
- **Description cut at 500 characters with an ellipsis, no "Read more":** confirmed (§5.4, §7.4). See **F7**.
- **Out-of-stock badge not tappable on G-02, new 12 px helper "لإزالته من السلة افتح السلة / To remove it, open the cart":** confirmed (§4 G-02-C07/C08). See **F4** (text size) and **F8** (no action available on this screen).
- **Empty state declared unreachable by rule:** confirmed and the reasoning holds (§6.2 — every optional element has its own fallback; a product that cannot be shown at all is routed to the error state instead). The only gap found is the undefined "no name in either language" edge case (**F13 [Minor]**), which is a data-integrity corner case, not a missing empty state.
- **Reload/direct URL behaves differently than entry from G-01** (a fetch, a skeleton, and back to the top of G-01 with no remembered position): confirmed (§3.1 row 3, §6.1, acceptance criteria 4–5). See **F10**.
- **Image never cropped (4:3, contain) and not tappable, no zoom:** confirmed (§5.2, §7.6). See **F3**.
- **12 px is the minimum text size on this screen:** confirmed (§7.5). See **F4**, **F5**.
- **Arabic/English copy parity and English-reader fallback:** every UI string in the elements table is provided in both languages with matching meaning; the one gap is the silent Arabic fallback for missing English catalog content with no marker distinguishing it from a translated string. See **F2**.

### Consistency against G-01 (stepper, max-10 cap, badge wording, floating cart bar)

Checked line by line against G-01 §4/§5.4/§5.5/§7.1/§7.3:
- Stepper minimum/maximum (1–10), removal-at-1 behavior, and the 300 ms re-render lock: worded identically (G-02 §7.1 explicitly restates G-01 §7.1 "so the technical team never has to guess").
- Out-of-stock badge wording, including the cart-count suffix ("غير متوفر — في سلتك {N} / Out of stock — {N} in your cart"): identical text in both files.
- Max-quantity helper ("الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10") and its trigger condition (quantity exactly 10): identical.
- Floating cart bar — visibility rule, item-count wording table, "عرض السلة / View cart" label, total format and rounding: G-02 §5.5 states it is "Identical to G-01 §5.5" and the wording matches.

**No contradiction found** between G-02 and G-01 on any of these four points. The only difference is cosmetic and explained by available width: G-01's stepper buttons are 40×40 px with an extended 44×44 px tap zone (card is 171 px wide), while G-02's are 48×48 px with the tap area equal to the visible shape (content width is 358 px). This is a size difference, not a wording or behavior difference, and is not raised as a finding.

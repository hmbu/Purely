# Tester report — G-08 Store unavailable (المتجر غير متاح)

Author: Tester (Agent 2)
Screen under test: `/spec/screens/G-08.md`
Referenced: `/spec/screens/G-01.md` (catalog load rules, language toggle rules, empty state), `/spec/screens/G-07.md` (the screen G-08 cannot reach)
Method: seven personas, step-by-step walkthrough, problems only, no proposed fixes.

---

## Persona 1 — Guest in a hurry at 2 AM who wants one product fast

**Steps:**
1. Scans the QR code at 2 AM. Catalog request fails, is unreadable, or does not answer within 10 seconds (G-01 §6.1). G-08 retryable variant replaces G-01 entirely (§3.1).
2. Reads C02 "تعذّر فتح المتجر / The store could not open" and C03 "تحقّق من اتصال الإنترنت ثم أعد المحاولة / Check your internet connection and try again."
3. Taps "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" (B02). Trying state for 800 ms–10 s (§5.4).
4. It fails again. C04 "إذا استمرت المشكلة، اتصل بالاستقبال من هاتف الغرفة / If the problem continues, call reception from your room phone" now appears (§5.3).
5. Taps Retry several more times, still failing.

**Findings:**
- **[Medium]** C04's only actionable instruction is to use the **room phone**. It is text, deliberately never a `tel:` link and never a printed number (§5.3, §5.6: "there is no phone number and nothing tappable"). At 2 AM, a guest holding only their mobile phone is told to physically get up and locate a different device to solve a problem they discovered on this one; there is no number they could dial directly from the phone in their hand even if they wanted to call while still in bed or from wherever they happen to be. The design reasoning given (the app does not know the hotel's internal extension) is understandable, but the practical result for this exact persona — trying to move fast, at the worst hour to be getting out of bed — is real added friction with no on-screen mitigation.
- No blocking issue found in the Retry mechanism itself for this persona: B02 is a single, large (358×56 px), always-live button, and it does successfully get the guest into G-01 the moment a connection succeeds (§5.4 rule 4), with the cart and any prior state untouched (§3.1).

## Persona 2 — Foreign guest who cannot read Arabic

**Steps:**
1. This is possibly the guest's very first contact with the product (first scan fails before any language choice was ever saved). G-08 renders in Arabic by default (locked decision 7, §1).
2. Cannot read C02, C03, or C04.
3. Looks for a way to change language. B01 sits at the header's start edge (right edge in Arabic), showing the word "English" written in Latin script — the only non-Arabic text on the entire screen.
4. Taps it. Screen re-renders in English, LTR.

**Findings:**
- No issue found. The single Latin-script word "English" stands out clearly against an otherwise all-Arabic screen, which is a stronger visual cue than G-01's toggle enjoys (there it competes with other header content). B01 is confirmed never disabled or hidden, including while a Retry is running (§4, acceptance criterion 12), so this guest can switch language even mid-attempt. This is the one screen in the product built specifically to serve this persona at their point of maximum need (§9 decision 13), and it does so correctly as specified.

## Persona 3 — Elderly guest with poor eyesight

**Steps:**
1. Sees the 56 px icon, the 20 px bold title, 16 px body text, and the 358×56 px Retry button.
2. Reads C04's 14 px reception line once it appears.
3. Taps Retry, a large, easy-to-hit target.

**Findings:**
- **[Minor]** The same generic gray exclamation-mark icon (C01) is used for both variants — a transient connectivity problem and a fundamentally invalid link (§5.2: "The same icon in both variants"). For a guest relying more on shape/icon recognition than on reading small print, nothing about the icon itself signals which of the two very different situations they are in; they must read and comprehend the differing title text to know whether retrying is even worth attempting (in the invalid-link variant there is in fact no Retry button, so this matters).
- No other issue found: at 20 px/16 px/14 px this screen's text is at or above every size used on G-01, and the single full-width button is easier to hit accurately than G-01's smaller controls. This is a genuine improvement for this persona relative to G-01.

## Persona 4 — Guest who typed the wrong room number

Not applicable to this screen. Room-number entry happens later, at checkout on G-04, which requires a catalog to have already loaded — a guest on G-08 has not yet reached that point in the journey at all. G-08 shows no room number, no order data, and no field of any kind (§7.4: "G-08 renders no data of any kind"). No finding.

## Persona 5 — Guest who added a product that went out of stock before submitting

Not applicable in the ordinary sense: an item going out of stock is discovered on G-03 or at submission (M-04), both of which require a catalog to already be loaded — i.e., the guest is already past G-08. The one adjacent case is a guest with a cart already built who then triggers a page reload that fails to reload the catalog (e.g. mid-session connectivity drop): they land on the retryable variant with their cart preserved untouched (§3.1: "What G-08 does not disturb"). No finding: this is functionally identical to the general Retry walkthrough already covered under other personas, and the spec correctly does not attempt to show or resolve stock status on this screen, since it renders no data at all.

## Persona 6 — Guest with a weak connection that drops during submission

**Steps:**
1. Guest previously submitted an order successfully; it is now in status "New" (still cancellable per locked decision 6) or later.
2. Later, on a weak or intermittently dropping connection, the guest reloads the page (or rescans the QR) — perhaps specifically because they are worried about the connection and want to check on their order.
3. The catalog fetch fails or times out. G-08 (retryable variant) replaces whatever screen would have loaded.
4. The guest wants to reach G-07 (My orders) to check status, or G-06 to cancel while still possible. Looks at G-08's header and body for any such control.
5. Finds only: the language toggle (B01) and "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" (B02). No "طلباتي / My orders," no back arrow, no other link of any kind (§4, §5.1, §5.6).

**Findings:**
- **[Critical]** G-07 needs no catalog at all and would render correctly and instantly if reached (G-07 §3.1: "G-07 sends no catalog request on any entry... and never shows G-08"), yet G-08 offers **no path whatsoever** to G-07 or G-06. The guest's only way to reach their own order tracking or cancellation control is to make the entirely unrelated catalog request succeed first. If the order is still status "New" and the guest specifically wants to cancel it before staff acceptance (locked decision 6, a time-limited window), every second spent retrying a catalog load that has nothing to do with that order is a second closer to the order being accepted and the cancel option disappearing for good. This can cause the guest to miss the cancellation window entirely, purely because of a failure in a part of the product (the product catalog) that is irrelevant to the action they are trying to take. The Thinker's own file already flags this exact gap for the reviewer (§5.6, §9 decision 17); this report confirms it independently as Critical, since it can make the guest fail to complete a locked-decision journey (cancel-before-acceptance) through no fault of their own.

## Persona 7 — Guest who wants to change the order after submitting it

**Steps:**
1. Guest has an active or recently finished order and, for any reason (weak connection per Persona 6, a stale bookmark, closing and reopening the browser), lands on G-08 while trying to reach My Orders or tracking.
2. If the classifier (§7.1) determines the link is genuinely **invalid** rather than a transient failure, the **invalid-link variant** is shown instead: same missing paths as the retryable variant, but this time with **no Retry button at all** (§5.2, §4: "Not rendered at all in the invalid-link variant").
3. The only instruction given is C03: "امسح رمز QR الموجود في غرفتك مرة أخرى / Scan the QR code in your room again," plus C04's reception line.

**Findings:**
- **[Critical]** The gap already identified under Persona 6 is strictly worse in the invalid-link variant: there is no Retry to eventually succeed through, so the guest's only documented path back into the product at all is to physically go and rescan the correct QR code, or call reception. If the guest is not currently in the room (or does not have the physical code at hand for any reason), the interface offers zero way to reach an order they know exists on this same device, even though the record and G-07's ability to render it are both sitting untouched in local storage the whole time (§3.1: "What G-08 does not disturb"). A guest who specifically wants to change or cancel an in-flight order is left with no in-app recourse whatsoever in this variant.

## Cross-cutting checks (not tied to a single persona)

**Retry has no limit, no back-off, no counter, no growing message (§5.4 rules 5–7):**
- **[Minor]** Confirmed as specified: ten rapid taps produce ten identical attempts with an identical failure message each time (only C04's appearance after the first failure changes anything, and it does not change again after that). This is a deliberate design choice ("a cap would strand precisely the guest it claims to protect," §5.4 rule 7) and does not block the guest, but the complete absence of any acknowledgment of repetition (e.g., after ten failures the guest sees exactly the same two sentences as after one) may read as unresponsive to a guest who has now tried for a while.

**Connection returning on its own (§5.5):**
- **[Medium]** By explicit design, G-08 "does not watch the network... never reloads or navigates by itself." A guest who tries Retry a few times, fails, and gives up (stops interacting, sets the phone down) will never be told or moved forward even if the hotel's connection issue resolves itself minutes later — they remain on the error screen indefinitely until they personally choose to tap Retry or reload again. Combined with the reception line's total reliance on the guest acting (§5.3), a guest who has mentally "given up" has no path back into the product except their own unprompted initiative.

**Success defined as leaving the screen; empty catalog is success (§6.2, §6.4):**
- Confirmed consistent with G-01: a valid-but-empty catalog is treated as success and lands on G-01's own empty state, never back on G-08. No finding; this matches the instruction and avoids a G-08/G-01-empty ping-pong.

**Unclassifiable failures default to retryable (§7.1 rule 3):**
- Confirmed as specified — no "unknown" case is left undefined, and the bias toward offering Retry rather than withholding it is stated and consistent. No finding.

**Language toggle and consistency with G-01/the map:**
- Toggle behavior (default Arabic, no reload, saves choice, label is the other language's name) is reused verbatim from G-01 §7.4 and is implemented identically here (§7.3) — confirmed consistent, no finding.
- G-08 shows no order-related data of any kind, so the five canonical status labels and Western-digit conventions have nothing to check against on this screen — confirmed not applicable, no conflict found.
- Tap targets: B01 ≥44×44 px, B02 358×56 px — both meet or exceed the 44×44 px convention, no finding.

### Critical findings
1. **(Persona 6)** G-08's retryable variant offers no path to G-07 (My orders) or G-06 (tracking), even though G-07 needs no catalog and would render fine; a guest whose order is still status "New" can lose the entire cancel-before-acceptance window (locked decision 6) purely because an unrelated catalog request keeps failing.
2. **(Persona 7)** The same gap is absolute in the invalid-link variant, which has no Retry button at all: a guest with an in-flight order and no way to immediately rescan the correct QR code has zero in-app path to their own orders, even though the record and G-07 are both fully intact and reachable in principle.

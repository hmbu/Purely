# Tester report — G-03 Cart (السلة)

Author: Tester (Agent 2)
Screen under test: `/spec/screens/G-03.md` (Draft)
Reference for consistency checks: `/spec/screens/G-01.md` (Approved), `/spec/screens-map.md`

G-03 is the first of the two stock gates (map §4 decision 9) and the screen the Reorder merge runs on (map §4 decision 7). Persona 5 and Persona 7 are therefore treated as the priority personas below, per the task brief.

Findings are tagged **[Critical]**, **[Medium]**, or **[Minor]**, numbered F1–F26, and reported without proposed fixes.

---

## Persona 1 — A guest in a hurry at 2 AM who wants one product fast

**Steps:** Adds one product on G-01 → taps the floating cart bar → lands on G-03 in the success state (no skeleton for this entry path) with the line already visible → the availability check (§5.6) starts in the background; C08 reads "جارٍ التحقق من التوفر… / Checking availability…" and "إتمام الطلب / Checkout" (B05) sits at 50% opacity → guest, wanting to move fast, taps "Checkout" anyway before the check resolves.

**Where they get stuck:** The tap on the disabled button does nothing at all — "no shake, no toast, no tooltip" (§5.5, B05 row). The only signal of *why* is the 14px helper line above the button. A guest scanning the screen quickly at night, focused on the big dark "Checkout" pill, can easily miss the small helper text and simply conclude the button is broken, tapping it again with the same (non-)result.

**Findings:**
- **F1 [Medium]** — "Checkout" can be disabled for up to 5 seconds on every entry with zero feedback on tap (no shake/toast/tooltip per §5.5), and the only explanation is a single 14px line (C08) placed above it. A hurried guest has no positive confirmation that the app registered the tap or is doing anything at all.
- **F2 [Minor]** — The availability check "runs once on every entry… re-entering G-03 … runs it again" (§5.6). A guest bouncing G-03 → G-04 → back → G-03 (e.g., to double check something) pays the up-to-5-second wait again each time, with no session-level caching of a check that already succeeded moments earlier.

## Persona 2 — A foreign guest who cannot read Arabic

**Steps:** Has already switched to English on G-01 (the only place the toggle exists, map §2.3 rule 6) → adds a product → opens G-03, which renders fully in English with no toggle of its own → reviews the cart without difficulty.

**Where they get stuck:** Only if they reach G-03 *before* ever visiting G-01 in a state where the language was already set correctly — for example, a browser reload landing directly on the G-03 URL with no saved preference (default Arabic, §1 "Language" note), or arriving via G-06 → "Reorder" in a session where the language was never switched.

**Findings:**
- **F3 [Minor]** — G-03 has no language control of its own (§5.1: "No language toggle"). A guest who lands here first, in the wrong language, must leave to G-01, find B01, switch, and return — which re-triggers the availability check (§5.6) a second time on re-entry, compounding the detour with another up-to-5-second wait.

## Persona 3 — An elderly guest with poor eyesight

**Steps:** Adds two products on G-01, opens G-03, reads the two lines, notices the "Checkout" button looks slightly dimmer than expected, tries to read the small text above it → later, wanting to reduce one line from 2 to 1, taps "−" once, but the tap that actually registers is a second, unintended tap because the first one seemed to produce no visible change fast enough.

**Where they get stuck:**
1. The single explanation for a disabled "Checkout" is a 14px line (C08) with no bold or color emphasis (§5.5), sitting above a button whose only disabled cue is 50% opacity — no icon, no lock symbol, no border change. Two subtle signals stacked on top of each other are easy to miss for low vision.
2. The out-of-stock badge (C06, and the identical pattern already present in G-01's C07) is specified as "gray filled pill, gray text 14 px" (§4) — low text-on-fill contrast on exactly the element meant to draw attention to a problem.
3. Tapping "−" one time too many: because "−" at quantity 1 removes the line immediately, "with no confirmation and no undo" (§5.8 decision 3, §7.1), a guest who misjudges the current count on the small 16px quantity display (C03) — or simply taps again out of uncertainty after the first tap — can delete a line outright with no warning. Recovery requires leaving G-03, going back to G-01/G-02, re-finding and re-adding the exact product, then re-entering G-03 (which re-runs the up-to-5-second availability check, F2).

**Findings:**
- **F4 [Medium]** — The only explanation for a disabled "Checkout" (C08) is small (14px), unstyled text; combined with the 50%-opacity-only disabled cue on B05, a low-vision guest has two weak, stackable signals and no strong one.
- **F5 [Medium]** — The out-of-stock badge (C06) is "gray filled pill, gray text," a low-contrast combination on the one element meant to flag a problem to the guest.
- **F6 [Minor]** — "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10" (C04) is kept at 11px, the smallest text on the screen (§7.7), unchanged from G-01's own smallest-text choice.
- **F7 [Medium]** — Tapping "−" once too many at quantity 1 deletes the line instantly with no confirmation and no undo (§5.8 decision 3). The only recovery path is leaving the screen, re-finding the product elsewhere, and re-adding it — with no in-place "undo" affordance of any kind, and no visual difference between a "−" that will decrement and a "−" that will delete.

## Persona 4 — A guest who typed the wrong room number

**Steps:** This persona's core problem (a mistyped room number) happens entirely on G-04/M-01, not on G-03. To fix it after submission, the only path in the map is: G-06 → "Cancel order" → M-02 (only while status is New) → order becomes Cancelled → G-06 → "Reorder" → **G-03**, where the merge (§5.7) rebuilds the cart before the guest can retype the room number correctly on G-04.

**Where they get stuck:** Not on G-03 itself, but the screen is unavoidably in the middle of the only fix this persona has. Every correction of a room-number typo costs this guest a full pass through G-03's Reorder loading state and its up-to-5-second availability check, even though nothing about their actual cart needs re-checking (it's the same order they just had, moments earlier).

**Findings:**
- **F8 [Minor]** — (Observation, not a G-03 defect) The only route to correct a submitted order's room number always re-enters G-03 through the Reorder merge, adding a full availability re-check and cart re-review to what is, for this persona, purely a typo fix. This is a consequence of decisions made at G-06/map level, but it is worth noting that G-03 offers no "skip the check, I just want to resubmit exactly what I had" shortcut.
- **F9 [Minor]** — G-03's own entry description guarantees only that "the cart is exactly as the guest left it" when returning from G-04 (§3.1); nothing on G-03 or referenced from it states whether G-04's own fields (room number, notes, payment method, the optional cash amount) survive a G-04 → G-03 (back/"Edit cart") → G-04 round trip. A guest going back to double-check a quantity while also worried about their room number has no stated guarantee the room number they had already typed is still there.

## Persona 5 — A guest who added a product that went out of stock before submitting

This is the priority persona for G-03: the screen exists specifically to run the first stock gate.

**Steps:** Adds Product A and Product B on G-01 → opens G-03 → the availability check runs; Product B comes back "out of stock" → B's line switches within 100ms to its out-of-stock form: image at 50% opacity, "غير متوفر / Out of stock" badge, no stepper, "إزالة / Remove" in its place, line total still shown (§5.4) → C08 reads "أزل المنتج غير المتوفر للمتابعة / Remove the out-of-stock item to continue" and "Checkout" is disabled → guest taps "Remove" → line B disappears, totals update, "Checkout" re-enables.

**Where they get stuck / findings:**
- **F10 [Medium]** — Once a line is marked out of stock, "the quantity the guest had chosen is no longer shown anywhere on the line" (§5.4). The one screen dedicated to reviewing the cart before checkout hides the exact number the guest wanted; that information only still exists as a badge suffix on G-01's card, which the guest isn't looking at while on G-03.
- **F11 [Medium]** — There is no way to remove several out-of-stock lines at once: "with several such lines the guest taps Remove on each" (§5.4, §5.8 decision 2/11); the only bulk-removal mechanism (M-04) exists later, at submission, not here.
- **F12 [Medium]** — When the availability check fails or times out (5s), no mark is written and "Checkout" is simply re-enabled (§5.6 row 3, §6.3 item 1), with the only signal being the small C08 line "تعذّر التحقق من التوفر… / Could not check availability…". A guest who doesn't notice or read that line proceeds through G-04, types a room number, and confirms at M-01, only to discover unavailable items at M-04 — meaning the *first* stock gate can silently do nothing on a weak connection, and the guest pays the cost later, after investing more effort.
- **F13 [Medium]** — An "available" response from the check never clears an existing out-of-stock mark, and G-03 itself never clears one either (§5.6 row 1, §5.8 decision 5). A product that was marked unavailable earlier in the session — including by a transient "not found" response — stays blocked on this screen for the rest of the session even if it becomes purchasable again, with no manual refresh control and no message telling the guest that reloading the page might restore it.
- **F14 [Minor]** — Cart lines are never tappable and there is no path from G-03 back to G-02 (§5.8 decision 11). A guest deciding whether to remove an unavailable item (e.g., wanting to double-check its description, or look for a similar product) cannot get more information or find an alternative from this screen; they must remember the item and start over on G-01.
- **F15 [Medium]** — An out-of-stock line's price still counts toward "Total" in the bottom bar until it is removed (§5.5, §7.2), at the exact same moment "Checkout" is disabled *because* of that same line. The guest sees a total they explicitly cannot pay for as shown, with no marker in the totals block itself connecting the inflated number to the disabled button — only the separate C08 helper line ties the two together.

## Persona 6 — A guest with a weak connection that drops during submission

G-03's own network-dependent behavior is the catalog fetch on reload (10s limit) and the availability check (5s limit); actual submission happens on G-04, so this persona's experience on G-03 is about these two waits.

**Steps:** Reloads the browser directly on the G-03 URL on a weak hotel Wi-Fi connection → sees the skeleton (C11) → the catalog fetch takes close to its 10-second limit → once it succeeds, the availability check starts and takes close to its own 5-second limit before the lines and bottom bar appear fully interactive.

**Where they get stuck / findings:**
- **F16 [Medium]** — On this reload path, two sequential network waits stack (catalog fetch up to 10s, then the availability check up to 5s, §6.1) for a combined worst case near 15 seconds, shown only as one generic pulsing skeleton with no distinction between the two phases and no percentage or message. A guest on a weak connection has no way to tell whether the two phases are progressing or whether the app has stalled.
- **F17 [Medium]** — Because the availability response can arrive at any point within its 5-second window while the guest is already interacting with a line, a line can switch in place from its available form (stepper B02/B03) to its out-of-stock form ("Remove" button) mid-interaction (§5.4, §5.6). On a variable-latency connection, a tap timed at that moment risks landing on "Remove" instead of the "+"/"−" the guest meant to tap, since the two controls occupy the same position moments apart with no transition warning.
- **F18 [Minor]** — There is no manual retry for a failed availability check on G-03 itself: "there is no Retry control on this screen" (§6.3 item 1). A guest on a weak connection whose check failed must leave the screen (e.g., to G-04 and back) to get the check to run again, since nothing else on G-03 retriggers it.

## Persona 7 — A guest who wants to change the order after submitting it (via Reorder)

This persona reaches G-03 through the Reorder merge (§5.7), the second priority flow for this screen per the task brief.

**Steps:** On G-06, for a Delivered or Cancelled order, taps "Reorder" → G-03 shows the loading skeleton while one availability check covers the current cart plus the previous order's products (up to 5s) → the merge runs: available products get their quantity added to any existing line (capped at 10, excess dropped silently, §5.7 step 2), unavailable products are skipped and counted → the success state appears with the merged cart, and the toast (C10) appears if at least one product was skipped.

**Where they get stuck / findings:**
- **F19 [Critical]** — The merge caps a line's quantity at 10 and "any excess is dropped silently… No toast for the cap" (§5.7 step 2, §5.8 decision 8). If the guest's existing cart quantity plus the previous order's quantity for the same product exceeds 10 (e.g., 5 already in the cart + 8 from the previous order), the guest receives only what fits under the cap with the sole trace being the small "Max 10" tag (C04) next to the resulting number — nothing states that units were dropped or how many. A guest can easily believe the reorder fully succeeded, proceed through checkout, and only discover the shortfall when staff deliver fewer items than expected — a mismatch between what the guest believes was ordered and what is actually charged and delivered.
- **F20 [Medium]** — The merge combines the previous order's lines into whatever is already in the cart with "no 'replace cart?' modal" (map §4 decision 7) and no visual marker anywhere on G-03 distinguishing a pre-existing line from one just created or increased by the merge (§5.3, §5.4 define no such marker). A guest reordering while unrelated items are already in the cart cannot tell, from this screen, which quantities came from where.
- **F21 [Medium]** — The post-merge toast (C10) reports only a count of distinct skipped products ("لم تتم إضافة {N} منتجات لأنها لم تعد متوفرة / {N} items were not added because they are no longer available," §5.7) and never names them. A guest specifically trying to recreate a previous order has no way, from the toast or from the screen, to know *which* items were dropped — they must compare the resulting cart against their memory of the previous order, item by item.
- **F22 [Minor]** — Tapping back during the Reorder loading state abandons the merge and returns the guest to G-06 with the cart untouched (§6.1, §3.2), but nothing is stated about G-06 acknowledging that "Reorder" was cancelled or offering to try again; the action simply appears to have done nothing.

## Cross-cutting checks (against G-01, G-02, and the map)

- **F23 [Medium]** — G-01's floating cart-bar total is defined as "sum over all cart lines of (quantity × unit price **as currently loaded in the catalog**)" (G-01 §7.2), which presumes the product still has a catalog entry. G-03 explicitly defines a fallback for a product "absent from the session catalog copy" (fully delisted, not merely out of stock): render from "the line's own snapshot" and treat it as out of stock (G-03 §5.2 item 2, §5.8 decision 10). G-01's file has **no equivalent fallback rule** for its own cart-bar total in that same situation. If a product is delisted mid-session, there is nothing in either file that guarantees G-01's cart bar and G-03's totals block compute or display the same amount for the identical cart.
- **F24 [Minor]** — Verified, no defect: the empty state correctly omits the entire bottom bar (S03, including "Checkout") rather than showing a disabled "Checkout" next to "Continue shopping" (§5.8 decision 1; G-03-B05 "Not rendered in the empty state"; criterion 8). This matches the check requested in the task and passes.
- **F25 [Minor]** — Verified, no defect: an available line can be removed only by "−" at quantity 1 — no trash icon, swipe, "clear cart," long-press, confirmation, or undo (§5.8 decision 2/3) — and an out-of-stock line only by the dedicated "إزالة / Remove" button. Both rules and the stepper's left/right button order match G-01's identical rule and geometry exactly (§5.3: "same as G-01, so the two screens' steppers look identical"). No inconsistency found between the two screens on this specific point (see F7 above for the separate concern about the removal gesture itself having no confirmation/undo, which is a UX finding, not a cross-screen inconsistency).
- **F26 [Minor]** — Verified, largely consistent: the max-10 cap, its helper text, and the currency/decimal formatting are identical in wording and behavior between G-01 and G-03. The one deliberate difference is the out-of-stock badge: G-01's badge appends "— في سلتك {N} / — {N} in your cart" whenever the cart holds the product, while G-03's badge (C06) never shows a quantity at all, because G-03 hides the chosen quantity entirely on an out-of-stock line (§5.4). This is the same underlying gap already raised as F10, cross-referenced here as the formal G-01-vs-G-03 wording comparison the task asked for.

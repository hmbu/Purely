# Tester report — G-01 Store (المتجر)

Author: Tester (Agent 2)
Screen under test: `/spec/screens/G-01.md`
Method: seven personas, step-by-step walkthrough, problems only, no proposed fixes.

---

## Persona 1 — Guest in a hurry at 2 AM who wants one product fast

**Steps:**
1. Scans the room QR code. Browser opens the store URL. Skeleton (C10) is shown.
2. Waits for the catalog to load.
3. Wants to find one specific product (e.g. "a bottle of water") without browsing everything.
4. Scrolls or taps a category chip to find it.
5. Taps "أضف / Add" (B04) on the product card.
6. Taps the floating cart bar (B07) to go to G-03.

**Findings:**

- **[Medium]** There is no search field on G-01 (confirmed absent per the screen's own §5.6 and map decision), only category chips and scrolling. A guest who wants exactly one known product fast has no faster path than scrolling/scanning categories manually. This is a real friction point for this specific persona even though it was already decided at the map stage; flagging it here because it directly affects the "one product fast" goal.
- **[Medium]** The loading state can last up to **10 seconds** (§6.1) with only a static, slowly pulsing gray skeleton (C10) and no percentage, spinner, or elapsed-time cue, and no way to cancel/retry early. A guest in a hurry (or on a weak connection, persona 6) has no choice but to watch a silent skeleton for up to 10 full seconds before anything happens (success or G-08).
- **[Minor]** Rapid double-tapping "Add" (B04) before the documented ~100 ms UI update completes is not addressed anywhere in the spec (§4, §5.4, §5.5, acceptance criterion 19). It is unclear whether a fast double-tap could register twice (e.g. quantity becomes 2 instead of 1, or two competing "add" events race), which is exactly the kind of tap behavior a guest trying to move fast at 2 AM would produce.
- No blocking issue found for the simple "add one item and go to cart" path itself — Add → stepper → cart bar → G-03 works in the number of taps the spec describes.

## Persona 2 — Foreign guest who cannot read Arabic

**Steps:**
1. Scans the QR code. G-01 opens in Arabic (RTL) by default regardless of browser language (§1, §7.4), since this is the first visit on this device.
2. Cannot read any of the Arabic copy (hotel name, "الدفع عند الاستلام في غرفتك", category names, product names).
3. Looks for a way to change language. Finds B01 at the header's start edge (right edge, since interface is RTL), showing the word "English" written in English.
4. Taps B01. Interface re-renders in English, LTR, with B01 now reading "العربية".
5. Continues browsing product names, categories, prices in English.

**Findings:**

- **[Medium]** Per §5.1 and §5.3: "If the English value is missing, the Arabic value is shown in the English interface too (never a blank)" — this applies to the hotel name, category names, and (per §5.4 item 2) product names. This means that after this persona does everything right (finds the toggle, switches to English), any product or category the hotel didn't translate will still display in Arabic script inside an otherwise-English interface. For the one persona this toggle exists to serve, this is a real gap: they may not be able to tell products apart by name at all if several go untranslated, relying only on image and price.
- **[Minor]** B01 (language toggle) sits at the header's start edge, which is the **right** edge under the default Arabic/RTL layout. A foreign guest with no RTL familiarity may not instinctively look at the right edge first for a settings/language control. The label itself is well designed (plain "English" text, not an icon or flag), which mitigates this once found.
- No other blocking issues found: currency, "Out of stock", "Max 10", empty-state text, and the "Pay on delivery to your room" line are all confirmed bilingual with matching meaning (see cross-cutting section below).

## Persona 3 — Elderly guest with poor eyesight

**Steps:**
1. Opens the store, sees the header, category bar, and product grid.
2. Tries to read the hotel name (18 px), category chip labels, product names (14 px), prices (14 px), and the "طلباتي / My orders" label (10 px, the smallest text on the screen per §7.6).
3. Tries to tap "+" / "−" (B05/B06, 40×40 px) and category chips (B03, 32 px tall) accurately.
4. Adds a product, looks for confirmation that it worked.

**Findings:**

- **[Medium]** §7.6 states the minimum text size on the screen is 10 px ("My orders" label, B02), with most other copy at 11 px, and product name/price at only 14 px. Nowhere does the spec state whether the layout supports OS/browser text scaling or pinch-zoom, which matters a great deal for this persona. Given fixed card widths (171 px) and 2-line name truncation, it is unclear whether zooming in would still work correctly or would break the truncation/ellipsis logic.
- **[Medium]** Contradiction on tap-target size: the file's own conventions line (top of file, line 6) and §7.6 both assert "every tap target at least 44 × 44 px," and §7.6 explicitly lists "Add," "+," "−" among the elements this applies to. But the elements table gives B04 "Add" a height of exactly **40 px**, and B05/B06 ("−"/"+") as exactly **40 × 40 px**, with no stated invisible tap-area extension — unlike B03 (category chip), which explicitly says "32 px tall... tap area extended to 44 px tall." It is unresolved whether B04/B05/B06 actually have an expanded invisible tap area or are genuinely 40 px, which is smaller than the stated minimum and matters directly for an elderly guest's tap accuracy.
- **[Minor]** The font size of the category chip label (B03) is never specified anywhere in the elements table or in §5.3, unlike almost every other text element on the screen (which all get explicit px values). This is a gap regardless of persona, but it is specifically relevant here since undersized chip text would be hard to read.
- **[Minor]** B02 "My orders" combines the smallest text on the screen (10 px) with a 24 px icon and no visible badge/count; an elderly guest (or a foreign guest) may not register what the icon means without reading the small label underneath it.
- No finding on the button→stepper transition mechanism itself (it is a real UI change, not purely a color shift), but combined with "no toast" (§5.6, confirmed — see cross-cutting section) an elderly guest with poor eyesight may not notice at a glance that "Add" was successfully tapped if their eyes are on the product image rather than the small action row.

## Persona 4 — Guest who typed the wrong room number

Room-number entry is not part of G-01 at all (locked decision 1 puts it on G-04 at checkout); G-04 and M-01 are not yet specced/tested, so the actual mistake and any correction flow ("Edit room number" on M-01) cannot be evaluated here. Reporting only what G-01 itself does or fails to do when this guest is on G-01:

- **[Minor]** The active-order banner (C03) shown on G-01, after an order is submitted, displays only the order number and status label (§5.2: "طلبك رقم {order number} — {status label}"). It never shows the room number. So a guest who suspects they may have mistyped their room number, and returns to (or lands back on) G-01, gets no cue from this screen either confirming or refuting which room the active order is going to — they would have to open G-06 (not yet specced) to check. This is not a defect introduced by G-01 (room number was never meant to live here), just a note that G-01 offers no help for this persona's specific worry.
- No other G-01-owned issue found for this persona; the actual fix path (cancel via M-02 while status is New, then reorder) plays out on screens not yet written.

## Persona 5 — Guest who added a product that went out of stock before submitting

**Steps:**
1. Adds Product X to the cart on G-01 while it is shown as available (stepper visible, quantity 1+).
2. Continues browsing; time passes; Product X goes out of stock server-side.
3. G-01 does not re-fetch the catalog on its own (per §5.6: "Stock is re-checked on G-03 and again at submission (M-04)" — no live polling on G-01, no pull-to-refresh).
4. Eventually navigates to G-03/G-04 where the actual stock recheck happens (not this screen).
5. Guest goes back to G-01 (e.g. via back arrow from G-03).

**Findings:**

- **[Medium]** §5.4 item 4 explicitly states: "No stepper is shown even if the cart already contains this product" once a card is marked out of stock — the action row is fully replaced by the C07 "غير متوفر / Out of stock" badge, with no visible reference to the quantity the guest already added. Meanwhile the floating cart bar (S05/B07) "still counts it" per the same line. This creates a visible contradiction on the same screen: the card looks like the item was never selected (no quantity shown, no stepper), while the cart bar total/count still includes it. A guest in this exact persona could reasonably conclude their earlier selection was lost and re-browse or get confused about the cart total, when in fact nothing was removed.
- **[Medium]** It is unclear whether G-01 itself learns about a stock change that G-03 discovers during its recheck. §3.1 (entry table) says returning to G-01 from G-03 shows "card steppers reflect any change made on G-03," but §6.1/§5.6 also state G-01 does not re-fetch the catalog except on full reload or the empty-state "Refresh." If a product becomes out of stock only in G-03's recheck (not yet reflected in G-01's original catalog load), it is not specified whether G-01, on returning, will actually show that card as out-of-stock (C07) or still show it as available with a working stepper — i.e., whether the two screens can visibly disagree about the same product's availability. This ambiguity sits at the seam between G-01 and G-03/M-04 (not fully resolved by this screen's file alone).
- No blocking issue for the final journey: the spec confirms two further stock gates exist downstream (disabled "Checkout" on G-03, and M-04 at submission), so this persona is not stopped from completing an order, only potentially confused by G-01's own display while it happens.

## Persona 6 — Guest with a weak connection that drops during submission

The actual drop (during order submission) happens on G-04, and its recovery is handled by M-03 "Order not sent" — none of that is on G-01 or yet specced. Reporting only what G-01 does for a guest on a weak connection in general, and what they see if/when they land back on G-01:

- **[Medium]** During the initial catalog load, a weak connection could easily stretch toward the stated 10-second ceiling (§6.1) before falling back to G-08. For the whole 10 seconds the guest sees only a static pulsing skeleton with no manual "try again now" option and no indication of how much longer to expect — on a connection that is dropping in and out, this is a long, silent wait with no guest-initiated escape hatch.
- **[Medium]** The active-order status fetch (§5.2) is stated to fail silently ("the banner shows the last known status from the device without any error text") and is not retried until the next full load of G-01. On a weak/dropping connection this failure could recur repeatedly, so the guest could see a stale status (e.g. still "جديد / New") for an extended period with zero indication that the displayed status might be outdated, which could erode trust in the banner without the guest ever being told anything is wrong.
- No finding that a weak connection breaks anything on G-01's own core browsing/adding functions — Add/+/− are explicitly device-only actions with no server call (§7.1), so browsing and building a cart works offline-tolerant on this screen specifically.

## Persona 7 — Guest who wants to change the order after submitting it

Editing an already-submitted order is not offered anywhere in the spec (by design — map §3 states no editing after submission in v1; only cancel-while-New via M-02, then reorder via G-06 → G-03). Those mechanics live on G-06/M-02/G-03, not yet specced/tested. Reporting only what G-01 itself contributes to or fails to guard against:

- **[Critical]** G-01 simultaneously shows (a) the active-order banner (C03), telling the guest they already have an order New/Accepted/On the way, and (b) a fully functional product grid where Add/+/− work exactly as normal, with no warning, disabling, or confirmation tied to the fact that an order is already in flight. A guest who wants to "change" their order (e.g. add one more item) by browsing G-01 and tapping "Add" again has no way to know from this screen whether doing so will modify their existing order or silently create a brand-new, separate order — and per the current spec set, it is the latter (there is no order-editing feature, and G-03/G-04 do not appear to check for an existing active order either). This gap first becomes visible on G-01 because it is the only screen that shows both the "you have an active order" state and the "add products" controls at the same time, with nothing connecting the two. If unresolved, this can produce a genuine operational error: staff and guest each believing they are dealing with one order, when a second, separate order has actually been created.
- No other G-01-specific issue for this persona; the cancel/reorder mechanics themselves are on screens not yet written.

## Cross-cutting checks (not tied to a single persona)

**Max-quantity-10 rule and its inline helper (§4, §5.4, §7.1):**
- **[Minor]** The spec never explains to the guest why the cap is 10 (no tooltip, no rationale text) — only the bare "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10" (C08) appears once the limit is hit. A guest ordering for a group (e.g. wanting 12 waters) has no way to tell this is an intentional store rule rather than a glitch, and no alternate path (e.g. contact reception) is suggested anywhere on this screen.

**Out-of-stock card behavior:**
- Covered above under Persona 5 — the "no stepper even when already in the cart" behavior is confirmed present exactly as the spec states, and is flagged as a Medium finding for the visible contradiction with the cart bar's running total.

**Active-order banner (fetched once, no polling, most-recent-only, silent failure):**
- **[Medium]** When two or more orders are active, §5.2 states the banner "shows the most recently submitted one" and the others are "reachable from G-07," but the banner gives no hint that another active order exists (no "+1 more," no count). A guest with two active orders has no cue on G-01 that a second one is out there unless they think to open "My orders" (B02) on their own.
- Silent-failure-on-stale-status finding is covered above under Persona 6.

**10-second load timeout to G-08:**
- Covered under Personas 1 and 6 (Medium, both times for the same underlying gap: no early bail-out, no progress indication during the wait).

**Absence of any toast when an item is added:**
- No blocking issue found; the in-place stepper appearance and the cart bar update are a deliberate, documented replacement for a toast (§5.6). The one persona-specific risk raised is under Persona 3 (elderly, poor eyesight) — see above.

**Arabic/English copy parity:**
- Checked every bilingual string in the elements table and content sections (header lines, banner lines, status labels, empty-state text, "Add"/"Out of stock"/"Max 10", item-count wording, cart bar labels, "Refresh"). All pairs convey matching meaning; the Arabic item-count table (§7.3) correctly uses distinct Arabic singular/dual/plural forms (منتج واحد / منتجان / N منتجات / N منتجًا) against a single English "N item(s)" pattern — this is a legitimate grammatical difference, not an inconsistency. **No Arabic-only or English-only UI copy was found.**

**Tap-target sizes and text sizes:**
- Covered above under Persona 3 (Medium finding on the 44×44 px rule vs. the 40 px dimensions given for B04/B05/B06, and Minor finding on the missing chip font size).

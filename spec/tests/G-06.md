# Tester findings — G-06 Order tracking

Author: Tester (Agent 2)
Screen file tested: `/spec/screens/G-06.md` (status: Draft)
Cross-referenced: `/spec/screens/G-01.md` (Approved — canonical status labels, conventions, active-order banner behavior), `/spec/screens/G-04.md`, `/spec/screens/M-01.md`, `/spec/screens/G-05.md` (all Draft, referenced for the room-number safety chain and the client-order-key contract), `/spec/screens-map.md`.
Method: walked the screen as each of the seven personas in `CLAUDE.md` §"Agent 2 — Tester". Persona 4 (wrong room number) and persona 7 (wants to change the order) were given the deepest walk-through, per instruction, since G-06 is the screen where cancellation — the only repair path in version 1 — actually lives. Findings are reported only; no fixes are proposed.

---

## Persona 1 — Guest in a hurry at 2 AM, wants one product fast

Steps: taps "Track order" from G-05, lands on G-06 in status New.

- The screen draws synchronously from the device record (no skeleton, no spinner, §6.1), which is fast for this persona.
- Polling happens automatically every 20 seconds without any action from the guest, which genuinely helps this persona: they can check once and not have to remember to refresh.
- **Finding G-06-01 (Minor).** If the guest checks once, backgrounds the tab, and goes on with their evening, there is no sound, vibration, or notification of any kind when the status later changes (polling itself pauses while the tab is hidden, §5.5 rule 5, and resumes silently on return). This is a limitation inherent to a login-free browser tab with no push mechanism rather than a defect specific to this file, but it means a hurried guest who wants to "fire and forget" must still remember to reopen the tab to learn anything changed.
- No other friction found for this persona.

## Persona 2 — Foreign guest who cannot read Arabic

Steps: same entry, no language toggle on G-06 (§5.1: "the toggle lives on G-01 and G-08 only").

- If the language was already switched on G-01, everything renders correctly in English, including the status labels (drawn verbatim from the shared table). No problem found in that path.
- Otherwise, the same gap as G-05 applies here: no toggle and no cue that one exists (see the G-05 test file, finding G-05-02, for the shared reasoning). Not re-scored separately here since it is the identical, single cross-screen decision (map §2.3 rule 6), not a distinct new problem on this file.
- **No new finding beyond the one already recorded against G-05.**

## Persona 3 — Elderly guest with poor eyesight

Steps: same entry.

- Text sizing rules are generous and explicit (14 px floor, 28 px room number, 22 px status headline, stated contrast ratios, no color-only signaling, pinch-zoom/text-scaling never disabled). This is a well-specified screen for this persona in isolation.
- **Finding G-06-02 (Minor).** Across the three checkpoints where the guest is shown the same room number for safety reasons, the type size shrinks each time: 64 px on M-01, 48 px on G-05, and only 28 px on G-06 — the exact screen where the file itself says "the mistake still costs nothing" and where the number must be read *while a repair is still possible*. A guest with poor eyesight experiences steadily decreasing visual salience of the one value the whole mechanism exists to protect, at the last and most consequential of the three viewings, even though 28 px remains the largest text on this particular screen.
- Timeline "not yet reached" steps are gray at the stated minimum contrast (4.5:1); this is workable but is the least-contrasty text on the screen sitting next to a 28 px, 7:1-contrast room number a few lines above — no defect found here, just noted as a comparatively weaker area for this persona.

## Persona 4 — Guest who typed the wrong room number

This is one of the two decisive personas for this screen; walked in depth, following on from the G-05 walkthrough.

Steps: the guest reaches G-06 (via "Track order" or the G-01 active-order banner) with the wrong room number ("350" instead of "305") still unnoticed, order in status New.

- The room-number line (C06) is on its own line, 28 px bold, directly under the status headline and above the timeline — never in a collapsed section, matching the binding requirement from `/spec/reviews/G-04.md` R3. Reading it here is the third and last chance while cancellation is still possible. No defect found in the placement itself.
- **Finding G-06-03 (Medium) — the "visible together" arithmetic has a gap.** §5.9 proves, "by arithmetic rather than by hope," that C06 and B02 ("Cancel order") are both visible without scrolling at 390 × 664 px while status is New, using a sub-total of 224 px (header + top padding + C03 + gap + C04 + gap + C06). This sum **omits C05**, the stale-status notice, even though the very same file's layout order (§4) places C05 between C04 and C06, and the polling rule (§5.5) allows C05 to appear while the status is still New — for example, exactly the persona-6 case where a guest's very first status poll fails on a weak connection. The stated proof is therefore not actually complete on its own terms. In practice the documented 292 px of slack between C06 and the fixed action bar likely still absorbs C05's height, so this is a documentation/consistency gap more than a proven functional break — but it is precisely the kind of thing an implementer auditing "the arithmetic" literally would trip over, on the one screen where the arithmetic is offered as the guarantee itself.
- Walking the repair once the wrong number is noticed: tap "Cancel order" (B02) → M-02 → confirm → G-06 re-renders with status Cancelled, C06 switches to "كان هذا الطلب للغرفة {room} / This order was for room {room}", and "Reorder" (B03) appears. Tapping Reorder hands the previous lines to G-03 (product identifiers and quantities only — no room number, confirmed against §5.7), and the subsequent G-04 checkout starts with an empty, unprefilled room-number field (confirmed against G-04 §5.3 "no pre-fill"). This chain was walked end-to-end and works: the guest can place a corrected order.
- **Finding G-06-01 (Critical) — no repair, and no guidance toward one, once status has passed New.** If staff accept the order before the guest notices the wrong number, "Cancel order" disappears (by design, locked decision 6) and is not replaced by anything. The ordinary Accepted & preparing / On the way view shows only the order details and "Back to store" — no reception phone mention, no "call the front desk" line, nothing. The file is explicit that this is deliberate: §5.10 states the product's only three places that ever mention calling reception are the "already accepted" race banner (C02), the "order not found" error state (C15), and G-07's empty state — and states plainly there is "no contact staff, chat, call button" anywhere else. A guest who realizes, mid-Accepted or mid-On-the-way, that the order is going to the wrong room has **no on-screen instruction at all** for what to do next, at exactly the moment an operational error (delivery to a stranger's door) is about to occur. This is the same gap that recurs under persona 7 below, generalized beyond room-number errors, and it is the single largest finding produced by testing these two files.

## Persona 5 — Guest who added a product that went out of stock before submitting

Steps: as with G-05, resolved upstream by M-04 before any order record with New status is ever created.

- G-06 reads and displays only the stored line, quantity, and unit price from the record (§7.1: "never re-priced against the current catalog"); a product that later drops out of the live catalog entirely still renders correctly here from the stored snapshot. This is a deliberate, working design and was checked against §5.0/§7.1: no gap found for the pre-submission out-of-stock scenario, which simply cannot reach this screen.
- A related but distinct case exists in principle — an item becoming unavailable to fulfil *after* acceptance — and the file's only mechanism for that is a hotel-initiated Cancelled status with an optional one-line staff reason (map §4 decision 15, rendered via C08). This was checked and functions as documented (see the Cancelled walk-through below); it is not a gap in this file, since the map explicitly assigns this outcome to the existing Cancelled status rather than to a new one.
- **No finding specific to this persona beyond what is already covered above and below.**

## Persona 6 — Guest with a weak connection that drops during submission

Since submission itself has already completed by the time G-06 exists (an order record with status New is only written after a success response, M-01 §7.4), this persona's original scenario does not recur on this screen. However, a weak/dropping connection is directly relevant to G-06's own live-polling behavior, so it was walked through in that form instead, per instruction to report what the screen itself does.

- **Finding G-06-04 (Medium) — staleness is invisible until a poll definitively fails.** The status shown is refreshed at most every 20 seconds (§5.5 rule 2), with a 10-second per-attempt timeout (rule 3). Between two consecutive successful polls, and during the up-to-10-second window while a request is in flight, the screen looks pixel-identical whether the shown status is "just confirmed" or "up to ~30 seconds old" — there is deliberately no "last updated" indicator, no subtle refresh cue, and no loading state for the poll itself (§5.5 rule 9, §6.1). The only signal that anything might be stale is C05, which appears only once a request has definitively failed or timed out. A guest with a merely slow (not fully dropped) connection, or simply checking during the routine 20-second gap, is given full visual confidence that the status is live when it may not be — this is the exact "stale status believed to be live" risk named in the task.
- **Finding G-06-05 (Medium) — the failed-poll message doesn't convey how stale "last known" really is.** C05's copy ("Could not update the status — this is the last known status") is fixed and never escalates, no matter how long the failure has persisted, and never states when the shown status was last confirmed. A guest whose connection has been down for ten minutes sees the identical single line as a guest whose very first poll failed a second ago. Since polling and screen content otherwise proceed silently, a guest reading only this message has no way to judge whether one status change might have been missed, or several.
- Recovery: the file states polling "keeps trying every 20 seconds, so the screen heals by itself when the connection returns" (§3.3), and "Cancel order" is explicitly never hidden or disabled while a poll is failing or the device is offline (§5.6 rule 6). Both were checked and hold up: the guest is never blocked from cancelling by a connectivity problem, which is a genuine strength of this file.

## Persona 7 — Guest who wants to change the order after submitting it

This is the second decisive persona for this screen, and it converges on the same core problem already surfaced under persona 4.

Steps: the guest opens G-06 wanting to add an item, correct a quantity, switch payment method from Card to Cash (or the reverse), or fix a note — not a room-number problem.

While status is New, the walk is identical to the persona-4 repair chain above and works: Cancel → M-02 → Cancelled → Reorder → G-03 (freely editable) → G-04 (clean, unprefilled fields for room number, notes, and payment). This was re-confirmed for this persona and holds up as a full, working remedy, provided the mistake is caught before acceptance.

- **Finding G-06-06 (Medium) — the general-purpose nature of "Cancel order" is not communicated anywhere.** Nothing on the New-status view suggests to the guest that cancellation is a valid remedy for *any* dissatisfaction with the order, not only a wrong room number; the button's label and helper text are generic ("Cancel order"), which is fine, but no companion text anywhere on G-06 tells the guest that "cancel, then reorder" is the way to change items, quantities, payment method, or notes. A guest may not think to use "Cancel order" for a non-room-number complaint at all, especially since the room-number safety messaging on the two preceding screens (M-01, G-05) has already framed the cancel-then-reorder pattern specifically around the room number.
- **Finding G-06-01 (Critical, restated for this persona) — no repair once status passes New.** Exactly as under persona 4: once the order is Accepted & preparing or On the way, there is no cancel control and no substitute messaging of any kind about how to change anything. For this persona specifically, "I want to change my order" has version 1's only documented answer being "cancel while New, then reorder" (per the map's own journey-coverage table and this file's §5.11) — but that answer is never stated on the screen itself once the window to use it has closed, leaving a guest who wants to add a forgotten item, or change a detail, with zero on-screen next step for the remainder of the order's life.
- Reorder after Delivered or Cancelled was also walked for a *satisfied* variant of this persona ("I want the same order again") and works cleanly: B03 hands (product identifier, quantity) pairs to G-03, the device cart is untouched by G-06 itself, and a line for a since-discontinued product is still handed over for G-03 to skip and report. No issue found with this half of the Reorder contract.

## Additional consistency and factual checks (not persona-specific)

- **Status labels.** All five canonical labels (New / Accepted & preparing / On the way / Delivered / Cancelled, in both languages) are reproduced verbatim from G-01 §5.2 in the elements table and throughout the timeline and headline copy. No mismatch found.
- **Currency format, digits, tap targets.** Currency placement (after the amount in Arabic, before it in English), two-decimal formatting, Western digits, and the 44 × 44 px minimum tap-target convention are all applied consistently with G-01 and G-04/G-05. No violation found.
- **Room-number copy.** The room-number sentence and its tense variants are consistent with the phrase established on M-01/G-05 for the three active statuses, and change tense appropriately for Delivered and Cancelled. No inconsistency found in the wording itself.
- **"Order not found" is a state, not a redirect.** Confirmed as specified (§6.3): the guest is shown a three-line explanation and a working "Back to store" button rather than being silently bounced. This is not a dead end.
- **Finding G-06-07 (Minor/consistency note).** G-05 handles a missing device record by silently redirecting to G-01 with no message (G-05 §3.3), while G-06 handles the equivalent "nothing to show for this order number" situation with a full explanatory error state (§6.3). Both are individually justified in their own files (G-05's job is already done; G-06's job is to answer a question the guest is actively asking), but it is worth flagging to the reviewer as two different responses to the same underlying condition, occurring on two adjacent screens in the same flow.
- **No timestamps beyond order time.** Confirmed as designed (§5.3, §9 item 5): no per-status timestamps, and a Delivered order shows no delivery time anywhere.
  - **Finding G-06-08 (Medium).** A guest who wants to confirm how long delivery actually took (for their own recollection, or to raise a question with reception later) has nothing on this screen beyond the single order-creation time (C10); there is no delivered-at time even on a finished, Delivered order.
- **Cancelled timeline rendering.** Walked the three example cases in §5.3 (cancelled while New; cancelled after acceptance; cancelled while On the way) against the acceptance criteria (#10). All three render the documented number of rows (2, 3, 4 respectively), with later steps not drawn at all, and no gray "skipped" placeholders. This works as specified.
  - **Finding G-06-09 (Minor).** For an order cancelled by the guest almost immediately after submission, the "New" row still shows the same filled "reached" checkmark used for a normally progressing order. A guest scanning quickly could read the checkmark as "this step went fine" rather than "this is simply where the order stopped seconds after starting." A minor readability nuance, not a functional problem.
- **Silent hotel-initiated cancellation.** Walked a guest whose order was cancelled by the hotel after acceptance (New → Accepted & preparing → Cancelled). The in-screen rendering is internally consistent: timeline shows two reached rows plus a Cancelled row, C08 correctly attributes the cancellation to the hotel (with the optional reason when supplied), C06 switches tense, and the action bar swaps to Reorder + Back to store. However:
  - **Finding G-06-10 (Medium).** There is no notification of any kind if the guest is not actively looking at G-06 when this happens (no push mechanism exists in this login-free, no-app product, so this is a structural limitation rather than a pure spec defect). This is compounded by G-01's active-order banner, which is hidden identically whether the underlying order ended in Delivered or in Cancelled (G-01 §4, C03 row: hidden whenever the status is not New / Accepted & preparing / On the way) — so a guest glancing at the store afterward cannot tell, from the banner's absence alone, whether their order was delivered or cancelled without opening G-06 or G-07 to check.
- **The cancellation race ("already accepted").** Walked the case where the guest taps "Cancel order" a moment after staff have already accepted it. G-06 correctly shows the real (post-acceptance) status and the C02 banner, with no second modal, exactly as documented.
  - **Finding G-06-11 (Medium).** C02's second line — "للسؤال عنه، اتصل بالاستقبال من هاتف الغرفة / To ask about it, call reception from your room phone" — is ambiguous about whether the phone call can still change the outcome (stop or amend the order) or is only for asking a question after the fact. A guest in this exact, time-pressured moment (they just tried and failed to cancel) may not know whether calling is worth the effort, since the line only promises they can "ask about it."
- **Reorder handoff.** Confirmed the contract in §5.7 is followed to the letter as far as this file's own description goes: only (product identifier, quantity) pairs are handed to G-03, no cart read/write happens on G-06, no availability check, no max-10 cap, and no toast originates here. No finding.

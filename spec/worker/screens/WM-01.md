# WM-01 — Confirm delivery and payment (تأكيد التوصيل والدفع)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/worker/worker-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 3, 5, 7; `/spec/worker/worker-map.md` §4 decisions 1, 2, 9, 10, 14; `/spec/staff/screens/SM-02.md` — **the sheet this one is ported from; every value, size, sentence and rule not listed under "What differs" below is SM-02's, unchanged**; `/spec/staff/screens/SM-01.md` preamble (the staff modal conventions); `/spec/worker/screens/W-02.md` §5.8 (the four outcomes W-02 expects); `/spec/screens/G-01.md` §5.2, §7.2.

Conventions reused, not re-decided: the staff modal conventions of the SM-01 preamble (bottom sheet over a 50 % black backdrop covering W-02 including its action bar, 85 % maximum height, 14 px text floor, pinned footer with "رجوع / Back" 56 px above the dark two-line confirm 64 px, 16 px apart, 300 ms arming delay, not dismissable while its request is in flight); Western digits; currency placement and two decimals; change = amount − total, two decimals, half up; "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; the action-failure sentence of S-02-C15; no toast.

**What differs from SM-02, and why.**
1. It opens on **W-02** on the worker's phone, and the request carries the **worker's** identity. SM-02 stays on the desk for deliveries the desk records itself (Amendment A2 to SM-02).
2. It has **four outcomes, not three**: the order may have been **reassigned** to a colleague while the worker stood at the door.
3. It carries **one more line, C08**, the refusal hint — the door is exactly where a guest refuses or cannot pay, and the worker cannot cancel (worker-map §4 decision 2).
4. The shift ending while the sheet is open hands over to W-03 instead of a session-expired bar.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** WM-01
- **Name:** تأكيد التوصيل والدفع / Confirm delivery and payment
- **Type:** Modal (bottom sheet) on top of **W-02**. No URL, no history entry. It is the only place in the worker app where an order becomes Delivered.
- **Language:** the worker app's saved language. No toggle.

---

## 2. Purpose

At the door, state the room, the payment method, the amount to collect and the change to give, and record the order as Delivered on one confirmed tap.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 How the modal opens

| From | Trigger | What WM-01 receives | What it shows on opening |
|---|---|---|---|
| W-02 | Tap W-02-B02 "تم التوصيل والدفع / Delivered and paid" while the status is **On the way**. That tap sends nothing | From W-02, read-only, as W-02 shows them: order number, room number, payment method, order total, the amount the guest stated if any | The ready state: C01–C06 and C08, footer with B01 and B02; C07 hidden |

WM-01 sends nothing on opening. While it is open, W-02's refresh is paused.

### 3.2 Where each action leads

| Action | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap B02 "سلّمتُ الطلب واستلمتُ المبلغ / Handed over and payment taken" | In-flight state; one delivered request. Then by outcome (§5.5): **delivered** → closes, W-02 shows Delivered; **cancelled** → closes, W-02 shows Cancelled with its banner; **reassigned** → closes, W-02 shows its reassigned state; **failed** → stays open with C07 |
| B01 "رجوع / Back", backdrop, browser back, Escape, or a downward swipe on the sheet's top 24 px | Closes; nothing sent; the order stays **في الطريق / On the way**; W-02's refresh resumes with one immediate request. All ignored while a request is in flight |
| The shift is found ended while WM-01 is open and nothing is in flight | Closes as on "رجوع / Back", and W-02 hands over to W-03 with the return target W-02. With a request in flight, its outcome is applied first |
| Browser reload | Reloads to W-02 for the same order with no modal |

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What WM-01 reads, writes, and never touches

Reads the five values W-02 hands it and the worker identity from the worker session. Writes nothing to the phone. Sends one request per confirmed tap: this order is Delivered. **Never** records the method actually used or the amount handed over (staff-map §4 decision 16); lets anything be changed; shows items, notes, the order number or the guest's mobile; cancels.

### 5.1 Sheet layout

SM-02 §5.1, with C08 added between C05 and C06. The room (40 px) is the largest text and the amount (28 px) the second, each alone on its line, the room above. At a 390 × 664 px viewport the scrolling content is about 470 px against an 85 % ceiling of 564 px less the footer (about 172 px), so the scrolling area scrolls slightly; the title, the room and the amount are visible on open, and the footer is always visible.

### 5.2 The room block (C02)

SM-02 §5.2, unchanged: the last moment a wrong door can be caught.

### 5.3 The money lines (C03, C04, C05)

SM-02 §5.3's table, word for word:

| Order | C03 | C04 | C05 (Arabic) | C05 (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Card** | الطريقة: **بطاقة** / Method: **Card** | {total} | خذ المبلغ على جهاز الدفع | Take the payment on the card terminal |
| **Cash**, stated amount **greater** than the total | الطريقة: **نقدًا** / Method: **Cash** | {total} | سيدفع بـ **{amount} ر.س** — أعطِه باقيًا **{change} ر.س** | Paying with **SAR {amount}** — give back **SAR {change}** change |
| **Cash**, stated amount **equal** to the total | الطريقة: **نقدًا** / Method: **Cash** | {total} | سيدفع بالمبلغ المطابق — لا يوجد باقٍ | Paying the exact amount — no change |
| **Cash**, no amount, or an amount **not greater** than the total | الطريقة: **نقدًا** / Method: **Cash** | {total} | لم يحدّد الضيف المبلغ مسبقًا — أعطِه الباقي مما يدفعه | The guest did not state an amount in advance — give change from what they pay |

{change} is the same figure W-02-C13 printed before the walk. A card order with an amount shows the Card row.

### 5.4 The buttons and the two lines above the footer

- **B02** and **C06**: SM-02 §5.4, unchanged. The confirm is first person and names both halves of the act; it prints "الحالة بعد الضغط: تم التوصيل / Status after tapping: Delivered".
- **C08** tells the worker, at the door, what to do when the handover cannot complete: call the desk, which cancels (worker-map §4 decision 2). B01 "رجوع / Back" closes the sheet with the order still On the way, so the worker can phone.
- Both buttons ignore taps for the first 300 ms after the sheet starts to appear, because the sheet rises under the thumb that just tapped W-02-B02.

### 5.5 The request, and the four outcomes W-02 expects

The request carries the order number, the target status **Delivered**, and the **worker's** identity; no amount, no method, nothing about the guest. It is sent only after the device has confirmed, from the latest data, that the order is still assigned to this worker. **Timeout 15 seconds.** Idempotent by target status.

| What the device observed | Outcome handed to W-02 | What WM-01 does |
|---|---|---|
| The order is now **Delivered** (by this request, by an earlier attempt whose response was lost, or by the desk on the worker's behalf) | **delivered** | Closes within 100 ms, no message. W-02 renders Delivered with W-02-C16; C03 as well when the desk recorded it |
| The order is **Cancelled** | **cancelled** | Closes. W-02 renders the Cancelled state with W-02-C02 and C15 |
| The order is **assigned to someone other than this worker** (checked before sending, or refused by the system) | **reassigned** | Closes. W-02 renders its reassigned state (W-02 §6.5) |
| Anything else: not sent, dropped, no answer in 15 seconds, an error of any kind, unreadable, or any other status | **failed** | **Stays open** with C07; W-02 unchanged beneath it; still On the way |
| A response after the timeout | Nothing | Discarded |

### 5.6 The failure case

SM-02 §5.6, unchanged: the sheet stays open, C07 appears, B02 is the retry, retrying is safe, and there is no counter, cap, automatic retry or offline queue.

### 5.7 What is deliberately not on this modal

SM-02 §5.7, unchanged — no items, no order number, no amount-received field, no method switch, no notes, no close "×", no toast — plus: **no cancel control** (C08 names the desk instead) and **no guest mobile** (it is on W-02, one tap behind "رجوع / Back").

## 6. States

### 6.0 Ready

Backdrop; sheet with C01–C06 and C08; footer with B01 and B02; C07 hidden. Focus on C01; announced as a dialog; 300 ms arming delay.

### 6.1 Loading — the delivery in flight

SM-02 §6.1, unchanged: entered synchronously on the tap; B02 disabled and "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; B01 at 50 % and inert; backdrop, browser back, Escape and swipe ignored; at most 15 seconds.

### 6.2 Empty

Not reachable: W-02 holds every value WM-01 shows.

### 6.3 Error

The *failed* outcome, C07 in the footer. Nothing is typed, so there is no field error message.

### 6.4 Success

The *delivered* outcome: WM-01 and the backdrop are removed within 100 ms, no checkmark, no toast; W-02 reports it. *Cancelled* and *reassigned* close the same way and are reported by W-02.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on WM-01.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

SM-02 §7.1, unchanged, with "W-02" in place of "S-02": room number byte for byte at 40 px; method "card" → بطاقة / Card, "cash" → نقدًا / Cash; total as stored, 28 px bold, never recomputed; change = amount − total, two decimals, half up, ignored for a card order.

### 7.2 Request bounds

One delivered request per tap of B02, after the assignment check; timeout **15 seconds**; idempotent; never queued, retried automatically or sent optimistically. It carries the order number, the target status and the worker's identity.

### 7.3 Tap handling, language, sizes and accessibility

- SM-02 §7.3–§7.5, unchanged, with C08 at 14 px. **Nothing smaller than 14 px.** B01 sheet width × 56 px, B02 × 64 px. Room contrast at least 7:1, everything else at least 4.5:1.
- Screen reader order: the dialog, C01, C02 with the room digits one at a time, C03, C04, C05, C08, C06, then the buttons; C07 announced when it appears; W-02 behind the backdrop not reachable by focus.
- Motion: the 200 ms slide-up only.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. WM-01 opens only from W-02-B02 in status On the way, and neither that tap nor the opening sends a request.
2. On open, the sheet shows "تأكيد التوصيل والدفع / Confirm delivery and payment", the room at 40 px bold alone on its line, the method line, "المبلغ المطلوب / Amount to collect" with the stored total at 28 px bold, one instruction line, C08, C06, and the footer with "رجوع / Back" above the confirm; C07 hidden.
3. The four payment cases read exactly as SM-02 §5.3, and the change equals the figure W-02-C13 shows.
4. C08 reads "الضيف رفض الطلب أو لا يوجد دفع؟ اتصل بمكتب الروم سيرفس ليلغيه / Guest refused or can't pay? Call the room service desk to cancel" and is not a link; no cancel control exists on WM-01.
5. B02 reads "سلّمتُ الطلب واستلمتُ المبلغ / Handed over and payment taken" and "الحالة بعد الضغط: تم التوصيل / Status after tapping: Delivered"; both buttons ignore taps for the first 300 ms.
6. Tapping B02 sends exactly one request carrying the order number, the target status Delivered and the worker's identity, and no amount or method; B02 is disabled and reads "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…" within 100 ms.
7. While the request is in flight, B01 is at 50 % and inert, and the backdrop, browser back, Escape and swipe do nothing; the in-flight state ends within 15 seconds.
8. When the order is now Delivered, WM-01 closes within 100 ms with no message and W-02 shows "تم التوصيل / Delivered" and "تم تسجيل التوصيل والدفع / Delivery and payment recorded".
9. When the desk cancelled the order meanwhile, WM-01 closes and W-02 shows the Cancelled state with its banner.
10. When the desk reassigned the order to a colleague meanwhile, no delivered request is recorded under this worker, WM-01 closes, and W-02 shows its reassigned state.
11. When the request fails for any other reason, WM-01 stays open with C07 "لم يصل التحديث إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The update did not reach the system — try again", W-02 still shows On the way, and a retry sends the identical request.
12. "رجوع / Back", the backdrop, browser back, Escape or a downward swipe with nothing in flight close WM-01 with nothing sent, and the order stays On the way.
13. When the shift is found ended while WM-01 is open and nothing is in flight, WM-01 closes with nothing sent and W-03 opens with the return target W-02.
14. No item, order number, note, guest mobile or field appears on WM-01.
15. Every number uses Western digits; currency follows the amount in Arabic and precedes it in English, with two decimals.
16. No text is smaller than 14 px; the room's contrast is at least 7:1; a screen reader reads the room digit by digit before the money lines.
17. No toast appears at any point.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **WM-01 is SM-02 with the worker's identity**: same four values, same sentences, same sizes, same confirm label (preamble).
2. **A fourth outcome, reassigned**, because the desk can move an order while the worker stands at a door; the assignment is checked before sending so a delivery is never recorded under the wrong worker (§5.5).
3. **The refusal hint is on the sheet itself** (C08), because the door is where refusals happen and the worker has no cancel control.
4. **The shift ending while the sheet is open closes it and hands over to W-03**, with the return target W-02 (§3.2).

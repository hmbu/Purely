---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** W-01
- **Name:** طلباتي / My orders
- **Type:** Full worker screen with its own URL (example: `…/worker/`). It is the landing screen of the worker app: a phone with a live worker session that opens the worker URL lands here, not on W-03.
- **Language:** Arabic (RTL) on first open regardless of browser language; the header toggle switches to English (LTR) in place; the choice is saved on the device as the worker app's language, separately from the desk's (worker-map §4 decision 13).
- **Viewport:** specified at 390 px wide. At 768 px and above the list is a single centered column 480 px wide; nothing else changes.

---

## 2. Purpose

Show the worker every order assigned to them, with the room number as the largest thing on each card, and make a new assignment impossible to miss.

| Action on W-01 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap an order card (C07), including a cancelled card outside its "Got it" button | **W-02** for that order |
| Tap "فهمت / Got it" (B04) on a cancelled card | Stays on W-01; that card is removed and the acknowledgement is saved on the device (§5.4) |
| Tap the language toggle (B01) | Stays on W-01; re-rendered in the other language and direction, same scroll position |
| Tap the sound toggle (B02) | Stays on W-01; sound on or off, stated in the button's label |
| Tap "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" (B03) with **no** active order | **W-03**; the shift has ended and the phone is signed out |
| Tap "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" (B03) with **one or more** active orders | Stays on W-01; nothing ends; C03 is shown (§5.7) |
| An update finds that this worker is no longer on shift (12-hour limit reached, or the shift ended on another phone) | **W-03**, with its shift-ended line and the return target W-01 at the current scroll position (§5.8) |
| Tap "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" (B05) | Stays on W-01; an update request is sent and the loading state is shown |

W-01 has no back arrow; a browser back gesture on W-01 leaves the worker app.

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What W-01 reads, writes, and never touches

W-01 renders from the most recent successful update plus four device-held values: the worker app's language, its sound setting, the live worker session (member identity, name, sign-in time), and the set of cancelled orders already acknowledged in this shift.

W-01 **writes** to the system only "end my shift" (B03 with no active order). It writes to the device only the language, the sound setting and the acknowledgements. It sends no status change: those are sent from W-02 and WM-01 only.

W-01 **never** shows: an order not assigned to this worker; a New order; a total, an amount or change (worker-map §4 decision 10); the guest's mobile number (it is on W-02 only); any colleague's or supervisor's name; a guest identifier of any kind.

### 5.1 Header (S01)

Visual order in Arabic (RTL): [C01 shift line] … [B02 sound] [B01 language] [B03 End shift]. In English the row mirrors; nothing else changes. B02 always writes the sound state in words; there is no icon-only speaker (staff-map §4 decision 14).

### 5.2 The order card (C07)

One tappable block, contents top to bottom:

1. **Room number** — at the start edge, **40 px bold**, letter-spacing 0.06 em, preceded on the same line by "غرفة" / "Room" at 14 px regular; Western digits, leading zeros kept ("0305" stays "0305"), laid out left-to-right in both languages, never wrapped, never shrunk, never truncated, contrast at least 7:1. No other text on the card is larger than 16 px (worker-map §4 decision 9).
2. **Elapsed time** — end edge of the same line, 14 px, time since the **order was placed** (how long the guest has been waiting), in the wording of S-01 §7.2, recomputed every 30 seconds with no request.
3. **Order number** — second line, 14 px: "طلب رقم {order number}" / "Order {order number}", exactly as the guest sees it.
4. **Item count** — third line, 16 px, in the wording of G-01 §7.3 ("منتج واحد / 1 item", "منتجان / 2 items", "{N} منتجات / {N} items", "{N} منتجًا / {N} items"). **No total** is printed.
5. **Chip row** — fourth line, 8 px above it: C08 payment method, then C09 "ملاحظات / Notes" when notes exist, 8 px apart.
6. On a cancelled card, **C10** replaces the chip row and **B04** sits under it at the end edge.

Card height is whatever these lines need (about 132 px at 390 px width for an active card) and never less than 44 px.

### 5.3 Which orders are listed, grouping and order

A card is listed when, and only when, one of these holds:

- **Active:** `assignedTo` is this worker and the status is Accepted & preparing or On the way.
- **Cancelled, not yet acknowledged:** the order was assigned to this worker when the hotel cancelled it, the cancellation happened after this shift started, and this device has no acknowledgement for it (§5.4).

Groups, in this fixed order, top to bottom:

1. ملغى / **Cancelled** — at the top, because it is the one group that asks the worker to stop doing something.
2. تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير / **Accepted & preparing**
3. في الطريق / **On the way**

Rules:

- The order of the groups never changes and never depends on their counts. A group with zero cards renders nothing at all.
- Within Accepted & preparing and On the way, cards are sorted by the time the order was **assigned to this worker**, oldest first, so a new assignment is appended at the bottom of its group and pushes no existing card. Within Cancelled, by cancellation time, oldest first.
- An order that changes status between updates moves to its new group at that update.
- A card **never changes position or disappears while a touch is in progress**: an update that arrives during a touch is applied when the touch ends (S-01 §7.5).
- An order reassigned away is removed at the update that discovers it, with no marker on the list and a C05 notice at the top (worker-map §4 decision 7).
- An order that becomes **Delivered** leaves the list at the next update with no marker. The worker who confirmed it saw W-02's Delivered state; when the desk confirmed it on their behalf, the worker is not asked to acknowledge anything, because nothing is left to do.

### 5.4 Orders the desk cancelled (C10, B04)

- Discovered when an update shows that an order of this worker is now Cancelled by the hotel (the guest cannot cancel an accepted order — locked decision 6). The card moves to the Cancelled group with C10 and B04.
- **Line 2 of C10** prints the stored cancellation reason in the worker app's language (`cancelReasonAr` in Arabic, `cancelReasonEn` in English), falling back to the other language's stored reason when that one is empty, exactly as stored, never translated — the same rule as AM-03 §5.6. Line 2 is hidden when both are empty.
- **B04 "فهمت / Got it"** removes the card and saves "acknowledged" for that order number on the device. Acknowledgements are kept until the shift ends (B03 or the 12-hour limit), then discarded.
- A cancelled card is still tappable outside B04 and opens W-02 in its Cancelled state, where the items can be read in order to return them.
- The discovery plays the chime (§5.6 rule 2).

### 5.5 Updating the list — the numbers (worker-map §4 decision 6)

1. **On open:** one update request, sent as soon as the screen is drawn.
2. **On every change notification** from the system while the page is visible: one update request, unless one is already in flight, in which case one more is sent when it ends.
3. **Fallback poll every 15 seconds**, measured from the end of the previous attempt, so two requests are never in flight at once.
4. **Timeout: 10 seconds.** A late response is discarded.
5. **Paused while the page is hidden** (phone locked, another app, another tab); **one immediate request** on return, which is a new chime baseline (§5.6 rule 3).
6. **On success:** the list is rebuilt, C04 is hidden, the shift check of §5.8 and the chime rules of §5.6 run, C05 notices are created for orders that left by reassignment. The rebuild keeps the scroll position and respects the touch rule of §5.3.
7. **On failure:** the last successful list stays on screen and C04 shows the time since the last success; after **2 minutes** it becomes a dark-filled bar. Retries continue; there is no retry button while a list is on screen.
8. **No pull-to-refresh and no manual refresh button** while a list exists; B05 exists only in the cold-load failure state.

### 5.6 The chime

1. The chime is the desk's single **2-second** tone (S-01 §5.6 rule 1), at the device's media volume. It is the only sound in the worker app.
2. **It plays** once on any successful update that contains, compared with the previous successful update of this page, at least one of: an order newly assigned to this worker (by an accept or by a reassignment from a colleague); an order of this worker newly cancelled by the hotel. Several in one update play **one** chime.
3. **Baseline:** the first successful update after a sign-in, a reload, or a return from a hidden page plays **no** chime.
4. **No repeat.** There is no repeating chime on the worker phone (worker-map §4 decision 6).
5. **Muting** is by B02 only, saved on the device, stated in words in the header and in C11 line 4.
6. **When the browser refuses to play audio** (no touch since the page loaded, usually after a reload), nothing is attempted again until the worker taps B02, which reads "الصوت: متوقف (انقر للتفعيل) / Sound: off (tap to enable)". The PIN typed on W-03 is what arms audio in the normal flow, because W-03 → W-01 is an in-page navigation (W-03 §5.4).
7. No reassignment away, no status change made by the desk on the worker's behalf, and no delivery plays a chime.

### 5.7 Ending the shift (B03, C03)

- **No active order:** the tap sends the end-shift request; when the system confirms it (§7.2), the shift has ended (the desk's picker shows the worker as "خارج الوردية / Off shift" from its next update), deletes the worker session and the acknowledgements on this phone, releases the screen wake lock, and opens W-03 with no return target. No confirmation modal: the cost of a mis-tap is four digits.
- **One or more active orders:** nothing ends. C03 appears under C02: "لا يمكن إنهاء الوردية وما زالت معك طلبات — سلّمها أو اطلب من المكتب تحويلها / You cannot end your shift while you still have orders — deliver them or ask the desk to reassign them". It disappears when the active count reaches 0 or the worker leaves W-01. B03 is not disabled: the tap is what explains why it did not work (the SM-01 B06 rule).

### 5.8 The shift found ended

On every successful update, W-01 checks that this worker is still on shift: a shift entry exists for them in the system and it started less than **12 hours** ago on the device clock. If not — the 12-hour limit passed, or the shift was ended on another phone (worker-map §4 decisions 4, 5) — W-01 ends the shift in the system if it is still recorded, deletes the session on this phone, and opens **W-03** with its shift-ended line (W-03-C02) and the return target W-01 at the current scroll position. The same check runs every 60 seconds without a request, so the 12-hour limit is applied even while updates are failing.

### 5.9 Screen wake lock

While W-01 is visible with a live worker session, it requests the browser's screen wake lock, and requests it again when the page returns from hidden. Nothing is shown when the browser does not support it or refuses. It is released on W-03.

### 5.10 What is deliberately not on this screen

- **No action buttons on an active card** — no "on my way", no "delivered": every act happens on W-02 after the room and items are read (staff-map §4 decision 6, applied here).
- **No cancel, no accept, no reassign, no "reject order"** (worker-map §4 decisions 1, 2).
- **No money** — no total, no amount, no change (worker-map §4 decision 10).
- **No guest mobile number** (it is on W-02 only).
- **No history tab, no search, no filter, no sort control.**
- **No colleague's name, no supervisor's name.**
- **No toast, and no modal opens from W-01.**

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

Triggered by the first update after a sign-in, a reload, or B05. On screen: the header complete and working; C02 with {N} as "—"; C12's two placeholder cards; C04 hidden. It ends at the first response and never lasts longer than the 10-second timeout; a failure with no earlier success goes to §6.3.

### 6.2 Empty

No active card and no unacknowledged cancelled card. C02 reads "طلباتي (0) / My orders (0)" and C11 is shown, centered:
- Line 1 (20 px, bold): "لا توجد طلبات لك الآن" / "No orders for you right now"
- Line 2 (16 px): "ستسمع نغمة عندما يحوّل المكتب طلبًا إليك" / "You will hear a chime when the desk sends you an order"
- Line 3 (14 px): "أبقِ هذه الصفحة مفتوحة والشاشة مضاءة" / "Keep this page open and the screen on"
- Line 4 (14 px, **only while the sound is off**): "الصوت متوقف الآن — لن تسمع النغمة" / "Sound is off — you will not hear the chime"

Updates continue in this state. C05 notices, when present, are shown above C11.

### 6.3 Error

**One rendered error state: the cold-load failure** — no update has succeeded since this page loaded and the most recent request failed, timed out or was unreadable. On screen: the header; C02 with "—"; C13's three lines and B05 "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" 24 px beneath:
- Line 1 (20 px, bold): "تعذّر تحميل طلباتك" / "Could not load your orders"
- Line 2 (16 px): "تحقّق من الاتصال بالشبكة" / "Check the network connection"
- Line 3 (14 px): "إذا تكرّر ذلك، اتصل بمكتب الروم سيرفس" / "If this keeps happening, call the room service desk"

Automatic retries continue every 15 seconds. A failed update after an earlier success is **not** this state; it is C04 over the last known list.

Nothing is typed on W-01, so there is no field error message. The end-shift refusal is C03 (§5.7), not an error state.

### 6.4 Success

An update arrived: the non-empty groups and their cards, C02 with its count, C04 hidden. Changes repaint within 100 ms with no animation, no highlight and no scroll change. A new assignment is announced by the chime, the count in C02 and the card itself.

### 6.5 Sound-off

Not a separate layout: B02 states it and C11 line 4 states it in the empty state.

### 6.6 Shift ended

Not rendered on W-01: the screen hands over to W-03 (§5.8).

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on W-01 and no field element exists. The rules below govern the values it displays and the requests it makes.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Room number (C07) | The order's room number string | Byte for byte, leading zeros kept, 1–5 Western digits, 40 px bold, never wrapped, never shrunk, never re-validated |
| Order number (C07) | The order | Exactly as stored; no "#", no padding |
| Status label (C06) | The order's status | One of the canonical labels of G-01 §5.2, verbatim in the current language. A status outside the five makes the card unrenderable, and it is skipped with no error text |
| Item count (C07) | Sum of line quantities | G-01 §7.3 wording, verbatim |
| Payment method (C08) | The order's payment choice | "card" → بطاقة / Card; "cash" → نقدًا / Cash. No amount, whatever is stored |
| Notes presence (C09) | The order's notes | Chip shown for non-empty, non-whitespace notes; the notes themselves are read on W-02 |
| Elapsed time (C07) | Order time against the device clock | S-01 §7.2 wording; recomputed every 30 seconds |
| Assignment time (sorting) | The last assignment of this order to this worker (`assignLog` row with `to` = this worker) | Used for sorting only; never printed |
| Cancellation reason (C10) | Stored reasons | §5.4; exactly as stored, never translated |
| Worker name (C01) | The live worker session's member name | Display-name rule (worker-map §4 decision 17), cut at 18 characters with "…" |
| {N} in C02 | Count of active cards | Western digits; cancelled cards not counted |

### 7.2 Request bounds

- One update on open; one per change notification (never two in flight); a fallback every **15 seconds** from the end of the previous attempt; timeout **10 seconds**; paused while hidden, one immediate request on return.
- "End shift" (B03 with no active order): one request; timeout **15 seconds**; B03 reads "جارٍ الإنهاء… / Ending…" and ignores taps while it is in flight. **On success** the phone deletes its session and opens W-03. **On failure** (not sent, dropped, no answer in 15 seconds, unreadable) nothing is deleted, the worker stays on W-01, still on shift, and C03 variant FAILED says so; the next tap on B03 sends again. The phone never signs itself out on a request that did not reach the system, because the desk would keep assigning orders to a worker it still sees on shift, to a phone nobody is watching.
- Every request carries the worker's identity and the device's hotel link; none carries anything about a guest.

### 7.3 Tap handling

- Cards, B01–B05 each respond to a single tap with the 300 ms debounce; a double-tap on a card opens W-02 once.
- A tap on B04 never also counts as a card tap.
- A tap that begins on a card and ends elsewhere does nothing.
- No long-press, no swipe, no pull-to-refresh.

### 7.4 Language

- Every fixed string renders in the worker app's saved language; B01 is one of the two places it changes (the other is W-03).
- Room numbers, order numbers and stored reasons are never translated or reformatted by a switch.

### 7.5 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: the room number **40 px bold**; C02 22 px bold; the item count, group headings (bold) and C03 16 px; elapsed time, order number, C01, C04, C05, C10 and the smaller C11/C13 lines 14 px; chips 12 px. **Nothing on W-01 is smaller than 12 px**, and no sentence is smaller than 14 px.
- Tap areas: every card (full width × at least 44 px); B01, B02, B03 44 × 44 px minimum; B04 120 × 44 px; B05 200 × 48 px.
- Contrast: the room number at least **7:1**; every other text at least 4.5:1, including chips, the dashed cancelled card and the dark refresh bar. Nothing is signalled by colour alone.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled; cards grow and the list scrolls under the sticky header; nothing is truncated except the worker's name.
- Screen reader: each card is announced as one item — "غرفة {room}، طلب رقم {n}، {item count}، {chips}" / "Room {room}, order {n}, {item count}, {chips}" — with the room digits read one at a time. A cancelled card is announced with C10's lines and then B04. A new assignment is announced once as "طلب جديد لك: غرفة {room} / New order for you: room {room}"; a C05 notice and C03 are announced when they appear.
- Motion: none except the skeleton's opacity pulse.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Opening the worker URL on a phone with a live worker session shows W-01, with the first update request sent before any card is drawn; with no live worker session it shows W-03.
2. W-01 lists exactly the orders whose `assignedTo` is the signed-in worker and whose status is Accepted & preparing or On the way, plus unacknowledged desk-cancelled orders of this shift; no New order and no order assigned to anyone else ever appears.
3. Groups appear in the fixed order Cancelled, Accepted & preparing, On the way, each heading carrying its canonical label verbatim plus its count; an empty group renders nothing.
4. Within Accepted & preparing and On the way, cards are sorted by the time the order was assigned to this worker, oldest first; a new assignment appears at the bottom of its group and moves no existing card.
5. Every active card shows, in order: "غرفة / Room" and the room number at 40 px bold, the elapsed time since the order was placed, the order number, the item count, and the chip row; no total, amount or change appears anywhere on W-01.
6. The room number keeps leading zeros, uses Western digits, is laid out left-to-right in both languages, is never wrapped or truncated, and has contrast of at least 7:1.
7. C08 reads "بطاقة / Card" or "نقدًا / Cash" with no amount; C09 "ملاحظات / Notes" appears when and only when the order has non-whitespace notes.
8. When the desk accepts an order and assigns it to this worker, its card appears on W-01 without a reload, within 15 seconds at most, and exactly one 2-second chime plays (sound on, audio armed).
9. When the desk reassigns an order from a colleague to this worker, its card appears and one chime plays.
10. Several new assignments in one update play one chime; the first successful update after sign-in, reload or return from a hidden page plays none.
11. No chime ever repeats on W-01; a reassignment away, a delivery, and a status change made by the desk play no chime.
12. When the desk reassigns one of this worker's orders to a colleague, its card disappears at the next update, C05 "حُوِّل طلب الغرفة {room} إلى زميل — لم يعد عليك توصيله / The order for room {room} was moved to a colleague — you no longer deliver it" appears at the top for 60 seconds, and no colleague's name appears.
13. When the desk cancels one of this worker's orders, its card moves to the Cancelled group with C10 "ألغى المكتب هذا الطلب — لا توصله وأعد المنتجات / The desk cancelled this order — do not deliver it, return the items" and the stored reason, one chime plays, and the card stays until "فهمت / Got it" is tapped.
14. After "فهمت / Got it", the card is removed and does not return after a reload during the same shift; an unacknowledged cancelled card does return after a reload.
15. Tapping a card (outside B04) opens W-02 for that order; two taps within 300 ms open it once.
16. Tapping "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" with no active order opens W-03 with no confirmation once the system confirms, and the desk's picker then shows this worker as off shift; if the request fails, the worker stays on W-01, still on shift, with C03 "لم يصل إنهاء الوردية إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / Ending your shift did not reach the system — try again".
17. Tapping "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" with at least one active order ends nothing and shows C03 "لا يمكن إنهاء الوردية وما زالت معك طلبات — سلّمها أو اطلب من المكتب تحويلها / You cannot end your shift while you still have orders — deliver them or ask the desk to reassign them".
18. Twelve hours after sign-in, or when the shift is ended on another phone, W-01 opens W-03 with its shift-ended line, and a correct PIN returns to W-01.
19. Updates are requested on open, on each change notification and every 15 seconds after the previous attempt ended; two are never in flight; an attempt unanswered after 10 seconds is a failure; updates pause while the page is hidden.
20. After a failed update following an earlier success, the last list stays and C04 shows "تعذّر تحديث طلباتك — آخر تحديث {duration} / Could not refresh your orders — last updated {duration} ago", turning into a dark bar after 2 minutes; it disappears on the next success with no tap.

23. B02 always states the sound setting in words; turning it on plays one chime; with audio blocked it reads "الصوت: متوقف (انقر للتفعيل) / Sound: off (tap to enable)".
24. B01 switches every string and the direction in place without a reload and saves the worker app's language without changing the desk's.
25. No card carries an action button other than B04 on a cancelled card; no swipe, long-press or pull-to-refresh does anything.
26. No guest mobile number, guest name, colleague's name or supervisor's name appears on W-01.
27. No text is smaller than 12 px and no sentence smaller than 14 px; every tappable element has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px; pinch-zoom works.
28. A screen reader announces each card as one item beginning with the room number read digit by digit, and announces a new assignment once.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **Three groups — Cancelled on top, then the two active statuses in lifecycle order** (§5.3). Cancelled leads because it asks the worker to stop.
2. **Active cards are ordered by assignment time, oldest first**, so a reassigned-in order lands at the bottom and nothing moves under a finger (§5.3).
3. **A desk cancellation stays until "Got it", saved on the device for the shift** (§5.4); a reassignment away leaves at once with a 60-second notice (§5.3, worker-map §4 decision 7); a delivery leaves silently (§5.3).
4. **The card prints the payment method but no money** (§5.2, worker-map §4 decision 10).
5. **Elapsed time counts from the order time**, because the question the worker needs answered is how long the guest has waited (§5.2).
6. **Change notification plus a 15-second fallback poll**; one chime per update with a new assignment or a new desk cancellation; no repeat (§5.5, §5.6).
7. **"End shift" is refused, with a line, while orders are held**, and never disabled (§5.7).
8. **A failed "End shift" request signs nothing out** (§7.2): the worker stays on shift, and C03 variant FAILED says the shift did not end, so the desk never assigns to a phone its owner believes is off.
9. **The shift check runs on every update and every 60 seconds** without a request (§5.8).
10. **The screen wake lock is requested silently** (§5.9); the empty state tells the worker to keep the page open.

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** W-01
- **Name:** طلباتي / My orders
- **Type:** Full worker screen with its own URL (prototype: `worker/index.html#/orders`). It is the landing screen of the worker app: a phone with a live worker session that opens the worker URL lands here; one with no live session lands on W-03.
- **Language:** Arabic (RTL) on first open regardless of browser language; the header toggle switches to English (LTR) in place; the choice is saved on the device as the worker app's language, separately from the desk's (worker-map §4 decision 13).
- **Viewport:** specified at 390 px wide; the app column never grows beyond 390 px.

---

## 2. Purpose

Show the worker every order assigned to them, with the room number as the largest thing on each card, make a new assignment impossible to miss, and say plainly when an order has left their hands.

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 Entry

| From | Trigger | What W-01 shows on arrival |

| W-03 Delivery worker sign-in | A correct worker PIN | Top of the list; the loading state until the first response, which is the chime baseline (§5.5) |

| The worker URL on a phone with no live worker session | Browser load | **W-03**, not W-01 |

### 3.2 Exit

| Action on W-01 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap an order card (C07) | **W-02** for that order |
| Tap "فهمت / Got it" (B04) on a notice | Stays; that notice is removed |

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What W-01 reads, writes, and never touches

W-01 renders from the most recent successful refresh plus three device-held values: the worker app's language, the live worker session (member, name, shift start), and the moment this worker last left W-01 (§5.4).

W-01 **writes** nothing to the system. It writes to the device only the language and the "last left W-01" moment. Ending the shift is WM-02's.

W-01 **never** shows: an order not assigned to this worker; a New order; a total, an amount or change (worker-map §4 decision 9); the guest's mobile number (it is on W-02 only); any colleague's or supervisor's name; a guest identifier of any kind.

### 5.1 Which orders are listed, and in what order

- An order is listed when its `assignedTo` is this worker and its status is تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير / Accepted & preparing or في الطريق / On the way.
- **One list**, no groups by status: each card states its own status. The list is sorted by the time the **order was placed**, oldest first, then by order number — the guest who has waited longest is at the top.
- An order that becomes Delivered by this worker leaves the list with no notice (the worker saw W-02's delivered record) and appears in "Delivered today".
- An order that leaves for any other reason gets a C05 notice (§5.3).

### 5.2 The order card (C07)

One tappable block, contents top to bottom:

1. **Room line** — "غرفة / Room" at 14 px, then the room number at **40 px bold**, letter-spacing 0.06 em, black, Western digits, leading zeros kept ("0305" stays "0305"), laid out left-to-right in both languages, never wrapped. At the end edge of the same line, C15 "جديد / New" when it applies. No other text on the card is larger than 16 px (worker-map §4 decision 8).
2. **Status** — 16 px bold, the canonical label verbatim: "تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير / Accepted & preparing" or "في الطريق / On the way".
3. **Contents and method** — 16 px: "{item count} · {method}", with the method bold: the item count in the wording of G-01 §7.3 ("منتج واحد / 1 item", "منتجان / 2 items", "{N} منتجات / {N} items", "{N} منتجًا / {N} items"); the method "بطاقة / Card" or "نقدًا / Cash". **No total, no amount, no change.**
4. **Meta line** — 14 px gray: "طلب رقم {no} / Order {no}" at the start edge and "حُوّل لك {ago} / Assigned to you {ago}" at the end edge, where {ago} is the time since the order was last assigned to this worker in the wording of S-01 §7.2 ("منذ أقل من دقيقة / less than a minute ago", "منذ دقيقة / 1 min ago", "منذ دقيقتين / 2 min ago", "منذ {n} دقائق / {n} min ago", "منذ {n} دقيقة / {n} min ago", "منذ {h} و{m} دقيقة / {h} h {m} min ago"), recomputed every 30 seconds with no request.

### 5.3 The gone-order notice (C05, B04)

Created by a refresh (or by W-02) for each order that was active on this phone and no longer is, for a reason other than this worker's own delivery. One notice per order: a newer reason replaces the older notice for the same order. Lines:

| Line | When | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (16 px bold), reassigned | The order is now assigned to someone else | تم تحويل هذا الطلب لعامل آخر | This order was reassigned to another worker |
| 1, cancelled | The desk cancelled the order | ألغى المكتب هذا الطلب | The desk cancelled this order |
| 1, delivered by the desk | The order is Delivered and its log row was written by someone other than this worker | سجّل المكتب توصيل هذا الطلب نيابةً عنك | The desk recorded this order as delivered for you |
| 1, not mine | (from W-02 only) The order was never assigned to this worker | هذا الطلب ليس محوّلًا لك | This order is not assigned to you |
| 1, not found | (from W-02 only) No such order | لم نعثر على هذا الطلب | We could not find this order |
| 2 (14 px) | Whenever the room is known | طلب رقم {no} · غرفة {room} | Order {no} · Room {room} |
| 3 (14 px) | Cancelled, when a reason is stored | السبب: {reason} | Reason: {reason} |
| 4 (14 px) | Reassigned or cancelled | لا تتوجّه به إلى الغرفة. إن كان معك فأعده إلى الرف. | Do not take it to the room. If you have it with you, return it to the shelf. |
| B04 | Always | فهمت | Got it |

- {reason} is the stored cancellation reason in the worker app's language (`cancelReasonAr` in Arabic, `cancelReasonEn` in English), falling back to the other language's when that one is empty, exactly as stored, never translated, cut at 120 characters with "…".
- The colleague's name is never shown. No chime plays for a notice; line 1 is announced to screen readers when the notice appears.
- An order that comes back to this worker (reassigned back) drops its notice.
- Notices are held in page memory only: a reload or a sign-out clears them.

### 5.4 The "New" marker (C15)

- The phone saves, per worker, the moment the worker last **left** W-01: opening an order, hiding the page (phone locked, another app), or ending the shift.
- A card carries C15 when its latest assignment to this worker is later than that moment. While the worker stays on W-01, every order that arrives carries it; when they return to W-01 (from W-02 or from a hidden page) the moment has moved, so only orders assigned since then carry it.
- A worker's very first sign-in on a phone has no saved moment, so every card carries C15.

### 5.5 Refreshing the list and the chime

1. **When:** on open; on every change notification from the system; when the page becomes visible; and as a fallback **15 seconds** after the previous attempt ended (the fallback is skipped while the page is hidden). Never two in flight: a trigger during a request queues exactly one more.
2. **Timeout:** 15 seconds. A late response is discarded.
3. **On success:** the list, C06's count and "Delivered today" are rebuilt; C04 is hidden; C05 notices are created (§5.3); the screen is repainted.
4. **On failure:** the last successful list stays on screen and C04 appears with B05; retries continue on the fallback cadence. With no success yet since this sign-in or page load, C04 shows its "could not load" sentence and no cards.
5. **The chime** is the desk's two soft notes (880 Hz then 660 Hz, about 2 seconds in all), at the device's media volume. It plays **once** on a successful refresh that contains at least one active order that was not in the previous successful refresh (a new assignment or a reassignment to this worker). Several in one refresh play one chime. The first successful refresh after a page load or a sign-in is the **baseline** and plays nothing. There is no repeat, and nothing else chimes.
6. **Audio permission.** A browser plays sound only after a touch or key press on the page. Typing the PIN on W-03 is that touch in the normal flow (W-03 → W-01 is an in-page navigation). After a reload, C14 is shown until the worker touches the screen; a chime that the browser refuses shows C14 again.
7. Each new order is announced to screen readers as "طلب جديد لك: غرفة {room} / New order for you: room {room}", with the room digits read one at a time.

### 5.6 The shift found ended

Every second, and on every change to the staff record, the app checks that this worker is still on shift (worker-map §4 decision 4). If not — the shift was ended on another tab or phone — the session on this phone is cleared and W-03 opens, with no message. **Not in the prototype — build as specified:** the same check treats a shift that started 12 or more hours ago as ended.

### 5.7 Screen wake lock

**Not in the prototype — build as specified.** While W-01 is visible with a live worker session, it requests the browser's screen wake lock, and requests it again when the page returns from hidden. Nothing is shown when the browser does not support it or refuses. It is released on W-03.

### 5.8 What is deliberately not on this screen

- **No action buttons on a card** — no "on my way", no "delivered": every act happens on W-02 after the room and items are read (staff-map §4 decision 6, applied here).
- **No cancel, no accept, no reassign, no "reject order"** (worker-map §4 decisions 1, 2).
- **No money** — no total, no amount, no change, on cards or in "Delivered today" (worker-map §4 decision 9).
- **No guest mobile number** (it is on W-02 only).
- **No sound toggle** (worker-map §4 decision 6).
- **No search, no filter, no sort control, no pull-to-refresh.**
- **No colleague's name, no supervisor's name.**
- **No toast.**

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

After a sign-in or a page load, until the first refresh answers. On screen: the header, C01, C14 when audio is not armed, and C12's two placeholder cards. It ends at the first response (at most 15 seconds); a failure goes to §6.3.

### 6.2 Empty

A successful refresh with no active order: C11 centered, "لا توجد طلبات محوّلة لك الآن / No orders assigned to you right now" and "تظهر هنا فور أن يحوّلها لك مكتب الروم سيرفس، مع نغمة / They appear here as soon as the room service desk assigns them to you, with a chime". C05 notices, when present, are above it; S03 "Delivered today", when it has rows, below it. Refreshing continues.

### 6.3 Error

- **Cold failure** — no refresh has succeeded since this sign-in or page load and the latest failed: C04 "تعذّر تحميل طلباتك — تحقّق من الشبكة / Could not load your orders — check the network" with B05 "إعادة المحاولة / Try again"; no cards, no skeleton.
- **Stale** — a refresh failed after an earlier success: C04 "تعذّر تحديث طلباتك — هذه آخر قائمة معروفة / Could not refresh your orders — this is the last known list" with B05, above the last known list.
- Automatic retries continue every 15 seconds in both cases; the first success hides C04.

Nothing is typed on W-01, so there is no field error message.

### 6.4 Success

C06 and the cards (or C11), notices when present, "Delivered today" when it has rows, C04 hidden. A new assignment is announced by the chime, by C15 on its card, by C06's count and by the screen-reader line.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on W-01 and no field element exists. The rules below govern the values it displays and the requests it makes.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Worker name (C01) | The worker session's roster name | Exactly as stored, e.g. "خالد / Khalid" |
| Shift start (C01) | The shift record | `HH:MM`, 24-hour, Western digits |
| Room number (C07, C05, C16) | The order's room number string | Byte for byte, leading zeros kept, left-to-right, never re-validated |
| Order number (C07, C05, C16) | The order | Exactly as stored; no "#", no padding |
| Status (C07) | The order's status | One of the canonical labels of G-01 §5.2, verbatim |
| Item count (C07) | Sum of line quantities | G-01 §7.3 wording, verbatim |
| Payment method (C07) | The order's payment choice | "card" → بطاقة / Card; "cash" → نقدًا / Cash. No amount, whatever is stored |
| Assigned ago (C07) | The latest `assignLog` row whose `to` is this worker | S-01 §7.2 wording; recomputed every 30 seconds |
| Cancellation reason (C05) | Stored reasons | §5.3; exactly as stored, never translated, cut at 120 characters |
| Delivery time (C16) | The order's Delivered log row | `HH:MM`, 24-hour; "سجّله المكتب / Recorded by the desk" when that row was written by someone other than this worker |
| {n} in C06 and B06 | Counts | Western digits |

### 7.2 Request bounds

- One refresh on open, on each change notification, on becoming visible, and every 15 seconds after the previous attempt ended (not while hidden); timeout **15 seconds**; never two in flight.
- W-01 sends no action. Every request carries the worker's identity; none carries anything about a guest.

### 7.3 Tap handling

- B01, B03, B05, B06 and the cards ignore a second tap on the same control within 300 ms; a double-tap on a card opens W-02 once.
- A tap on B04 never also counts as a tap on anything else.
- No long-press, no swipe, no pull-to-refresh.

### 7.4 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: the room number **40 px bold**; C11 line 1 20 px bold; card status, card contents line, C06, B06, C05 line 1 and B04 16 px; C02 18 px bold; C01, C14, C04, the meta line, C05 lines 2–4, C11 line 2 and C16 14 px; C15 12 px bold. Nothing is smaller than 12 px, and no sentence is smaller than 14 px.
- Tap areas: every card (full width × at least 44 px); B01, B03 at least 44 × 44 px; B04 full notice width × 44 px; B06 full width × 48 px. **B05 is 40 px tall** in the prototype (the shared small-button size), below the 44 px convention; build it at **44 px** (**not in the prototype**).
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled.
- Screen reader: each card is announced as one item — "غرفة {room}، {status}، {item count}، {method} / Room {room}, {status}, {item count}, {method}" — with the room digits read one at a time. A new assignment is announced once (§5.5 rule 7); a C05 notice is announced as an alert when it appears; C04 as a status. B06 reports whether it is expanded.
- Motion: none.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Opening the worker URL on a phone with a live worker session shows W-01; with no live worker session it shows W-03.
2. W-01 lists exactly the orders whose `assignedTo` is the signed-in worker and whose status is Accepted & preparing or On the way; no New order and no order assigned to anyone else ever appears.
3. The list is one list under "للتوصيل الآن — {n} / To deliver now — {n}", sorted by order time, oldest first.
4. Every card shows, in order: "غرفة / Room" and the room number at 40 px bold, the status label verbatim, "{item count} · {method}", and "طلب رقم {no} / Order {no}" with "حُوّل لك {ago} / Assigned to you {ago}"; no total, amount or change appears anywhere on W-01.
5. The header shows "طلباتي / My orders", the language toggle and "إنهاء الوردية / End shift"; under it C01 reads "{name} — في الوردية منذ {HH:MM} / {name} — on shift since {HH:MM}".
6. When the desk accepts an order and assigns it to this worker, its card appears on W-01 without a reload, carries "جديد / New", and exactly one chime plays (audio armed).
7. When the desk reassigns an order from a colleague to this worker, its card appears and one chime plays.
8. Several new assignments in one refresh play one chime; the first successful refresh after a page load or a sign-in plays none; no chime ever repeats.
9. A card carries "جديد / New" only when the order was assigned to this worker after they last left W-01; after opening W-02 and coming back, the cards seen before no longer carry it.
10. When the desk reassigns one of this worker's orders to a colleague, its card disappears and C05 appears at the top with "تم تحويل هذا الطلب لعامل آخر / This order was reassigned to another worker", "طلب رقم {no} · غرفة {room} / Order {no} · Room {room}" and "لا تتوجّه به إلى الغرفة. إن كان معك فأعده إلى الرف. / Do not take it to the room. If you have it with you, return it to the shelf."; no colleague's name appears; no chime plays.
11. When the desk cancels one of this worker's orders, C05 reads "ألغى المكتب هذا الطلب / The desk cancelled this order" with the order line, "السبب: {reason} / Reason: {reason}" when a reason is stored, and the "do not take it" line.
12. When the desk records one of this worker's orders as delivered on their behalf, C05 reads "سجّل المكتب توصيل هذا الطلب نيابةً عنك / The desk recorded this order as delivered for you", and the order appears in "Delivered today" with "سجّله المكتب / Recorded by the desk".
13. Every C05 stays until "فهمت / Got it" is tapped; there is no timer and no separate cancelled group.
14. When W-02 is open on an order that is reassigned or cancelled, the phone returns to W-01 with that order's C05.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **One list, oldest order first** (§5.1): the guest who has waited longest is on top, and each card names its own status, so there is nothing to scan across groups.
2. **"New" means "since you last looked"**, saved per worker on the device (§5.4), so a worker returning from a delivery sees at once what arrived meanwhile.
3. **Every departure other than the worker's own delivery gets a notice that stays until "Got it"** (§5.3): reassigned, cancelled, delivered by the desk. No timer, no separate group; the notice tells the worker what to do with anything in hand.
4. **The card prints the payment method but no money** (§5.2, worker-map §4 decision 9).
5. **"Assigned to you {ago}" on the card, order time for sorting**: the card answers "how long have I had this", the order answers "who has waited longest".
6. **Change notification plus a 15-second fallback**; one chime per refresh with a new active order; no repeat; no sound toggle (§5.5).
7. **"Delivered today" is collapsed and money-free** (W-01-S03): it answers "did I deliver it?" without turning the phone into a cash register.
8. **"End shift" always opens WM-02**; it is never refused (worker-map §4 decision 4).

# W-02 — Order detail (تفاصيل الطلب)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/worker/worker-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 3, 5, 6, 7; `/spec/worker/worker-map.md` §4 decisions 1–19; `/spec/staff/screens/S-02.md` — the desk screen this one is ported from: §5.2 (room block and first-screenful arithmetic), §5.3 (item rows), §5.5 (the four payment cases, verbatim), §5.6 (refresh numbers), §5.7 (sending an action and its refusals), and the guest-mobile note (C19); `/spec/staff/screens/SM-02.md` (what WM-01 ports); `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` §4 decisions 4, 5, 18–20; `/spec/screens/G-01.md` §5.2, §7.2, §7.3.

Conventions reused verbatim: Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label after the amount in Arabic and before it in English, two decimals always; G-01 §7.3 item-count wording; 44 × 44 px minimum tap areas; the 300 ms tap debounce; browser zoom never disabled; no toast.

**What changes from S-02, and why.** W-02 is S-02 with the desk's powers removed: no accept, no assign, no reassign, no cancel. What is left is what a person carrying the tray needs — the room at 64 px, the guest's mobile to call when the door stays shut, the items, the notes, the money, one forward button, and one line telling them who can cancel.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** W-02
- **Name:** تفاصيل الطلب / Order detail
- **Type:** Full worker screen with its own URL and history entry (example: `…/worker/order/1042`).
- **Language:** the worker app's saved language. No toggle on W-02 (it is on W-01 and W-03).

---

## 2. Purpose

Show one assigned order completely enough to prepare it, walk it to the right room and take payment, and move it forward in one tap.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 Entry

| From | Trigger | What W-02 shows on arrival |
|---|---|---|
| W-01 My orders | Tap a card | The success state drawn from W-01's last response on the first frame, plus one immediate order request. No skeleton |
| W-02 itself | A reload, or the order URL opened directly | The loading state (§6.1) until the first order response |
| WM-01 | Any outcome | The same W-02, re-rendered per §5.8 |
| W-03 | A correct PIN, when W-03 was reached because the shift ended while W-02 was open | This order again, re-requested |

### 3.2 Exit

| Action on W-02 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Back arrow (B01) or browser back | **W-01** at its saved scroll position; after a reload or direct URL, W-01 fresh at the top |
| Primary action (B02) in status **Accepted & preparing** | Stays; the order becomes **في الطريق / On the way**, or one of the refusals of §5.7 |
| Primary action (B02) in status **On the way** | Opens **WM-01**; the order becomes **تم التوصيل / Delivered** only when WM-01 is confirmed |
| Guest mobile link (B04) | The phone's dialer with the number filled in (`tel:`); W-02 stays open behind it |
| "العودة إلى طلباتي / Back to my orders" (B03) | **W-01**, same as B01 |
| An update or a refusal finds that this worker is no longer on shift | **W-03** with its shift-ended line and the return target W-02 for this order (W-01 §5.8, applied here on every order response) |

W-02 opens no modal other than WM-01 and never navigates by itself after a status change.

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What W-02 reads, writes, and never touches

W-02 renders from the order as the system holds it, and only when `assignedTo` is the signed-in worker (§6.3, §6.5). It **writes** exactly two things, each as one request: on-the-way (B02), and delivered (through WM-01). It **never**: cancels, accepts, assigns or reassigns; edits the order (staff-map §4 decision 18); shows a guest name or device identifier (the mobile number is the one exception, C08); shows who at the desk accepted, cancelled or acted; recomputes the total; shows unit prices.

### 5.1 Identity and status (C04, C05)

As S-02 §5.1: the order number the guest also sees, the elapsed time since the order was placed, and the canonical status at 22 px bold, the second-largest text on the screen.

### 5.2 The room block (C07)

S-02 §5.2, unchanged: **64 px bold**, alone on its line under "غرفة / Room", nothing printed beside it, byte for byte, never re-validated.

**First-screenful guarantee.** At a 390 × 664 px viewport with a 34 px safe-area inset, in status Accepted & preparing: header 56 + top padding 16 + C04 20 + gap 8 + C05 28 + gap 16 (C06 hidden) + C07 96 places the bottom of the room number at **240 px**; the action bar (12 + B02 64 + 12 + 34 = 122 px, no consequence line) has its top at **542 px**. With C03 (≈ 40 px + 8 px gap) and C06 (≈ 40 px + 8 px gap) both shown, the bottom of C07 is at **336 px**. The room number and B02 are both fully visible on the first painted frame, and nothing may be shrunk or collapsed to make the sum work. C08 (the mobile block) sits below the room block and is not part of the guarantee.

### 5.3 The guest's mobile number (C08, B04)

- The number is the stored normalised `+…` value, 22 px bold, left-to-right in both languages, as a `tel:` link: one tap opens the dialer. The helper line names its one use: "اتصل إذا لم يُجب أحد عند الباب / Call if nobody answers the door" (operations.html "الدفع عند الباب" rule 6).
- It is shown in every status, including Cancelled and Delivered, because a worker returning from a door may still need to call back about the items; it is hidden only when the order has no number.
- It is never on W-01 and never in WM-01 (worker-map §4 decision 11).

### 5.4 The primary action (B02)

| Status | Line 1 (18 px bold) | Line 2 (14 px) | What the tap does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accepted & preparing** | خرجت بالطلب / I'm on my way | الحالة بعد الضغط: في الطريق / Status after tapping: On the way | Sends the on-the-way request (§5.7) |
| **On the way** | تم التوصيل والدفع / Delivered and paid | يفتح تأكيد الدفع / Opens the payment confirmation | Opens **WM-01**; sends nothing |
| **Delivered**, **Cancelled** | *(B02 not rendered; B03 stands in its place)* | | |

- There is no consequence line and no confirmation modal for "I'm on my way": it closes nothing for the guest (their cancellation window closed at acceptance), and the only harm of a mistaken tap — the guest sees "On the way" a few minutes early — does not justify a second tap on every order.
- B02 is disabled only while its own request is in flight.

### 5.5 Payment (C13)

The label "الدفع عند الاستلام / Pay on delivery" and then **S-02 §5.5's four cases, word for word**: Card ("الطريقة: بطاقة — أحضر جهاز الدفع / Method: Card — bring the card terminal" + amount to collect); Cash with a stated amount greater than the total (+ "سيدفع بـ {amount} ر.س — أحضر باقي {amount − total} ر.س / Paying with SAR {amount} — bring SAR {amount − total} change"); Cash with the exact amount (+ "سيدفع بالمبلغ المطابق — لا يوجد باقٍ / Paying the exact amount — no change"); Cash with no usable amount (+ "لم يحدّد الضيف المبلغ — احمل باقيًا كافيًا / The guest did not state an amount — carry enough change"). "المبلغ المطلوب / Amount to collect" at 22 px bold. Change = amount − total, two decimals, half up. A card order with an amount ignores the amount.

### 5.6 Refreshing this order

1. **On open:** one order request.
2. **On every change notification** from the system while the page is visible, and as a fallback **every 10 seconds** from the end of the previous attempt (S-02 §5.6's cadence); never two in flight.
3. **Timeout 10 seconds**; a late response is discarded.
4. **Stops permanently** at Delivered, at Cancelled, in the reassigned state and in the error state.
5. **Pauses** while the page is hidden, while WM-01 is open and while an action is in flight; one immediate request on resuming.
6. **On success:** repaint within 100 ms, no animation, no scroll change. A forward change this phone did not make shows **C03**. A change to Cancelled renders the Cancelled state with **C02** and **C15**. An `assignedTo` that is no longer this worker renders the **reassigned** state (§6.5). A worker no longer on shift hands over to W-03 (§3.2).
7. **On failure:** the last known values stay and **C06** is shown until the next success.

### 5.7 Sending "on the way", and every way it can be refused

The request carries the order number, the target status On the way, and the worker's identity. It is sent only after the device has confirmed, from the latest data, that the order is still assigned to this worker; the system refuses it otherwise (worker-map §4 decision 1). Idempotent by target status.

1. **On tap:** B02 disabled, "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…", C17 cleared, refreshing paused.
2. **Timeout 15 seconds.** On timeout or transport failure B02 returns to its label and **C17** appears. Nothing else changes; the next tap sends again. No offline queue, no optimistic change (S-02 §5.7 rule 6).
3. **Success:** On the way is rendered; B02 shows the On-the-way pair; refreshing resumes.
4. **Already On the way or Delivered** (the desk acted on the worker's behalf, or the worker's other phone): the real status is rendered with **C03**.
5. **Cancelled** (the desk cancelled meanwhile): the Cancelled state with **C02** and **C15**; B02 disappears; B03 takes the bar.
6. **No longer assigned to this worker** (the desk reassigned it meanwhile): the **reassigned** state (§6.5).
7. **This worker is no longer on shift:** W-03 with the return target W-02 (§3.2).

### 5.8 Delivering (B02 in On the way → WM-01)

Tapping B02 in On the way sends nothing and opens WM-01. What comes back — exactly four outcomes (WM-01 §5.5):

1. **Delivered** — WM-01 closes; W-02 shows C05 "تم التوصيل / Delivered", C16 "تم تسجيل التوصيل والدفع / Delivery and payment recorded", B03 only, refreshing stopped. W-02 does not navigate by itself: the worker is at a door.
2. **Cancelled** — WM-01 closes; the Cancelled state with C02 and C15.
3. **Reassigned** — WM-01 closes; the reassigned state (§6.5).
4. **Failed** — WM-01 stays open with its error line; W-02 unchanged beneath it, still On the way.

### 5.9 The refusal hint (C14) — the worker's path to a cancellation

The worker cannot cancel (worker-map §4 decision 2). When the guest refuses the order at the door, disputes it, or cannot pay, the worker reads C14 — "الضيف رفض الطلب أو لا يوجد دفع؟ اتصل بمكتب الروم سيرفس ليلغيه / Guest refused or can't pay? Call the room service desk to cancel" — and phones the desk, which cancels with a reason (desk SM-01). The worker's phone then shows the Cancelled state with C02 at its next update, telling them to return the items. C14 is plain text: the desk's number is not stored in the system in version 1 (worker-map §6).

### 5.10 What is deliberately not on this screen

- **No cancel control of any kind**, no "reject", no "return to desk" button (worker-map §4 decision 2).
- **No accept, no assign, no reassign.**
- **No editing** (staff-map §4 decision 18), **no undo**, no partial delivery.
- **No unit prices, no images, no checkboxes** (S-02 §5.3).
- **No names**: not the supervisor who accepted, not who cancelled, not a colleague.
- **No toast, and no modal other than WM-01.**

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

Only on a reload or direct URL open. Header complete; gray placeholder blocks in the shape of C04, C05, C07 and three item rows, pulsing 100 %–60 % every 1.5 seconds; the action bar with B03 only. It ends at the first response or the 10-second timeout, then §6.3 on failure. Entering from W-01 there is no loading state.

### 6.2 Empty

**Unreachable.** Every order carries a number, a time, a room, at least one line, a payment method, a total and a status; an order missing any of them is unreadable and renders §6.3.

### 6.3 Error

One rendered error state, **C19**, with four triggers:
1. The system holds no order with this number.
2. The response is unreadable or missing a required value (§6.2).
3. The URL carries an order number the system does not recognise.
4. The order exists but is **not assigned to this worker** and was not assigned to them when this visit began (a URL typed or shared for someone else's order).

On screen: the header, C19's icon and three lines — "لم نعثر على هذا الطلب بين طلباتك / We could not find this order among your orders"; "قد يكون رقم الطلب غير صحيح، أو لم يُحوَّل إليك / The order number may be wrong, or it was not assigned to you"; "ارجع إلى طلباتك وافتحه من هناك / Go back to your orders and open it from there" — and B03 in the action bar. No order content is rendered and no further request is sent. A failed refresh (C06) and a failed action (C17) are not this state.

### 6.4 Success

| Status | Action bar | Body extras |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted & preparing | B02 "خرجت بالطلب / I'm on my way" | C14; C03 if the change came from elsewhere |
| On the way | B02 "تم التوصيل والدفع / Delivered and paid" | C14; C03 if the change came from elsewhere |
| Delivered | B03 | C16; C03 if the desk recorded it on the worker's behalf |
| Cancelled | B03 | C02, and C15 when a reason is stored |

### 6.5 Reassigned

Reached when an order that was assigned to this worker when the visit began (or at the previous update) is now assigned to someone else — found by a refresh, by a refusal of B02, or by WM-01's *reassigned* outcome. The whole body is replaced by **C18** — "حُوِّل طلب الغرفة {room} إلى زميل / The order for room {room} was moved to a colleague" and "لم يعد عليك توصيله. إن كانت المنتجات معك، اتصل بمكتب الروم سيرفس. / You no longer deliver it. If you have the items, call the room service desk." — with B03 in the action bar. The guest's mobile, the items, the notes and the payment are no longer shown: the order is someone else's now. Refreshing stops.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on W-02 and no field element exists.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Order number, elapsed time (C04) | The order | As S-02 §7.1 |
| Status (C05, B02) | The latest successful response | One of the canonical labels; a sixth value makes the order unreadable (§6.3) |
| Room number (C07, C18) | The order's room number | Byte for byte, leading zeros kept, 1–5 Western digits, 64 px bold in C07; in C18 printed inside the sentence at 20 px bold, left-to-right |
| Guest mobile (B04) | The order's stored normalised number | Printed as stored (`+` then 8–15 digits), left-to-right, never reformatted; `tel:` link to the same value |
| Quantities, product names, item count, total, notes | The order | As S-02 §7.1: names never truncated (fallback to the other language's stored name); total never recomputed; notes in full, never translated |
| Payment lines (C13) | Payment method, stated amount, total | S-02 §5.5's four cases |
| Cancellation reason (C15) | Stored reasons | The one in the worker app's language, falling back to the other; exactly as stored; never translated (W-01 §5.4) |

### 7.2 Request bounds

- **Order refresh:** on open, on each change notification, and every **10 seconds** from the end of the previous attempt; timeout **10 seconds**; paused while hidden, while WM-01 is open and while an action is in flight; stopped at Delivered, Cancelled, reassigned and error.
- **On-the-way action:** timeout **15 seconds**; idempotent by target status; never queued, retried automatically or sent optimistically.
- Every request carries the order number, the worker's identity and the device's hotel link; nothing about the guest.

### 7.3 Tap handling

- B01–B04 respond to a single tap with the 300 ms debounce; B02 stays disabled for the life of its request, so a double-tap sends one action.
- Nothing else is tappable: not the room number, the items, the notes, the payment block, the banners or the hint.
- No long-press, no swipe, no pull-to-refresh.

### 7.4 Language

Fixed strings in the worker app's saved language. Room number, order number, amounts, the mobile number, notes and stored reasons are never translated or reformatted.

### 7.5 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: room number **64 px bold**; C05, the quantity in C10, the mobile number and "المبلغ المطلوب / Amount to collect" 22 px bold; product names and notes 18 px; B02 line 1 and C01 18 px bold; headings, C11, labels and B03 16 px; every other line 14 px. **Nothing smaller than 14 px.**
- Tap areas: B01 44 × 44 px; B02 inner width × 64 px; B03 × 56 px; B04 inner width × 44 px.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **W-02 is S-02 minus the desk's powers**: same room block, item rows, payment block and first-screenful guarantee; no cancel, accept, assign or reassign (§5.10).
2. **The guest's mobile sits directly under the room block, in every status, as a `tel:` link with its one use written beside it** (§5.3).
3. **"I'm on my way" has no consequence line and no confirmation** (§5.4).
4. **The refusal hint is plain text in the body, shown while the order is active** (§5.9); the desk's number is not in the system.
5. **A desk cancellation is a permanent banner telling the worker to return the items**, with the stored reason and no canceller's name (§4 C02, C15).
6. **A reassignment away replaces the whole body**, keeping only the room in the sentence, because the order is now someone else's (§6.5).
7. **An order not assigned to this worker is indistinguishable from a missing one** (§6.3 trigger 4), so a URL never exposes another worker's guest.
8. **"On the way" is sent only after a fresh check that the order is still this worker's** (§5.7), mirroring the system's refusal.
9. **Four outcomes from WM-01, not three**: delivered, cancelled, reassigned, failed (§5.8).

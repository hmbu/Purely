# W-02 — Order detail (تفاصيل الطلب)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Approved** — reconciled with the built prototype (`worker/js/order.js`, `worker/js/core.js`). Where the prototype does not build something this file requires, the passage says **Not in the prototype — build as specified.**
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 3, 5, 6, 7; `/spec/worker/worker-map.md` §4 decisions 1–19; `/spec/staff/screens/S-02.md` — the desk screen this one is ported from: §5.2 (room block), §5.3 (item rows), §5.5 (the four payment cases, verbatim), §5.7 (sending an action and its refusals), and the guest-mobile note (C19); `/spec/staff/screens/SM-02.md` (what WM-01 ports); `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` §4 decisions 4, 5, 18–20; `/spec/screens/G-01.md` §5.2, §7.2, §7.3.

Conventions reused verbatim: Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label after the amount in Arabic and before it in English, two decimals always; G-01 §7.3 item-count wording; 44 × 44 px minimum tap areas; the 300 ms tap debounce; browser zoom never disabled; no toast.

**What changes from S-02, and why.** W-02 is S-02 with the desk's powers removed: no accept, no assign, no reassign, no cancel. What is left is what a person carrying the tray needs — the room at 64 px, the guest's mobile to call when the door stays shut, the items, the notes, the money, one forward button, and one line telling them who can cancel.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** W-02
- **Name:** تفاصيل الطلب / Order detail
- **Type:** Full worker screen with its own URL and history entry (prototype: `worker/index.html#/order/{order number}`).
- **Language:** the worker app's saved language. No toggle on W-02 (it is on W-01 and W-03).

---

## 2. Purpose

Show one assigned order completely enough to prepare it, walk it to the right room and take payment, and move it forward in one tap.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 Entry

| From | Trigger | What W-02 shows on arrival |
|---|---|---|
| W-01 My orders | Tap a card | The success state drawn on the first frame from W-01's last response, plus one immediate order request. No skeleton |
| W-02 itself | A reload, or the order URL opened directly | The loading state (§6.1) until the first order response |
| WM-01 | Any outcome | The same W-02, re-rendered per §5.7 |

### 3.2 Exit

| Action or event on W-02 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Back arrow (B01), browser back, or "العودة إلى طلباتي / Back to my orders" (B03) | **W-01** (a history step back when W-02 was opened from W-01; otherwise W-01 replaces W-02) |
| B02 in status **Accepted & preparing** | Stays; the order becomes **في الطريق / On the way**, or one of the outcomes of §5.6 |
| B02 in status **On the way** | Opens **WM-01**; the order becomes **تم التوصيل / Delivered** only when WM-01 is confirmed |
| Guest mobile link (B04) | The phone's dialer with the number filled in (`tel:`); W-02 stays open behind it |
| A refresh or an action answer finds the order **reassigned** to someone else, **cancelled** by the desk, **not assigned** to this worker, or **not found** | **W-01**, with a C05 notice for this order (W-01 §5.3) |
| A refresh finds the order **delivered by the desk** on this worker's behalf while W-02 showed it active | **W-01**, with the "delivered by the desk" notice |
| The shift is found ended | **W-03** |

W-02 opens no modal other than WM-01.

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What W-02 reads, writes, and never touches

W-02 renders the order as the system holds it, and only while `assignedTo` is the signed-in worker. It **writes** exactly two things, each as one request: on-the-way (B02), and delivered (through WM-01). It **never**: cancels, accepts, assigns or reassigns; edits the order (staff-map §4 decision 18); shows a guest name or device identifier (the mobile number is the one exception, C08); shows who at the desk accepted, cancelled or acted; recomputes the total; shows unit prices.

### 5.1 The room block (C07)

S-02 §5.2, unchanged: **64 px bold**, alone on its line under "غرفة / Room", nothing printed beside it, byte for byte, never re-validated. At a 390 × 664 px viewport the header (56), C04, C05 and C07 place the bottom of the room number at about **240 px**, and the action bar (12 + 64 + 12 + safe area) starts at about **540 px**, so the room number and B02 are both visible on the first frame; C03 and C06, when shown, push it down by about 50 px each and it still clears the bar.

### 5.2 The guest's mobile number (C08, B04)

- The stored normalised `+…` value, 22 px bold, left-to-right in both languages, as a `tel:` link: one tap opens the dialer. It exists so the worker can call when nobody answers the door (operations.html "الدفع عند الباب" rule 6).
- It is never on W-01 and never in WM-01 (worker-map §4 decision 10).

### 5.3 The primary action (B02)

| Status | Line 1 (18 px bold) | Line 2 (14 px) | What the tap does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accepted & preparing** | خرجت بالطلب / I'm on my way | الحالة بعد الضغط: في الطريق / Status after tapping: On the way | Sends the on-the-way request (§5.6) |
| **On the way** | تم التوصيل واستلام المبلغ / Delivered & paid | يفتح تأكيد الدفع / Opens the payment confirmation | Opens **WM-01**; sends nothing |
| **Delivered** (record) | *(B02 not rendered; B03 stands in its place)* | | |

- There is no confirmation for "I'm on my way": it closes nothing for the guest (their cancellation window closed at acceptance).
- B02 is disabled only while its own request is in flight.

### 5.4 Payment (C13)

The label "الدفع عند الاستلام / Pay on delivery", then **S-02 §5.5's four cases, word for word**:

| Case | Line 1 | Line 2 (22 px bold) | Line 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card (any stored amount is ignored) | الطريقة: بطاقة — أحضر جهاز الدفع / Method: Card — bring the card terminal | المبلغ المطلوب: {total} / Amount to collect: {total} | — |
| Cash, stated amount greater than the total | الطريقة: نقدًا / Method: Cash | المبلغ المطلوب: {total} / Amount to collect: {total} | **سيدفع بـ {amount} — أحضر باقي {change} / Paying with {amount} — bring {change} change** (bold) |
| Cash, stated amount equal to the total | الطريقة: نقدًا / Method: Cash | المبلغ المطلوب: {total} / Amount to collect: {total} | سيدفع بالمبلغ المطابق — لا يوجد باقٍ / Paying the exact amount — no change |
| Cash, no amount, an unreadable amount, or less than the total | الطريقة: نقدًا / Method: Cash | المبلغ المطلوب: {total} / Amount to collect: {total} | لم يحدّد الضيف المبلغ — احمل باقيًا كافيًا / The guest did not state an amount — carry enough change |

Amounts carry two decimals and the currency label ("45.00 ر.س" / "SAR 45.00"). Change = amount − total, computed in whole halalas.

### 5.5 Keeping this order current

1. **On open:** one request for this order.
2. **Then W-02 follows W-01's list refresh** (W-01 §5.5: every change notification, on becoming visible, and every 15 seconds): after each list refresh, if the order is still one of this worker's active orders, W-02 repaints from it; if it is not in the list, W-02 asks for the order itself once and applies the answer; if the list refresh failed, W-02 shows C06 over the last known order.
3. **Timeout 15 seconds** for every request.
4. **Held** while this screen's own request is in flight and while WM-01's request is in flight: their answers decide.
5. **Applying an answer:**
   - still this worker's and active → repaint; if the status changed by a hand other than this worker's, show C03; announce "{no}: {status}" to screen readers;
   - Delivered, recorded by this worker → the delivered record (§6.4);
   - Delivered, recorded by the desk → if W-02 was showing the order active, go to W-01 with the "delivered by the desk" notice; if W-02 was opened directly on it, the delivered record with C16's desk wording;
   - reassigned, cancelled, not this worker's, not found → go to W-01 with the matching C05 notice (W-01 §5.3).
6. The elapsed time in C04 is recomputed every 30 seconds with no request.

### 5.6 Sending "on my way" (B02 in Accepted & preparing)

1. The tap disables B02 and relabels it "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…" before anything is sent, and hides C17.
2. One request: this order, target On the way, this worker's identity. The system first checks that the order is still assigned to this worker ("not mine" otherwise).
3. **Done** — the order is now On the way and still this worker's, whether this request did it or the desk had already done it; when the desk had, C03 appears. W-02 repaints in On the way.
4. **Not mine / not found / no longer active** — go to W-01 with the matching notice.
5. **Anything else** (not sent, dropped, no answer in 15 seconds, an error, unreadable) — nothing is assumed: the status stays as it was, and C17 appears above B02. The next tap on B02 is the retry. There is no automatic retry and no offline queue.

### 5.7 What comes back from WM-01

| WM-01 outcome | W-02 |
|---|---|
| Delivered (by this request, or the order was already Delivered and still this worker's) | The delivered record (§6.4); "تم التوصيل / Delivered" announced |
| Gone — not mine, not found, or Cancelled | Returns to W-01 with the matching C05 notice |
| Closed with "رجوع / Back" | Unchanged; one immediate request for this order |

### 5.8 What is deliberately not on this screen

- **No cancel** (worker-map §4 decision 2); C14 tells the worker who can.
- **No accept, assign or reassign.**
- **No cancelled or reassigned view**: such an order leaves W-02 for W-01 at once, with a notice.
- **No unit prices, no product images.**
- **No toast.**

### 5.9 Screen wake lock

**Not in the prototype — build as specified.** While W-02 is visible with a live worker session it holds the screen wake lock exactly as W-01 §5.7.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

Opened by reload or direct URL, until the first answer: the header, gray placeholder bars in the body, and B03 in the action bar. Never shown when opened from a W-01 card.

### 6.2 Empty

Not applicable: an order always has at least one item line.

### 6.3 Error

- **Load failure** — the first request failed (not sent, dropped, timed out, unreadable) and nothing had been shown: C19 "تعذّر تحميل هذا الطلب / Could not load this order" + "تحقّق من الشبكة ثم ارجع إلى طلباتي وافتحه من هناك / Check the network, then go back to your orders and open it from there", and B03. A system answer of "no such order" or "not yours" is not this state: it returns to W-01 with a notice.
- **Stale** — a later check failed while the order is on screen: C06 over the last known order; B02 stays usable.
- **Action failed** — C17 above B02 (§5.6 rule 5).

### 6.4 Success

- **Active** (Accepted & preparing or On the way): the full body with C14, and B02 in the bar.
- **Delivered record** — after this worker's WM-01 succeeded, or when the order was opened directly after delivery: the full body with C05 "تم التوصيل / Delivered", C16 in place of C14, no C03 and no C06, and B03 in the bar. Read-only; no further refresh changes it.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on W-02.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Room number (C07) | The order | Byte for byte, leading zeros kept, 64 px, left-to-right |
| Order number (C04) | The order | Exactly as stored |
| Elapsed (C04) | Order time against the device clock | S-01 §7.2 wording, recomputed every 30 seconds |
| Status (C05) | The order | Canonical label, verbatim |
| Guest mobile (B04) | The order's stored number | A `tel:` link only for "+" then 8–15 digits; otherwise plain text; hidden when empty |
| Item rows (C10) | The order's lines | The product name in the interface language, falling back to the other language; quantity first |
| Notes (C12) | The order | Exactly as stored, line breaks kept |
| Payment lines (C13) | Payment choice, stated amount, total | §5.4 |
| Delivered record wording (C16) | Who wrote the Delivered log row | This worker → "تم تسجيل التوصيل والدفع / Delivery and payment recorded"; anyone else → "سجّل المكتب التوصيل نيابةً عنك / The desk recorded the delivery for you" |

### 7.2 Request bounds

- Order check: on open, and after list refreshes when the order is missing from the list; timeout **15 seconds**.
- "On my way": one request per tap; timeout **15 seconds**; never queued, retried automatically or sent optimistically.
- Every request carries the worker's identity; none carries anything about the guest.

### 7.3 Tap handling, language, sizes and accessibility

- B01, B02 and B03 ignore a second tap within 300 ms. B02 is disabled for the life of its request.
- Fixed strings in the worker app's language; room numbers, order numbers, names of products in the chosen language, notes and phone numbers are never translated.
- Text sizes: room number 64 px bold; C05, C10 quantities, C13 amount line and B04 22 px bold; C01, item names, notes and B02 line 1 18 px; C09, C11, C12 label, C13 lines, C16, C08 label and B03 16 px; C03, C04, C06, C14, C17 and B02 line 2 14 px. Nothing smaller than 14 px.
- Tap areas: B01 44 × 44 px; B04 44 px tall; B02 full width × 64 px; B03 full width × 56 px.
- Screen reader: the room block is announced as "غرفة {digits one at a time} / Room {digits}"; each item row as "{N} × {name}"; a status change as "{no}: {status}"; C17 as an alert; C06 as a status.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Tapping a W-01 card opens W-02 for that order with its content on the first frame and no skeleton; a reload shows placeholders until the first answer.
2. The room number is 64 px bold under "غرفة / Room", left-to-right, leading zeros kept, and nothing on the screen is larger.
3. C08 shows "جوال الضيف / Guest mobile" and the stored number as a `tel:` link that opens the dialer; with no stored number C08 is absent.
4. Items are listed with "×{N}" first and the full product name, never truncated, under "ما يجب تجهيزه — {item count} / To prepare — {item count}".
5. The payment block prints one of the four cases of §5.4 word for word, with "المبلغ المطلوب: {total} / Amount to collect: {total}" at 22 px bold.
6. In Accepted & preparing, B02 reads "خرجت بالطلب / I'm on my way" and "الحالة بعد الضغط: في الطريق / Status after tapping: On the way"; one tap sends exactly one request, shows "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…" while in flight, and on success shows "في الطريق / On the way".
7. In On the way, B02 reads "تم التوصيل واستلام المبلغ / Delivered & paid" and "يفتح تأكيد الدفع / Opens the payment confirmation"; tapping it opens WM-01 and sends nothing.
8. A failed "on my way" leaves the status unchanged and shows "لم يصل التحديث إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The update did not reach the system — try again" above B02.
9. When the desk had already marked the order On the way, the worker's tap is treated as done and C03 "غيّر المكتب حالة هذا الطلب نيابةً عنك / The desk changed this order's status for you" appears.
10. C14 "الضيف رفض الطلب أو لا يوجد دفع؟ اتصل بمكتب الروم سيرفس ليلغيه / Guest refused or can't pay? Call the room service desk to cancel" is shown while the order is active; no cancel control exists.
11. When the desk reassigns or cancels the order while W-02 is open, the phone returns to W-01 and shows that order's notice; W-02 never shows a cancelled or reassigned order.
12. When the desk records the delivery while W-02 shows the order active, the phone returns to W-01 with "سجّل المكتب توصيل هذا الطلب نيابةً عنك / The desk recorded this order as delivered for you".
13. After WM-01 succeeds, W-02 shows "تم التوصيل / Delivered", C16 "تم تسجيل التوصيل والدفع / Delivery and payment recorded", no B02, and "العودة إلى طلباتي / Back to my orders".
14. Opening the URL of an order not assigned to this worker returns to W-01 with "هذا الطلب ليس محوّلًا لك / This order is not assigned to you"; of a non-existent order, with "لم نعثر على هذا الطلب / We could not find this order".
15. When the first request fails, C19 "تعذّر تحميل هذا الطلب / Could not load this order" and B03 are shown; when a later check fails, C06 "تعذّر تحديث هذا الطلب — هذه آخر حالة معروفة / Could not refresh this order — this is the last known status" appears over the order.
16. No total or amount is shown anywhere but C11 and C13; no guest name, no desk member's name and no colleague's name appears.
17. **Not in the prototype — build as specified.** While W-02 is visible the screen does not dim, where the browser supports the wake lock.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **An order that leaves the worker's hands leaves W-02** (§5.5 rule 5): reassigned, cancelled and desk-delivered orders go back to W-01 with a notice, instead of a special state on this screen. One place explains every departure.
2. **"Delivered & paid" names both acts the worker performs at the door** (§5.3), and WM-01 confirms them.
3. **No confirmation for "I'm on my way"** (§5.3).
4. **W-02 rides on W-01's refresh** (§5.5): one live list per phone, and a direct check only when the order drops out of it.
5. **The desk acting for the worker is always disclosed** (C03, C16's desk wording).

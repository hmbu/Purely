# WM-02 — End shift (إنهاء الوردية)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Approved** — written from the built prototype (`worker/js/orders.js`, WM-02 section; `worker/js/app.js`, `App.endShift`). Where the prototype does not build something this file requires, the passage says **Not in the prototype — build as specified.**
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decision 7; `/spec/worker/worker-map.md` §4 decisions 3, 4, 5; `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` Amendment A2 decision 31; `/spec/staff/screens/SM-01.md` preamble (the staff modal conventions); `/spec/worker/screens/W-01.md` (B03, which opens this sheet).

Conventions reused, not re-decided: the staff modal conventions of the SM-01 preamble — bottom sheet over a 50 % black backdrop covering W-01, 85 % maximum height, 390 px maximum width, pinned footer with "رجوع / Back" (outlined) above the dark confirm, 300 ms arming delay; Western digits; no toast.

**Why this sheet exists.** Ending a shift takes the worker off the desk's list: from that moment the desk cannot assign orders to them. A mis-tap in a pocket must not do that silently, and a worker who still carries orders must be told that those orders do not leave with them. The sheet asks once and never refuses: a worker who must go, goes, and the desk reassigns what is left.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** WM-02
- **Name:** إنهاء الوردية / End shift
- **Type:** Modal (bottom sheet) on top of **W-01 My orders**. No URL; it pushes one same-URL history entry only so that browser back closes it.
- **Language:** the worker app's saved language. No toggle.

---

## 2. Purpose

Confirm that the worker wants to end their shift and sign out, and warn them when orders are still assigned to them.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 How the modal opens

| From | Trigger | What WM-02 receives | What it shows on opening |
|---|---|---|---|
| W-01 | Tap W-01-B03 "إنهاء الوردية / End shift". The tap sends nothing | The number of active orders (Accepted & preparing or On the way) W-01 lists at that moment | C01, C02, C03 when that number is at least 1, and the footer with B01 and B02 |

The number is taken when the sheet opens and is not updated while it is open.

### 3.2 Where each action leads

| Action | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap B02 "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" | The shift ends in the system (the desk's SM-03 then lists the worker as "خارج الوردية / Off shift"), the worker session on this phone is cleared, the "last left W-01" moment is saved (W-01 §5.4), and **W-03** opens. Orders still assigned to the worker stay assigned to them |
| "رجوع / Back" (B01), backdrop, browser back or Escape | Closes; nothing sent; the worker is still on shift, on W-01 |
| The shift is ended elsewhere while the sheet is open | The sheet is dropped and W-03 opens |
| Browser reload | Reloads to W-01 with no sheet; still on shift |

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What WM-02 reads, writes, and never touches

Reads the number of active orders W-01 handed it. Sends one thing: "end this worker's shift". On success it clears the worker session on this phone and saves the "last left W-01" moment. It **never** reassigns, cancels or changes any order; never signs the desk in or out; never refuses to end the shift.

### 5.1 Ending the shift with orders still held

- Ending the shift is **allowed** whatever the worker holds (worker-map §4 decision 4). C03 is a warning, not a refusal: it says how many orders are still assigned, that they stay assigned to this worker until the desk reassigns them, and that the worker must tell the desk before leaving.
- The orders keep their status and their `assignedTo`. The desk sees them on S-01 under "المندوب: {name} / Worker: {name}" and moves them with S-02-B06 "تحويل لعامل آخر / Reassign" (**not in the prototype:** S-01-C14 adds " — خارج الوردية / — off shift").
- The sheet shows no order details: the count is enough to prompt the call to the desk.

### 5.2 What is deliberately not on this modal

- No list of the held orders, no reassign control, no "hand over to" picker: reassigning is a desk act.
- No reason field, no close "×", no toast.

## 6. States

### 6.0 Ready

Backdrop; C01, C02, C03 when orders are held; footer with B01 and B02. Focus on C01; 300 ms arming delay on every control.

### 6.1 Loading

In the prototype, ending the shift is immediate; there is no in-flight state.

**Not in the prototype — build as specified.** In production "end my shift" is one request with a **15-second** timeout. While it is in flight B02 is disabled and reads "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…", B01 is disabled, and the backdrop, browser back and Escape are ignored.

### 6.2 Empty

With no active order, C03 is absent; the sheet is C01, C02 and the footer.

### 6.3 Error

**Not in the prototype — build as specified.** When the end-shift request fails (not sent, dropped, no answer in 15 seconds, unreadable), nothing is cleared: the worker stays signed in and on shift, the sheet stays open, and C04 "لم يصل إنهاء الوردية إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / Ending your shift did not reach the system — try again" appears; B02 is the retry. The phone never signs itself out on a request that did not reach the system, because the desk would keep assigning orders to a worker it still sees on shift, to a phone nobody is watching.

### 6.4 Success

The shift has ended: the sheet and W-01 are replaced by W-03, with no message.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on WM-02.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| {count} in C03 | Active orders on W-01 when the sheet opened | 1 → "طلب واحد / 1 order"; 2 → "طلبان / 2 orders"; 3–10 → "{n} طلبات / {n} orders"; 11 and more → "{n} طلبًا / {n} orders"; Western digits |

### 7.2 Request bounds

- One end-shift action per confirmed tap of B02. (**Not in the prototype:** as a request, timeout 15 seconds, never retried automatically.)
- It carries the worker's identity only.

### 7.3 Tap handling, language, sizes and accessibility

- Every control ignores taps for 300 ms after the sheet starts to appear. W-01-B03 ignores a second tap within 300 ms, so the sheet opens once.
- Fixed strings in the worker app's language.
- Text sizes: C01 and B02 18 px bold; C03 and B01 16 px; C02 14 px. Nothing smaller than 14 px. B02 full sheet width × 64 px.
- Screen reader: the dialog is labelled by C01 and focus starts on it; W-01 behind the backdrop is not reachable.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Tapping W-01-B03 "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" opens WM-02 and sends nothing.
2. The sheet shows "إنهاء الوردية؟ / End your shift?" and "ستخرج من التطبيق ولن تصلك طلبات جديدة حتى تسجّل دخولك مرة أخرى. / You will be signed out and will not receive new orders until you sign in again.", with "رجوع / Back" and "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" in the footer.
3. With one active order, C03 reads "معك طلب واحد لم يُسلَّم بعد. يبقى محوّلًا لك حتى يحوّله المكتب لعامل آخر — أبلغ المكتب قبل أن تغادر. / You still have 1 order not delivered. It stays assigned to you until the desk reassigns it — tell the desk before you leave."; with 2, "طلبان / 2 orders"; with no active order, C03 is absent.
4. Tapping "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" with orders still held is **not** refused: the shift ends, the phone shows W-03, and the held orders stay assigned to this worker with their status unchanged.
5. After ending the shift, the desk's SM-03 lists the worker as "خارج الوردية / Off shift" and does not let the supervisor choose them.
6. Ending the shift on the worker app never signs the desk out.
7. "رجوع / Back", the backdrop, browser back or Escape close the sheet with nothing sent, and the worker is still on shift on W-01.
8. Taps in the first 300 ms after the sheet appears do nothing.
9. **Not in the prototype — build as specified.** A failed end-shift request leaves the worker signed in and on shift, keeps the sheet open, and shows "لم يصل إنهاء الوردية إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / Ending your shift did not reach the system — try again".

---

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **Confirm, never refuse** (§5.1): a worker who must leave can always end the shift; the warning makes sure the desk hears about the orders left behind.
2. **Held orders stay assigned to the worker** until the desk reassigns them (§5.1), so nothing is silently orphaned and the desk still sees who had them.
3. **The count is taken when the sheet opens** (§3.1), which is what the worker just saw on W-01.
4. **A failed end-shift signs nothing out** (§6.3), not in the prototype, because a phone that believes it is off shift while the desk sees it on shift is the worst of both.

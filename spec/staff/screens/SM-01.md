**Staff modal conventions (this file sets them; SM-02 reuses them by reference).** A staff modal is a **bottom sheet** built on the guest modal conventions of `/spec/screens/M-03.md` §preamble — full viewport width, anchored to the bottom edge, top corners rounded 16 px, light filled, 24 px padding on the sides and top, 16 px at the bottom plus the device's safe-area inset, sliding up over 200 ms on open and removed instantly on close, at most 85 % of the viewport height, over a **backdrop** in black at 50 % opacity that covers the whole of S-02 **including its fixed action bar (S-02-S04)**; title 18 px bold, start-aligned; focus moves to the title on open and the sheet is announced as a dialog — with four staff differences: (1) the text floor is **14 px**, the floor of S-02; (2) the buttons are the staff sizes of S-02 — the confirm button is dark filled, **64 px** tall, two lines (line 1 18 px bold, line 2 14 px), and "رجوع / Back" is outlined, **56 px** tall, 16 px bold; (3) the two buttons sit in a **footer pinned to the bottom of the sheet**, "رجوع / Back" above and the confirm button below with **16 px** between them, and everything above the footer scrolls inside the sheet; (4) while a request is in flight the sheet **cannot be dismissed**, for at most 15 seconds (§9 item 9).

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** SM-01
- **Name:** إلغاء الطلب / Cancel order
- **Type:** Modal (bottom sheet) on top of **S-02 Order detail**. No URL, no history entry (map §3.3 rule 2). It is the only place in the staff interface where an order is cancelled.
- **Language:** the staff interface language saved on the device. No language toggle. The reason preview (C08) is the one part of the sheet shown in the **order's** language, because it is what the guest will read (§5.3).

---

## 2. Purpose

Cancel one order with a reason the guest will read on their phone, and hand S-02 one of the three outcomes it expects.

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What SM-01 reads, writes, and never touches

SM-01 **reads** the two values S-02 hands it (§3.1) and the signed-in staff identity from the session.

SM-01 **writes** nothing to the device. It sends **one** request per confirmed tap: cancel this order, with this reason.

SM-01 **never**: shows or edits the order's items, quantities, room number, notes or payment (map §4 decision 18); puts the staff member's name into the reason string (§7.1); translates a typed line; offers any status other than Cancelled.

### 5.1 Sheet layout

- The sheet asks for one thing — why — and states one consequence: the guest will read it and it cannot be undone (C02).
- **No room number and no order number appear on the sheet.** SM-01 exists only on top of one order's S-02, and it is reached only by scrolling past that order's 64 px room number to S-02-B03 at the end of the body; and the map fixes a room-number size for the board card, S-02 and SM-02 only (map §4 decision 4), because those are the surfaces a room is read from. A room number on this sheet would have to be the largest text on it (map §4 decision 4) and would push the four presets below the fold.
- Height at a 390 × 664 px viewport: the scrolling content is about 560 px and the footer about 172 px (12 + B05 56 + 16 + B06 64 + 24 including the safe-area share), so the scrolling area scrolls; the title, C02 and the first presets are visible on open, and the footer is always visible.
- **With the keyboard open** (F01 focused), the sheet stays above the keyboard, the scrolling area scrolls so F01 and C08 are both in view, and the footer stays pinned at the bottom of the sheet, directly above the keyboard. The keyboard's Enter / Done key closes the keyboard and does **not** confirm.

### 5.2 The four preset reasons (B01–B04) — the reason is required

Exactly four presets, in this fixed order, each a pair of strings — the Arabic one and the English one:

| # | Arabic | English | When staff use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| B01 | المنتج غير متوفر | Item not available | A product in the order ran out (S-01 §5.8) |
| B02 | الغرفة غير صحيحة أو غير مشغولة | Room number is wrong or not occupied | The room does not exist or is empty (S-02 §5.2) |
| B03 | لا أحد يجيب في الغرفة | No answer at the room | Staff knocked and nobody came |
| B04 | بطلب من الضيف | At the guest's request | The guest phoned reception to cancel after the order was accepted — the channel G-06 C16 variant B tells them to use |

- **One pair per preset, used in two places:** the row label shown to staff is the preset in the **staff** language; the text sent is the same preset in the **order's** language (§7.1). Each pair was written to read correctly after G-06's prefix "ألغى الفندق هذا الطلب: / The hotel cancelled this order:".
- **Required.** None is selected on open. B06 without a selection shows C04 and sends nothing (§7.3).
- **Single choice.** Tapping a preset selects it and unselects any other; tapping the selected one again changes nothing; once one is selected there is no way back to none. There is no "Other" preset: the free line adds detail to a preset, it never replaces one (§5.3).

### 5.3 The free line (F01) and what the guest will read (C08)

- F01 is **optional**: up to **60 characters** added after the preset. It is sent **exactly as typed** (after trimming), never translated (G-06 §5.3).
- **C06 tells staff which language to write in** — the order's language, named in the staff language — because G-06 prints the line verbatim to a guest who may not read the staff member's language (map §4 decision 15).
- **C08 shows the guest's whole sentence before it is sent**, in the order's language and direction: G-06 C08's hotel-cancelled wording with the reason, word for word — "ألغى الفندق هذا الطلب: {reason}" for an Arabic order, "The hotel cancelled this order: {reason}" for an English order. The preview is laid out right-to-left for an Arabic order and left-to-right for an English order, whatever the staff interface direction.
- The combined reason is built by §7.1 and is never longer than 99 characters, inside G-06's 120-character bound, so the guest never sees it cut.

### 5.4 The two buttons (B05, B06)

- **B06 "تأكيد الإلغاء / Confirm cancellation"** is the dark filled primary, at the bottom of the footer, and prints the canonical label it writes on line 2: "الحالة بعد الضغط: ملغى / Status after tapping: Cancelled" (map §4 decision 5).
- **B05 "رجوع / Back"** is outlined, above B06, 16 px apart.
- **Why the confirm is primary and at the bottom here, unlike the guest's M-02.** The guest's cancel sheet puts the safe answer under the thumb because a single mis-tap there cancels. On SM-01 a single mis-tap cannot cancel: the confirm does nothing until a preset has been chosen, and choosing one is a separate, deliberate tap on a row labelled with a reason. The staff member reached this sheet by scrolling past the room, the items and the payment to a button placed at the end of S-02 on purpose (S-02 §5.8), so the forward action gets the primary position.
- **All six controls ignore taps for the first 300 ms** after the sheet starts to appear, so the tap that opened it cannot land on a preset or a button.

### 5.5 The request, and the three outcomes S-02 expects

The request carries: the order number, the target status **Cancelled**, the reason string of §7.1, and the signed-in staff identity. It carries nothing about the guest. **Timeout: 15 seconds** (map §4 decision 20). It is idempotent by target status: a second request for an order already Cancelled changes nothing on the server.

The response is classified into **exactly the three outcomes of S-02 §5.8**, and never into a fourth:

| What the device observed | Outcome handed to S-02 | What SM-01 does |
|---|---|---|
| The order is now **Cancelled** by the hotel, and the staff identity stored with the cancellation is the signed-in staff member (this request, or an earlier attempt of the same staff member whose response was lost) | **cancelled** | Closes within 100 ms, with no message of its own. S-02 renders C05 "ملغى / Cancelled", hides B02 and B03, shows B04, and shows C13 "ألغاه {staff name}: {reason} / Cancelled by {staff name}: {reason}" with the reason the server stored |
| The order is **Cancelled by the guest** | **refused — cancelled**, guest | Closes. S-02 renders the Cancelled state with C02 variant G (S-02 §5.7 rule 5) |
| The order is **Cancelled by the hotel under another staff identity** | **refused — cancelled**, another device | Closes. S-02 renders the Cancelled state with C02 variant H |
| The order is **Delivered** | **refused — delivered** | Closes. S-02 renders the Delivered state with C03 "تغيّرت حالة هذا الطلب من جهاز آخر / This order's status was changed on another device" (S-02 §5.8 outcome 2) |
| Anything else: not sent, connection dropped, no response in 15 seconds, an error response of any kind (including one saying the order does not exist or the session is not valid), an unreadable response, or a readable response carrying a status other than Cancelled or Delivered | **failed** | **Stays open** (§5.6). S-02 is unchanged beneath it |
| A response arriving after the 15-second timeout | Nothing | Discarded. The sheet is already in the failed state and does not change by itself |

- **Why "order does not exist" is *failed* and not a fourth outcome:** S-02 §5.8 defines exactly three outcomes. When the staff member taps "رجوع / Back", S-02's refresh resumes with one immediate request and its own error state (S-02 §6.3) reports an order that no longer exists, with its own words.
- **Why a rejected session is *failed*:** the device closes SM-01 itself at the 12-hour limit (§3.2), so a request with an expired session can only come from a device clock that disagrees with the server's. The failed line is true — the cancellation was not recorded — and the next tap, or the device's own 12-hour check moments later, resolves it.
- **The race is decided by the server** (map §4 decision 8). SM-01 does not check the status before sending.

### 5.6 The failure case

1. The sheet stays open, re-rendered in place with no second slide-up.
2. **C09** appears in the footer above the buttons: "لم يصل الإلغاء إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The cancellation did not reach the system — try again".
3. **The selected preset and the typed line are kept**, so the retry sends the identical reason. B06 returns to its two-line label and is the retry; B05 still closes with nothing changed.
4. Retrying is safe: the request is idempotent by target status, and an attempt that did land is recognised as *cancelled* by the staff identity (§5.5).
5. There is no attempt counter, no retry cap, no automatic retry and no second failure wording.
6. No error is shown on S-02 for a failed cancellation (S-02 §5.8 outcome 3).

### 5.7 What is deliberately not on this modal

- **No room number, no order number, no items, no total, no payment** (§5.1).
- **No "Other" preset and no free-text-only reason** (§5.2): a guest must never read an empty or shorthand reason.
- **No staff name anywhere in what is sent** (§7.1); it appears on S-02-C13 only.
- **No translation control and no auto-translation** of the typed line.
- **No "cancel and re-place", no edit, no un-cancel** (map §4 decision 18; locked decision 5).
- **No close "×"**: B05, the backdrop, browser back, Escape and the swipe are the dismissals.
- **No toast and no confirmation after closing**: S-02's Cancelled state is the report.

## 6. States

### 6.0 Ready (the state on open)

Backdrop; sheet with C01, C02, C03, B01–B04 (none selected), C05, F01 (empty, not focused), C06, C07 "0/60", footer with B05 and B06. C04, C08 and C09 hidden. Focus on C01; the sheet is announced as a dialog. All controls ignore taps for the first 300 ms.

### 6.1 Loading — the cancellation in flight

Triggered by a tap on B06 with a preset selected. Entered **synchronously on the tap**, so a second tap cannot find B06 active.

On screen: every element keeps its content; C09 is hidden if it was shown; B06 is disabled and reads "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; B05 at 50 % opacity and inert; B01–B04 and F01 inert, the keyboard closed; the backdrop, browser back, Escape and the swipe are ignored. No spinner, no countdown.

It ends at the first of a response or the 15-second timeout. **The sheet cannot be dismissed during those at most 15 seconds**, because a *refused* answer arriving after an early close would have nowhere to go and S-02 would keep offering actions on an order that is already Delivered or Cancelled.

### 6.2 Empty

Not reachable as a data state: every value on the sheet is fixed copy or one of the two values S-02 always holds. **Nothing selected** is the ready state, not an empty state. If the order's language is missing from S-02's data, Arabic is used (§7.1), so C06 and C08 always have a value.

### 6.3 Error

Two error cases, both inside the sheet:

| Case | Element | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| B06 tapped with no preset selected | C04 under the presets | اختر سببًا للإلغاء / Choose a reason for cancelling |
| The request ended in the *failed* outcome | C09 in the footer | لم يصل الإلغاء إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The cancellation did not reach the system — try again |

F01 has no error message: it is optional and hard-capped (§7.2).

### 6.4 Success

The *cancelled* outcome. SM-01 and the backdrop are removed within 100 ms, with no checkmark, no message and no toast. S-02 reports it (§5.5). The two *refused* outcomes close the sheet the same way and are likewise reported by S-02.

## 7. Field rules

### 7.1 The reason string sent (referenced by S-02 §5.8)

| Rule | Decision |
|---|---|
| Required | **Yes** — a preset (B01–B04). |
| Language of the preset text | The **order's interface language** as submitted (G-04 §7.8), carried in the order data S-02 holds. The staff interface language plays no part. If the order's language is missing or is neither Arabic nor English, **Arabic** is used (locked decision 7: the default language). |
| Composition | Free line empty after trimming: `{preset}`. Free line not empty: `{preset} — {free line}`, joined by a space, an em dash and a space. |

| Stored and shown | Stored on the order exactly as sent. The guest reads it on G-06 C08 after "ألغى الفندق هذا الطلب: / The hotel cancelled this order:"; staff read it on S-02-C13. Nobody translates it. |

### 7.2 F01 — Short addition for the guest (إضافة قصيرة للضيف / Short addition for the guest)

| Rule | Decision |
|---|---|
| Required | **No.** |

### 7.4 Tap handling

- Every control ignores taps for 300 ms after the sheet starts to appear; after that each responds to a single tap with the 300 ms debounce.
- B06 is disabled for the whole life of its request, so a double tap sends one request.
- After B05, the backdrop, browser back, Escape or the swipe, SM-01 ignores further taps until it is removed, so a double tap cannot reach S-02 beneath.
- Nothing else on the sheet is tappable except C05, which focuses F01.

### 7.5 Language

- Fixed strings render in the staff language saved on the device; there is no toggle on SM-01.
- C06 names the order's language in the staff language. C08 line 2 is in the order's language. The typed line is never translated and never reformatted.

### 7.6 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: C01 18 px bold; B06 line 1 18 px bold; C03, C05, preset labels, F01 text, C08 line 2 and B05 16 px (C03, C05, B05 bold); C02, C04, C06, C07, C08 line 1, C09 and B06 line 2 14 px. **Nothing on SM-01 is smaller than 14 px.**
- Tap areas: each preset row the sheet width × 56 px; F01 × 48 px; B05 × 56 px; B06 × 64 px.
- Contrast: every text at least 4.5:1, including B05 at 50 % opacity. Selection is carried by the radio dot, the border weight and the bold label, never by colour alone.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled; the scrolling area grows and scrolls, and the footer stays pinned.
- Screen reader: on open, the dialog is announced and C01, C02 are read; B01–B04 are a radio group labelled by C03, each announced with its selected state; F01 is announced with C05, C06 and the counter; C08 is announced when it first appears, and line 2 is read in the order's language; C04 and C09 are announced when they appear; "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…" is announced once on entering the in-flight state. S-02 behind the backdrop is not reachable by focus.
- Motion: the 200 ms slide-up is the only movement.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **The four presets are "Item not available", "Room number is wrong or not occupied", "No answer at the room" and "At the guest's request"** (§5.2). The first two are the wordings S-01 §5.8 and S-02 §5.2 already quote; the third is the doorstep case S-02 §5.8 names; the fourth is the path G-06 C16 variant B sends a guest down after acceptance.
2. **Each preset is one bilingual pair: staff read it in their language, the guest receives it in the order's language** (§5.2, §7.1).
3. **The free line complements a preset and never replaces one; there is no "Other"** (§5.2), so a guest never reads an empty or shorthand reason.
4. **The combined string is `{preset} — {free line}` and is at most 99 characters**, so G-06's 120-character cut never reaches it (§7.1).
5. **The order's language falls back to Arabic when missing** (§7.1), the product's default language.
6. **C06 names the language to write in, and C08 previews the guest's exact sentence** before it is sent (§5.3), because G-06 prints it verbatim and never translates.
7. **The confirm is never disabled for a missing reason; the tap shows what is missing** (§4, §7.3) — the same rule as S-02-B02, which is disabled only while its own request is in flight.
8. **Confirm is the primary button, at the bottom, unlike the guest's M-02**, because the required preset makes a single mis-tap unable to cancel anything (§5.4).
9. **The sheet cannot be dismissed while its request is in flight, for at most 15 seconds** (§6.1). Map §3.3 rule 2 says no staff modal is undismissable; this file reads it as "no staff modal traps the staff member at rest", which holds: at rest, five routes close SM-01. A refused answer arriving after an early close would have nowhere to go.
10. **A successful retry is recognised by the staff identity stored with the cancellation**, so a lost response followed by a retry is reported as *cancelled*, not as "cancelled on another device" (§5.5).
11. **"Order does not exist" and a rejected session are *failed*, not new outcomes** (§5.5), because S-02 §5.8 fixes exactly three outcomes and S-02's own refresh and session rules already report both cases.
12. **No room number and no order number on the sheet** (§5.1): it sits on one order's S-02, and a room number here would have to be the largest text on the sheet.
13. **The staff modal conventions are set here** (preamble) — bottom sheet per the guest's M-03, 14 px floor, S-02's button sizes, pinned footer with Back above Confirm, 300 ms arming delay — and SM-02 reuses them.
14. **Recorded difference between the map and S-02, followed in favour of S-02:** the map's SM-01 row (§3.2) says that when the order was already Delivered or already Cancelled the sheet closes and "S-02 re-renders in that status with its banner". S-02 §5.8 outcome 2 specifies C03 — a line, not a banner — for a delivery, and C02 variant G or H — the banner — for a cancellation. This file follows S-02 (§5.5). The map is not edited here.
15. **Cross-file dependency recorded, not applied:** SM-01 needs the order's interface language, which S-02 holds in its order data but does not list in S-02 §7.1 because it never displays it. The order and board responses must carry it (G-04 §7.8 puts it in the submission payload).

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Approved** — reconciled with the built prototype (`staff/js/modals.js`, SM-03 section). Where the prototype does not build something this file requires, the passage says **Not in the prototype — build as specified.**
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 5, 6, 7; `/docs/operations.html` "التوزيع على العمّال" rules 2, 3, 6; `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` Amendment A2 and §4 decisions 5, 7, 8, 10, 20, 23–31; `/spec/staff/screens/S-02.md` Amendment A2 (the action-bar modes, B02 "Accept & assign" / "Assign", B06 "Reassign", C20, and the outcomes this sheet hands back); `/spec/staff/screens/SM-01.md` preamble (the staff modal conventions, reused unchanged); `/spec/worker/worker-map.md` §4 decisions 1, 6, 7.

Conventions reused, not re-decided: the **staff modal conventions of the SM-01 preamble** — bottom sheet over a 50 % black backdrop covering S-02 including S-02-S04, 85 % maximum height, pinned footer with "رجوع / Back" (outlined) above the dark two-line confirm (64 px), 300 ms arming delay, not dismissable while its request is in flight; Western digits; the in-flight label "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; the radio-row design of SM-01-B01; the display-name rule of staff-map §4 decision 30; no toast.

**Why this sheet exists.** The owner's rule is that orders go to the desk first and never straight to a delivery person, and that "there is no accepted order without a worker" (operations.html). So accepting an order and naming who will carry it are one act, and the same list is what moves an order from one worker to another. The sheet asks one question — *who?* — and its confirm button names the answer before it is tapped.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** SM-03
- **Name:** اختيار العامل / Choose the worker
- **Type:** Modal (bottom sheet) on top of **S-02 Order detail**. No URL. It is the only place where an order is accepted, assigned or reassigned.
- **Modes** (fixed on opening from the order's state, never changed while open):
  - **Accept** — the order is **New**. Opened from S-02-B02 "قبول وتحويل / Accept & assign".
  - **Assign** — the order is Accepted & preparing or On the way and has **no** assignee (an order moved forward before assignment existed, or by the prototype's guest demo bar). Opened from S-02-B02 "تحويل لعامل / Assign".
  - **Reassign** — the order is Accepted & preparing or On the way and **has** an assignee. Opened from S-02-B06 "تحويل لعامل آخر / Reassign".
- **Language:** the desk's saved language. No toggle.

---

## 2. Purpose

Choose who will prepare and deliver an order — an on-shift worker or the supervisor — and, in one confirmed tap, accept it to them or move it to them.

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 How the modal opens

| From | Trigger | Mode | What SM-03 receives | What it shows on opening |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S-02 | Tap S-02-B02 "قبول وتحويل / Accept & assign" (status New, live session). The tap sends nothing | Accept | The order as S-02 holds it; the signed-in supervisor | The ready state: C01, C02, C04, the rows (none selected), footer with B03 and B04 |

| S-02 | Tap S-02-B06 "تحويل لعامل آخر / Reassign" (Accepted & preparing or On the way, assignee present) | Reassign | The same, including the current assignee | The same, plus C03; the current assignee's row is marked "المسؤول الآن / Current" and disabled |

While SM-03 is open, S-02's order refresh is paused (S-02 §5.6). The rows follow shift changes live (§5.2).

### 3.2 Where each action leads

| Action | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap an enabled row (B01 or B02) | Stays; that row is selected, any other unselected; C06 and C07 hidden; B04's line 1 names the chosen person |
| Tap a disabled row (off shift, or current) | Nothing happens |
| Tap the confirm (B04) with **no row selected**, or with a selection that is no longer selectable | Stays; nothing sent; the selection is cleared and C06 "اختر عاملًا أو اختر نفسك / Choose a worker or yourself" appears |
| Tap B04 with a valid selection | In-flight state (§6.1): one request. Then by outcome (§5.5): **done** → SM-03 and the backdrop are removed and S-02 renders the result; **refused** → removed, S-02 renders the real state with its banner C02 or line C03; **failed** → stays open with C07 |
| "رجوع / Back" (B03), backdrop, browser back or Escape | Closes; nothing sent; S-02 unchanged; its refresh resumes with one immediate request. **Ignored while a request is in flight** |
| The desk session reaches 12 hours while SM-03 is open and nothing is in flight | Closes as on Back; S-02 shows its session-expired bar |
| Browser reload | Reloads to S-02 for the same order, no modal |

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What SM-03 reads, writes, and never touches

Reads the order S-02 hands it (§3.1), the signed-in supervisor from the desk session, and the worker roster with each worker's shift state (§5.2). Writes nothing to the device. Sends **one** request per confirmed tap: in the accept mode, "accept this order and assign it to this person"; in the assign and reassign modes, "assign this order to this person". **Never** changes the status in the assign and reassign modes; never offers a way to un-assign (an accepted order always has someone — operations.html rule 2); never shows the guest's mobile, items or amounts; never shows any worker's PIN; never signs a worker in or out.

### 5.1 The rows, and their order

- **One B01 row per roster member with the role worker**, whether on shift or not, then **B02 "أنا (المشرف) / Me (supervisor)" last**. Other supervisors are not listed.
- **Order:** first the workers **on shift**, sorted by shift start, **longest on shift first**; then the workers **off shift**, in roster order; then B02.
- **On shift** means the worker is signed in on a worker phone (W-03 started their shift and it has not been ended). The tag reads "في الوردية / On shift".
  - **Not in the prototype — build as specified.** A shift that started 12 or more hours ago counts as off shift (staff-map §4 decision 31).
  - **Not in the prototype — build as specified.** The on-shift tag adds the number of orders the worker holds in Accepted & preparing and On the way, including zero: "في الوردية · طلبات معه الآن: {N} / On shift · Orders in hand: {N}", so the supervisor sees who is free without leaving the sheet.
- **Off shift** rows are shown but disabled, tag "خارج الوردية / Off shift": the supervisor sees the whole team and knows who to phone, and cannot send an order to a phone nobody is holding.
- **Current** (reassign mode): the row of the person who holds the order now is tagged "المسؤول الآن / Current" (with " · خارج الوردية / · Off shift" when that worker is off shift) and disabled, so a reassign cannot be a no-op.
- **B02** is always enabled except when the supervisor is the current holder, so the desk can always take an order itself when no worker is available (operations.html rule 2). Choosing B02 makes the signed-in supervisor the assignee; S-02 then offers the forward steps as its own dark B02 (S-02 Amendment A2, Self mode).
- **No row is pre-selected**, in any mode, even when only one worker is on shift: the choice is always a deliberate tap on a name.

### 5.2 Keeping the list current while the sheet is open

1. The rows are drawn from the roster and the shift record when the sheet opens, and redrawn at once whenever the system reports a change to the staff record (a worker signing in or ending their shift on their phone). A worker who signs in while the sheet is open becomes a selectable on-shift row without closing it. Redrawing is skipped while a request is in flight.
2. When a redraw finds that the selected worker is no longer selectable (they ended their shift), the selection is cleared silently and B04 line 1 returns to its no-choice wording; the next tap on B04 shows C06.
3. **Not in the prototype — build as specified.** In production the roster and shift state come from the server: one request on opening (timeout 10 seconds), then one on every change notification and every 15 seconds as a fallback. While the first request is in flight, the rows area shows three gray placeholder rows, and B02 and the footer already work. If it fails, C08 replaces the rows, B02 stays available, and the fallback keeps retrying; C08 disappears when a list loads. A later failed update leaves the last list unchanged with no message.

### 5.3 The confirm-time check

When B04 is tapped, the device checks the selection against the latest list: if nothing is selected, or the selected person is no longer selectable (a worker who went off shift, or who became the current holder), **nothing is sent**, the selection is cleared, and C06 "اختر عاملًا أو اختر نفسك / Choose a worker or yourself" appears.

### 5.4 The accept mode and the guest's cancellation window

The accept mode opens from S-02, where C16 "بقبولك لن يستطيع الضيف إلغاء الطلب / Once you accept, the guest can no longer cancel it" sits above the button that opened it, and line 2 of B04 prints the status the tap writes. **SM-03 is a choice, not a confirmation**: it asks for the one thing accepting now needs — a name — and adds no "are you sure". Its weight is carried by where it is reached from: S-02, under a 64 px room number and the full item list.

### 5.5 The request, and the outcomes S-02 expects

The request carries the order number, the chosen person's identity and the signed-in supervisor's identity. **Timeout 15 seconds.** The race with a guest cancellation is decided by the system (staff-map §4 decision 8); SM-03 does not check the status before sending. The system also refuses a person who is not on the roster ("invalid"), which the sheet treats as a failure.

| What the system answers | Outcome | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Done: the order is assigned to the chosen person (accept mode: and is now Accepted & preparing) | **done** | Closes, no message. S-02 renders the new state; C20 names the assignee. When the status did not change, the screen reader announces "تم التحويل — المندوب: {name} / Assigned — worker: {name}" |
| Refused, and the order is now Cancelled | **refused — cancelled** | Closes. S-02 renders Cancelled with C02: variant G "ألغى الضيف هذا الطلب / The guest cancelled this order" when the guest cancelled, variant H "أُلغي هذا الطلب من جهاز آخر / This order was cancelled on another device" otherwise |
| Refused, and the order is now Delivered | **refused — delivered** | Closes. S-02 renders Delivered with C03 "تغيّرت حالة هذا الطلب من جهاز آخر / This order's status was changed on another device" |
| Accept mode, refused, but the order is already assigned to the chosen person and accepted by this supervisor (a retry after a lost response) | **done** | As done |
| Refused for any other reason (for example the order was accepted from another desk device) | **refused — changed** | Closes. S-02 renders the real status and assignee with C03 |
| Not sent, dropped, no answer in 15 seconds, "invalid", "not found", unreadable | **failed** | **Stays open** with C07; the selection is kept; B04 is the retry |

A response after the timeout is discarded; the sheet is already in the failed state. On a done reassign, the order leaves the previous worker's phone and appears on the new one at their next list update (worker-map §4 decisions 6, 7), where the previous worker sees W-01-C05.

### 5.6 What is deliberately not on this modal

- **No items, no amounts, no guest mobile**: the sheet sits on S-02, which shows them. The room number appears once, in C02, at body size.
- **No un-assign, no "leave unassigned"**, no status other than the one the accept mode writes.
- **No worker's phone number, PIN, or shift start time.**
- **No search and no sort control**: a hotel's worker list fits one screen; the order is fixed (§5.1).
- **No close "×"**, no toast.

## 6. States

### 6.0 Ready

Backdrop; C01, C02, C03 (reassign mode), C04, C05 when no worker is on shift, the rows (none selected); footer with B03 and B04 (line 1 without a name). C06 and C07 hidden. Focus on C01; announced as a dialog; 300 ms arming delay on every control.

### 6.1 Loading

**The request in flight**: entered synchronously on the tap; B04 disabled and "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; B03 disabled; rows inert; backdrop, browser back and Escape ignored; at most 15 seconds. (**Not in the prototype:** the worker-list loading of §5.2 rule 3.)

### 6.2 Empty

**No worker on shift**: C05 "لا يوجد عامل في الوردية الآن — سجّل دخول المندوب أو اختر نفسك / No worker is on shift — sign a worker in or choose yourself" under C04; the off-shift rows are shown disabled; B02 is available. A roster with no worker at all shows C05 and B02 only.

### 6.3 Error

| Case | Element | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm with nothing (valid) selected | C06 | اختر عاملًا أو اختر نفسك / Choose a worker or yourself |
| The request failed | C07 | لم يصل التحويل إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The assignment did not reach the system — try again |
| The worker list could not be loaded (**not in the prototype**) | C08 | تعذّر تحميل قائمة العمّال — يمكنك اختيار نفسك أو المحاولة بعد لحظات / Could not load the worker list — you can choose yourself or try again in a moment |

### 6.4 Success

The *done* outcome: SM-03 and the backdrop are removed; S-02 reports the result. The *refused* outcomes close the same way and are reported by S-02.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on SM-03. The rows are a single-choice control.

### 7.1 Row selection (B01, B02)

| Rule | Decision |
|---|---|
| Required | **Yes** — exactly one enabled row. |
| Default | None selected, in every mode. |
| Behavior | Radio: one at a time; re-tapping the selected row changes nothing; the selection returns to none only when §5.2 rule 2 or §5.3 clears it. |
| Selectable | On-shift workers who are not the current holder, and B02 unless the supervisor is the current holder. |
| Validation fires | On a tap of B04 (§5.3); a live redraw clears an invalid selection silently (§5.2 rule 2). |
| Error message | C06 — exact copy in §6.3. |

### 7.2 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Worker names (B01) | Roster name | Display-name rule (staff-map §4 decision 30): the part before " / " in Arabic, after it in English, whole when there is no " / "; never truncated; wraps |
| {choice} in B04, {current} in C03 | Roster name and role | Display-name rule; a supervisor gets "(المشرف) / (supervisor)" appended |
| On shift | Shift record | Signed in on a worker phone and not ended (**not in the prototype:** and started less than 12 hours ago) |
| Orders in hand {N} (**not in the prototype**) | Orders assigned to the worker in Accepted & preparing or On the way | Western digits, 0 printed |
| {current status} | The order's status as S-02 holds it | Canonical label, verbatim |

### 7.3 Request bounds

- Assignment request: one per confirmed tap; timeout **15 seconds**; never queued, retried automatically or sent optimistically. A retry of a lost accept is recognised as *done* (§5.5).
- Every request carries the supervisor's identity; nothing about the guest.

### 7.4 Tap handling, language, sizes and accessibility

- Every control ignores taps for 300 ms after the sheet starts to appear; B04 then ignores a second tap within 300 ms and is disabled for the life of its request.
- Fixed strings in the desk language; names by the display-name rule; the status label verbatim.
- Text sizes: C01 and B04 line 1 18 px bold; C03 line 1, C04 and row names 16 px; C02, C03 line 2, C05, row tags, C06, C07 and B04 line 2 14 px. **Nothing smaller than 14 px.** Rows sheet width × at least 56 px; B04 × 64 px.
- Selection is carried by the radio dot, border weight and bold name; disabled rows by a dashed border, muted text and their tag words — never by colour alone.
- Screen reader: the dialog is labelled by C01; the rows are one radio group labelled by C04, each announced with its name and tag, and disabled rows as unavailable; C05 announced as a status; C06 and C07 announced when they appear; S-02 behind the backdrop is not reachable.

20. While the request is in flight, B04 reads "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…", B03 is disabled, rows are inert, and the backdrop, browser back and Escape do nothing; the state ends within 15 seconds.
21. A failed request keeps the sheet open with "لم يصل التحويل إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The assignment did not reach the system — try again" and the selection unchanged; a retry after a lost accept is reported as done.
22. "رجوع / Back", the backdrop, browser back or Escape with nothing in flight close the sheet with nothing sent and S-02 unchanged.
23. Names follow the display-name rule: a roster name "خالد / Khalid" appears as "خالد" in Arabic and "Khalid" in English.
24. No item, amount, guest mobile or worker PIN appears on SM-03.
25. No text is smaller than 14 px; every row is at least 56 px tall; disabled rows are distinguishable in grayscale.
26. **Not in the prototype — build as specified.** An on-shift row reads "في الوردية · طلبات معه الآن: {N} / On shift · Orders in hand: {N}", with N including 0; a worker whose shift started 12 or more hours ago is shown off shift; a failed worker-list load shows C08 with B02 still selectable.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **One sheet, three modes** — accept & assign, assign, reassign — because they are the same question asked at different times (§1).
2. **Select, then confirm with the name on the button**, never tap-a-row-to-send: the list updates live, and a list that sends on tap is how an order goes to the wrong person (§5.1, staff-map §4 decision 10).
3. **No pre-selection, even with one worker on shift** (§5.1).
4. **Longest on shift first, then the rest in roster order; only a change of shift moves a row** (§5.1, §5.2).
5. **Off-shift workers are listed but disabled; the supervisor is always last and always available** (§5.1).
6. **The confirm-time check refuses to send to a worker who just went off shift**, and a live redraw clears such a selection silently (§5.2, §5.3); one error line, C06, covers both "nothing chosen" and "choice no longer valid".
7. **SM-03 is a choice, not a confirmation**: the accept mode adds no "are you sure" (§5.4), keeping staff-map §4 decision 7's reasoning.
8. **The race with the guest is decided by the system**; a refusal is reported by S-02's existing banner and line (§5.5).
9. **"Orders in hand" and the 12-hour shift limit are production requirements not built in the prototype** (§5.1); a failed worker list never blocks the desk, because "Me (supervisor)" stays available (§5.2 rule 3).

# SM-03 — Choose the worker (اختيار العامل)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/staff/staff-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 5, 6, 7; `/docs/operations.html` "التوزيع على العمّال" rules 2, 3, 6; `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` Amendment A2 and §4 decisions 5, 7, 8, 10, 20, 23–31; `/spec/staff/screens/S-02.md` Amendment A2 (B02 in status New, B06 "Reassign", C20 the assignment block, and the outcomes this sheet hands back); `/spec/staff/screens/SM-01.md` preamble (the staff modal conventions, reused unchanged) and §5.5 (how a lost response is recognised on retry); `/spec/worker/worker-map.md` §4 decisions 4, 6, 7, 17.

Conventions reused, not re-decided: the **staff modal conventions of the SM-01 preamble** — bottom sheet over a 50 % black backdrop covering S-02 including S-02-S04, 85 % maximum height, 14 px text floor, pinned footer with "رجوع / Back" (outlined, 56 px) above the dark two-line confirm (64 px), 16 px between them, 300 ms arming delay, not dismissable while its request is in flight; Western digits; the in-flight label "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; the radio-row design of SM-01-B01; the display-name rule of staff-map §4 decision 30; no toast.

**Why this sheet exists.** The owner's rule is that orders go to the desk first and never straight to a delivery person, and that "there is no accepted order without a worker" (operations.html). So accepting an order and naming who will carry it are one act, and the same list is what moves an order from one worker to another. The sheet asks one question — *who?* — and its confirm button names the answer before it is tapped.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** SM-03
- **Name:** اختيار العامل / Choose the worker
- **Type:** Modal (bottom sheet) on top of **S-02 Order detail**. No URL, no history entry. It is the only place where an order is accepted, assigned or reassigned.
- **Modes** (fixed on opening, never changed while open):
  - **Mode A — Accept & assign.** Opened from S-02-B02 "قبول وتحويل / Accept & assign" in status **New**.
  - **Mode R — Reassign.** Opened from S-02-B06 "تحويل لعامل آخر / Reassign" in status Accepted & preparing or On the way, when the order has an assignee.
  - **Mode R0 — Assign.** Opened from S-02-B06 "تحويل لعامل / Assign to a worker" in status Accepted & preparing or On the way, when the order has **no** assignee (an order moved forward before assignment existed, or by the prototype's demo bar).
- **Language:** the desk's saved language. No toggle.

---

## 2. Purpose

Choose who will prepare and deliver an order — an on-shift worker or the supervisor — and, in one confirmed tap, accept it to them or move it to them.

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 How the modal opens

| From | Trigger | Mode | What SM-03 receives | What it shows on opening |

On opening SM-03 requests the worker list once (§5.2); while it is open, S-02's order refresh is paused (S-02 §5.6 rule 5), and the worker list keeps updating (§5.2).

### 3.2 Where each action leads

| Action | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap an enabled row (B01 or B02) | Stays; that row is selected, any other unselected; C05 and C06 hidden; B04's line 1 names the chosen person |
| Tap a disabled row (off shift, or current) | Nothing happens |
| Tap the confirm (B04) with **no row selected** | Stays; nothing sent; C05 "اختر من سيوصل الطلب / Choose who will deliver the order" appears and the rows scroll into view |
| Tap B04 with a row selected whose person is **no longer on shift** in the latest list | Stays; nothing sent; the selection is cleared and C06 appears (§5.3) |
| Tap B04 with a valid selection | In-flight state (§6.1): one request. Then by outcome (§5.5): **done** → SM-03 and the backdrop are removed and S-02 renders the result; **refused** → removed, S-02 renders the real state with its banner or line; **failed** → stays open with C07 |
| "رجوع / Back" (B03), backdrop, browser back, Escape, or a downward swipe on the sheet's top 24 px | Closes; nothing sent; S-02 unchanged; its refresh resumes with one immediate request. **Ignored while a request is in flight** |
| The desk session reaches 12 hours while SM-03 is open and nothing is in flight | Closes as on Back; S-02 shows its session-expired bar |
| Browser reload | Reloads to S-02 for the same order, no modal |

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What SM-03 reads, writes, and never touches

Reads what S-02 hands it (§3.1), the signed-in supervisor from the desk session, and the worker list (§5.2). Writes nothing to the device. Sends **one** request per confirmed tap: in mode A, "accept this order and assign it to this person"; in modes R and R0, "assign this order to this person". **Never** changes the status in modes R and R0; offers a way to un-assign (an accepted order always has someone — operations.html rule 2); shows the guest's mobile, items or amounts; shows any worker's PIN; signs a worker in or out.

### 5.1 The rows, and their order

- **One B01 row per worker on the roster** (role worker), whether on shift or not, then **B02 "أنا (المشرف) / Me (supervisor)" last**. Other supervisors are not listed.
- **Order:** first the workers **on shift**, then the workers **off shift**, each group in roster order (the order the hotel's staff list stores them in, which never changes by itself), then B02. Roster order is used instead of name or load so that rows never re-sort because a count changed.
- **On shift** means: the worker is signed in on a worker phone and the shift started less than **12 hours** ago (staff-map §4 decision 31). An on-shift row shows "في الوردية · طلبات معه الآن: {N} / On shift · Orders in hand: {N}", where {N} counts that worker's orders in Accepted & preparing and On the way, **including zero**, so the supervisor can see who is free without leaving the sheet.
- **Off shift** rows are shown but disabled, with "خارج الوردية — لم يسجّل دخوله / Off shift — not signed in": the supervisor sees the whole team and knows who to phone, and cannot send an order to a phone nobody is holding.
- **Current** (mode R): the row of the person who holds the order now is marked and disabled, so a reassign cannot be a no-op.
- **B02** is always enabled (except as current), so the desk can always take an order itself when no worker is available (operations.html rule 2). Choosing B02 makes the signed-in supervisor the assignee; the forward actions then appear on S-02 as the supervisor's own primary button (S-02 Amendment A2).
- **No row is pre-selected**, in any mode, even when only one worker is on shift: the choice is always a deliberate tap on a name (the SM-01 preset rule).

### 5.2 Keeping the list current while the sheet is open

1. On opening, one request for the worker list (roster, shift state, orders in hand). Until it answers — at most 10 seconds — the rows area shows three gray placeholder rows pulsing 100 %–60 % every 1.5 seconds, B02 is already shown, and B04 works (B02 can be chosen at once).
2. Then an update on every change notification from the system, and as a fallback every **15 seconds** from the end of the previous attempt, timeout **10 seconds** — the board's cadence (S-01 §5.5). A worker who signs in while the sheet is open becomes selectable without closing it.
3. **Rows never move under a finger**: an update that arrives during a touch is applied when the touch ends (staff-map §4 decision 10). A worker whose shift starts moves from the off-shift group to the end of the on-shift group at that update.
4. If the first request fails, the placeholder rows are replaced by **C03** "تعذّر تحميل قائمة العمّال — يمكنك اختيار نفسك أو المحاولة بعد لحظات / Could not load the worker list — you can choose yourself or try again in a moment", B02 stays available, and the fallback cadence keeps retrying; C03 disappears when a list loads. If a later update fails, the last list stays unchanged with no message: the confirm-time check (§5.3) protects the choice.

### 5.3 The confirm-time check

When B04 is tapped with a worker selected, the device checks the latest list: if that worker is no longer on shift, **nothing is sent**, the selection is cleared, and C06 "خرج {name} من الوردية — اختر غيره / {name} is no longer on shift — choose someone else" appears. An update that finds the selected worker off shift clears the selection and shows C06 at once, without waiting for the tap. B02 is never subject to this check.

### 5.4 Mode A and the guest's cancellation window

The mode A context line carries staff-map §4 decision 7's sentence, "بقبولك لن يستطيع الضيف إلغاء الطلب / Once you accept, the guest can no longer cancel it", and line 2 of B04 prints the status the tap writes. **SM-03 is a choice, not a confirmation**: it asks for the one thing accepting now needs — a name — and adds no "are you sure". Its weight is carried, as before, by where it is reached from: S-02, under a 64 px room number and the full item list.

### 5.5 The request, and the outcomes S-02 expects

The request carries the order number, the chosen person's identity, the signed-in supervisor's identity, and — in mode A — the target status Accepted & preparing. **Timeout 15 seconds.** The race with a guest cancellation is decided by the system (staff-map §4 decision 8); SM-03 does not check the status before sending.

**Mode A — Accept & assign**

| What the device observed | Outcome | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| The order is now Accepted & preparing (or later), assigned to the chosen person, accepted by the signed-in supervisor — by this request, or by an earlier attempt whose response was lost | **done** | Closes within 100 ms, no message. S-02 renders "تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير / Accepted & preparing" with C20 naming the assignee |
| The order is Cancelled by the guest | **refused — guest cancelled** | Closes. S-02 renders Cancelled with C02 variant G (S-02 §5.7 rule 5) |
| The order is Cancelled by the hotel | **refused — cancelled elsewhere** | Closes. S-02 renders Cancelled with C02 variant H |
| The order is no longer New and was accepted by someone else, or to someone else | **refused — moved on** | Closes. S-02 renders the real status and assignee with C03 "تغيّرت حالة هذا الطلب من جهاز آخر / This order's status was changed on another device" |
| Anything else: not sent, dropped, no answer in 15 seconds, an error (including "no such order" and "no such member"), unreadable | **failed** | **Stays open** with C07; the selection is kept; B04 is the retry |

**Modes R and R0 — Reassign / Assign**

| What the device observed | Outcome | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| The order is Accepted & preparing or On the way and assigned to the chosen person (by this request, a lost earlier one, or another desk device choosing the same person) | **done** | Closes within 100 ms, no message. S-02's C20 names the new assignee; the status is unchanged. The order leaves the previous worker's phone and appears on the new one at their next update (worker-map §4 decisions 6, 7) |
| The order is Delivered | **refused — delivered** | Closes. S-02 renders Delivered with C03 |
| The order is Cancelled | **refused — cancelled** | Closes. S-02 renders Cancelled with C02 variant H |
| Anything else | **failed** | Stays open with C07; selection kept |

A response after the timeout is discarded; the sheet is already in the failed state.

### 5.6 What is deliberately not on this modal

- **No room number, no items, no amounts, no guest mobile**: the sheet sits on S-02, which shows them (the SM-01 §5.1 rule).
- **No un-assign, no "leave unassigned"**, no status other than the one mode A writes.
- **No worker's phone number, PIN, or shift start time.**
- **No search and no sort control**: a hotel's worker list fits one screen; order is fixed (§5.1).
- **No close "×"**, no toast.

## 6. States

### 6.0 Ready

Backdrop; C01, C02, rows (none selected), C04 when it applies; footer with B03 and B04 (line 1 without a name). C05, C06, C07 hidden. Focus on C01; announced as a dialog; 300 ms arming delay on every control.

### 6.1 Loading

Two kinds: **the worker list loading** (§5.2 rule 1 — placeholder rows, B02 and the footer usable), and **the request in flight**: entered synchronously on the tap; B04 disabled and "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; B03 at 50 % and inert; rows inert; backdrop, browser back, Escape and swipe ignored; at most 15 seconds.

### 6.2 Empty

**No worker on shift** (or, in mode R, none but the current assignee): C04 "لا يوجد عامل في الوردية الآن — سجّل دخول المندوب أو اختر نفسك / No worker is on shift — sign a worker in or choose yourself" above the rows; the off-shift rows are shown disabled; B02 is available. A roster with no worker at all shows C04 and B02 only.

### 6.3 Error

| Case | Element | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm with nothing selected | C05 | اختر من سيوصل الطلب / Choose who will deliver the order |
| The selected worker went off shift | C06 | خرج {name} من الوردية — اختر غيره / {name} is no longer on shift — choose someone else |
| The request failed | C07 | لم يصل التحويل إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The assignment did not reach the system — try again |
| The worker list could not be loaded | C03 | تعذّر تحميل قائمة العمّال — يمكنك اختيار نفسك أو المحاولة بعد لحظات / Could not load the worker list — you can choose yourself or try again in a moment |

### 6.4 Success

The *done* outcome: SM-03 and the backdrop are removed within 100 ms; S-02 reports the result. The *refused* outcomes close the same way and are reported by S-02.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on SM-03. The rows are a single-choice control:

### 7.1 Row selection (B01, B02)

| Rule | Decision |
|---|---|
| Required | **Yes** — exactly one enabled row. |
| Default | None selected, in every mode. |
| Behavior | Radio: one at a time; re-tapping the selected row changes nothing; no way back to none except §5.3 clearing it. |
| Selectable | On-shift workers (not current in mode R) and B02 (not current in mode R). |
| Validation fires | On a tap of B04, and on every list update (§5.3). |
| Error messages | C05 (nothing selected), C06 (selection went off shift) — exact copy in §6.3. |

### 7.2 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Worker names (B01 line 1, C02 {current}, C06 {name}, B04 {choice}) | Roster name | Display-name rule (staff-map §4 decision 30): the part before " / " in Arabic, after it in English, whole when there is no " / "; never truncated; wraps to a second line when long |
| On shift | Shift record | Signed in, started less than 12 hours ago |
| Orders in hand {N} | Orders assigned to the worker in Accepted & preparing or On the way | Western digits, 0 printed |
| {current status} | The order's status as S-02 holds it | Canonical label, verbatim |

### 7.3 Request bounds

- Worker list: on open, on each change notification, and every 15 seconds; timeout 10 seconds; stops when SM-03 closes.
- Assignment request: one per confirmed tap; timeout **15 seconds**; never queued, retried automatically or sent optimistically. A retry of a lost success is recognised as *done* (§5.5).
- Every request carries the supervisor's identity and the device's hotel link; nothing about the guest.

### 7.4 Tap handling, language, sizes and accessibility

- Every control ignores taps for 300 ms after the sheet starts to appear, then responds to a single tap with the 300 ms debounce. B04 is disabled for the life of its request. After a dismissal, further taps are ignored until the sheet is removed.
- Fixed strings in the desk language; names by the display-name rule; the status label verbatim.
- Text sizes: C01 and B04 line 1 18 px bold; row line 1, C04 and B03 16 px; C02, row line 2, C05, C06, C07 and B04 line 2 14 px. **Nothing smaller than 14 px.** Rows sheet width × at least 64 px; B03 × 56 px; B04 × 64 px.
- Contrast at least 4.5:1 everywhere, including disabled rows and B03 at 50 %. Selection is carried by the radio dot, border weight and bold name; disabled rows by a dashed border, the missing radio circle and their line 2 words — never by colour alone.
- Screen reader: the dialog, C01, C02, then C04 when present, then the rows as one radio group labelled by C01, each announced with its name, its line 2 and "غير متاح / unavailable" when disabled; C05, C06, C07 announced when they appear; S-02 behind the backdrop not reachable.
- Motion: the 200 ms slide-up only; rows never animate.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. SM-03 opens in mode A only from S-02-B02 "قبول وتحويل / Accept & assign" in status New, and in mode R or R0 only from S-02-B06 in Accepted & preparing or On the way; opening sends no status or assignment request.
2. The title reads "قبول وتحويل / Accept & assign" in mode A, "تحويل لعامل آخر / Reassign" in mode R, and "تحويل لعامل / Assign to a worker" in mode R0.
3. Every roster worker has a row; workers on shift come first, then workers off shift, each in roster order; "أنا (المشرف) / Me (supervisor)" is the last row.
4. An on-shift row reads "في الوردية · طلبات معه الآن: {N} / On shift · Orders in hand: {N}", with N including 0.
5. An off-shift row reads "خارج الوردية — لم يسجّل دخوله / Off shift — not signed in", has a dashed border and no radio circle, and a tap on it does nothing.
6. A worker whose shift started 12 or more hours ago is shown off shift even if they never signed out.
7. With no worker on shift, C04 "لا يوجد عامل في الوردية الآن — سجّل دخول المندوب أو اختر نفسك / No worker is on shift — sign a worker in or choose yourself" is shown and "أنا (المشرف) / Me (supervisor)" is selectable.
8. No row is selected on opening, in any mode.
9. Tapping B04 with nothing selected sends nothing and shows "اختر من سيوصل الطلب / Choose who will deliver the order".
10. After choosing Khalid in mode A, B04 reads "قبول وتحويل — خالد / Accept & assign — Khalid" on line 1 and "الحالة بعد الضغط: تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير / Status after tapping: Accepted & preparing" on line 2.
11. In mode R, the current assignee's row reads "الحالي — الطلب معه الآن / Current — has this order now" and cannot be selected; B04 line 2 reads "الحالة لا تتغيّر: {current status} / Status stays: {current status}".

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **One sheet, three modes** — accept & assign, reassign, assign — because they are the same question asked at different times (§1).
2. **Select, then confirm with the name on the button**, never tap-a-row-to-send: the list updates live, and a list that sends on tap is how an order goes to the wrong person (§5.1, staff-map §4 decision 10).
3. **No pre-selection, even with one worker on shift** (§5.1).
4. **Roster order inside each group; only a change of shift moves a row** (§5.1, §5.2).
5. **"Orders in hand" on every on-shift row, including zero** (§5.1) — the only load information the supervisor needs to choose.
6. **Off-shift workers are listed but disabled; the supervisor is always last and always available** (§5.1).
7. **The confirm-time check refuses to send to a worker who just went off shift** (§5.3).
8. **SM-03 is a choice, not a confirmation**: mode A adds no "are you sure" (§5.4), keeping staff-map §4 decision 7's reasoning.
9. **The race with the guest is decided by the system**; a refusal is reported by S-02's existing banner and line (§5.5).
10. **A failed worker list never blocks the desk**: "Me (supervisor)" stays available (§5.2 rule 4).

# S-01 — Orders board (لوحة الطلبات)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/staff/staff-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 3, 5, 6, 7; `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` §4 decisions 1–22; `/spec/screens/G-01.md` §5.2 (the five canonical status labels, verbatim), §7.2 (currency and digits), §7.3 (item-count wording), §7.6 (44 × 44 px touch targets); `/spec/screens/G-06.md` §5.6 (the guest's side of the cancellation race).

Conventions reused verbatim from the guest specification, not re-decided here: Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label from store settings, after the amount in Arabic ("45.00 ر.س") and before it in English ("SAR 45.00"), two decimals always; the item-count wording of G-01 §7.3; every tappable element has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px; browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled; no toast anywhere.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** S-01
- **Name:** لوحة الطلبات / Orders board
- **Type:** Full staff screen with its own URL. It is the landing screen of the staff interface: a device with a live session that opens the staff URL lands here, not on S-03.
- **Language:** Arabic (RTL) on first open regardless of browser language; the header toggle switches to English (LTR) in place, and the choice is saved on the device (map §4 decision 21).
- **Viewport:** specified at 390 px wide (the guest specification's basis). At 768 px and above the card list becomes two columns with a 24 px gutter; the header, the tab bar and every text size are unchanged, and the room number stays 40 px in both layouts.

---

## 2. Purpose

Show every order that still needs a staff member, oldest first, with the room number as the largest thing on each card, and make a new order impossible to miss.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 Entry (how the staff member arrives)

| From | Trigger | What S-01 shows on arrival |
|---|---|---|
| S-03 Staff sign-in | A correct PIN | Active tab, top of the list, first board request already sent; this response is the chime baseline (§5.6 rule 3), so a queue that existed before sign-in never chimes |
| S-02 Order detail | Back arrow, browser back, or "العودة إلى اللوحة / Back to the board" | The tab and the scroll position the staff member left (map §3.3 rule 3), re-rendered from the last board response, with one immediate refresh request |
| The staff URL on a device with a live session | Browser load or reload | Active tab, top of the list, loading state (§6.1) until the first board response; new chime baseline |
| The staff URL on a device with no live session, or a session past 12 hours | Browser load | **S-03**, not S-01 (§6.6 covers the case where the 12 hours elapse while S-01 is already open) |

### 3.2 Exit (where each action leads)

| Action on S-01 | Destination |
|---|---|
| Tap an order card (C04) | **S-02** for that order, pushed above S-01 |
| Tap the Active tab (B04) or the Done today tab (B05) | Stays on S-01; the list area is replaced with that tab's content, scrolled to the top; no request is sent (both lists come from the same board response, §5.5) |
| Tap the language toggle (B01) | Stays on S-01; the whole screen re-renders in the other language and direction, same tab, same scroll position, same sound setting |
| Tap the sound toggle (B02) | Stays on S-01; sound is turned on or off and the button's label states the new setting |
| Tap "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" (B03) | **S-03**, with the shift ended on this device |
| Tap "تسجيل الدخول للمتابعة / Sign in to continue" (B06) | **S-03**, keeping the board's tab and scroll position for the return |
| Tap "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" (B07) | Stays on S-01; a board request is sent and the loading state is shown again |

S-01 has no back arrow, and a browser back gesture on S-01 leaves the staff interface.

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What S-01 reads, writes, and never touches

S-01 renders **only** from the most recent successful board response plus three device-held values: the language, the sound setting, and the live session (staff name and sign-in time). It holds no order data of its own between sessions.

S-01 **writes** nothing to the server. It sends one kind of request — "give me the board" — and no status change, no cancellation and no acceptance ever originates here (map §4 decision 6).

S-01 **never**: shows a guest name, a phone number or any guest identifier (locked decision 4, map §4 decision 19); shows prices per unit, stock levels or catalog data; edits anything; deletes anything.

### 5.1 Header (S01) and tab bar (S02)

Layout, in visual order for Arabic (RTL): [C01 shift line] … [B02 sound toggle] [B01 language toggle] [B03 End shift]. In English the row mirrors with the direction; nothing else changes.

- C01 states who is on shift, so a staff member can see at a glance whose name every action is being recorded under (map §4 decision 2). A name longer than 18 characters is cut at 18 and closed with "…".
- B02 always writes the sound state in words. There is no icon-only speaker and no silent mute: a board that cannot chime must say so (map §4 decision 14).
- The tab bar holds exactly two tabs and no third. Each prints its own count, including zero.

### 5.2 The order card (C04)

The card is one tappable block. Its contents, top to bottom:

1. **Room number** — at the start edge, **40 px bold**, letter-spacing 0.06 em, Western digits, leading zeros kept ("0305" stays "0305"), laid out left-to-right in both languages, never wrapped, never shrunk, never truncated. It is the largest text on the board and no other text on the card is larger than 16 px, so it is never the second-largest number on its own line (map §4 decision 4). It is preceded by the word "غرفة" / "Room" at 14 px regular on the same line, before the number in both languages.
2. **Elapsed time** — at the end edge of the same line, 14 px, the wording of §7.2, recomputed every 30 seconds with no request.
3. **Order number** — second line, 14 px: "طلب رقم {order number}" / "Order {order number}", printed exactly as the guest sees it on G-05, G-06 and G-07.
4. **Status label** — on the same line as the order number, at the end edge, 14 px, one of the canonical labels of G-01 §5.2. **Shown only in the Done today tab**, where Delivered and Cancelled rows are mixed; in the Active tab the group heading (C03) already names the status, and repeating it on every card is noise.
5. **Order summary** — third line, 16 px: "{item count} · {total}" — the item count in the exact wording of G-01 §7.3 ("منتج واحد / 1 item", "منتجان / 2 items", "{N} منتجات / {N} items", "{N} منتجًا / {N} items") and the order total with two decimals and the currency label in the position fixed by G-01 §7.2.
6. **Chip row** — fourth line, 8 px above it, chips in this fixed order with an 8 px gap: C05 "متأخر / Late" (when it applies), C06 payment, C07 "ملاحظات / Notes" (when notes exist). The payment chip carries the change to bring when it is known, so a staff member can fill a pocket before leaving the desk: "نقدًا — باقي 155.00 ر.س" / "Cash — SAR 155.00 change" (map §4 decision 17).
7. **C08**, when it applies, replaces the chip row entirely (§5.7).

Card height is whatever these four lines need (about 132 px at 390 px width) and never less than 44 px. The whole card is the tap target.

### 5.3 The Active tab (B04) — grouping and order

The Active tab holds every order whose status is New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way, in **three groups in this fixed order**:

1. جديد / **New**
2. تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير / **Accepted & preparing**
3. في الطريق / **On the way**

Rules:

- The order of the groups never changes and never depends on their counts. New is first because it is the only group with a deadline attached to it.
- A group with zero orders renders **nothing at all** — no heading, no "no orders in this group" line, no gap.
- Within a group, orders are sorted by order time, **oldest first** (map §4 decision 10). A newly arrived order therefore appears at the bottom of the New group and pushes no existing card.
- A card **never changes position or disappears while a touch is in progress**: a refresh that arrives during a touch is applied when the touch ends.
- An order that changes status between refreshes moves to its new group at that refresh, keeping the oldest-first rule in its new group.
- There is no filter, no sort control, no search and no "assigned to me": the board shows the whole hotel's queue to whoever is on shift.

### 5.4 The Done today tab (B05)

- Holds every order that reached **Delivered** or **Cancelled** since the most recent **04:00 local time** (map §4 decision 9). At 04:00 the tab empties itself at the next refresh; no order is deleted anywhere, it simply leaves this list.
- Sorted **newest first** by the time the order reached its final status. If the board response carries no such time for a row, the order time is used instead, and the row is still placed by that value with no marker of any kind.
- Capped at **50 rows**. When more exist, the 50 newest are shown and the tab count reads "50"; nothing announces the cap, because a staff member looking further back is doing a task this interface does not serve in version 1.
- Each row is the same C04 card, with the status label (§5.2 item 4) shown and C05 "Late" never shown. Tapping it opens S-02, where a finished order is read-only apart from its "Back to the board" button.
- This tab is how a staff member answers "the guest in 512 says they ordered twenty minutes ago": the room number is the largest thing on every card here too.

### 5.5 Refreshing the board — the numbers

1. **On open:** one board request, sent as soon as the screen is drawn.
2. **Then every 15 seconds**, measured from the moment the previous attempt ended (a response arrived or its timeout elapsed), so two board requests are never in flight at once (map §4 decision 11).
3. **Timeout: 10 seconds.** An attempt that has not answered in 10 seconds is a failure; a response arriving after that is discarded.
4. **One response feeds both tabs.** The board request returns the active orders and the done-today rows together, so switching tabs sends nothing and is instant.
5. **Polling pauses** while the browser tab is hidden (backgrounded, another tab, the device's screen off) and resumes with **one immediate request** when it returns to the foreground. Nothing else changes while it is paused; the board keeps what it last drew, and C02 appears if the last attempt had failed.
6. **On success:** the lists are rebuilt, C02 is hidden, and the chime rules of §5.6 run. The rebuild keeps the scroll position exactly and respects the touch rule of §5.3.
7. **On failure:** the last successful lists stay on screen and **C02** is shown with the time since the last success (§7.2). After **2 minutes** without a successful refresh, C02 becomes a dark-filled bar with light text. The board keeps retrying every 15 seconds and heals itself when the connection returns; there is no retry button in this state, because the retry is already happening (map §4 decision 12).
8. **No pull-to-refresh and no manual refresh button** while data exists. B07 "Retry" exists only in the cold-load failure state (§6.3), where there is nothing on screen to keep.

### 5.6 The new-order alert (map §4 decision 14)

1. **The chime** is a single tone of **2 seconds**, played at the device's media volume. There is exactly one sound in this interface; nothing else on any staff screen makes a noise.
2. **When it plays:** on any successful board response that contains at least one order in status New whose order number was not present in the previous successful response of this session. Several new orders in one response play **one** chime, not one per order.
3. **The baseline.** The first successful response after a sign-in, a reload, or a return from a hidden tab establishes the baseline and plays **no** chime, so a queue that was already waiting never produces a chime storm at sign-in.
4. **The repeat:** while at least one order is in status New and is **Late** (placed more than 5 minutes ago, C05), the chime repeats every **60 seconds** until no Late New order remains. This is the only repeating alert.
5. **Muting** is by B02 only, is saved on the device, and is stated in words in the header at all times, in the empty state (C09 line 3), and nowhere else.
6. **When the browser refuses to play audio** (no user gesture since the page loaded — the usual cause is a reload), no sound is attempted again until the staff member taps B02, and B02 reads "الصوت: متوقف (انقر للتفعيل) / Sound: off (tap to enable)". The tap that signs in on S-03 is what arms audio in the normal flow (S-03 §5.4).
7. **No browser notification, no push, no vibration, no flashing, no full-screen takeover.** Version 1 requires the staff device to stay awake with the board in the foreground, and the map records the alternative as deferred.

### 5.7 The cancellation race, seen from the board (map §4 decision 8)

The guest may cancel at any moment while the status is New (locked decision 6), including between the refresh a staff member read and the tap they were about to make. The board never hides this and never guesses:

- When a refresh finds that an order which was in the Active list is now **Cancelled with the guest as the canceller**, the card stays **exactly where it is** and is re-rendered with C08 — "ألغى الضيف هذا الطلب — لا تُحضّره / The guest cancelled this order — do not prepare it" — at 60 % opacity with a dashed border, for **60 seconds** from that refresh.
- After 60 seconds it leaves the Active tab at the next refresh and appears in Done today as a Cancelled row. The 60 seconds are counted so that a staff member who glanced at the board and walked to the store room comes back to an answer instead of an absence.
- The card stays tappable throughout, and opening it shows S-02's Cancelled state with the same sentence (S-02 §5.7).
- An order cancelled **by the hotel** (through SM-01, by this staff member or another) leaves the Active list at the next refresh with **no** C08 marker, because the person who did it knows and S-02 already showed them the result.
- The board never blocks, dims or pre-disables a card because a cancellation might be in flight: the server decides the race when the action is sent (map §4 decision 8), and the only screen that can send one is S-02.

### 5.8 What is deliberately not on this screen

- **No action buttons on a card**: no accept, no "on the way", no delivered, no cancel, no swipe, no long-press (map §4 decision 6).
- **No search, no filter, no sort control, no date picker.**
- **No assignment**: no "take this order", no staff picker, no "mine / all" switch. The hotel runs one queue in version 1.
- **No counts of anything but orders**: no revenue line, no shift totals, no averages. Reporting is the admin dashboard, which is out of scope.
- **No product or stock control**: a product that ran out is handled by cancelling the order with the "المنتج غير متوفر / Item not available" reason in SM-01.
- **No guest identity, no contact control, no message to the guest**: the hotel's channel to a room is the room phone, and this interface does not dial it.
- **No toast** (guest convention, kept). No modal opens from S-01 at all.
- **No unread badge that must be cleared**: an order leaves the New group by being accepted, never by being looked at.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

Triggered by: the first board request after a sign-in, a browser reload, or B07 "Retry".

On screen: S01 header complete and fully functional (B01, B02, B03 all work); S02 tab bar with both tabs and their counts replaced by "—" until the first response; C12's four placeholder cards in the list area; no text in the list area; C02 hidden.

Rules: the loading state ends when the first response arrives, and never lasts longer than the 10-second timeout (§5.5 rule 3). On a timeout or a failure with no previous successful response, the screen goes to §6.3.

### 6.2 Empty

Two distinct empty states, one per tab, and neither is an error.

**Active tab empty** (no order in New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way) — C09, centered, 48 px below the tab bar:
- Line 1 (20 px, bold): "لا توجد طلبات الآن" / "No orders right now"
- Line 2 (16 px): "ستصلك نغمة عند وصول طلب جديد" / "A chime will sound when a new order arrives"
- Line 3 (14 px, **only while the sound is off**): "الصوت متوقف الآن — لن تسمع النغمة" / "Sound is off — you will not hear the chime"

The board keeps refreshing every 15 seconds in this state; the empty state is not a stopping point.

**Done today tab empty** — C10, same position:
- Line 1 (20 px, bold): "لم يكتمل أي طلب اليوم بعد" / "No orders have been completed today yet"
- Line 2 (16 px): "تظهر هنا الطلبات التي وُصِّلت أو أُلغيت منذ الساعة 04:00" / "Orders delivered or cancelled since 04:00 appear here"

### 6.3 Error

**One rendered error state: the cold-load failure.** It is shown when **no board response has ever succeeded in this session** and the most recent request failed, timed out, or was unreadable.

On screen: S01 header; S02 tab bar with both counts as "—" and both tabs inert; C13's three lines centered; B07 "إعادة المحاولة / Retry" 24 px beneath:
- Line 1 (20 px, bold): "تعذّر تحميل اللوحة" / "Could not load the board"
- Line 2 (16 px): "تحقّق من الاتصال بالشبكة" / "Check the network connection"
- Line 3 (14 px): "إذا تكرّر ذلك، أبلغ المناوب المسؤول" / "If this keeps happening, tell the duty manager"

Automatic retries continue every 15 seconds in this state as well, so the screen heals without a tap; B07 exists because a staff member watching a failed board must be able to do something with their hands.

**Failures that are not this state:** a failed refresh when a previous response succeeded is **not** an error state — the last known board stays on screen with C02 (§5.5 rule 7). A rejected action is never an S-01 state, because S-01 sends no actions.

There is no typed input on S-01, so there are no field error messages.

### 6.4 Success

A board response arrived. The Active tab shows its non-empty groups with their cards; the Done today tab shows its rows; C02 is hidden; the tab counts are numbers.

Update feedback when a refresh changes something: the lists repaint within 100 ms, with no animation, no highlight and no scroll change. The only feedback that a new order arrived is the chime (§5.6), the New group's count, and the card itself.

### 6.5 Sound-off

Not a separate layout: B02 states it, and C09 line 3 states it in the empty state. Everything else behaves identically.

### 6.6 Session expired (12 hours after sign-in)

The board **keeps working as a board**: it keeps refreshing every 15 seconds, keeps chiming, and keeps showing every card (map §4 decision 3). What changes:

- C11 appears under the tab bar with its two lines and B06 "تسجيل الدخول للمتابعة / Sign in to continue".
- C01 reads "الوردية منتهية / Shift ended" and carries no name.
- B03 "End shift" is hidden (there is no shift left to end); B06 stands in its place as the way back to S-03.
- Cards remain tappable and S-02 still opens, read-only: S-02 shows its own sign-in bar in place of the action bar (S-02 §6.5), so no status change, cancellation or delivery can be recorded without a name attached to it.
- Nothing is lost by signing in again: S-03 returns to this tab and scroll position (§3.2).

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on S-01 and no field element exists. The rules below govern the values it displays and the requests it makes.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Room number (C04) | Board response, the order's room number string | Printed byte for byte with leading zeros kept, 1–5 Western digits (G-04 §7.1 as amended), 40 px bold, never wrapped, never shrunk, never re-validated. It is never checked against a list of real rooms anywhere in this product |
| Order number (C04) | Board response | Exactly as stored; no "#", no thousands separator, no padding, identical to what the guest sees |
| Status label (C03, C04) | Board response | Always one of the five canonical labels of G-01 §5.2, verbatim in the current language. A status outside those five makes the row unrenderable, and the row is skipped with no error text and no gap |
| Item count (C04) | Board response, the sum of line quantities | The wording of G-01 §7.3, verbatim |
| Total (C04) | Board response, the order's stored total | Printed as stored, two decimals, currency per G-01 §7.2. **Never recomputed** from the lines — it is the amount the guest agreed to pay and staff collect |
| Payment and change (C06) | Board response, payment method and the guest's stated amount | §7.3 |
| Elapsed time (C04) | The order time in the board response, against the device clock | §7.2; recomputed every 30 seconds with no request |
| Notes presence (C07) | Board response, notes | The chip is shown when the notes are a non-empty, non-whitespace string. **The board never prints the notes themselves** — they are read in full on S-02, where there is room for them |
| Staff name (C01) | The live session created on S-03 | Cut at 18 characters with "…" |

### 7.2 Elapsed and stale durations

One wording table, used by the card's elapsed time (since the order time) and by C02's staleness (since the last successful refresh):

| Duration | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| under 1 minute | أقل من دقيقة | less than a minute |
| 1 minute | منذ دقيقة | 1 min ago |
| 2 minutes | منذ دقيقتين | 2 min ago |
| 3–10 minutes | منذ {N} دقائق | {N} min ago |
| 11–59 minutes | منذ {N} دقيقة | {N} min ago |
| 60 minutes and above | منذ {H} ساعة/ساعتين/ساعات و{M} دقيقة | {H} h {M} min ago |

- Arabic hour forms: 1 → "ساعة", 2 → "ساعتين", 3–10 → "{H} ساعات", 11 and above → "{H} ساعة".
- In C02 the same values are used without the word "منذ" where the sentence supplies it: "آخر تحديث منذ 3 دقائق / last updated 3 min ago".
- Western digits in both languages. The device clock is the reference; no time zone conversion is performed, and no absolute clock time is printed anywhere on S-01.

### 7.3 Payment chip and change (locked decision 3, map §4 decision 17)

| Order | Chip text (Arabic) | Chip text (English) |
|---|---|---|
| Card | بطاقة | Card |
| Cash, the guest stated an amount greater than the total | نقدًا — باقي {amount − total} ر.س | Cash — SAR {amount − total} change |
| Cash, the guest stated an amount equal to the total | نقدًا — المبلغ مطابق | Cash — exact amount |
| Cash, no amount stated, or an amount not greater than the total | نقدًا | Cash |

- The change is computed as (stated amount − order total), rounded to two decimals, half up.
- A record carrying "card" together with an amount shows the Card chip and ignores the amount, matching G-05 §9 item 12 and G-06 §7.1.
- The chip is a summary. The full sentence, including the instruction to carry change when no amount was stated, is on S-02 §5.5 and in SM-02.

### 7.4 Board request bounds

- One request on open; then one every **15 seconds** measured from the end of the previous attempt; never two in flight.
- Per-attempt timeout **10 seconds**; a late response is discarded entirely.
- Paused while the tab is hidden; one immediate request on return.
- The request carries the session's staff identity and nothing about any guest. The response carries no guest identifier of any kind (map §4 decision 19).
- There is no escalation after N failures: the cadence never changes, and no counter is displayed.

### 7.5 Tap handling

- Cards, tabs and header buttons each respond to a single tap; the 300 ms debounce convention of G-01 §7.1 applies to every one of them, so a double-tap on a card opens S-02 once.
- A tap that begins on a card and ends on another element does nothing.
- A refresh that arrives during a touch is applied when the touch ends (§5.3), so a card can never move out from under a finger.
- There is no long-press, no swipe action and no pull-to-refresh anywhere on the board.

### 7.6 Language

- Every fixed string renders in the language saved on the device (map §4 decision 21). The toggle B01 is one of the two places it can be changed (the other is S-03).
- Values that are not interface copy — room numbers, order numbers, amounts — are never translated or reformatted by a language switch.
- The status labels are the canonical bilingual pair of G-01 §5.2 and switch with the interface.

### 7.7 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: the room number **40 px bold**; the order summary line and tab labels 16 px; group headings 16 px bold; the elapsed time, the order number, the status label, C02, C11 and the shift line 14 px; chips 12 px. **Nothing on S-01 is smaller than 12 px**, and no sentence is smaller than 14 px.
- Tap areas: every card (full width × at least 44 px), both tabs (half width × 48 px), B01, B02, B03 (44 × 44 px minimum), B06 and B07 (44 px and 48 px tall).
- Contrast: the room number at least **7:1**; every other text at least 4.5:1, including chips, the gray placeholder state and the dark-filled bars. Nothing is signalled by colour alone — "Late", the payment chip, the guest-cancelled marker and the stale bar all carry words.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled. With enlarged text the cards grow and the list scrolls under the sticky header and tab bar; nothing is truncated except the staff name (18 characters) and nothing is hidden.
- Screen reader: each card is announced as one item — "غرفة {room}، طلب رقم {n}، {item count}، {total}، {chips}" / "Room {room}, order {n}, {item count}, {total}, {chips}" — with the room digits read one at a time ("zero three zero five"). Group headings are announced as headings. When a refresh adds orders to the New group, the count change is announced once as "{N} طلبات جديدة / {N} new orders"; individual card repaints are not announced.
- Motion: no animation anywhere except the loading skeleton's opacity pulse. There is no transition when lists rebuild.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Opening the staff URL on a device with a live session shows S-01, Active tab, with the first board request sent before any card is drawn.
2. Opening the staff URL on a device with no live session shows S-03 and never S-01.
3. The header (S01) and the tab bar (S02) stay fixed while the list scrolls; the header is 56 px tall and the tab bar 48 px.
4. C01 shows "الوردية: {staff name} / Shift: {staff name}" whenever a session is live, with a name longer than 18 characters cut and closed with "…".
5. B01 switches every visible string, the direction, the count wording and the currency position to the other language in place, without a reload, keeping the tab, the scroll position and the sound setting; the choice survives closing and reopening the tab.
6. B02 always states the sound setting in words; tapping it toggles sound, plays one chime when it is turned on, and saves the setting on the device.
7. Tapping B03 "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" opens S-03 immediately with no confirmation modal, and the device no longer holds a live session.
8. The Active tab renders exactly three possible groups, always in the order New, Accepted & preparing, On the way, each heading carrying its canonical label verbatim from G-01 §5.2 plus its count, and a group with zero orders renders nothing at all.
9. Within every group, orders are sorted oldest first; a newly arrived order appears at the bottom of the New group and changes the position of no existing card.
10. A board refresh that arrives while a finger is touching the list is applied only after the touch ends, and no card changes position or disappears during a touch.
11. Every card shows, in this order: "غرفة / Room" plus the room number at 40 px bold, the elapsed time, the order number, the order summary of item count and total, and the chip row; no other text on the card is larger than 16 px.
12. The room number is printed with leading zeros kept ("0305" is shown as "0305"), in Western digits, left-to-right in both languages, never wrapped and never truncated, with contrast of at least 7:1.
13. The status label appears on a card in the Done today tab and never on a card in the Active tab.
14. C05 "متأخر / Late" appears on a card when and only when the status is New and the order was placed more than 5 minutes ago.
15. The payment chip reads "بطاقة / Card" for a card order, "نقدًا — باقي {change} ر.س / Cash — SAR {change} change" when the guest stated an amount greater than the total, "نقدًا — المبلغ مطابق / Cash — exact amount" when the stated amount equals the total, and "نقدًا / Cash" when no usable amount was stated; the change equals the stated amount minus the total at two decimals.

17. Tapping anywhere on a card opens S-02 for that order; two taps within 300 ms open it once.
18. Tapping a tab switches the list instantly and sends no request; both tab counts are rendered, including zero.
19. The Done today tab contains every order that reached Delivered or Cancelled since the most recent 04:00 local time, newest first, capped at 50 rows, and contains no order in an active status.
20. One board request is sent on open and the next 15 seconds after the previous attempt ended; two board requests are never in flight at once; an attempt that has not answered within 10 seconds counts as failed.
21. Polling pauses while the browser tab is hidden and sends one immediate request when it returns to the foreground.
22. When a refresh fails after at least one earlier success, the last known lists stay on screen, C02 shows "تعذّر تحديث اللوحة — آخر تحديث {duration} / Could not refresh the board — last updated {duration} ago" with the duration wording of §7.2, and C02 becomes a dark-filled bar once 2 minutes have passed without a successful refresh.

25. A successful response that contains at least one order in status New not present in the previous successful response plays exactly one 2-second chime, regardless of how many such orders it contains.
26. The first successful response after a sign-in, a reload, or a return from a hidden tab plays no chime.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **Two tabs, three groups, one card design.** Active (grouped New / Accepted & preparing / On the way) and Done today share one card, one sorting rule per tab and one route into S-02 (§5.3, §5.4).
2. **Oldest first inside every group, and cards never move under a finger** (§5.3, §7.5): a new order lands at the bottom of New, and a refresh during a touch is deferred until the touch ends.
3. **Empty groups render nothing** — no heading and no placeholder — so the board's height is a true measure of the work outstanding (§5.3).
4. **The status label is printed on a card only in the Done today tab**, because in the Active tab the group heading already says it (§5.2 item 4).
5. **The room number is 40 px on a card and is the largest text on the board**, preceded by the word "غرفة / Room", with nothing else on the card above 16 px (§5.2 item 1; map §4 decision 4).
6. **15-second refresh, 10-second timeout, paused when hidden, one response for both tabs** (§5.5).
7. **Staleness is stated with its duration, and escalates to a dark bar after 2 minutes** (§5.5 rule 7, §7.2). The board never silently shows an old queue.
8. **One chime, 2 seconds, once per response, with a baseline that prevents a chime storm at sign-in, repeating every 60 seconds only while a New order is Late** (§5.6).
9. **The sound state is always written in words**, and an audio setting the browser has blocked says so and offers the tap that fixes it (§5.6 rules 5–6).
10. **Late is 5 minutes**, and it is both a chip and the trigger of the repeating chime (§5.2, §5.6).
11. **A guest cancellation leaves its card in place for 60 seconds, marked "do not prepare it"**, before it moves to Done today; a hotel cancellation leaves silently (§5.7).
12. **No action of any kind on a card, and no search, filter, sort or assignment** (§5.8). The board's whole job is to show what is outstanding and to open one order.
13. **Two empty states, one per tab, and neither stops the refresh** (§6.2). The Active empty state names the chime, and says when the chime is muted.
14. **One error state, and only for a cold load with nothing to show** (§6.3). A failed refresh with data on screen is a line, not a state.
15. **The expired session keeps the board alive and takes only the actions away** (§6.6; map §4 decision 3), so an unattended device can never swallow an order silently.
16. **Elapsed time is relative and recomputed every 30 seconds with no request; no absolute clock time appears on the board** (§7.2). The question a staff member asks is "how long has this been waiting", not "what time was it".
17. **The change to bring is computed on the card, not left as arithmetic** (§7.3), so the pocket is filled before the walk begins.
18. **Notes are flagged on the board and read on S-02** (§7.1): a card that quotes a note either truncates it or grows unpredictably, and both are worse than a chip.

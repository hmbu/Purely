# S-02 — Order detail (تفاصيل الطلب)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/staff/staff-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 3, 5, 6, 7; `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` §4 decisions 4–8, 11, 16–20; `/spec/screens/G-01.md` §5.2 (the five canonical status labels, verbatim) and §7.2–§7.3; `/spec/screens/G-04.md` §7.7 (the client order key contract — why an order may carry values the guest later tried to change) and §7.8; `/spec/screens/G-06.md` §5.3 (the guest's cancellation line and the 120-character bound on the staff reason), §5.6 (the four outcomes of a guest cancellation), §7.1.

Conventions reused verbatim from the guest specification: Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label after the amount in Arabic and before it in English, two decimals always; the item-count wording of G-01 §7.3; 44 × 44 px minimum tap areas; browser zoom never disabled; no toast.

**Why this screen carries the weight.** Every act that cannot be undone happens here and only here (map §4 decision 6). Accepting closes the guest's cancellation window for good (locked decision 6). Marking Delivered is a statement that money was collected. Cancelling puts a sentence in front of a waiting guest. So this screen is built to be read before it is tapped: the room number at 64 px — the same size the guest read at confirmation — the full item list with quantities first, the notes in full, and the amount to collect, all above a single primary button that names the status it is about to write.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** S-02
- **Name:** تفاصيل الطلب / Order detail
- **Type:** Full staff screen with its own URL and history entry. The URL carries the order number (example: `…/staff/order/1042`).
- **Language:** the language saved on the device (map §4 decision 21). S-02 has no language toggle; the toggle lives on S-01 and S-03.

---

## 2. Purpose

Show one order completely enough to prepare it, walk it to the right room and take payment, and move it to its next status in one tap.

---

S-02 opens no modal other than SM-01 and SM-02, and never navigates by itself: a status change made on another device re-renders this screen but never moves off it.

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What S-02 reads, writes, and never touches

S-02 renders from the order as the server holds it: the board response that opened it, then its own order responses (§5.6). It never renders a value the server has not confirmed, and it never renders a value from the guest's device.

S-02 **writes** to the server exactly four things, each as one request: accept, on-the-way, delivered (through SM-02), and cancel-with-reason (through SM-01).

S-02 **never**: edits the order (map §4 decision 18 — no items, no quantities, no room number, no notes, no payment method); shows a guest name, phone number or device identifier (map §4 decision 19); recomputes the total; re-prices a line against the catalog; shows unit prices at all (§5.3); deletes anything.

### 5.1 The identity of the order (C04, C05)

- **C04** carries the order number and the elapsed time on one 14 px line. The order number is the string the guest also sees on G-05, G-06 and G-07; it is what a staff member and a guest say to each other on the phone.
- **C05** is the current status as one of the five canonical labels of G-01 §5.2, printed verbatim at 22 px bold. It is the second-largest text on the screen after the room number, and it is never abbreviated or reworded.

### 5.2 The room block (C07) — the loudest thing on the screen

- The number is **64 px bold**, the same size the guest read on M-01 before sending. That is deliberate: the two people who can catch a wrong room see it at the same size, once each.
- It stands on its own line under the word "غرفة / Room" at 16 px. Nothing is ever printed beside it: no price, no order number, no elapsed time, no chip. The rule is absolute — **no other text is on the same line as the room number**, so the largest number on the screen is never mistaken for anything else (map §4 decision 4).
- The value is the record's room number byte for byte, with leading zeros kept ("0305" stays "0305"), Western digits in both languages, laid out left-to-right, never wrapped, never shrunk, never truncated, contrast at least 7:1.
- S-02 does **not** validate it against a list of real rooms; no screen in this product does (G-04 §5.3). A room that does not exist is discovered at the door and handled by cancelling with the reason "الغرفة غير صحيحة أو غير مشغولة / Room number is wrong or not occupied" (SM-01 §5.2).
- **First-screenful guarantee.** At a 390 × 664 px viewport with a 34 px safe-area inset and the status New, the header (56), the body's top padding (16), C04 (20), the gap (8), C05 (28), the gap (16, C06 hidden) and C07 (label 20 + number 76 = 96) place the bottom of the room number at **240 px**, while the action bar (12 + C16 at 18 + 6 + B02 at 64 + 12 + 34 = 146 px) has its top edge at **518 px**. The room number and the primary button are therefore both fully visible on the first painted frame, with 278 px left over for the items heading and the first item rows. With C02 shown (≈ 84 px including its gap) and C06 shown (≈ 40 px + 8 px gap) the bottom of C07 sits at **372 px**, still above the bar. **Nothing may be shrunk, collapsed or moved into an expandable region to make this sum work**, and C07 never renders below 64 px.

### 5.3 What to prepare (S03, C08, C09, C10)

- **C08** heads the section with the total item count in the wording of G-01 §7.3: "ما يجب تجهيزه — 3 منتجات" / "To prepare — 3 items".
- **C09** rows are **quantity first**: "×2" at 22 px bold in a fixed 56 px column, then the product name at 18 px. The quantity leads because a staff member scanning a list is answering "how many of what", and a quantity printed after a long name is read last or not at all.
- **Product names wrap freely and are never truncated**, however long they are. A truncated name on the guest's card is a cosmetic compromise; a truncated name here is a wrong item on a tray.
- **No per-line prices, and no unit prices anywhere on S-02.** The only money on this screen is the order total in C10 and the amount to collect in C12. Fewer numbers near the room number and the collect amount means fewer misreads, and staff have no use for a unit price: they collect one sum.
- **No product images** in version 1: a photo grid makes the list longer and the names are what the store room is organised by.
- **No checkboxes and no picking state**: nothing about a half-picked order is stored, so nothing about it can be shown (map §6, deferred).

That sentence, plus the fact that accepting is only possible from this screen under a 64 px room number and the full item list, is the whole of how acceptance is treated as consequential. **There is no confirmation modal for accepting** (map §4 decision 7), and **there is no undo**: an order accepted by mistake is cancelled through SM-01 with a reason, which is what the guest is honestly told.
- B02 is **never disabled to prevent an action**. It is disabled only while its own request is in flight (§5.7 rule 2).

### 5.6 Refreshing this order — the numbers

1. **On open:** one order request, sent as soon as the screen is drawn.
2. **Then every 10 seconds**, measured from the end of the previous attempt, so two are never in flight at once (map §4 decision 11). It is faster than the board's 15 seconds because this is the screen where an action is about to be sent and the race window matters.
3. **Timeout: 10 seconds.** A late response is discarded.
4. **Refreshing stops permanently** once the status is Delivered or Cancelled — both are final under locked decision 5 — and in the error state.
5. **It pauses** while the browser tab is hidden, while SM-01 or SM-02 is open, and while an action request of §5.7 is in flight; it resumes with one immediate request when the tab returns or the modal closes.
6. **On success:** the screen repaints within 100 ms — C05, C07's value (which never changes for a given order), the item rows, C12, B02's labels, B03's presence and C06 hidden — with no animation, no scroll change and no toast.
7. **On failure:** the last known values stay and **C06** is shown. It disappears on the first success. Unlike the board, S-02 prints no staleness duration: this screen shows one order that a staff member is about to act on, and the action itself is what resolves the doubt (§5.7).
8. **A status change this device did not make** — discovered by a refresh — repaints the screen and shows **C03** "تغيّرت حالة هذا الطلب من جهاز آخر / This order's status was changed on another device" for the rest of the visit, so a second staff member never believes they performed it (§5.7 rule 4).

### 5.7 Sending an action, and every way it can be refused

Every action carries the order number and **the target status**, and the server ignores a target the order already holds (map §4 decision 20), so a double-send is harmless.

1. **On tap:** B02 becomes disabled and reads "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; C15 is cleared; refreshing pauses.
2. **Timeout: 15 seconds.** On timeout or a transport failure the button returns to its normal label and **C15** appears: "لم يصل التحديث إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The update did not reach the system — try again". Nothing else changes, no status is assumed, and the next tap simply sends again.
3. **On success:** the returned status is rendered, B02's labels change to the next status's pair (or B02 is replaced by B04), C15 is hidden, and refreshing resumes. No sound, no animation, no toast.
4. **Refused because the order has already moved on** (another staff member accepted it, or it is already On the way): the screen renders the **real** status returned by the server and shows **C03**. The tap is not retried and nothing is lost — the work the staff member intended has already been done by someone else.
5. **Refused because the order is Cancelled** — this is the race of locked decision 6 and guest map §4 decision 11:
   - When the canceller was the **guest**: the screen renders the Cancelled state and shows **C02 variant G** — "ألغى الضيف هذا الطلب / The guest cancelled this order" + "لا تُحضّره ولا تتوجّه إلى الغرفة. / Do not prepare it and do not go to the room."
   - When the canceller was **the hotel on another device**: **C02 variant H** — "أُلغي هذا الطلب من جهاز آخر / This order was cancelled on another device" with the same second line.
   - In both cases B02 and B03 disappear, B04 takes the action bar, and C13 records who cancelled and why (§5.8 rule 6).
   - **It is a banner, not a modal**, matching the guest's own treatment of the same race (G-06 §5.6). A modal would be dismissed by the same reflex tap that just pressed accept; a dark-filled banner with an instruction stays on screen while the staff member's hand is already reaching for a tray.
   - C02 persists for the rest of the visit, has no close control, and is gone after leaving the screen — by then the status headline alone tells the truth.
6. **There is no offline queue.** An action that did not reach the server did not happen, and the interface never claims otherwise: no optimistic status change, no "will be sent when you are back online", no retry in the background.

### 5.8 Cancelling (B03 → SM-01)

- **B03 is rendered while the status is New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way**, and never when the order is Delivered or Cancelled. Staff may cancel at any point before delivery — a product ran out, the room does not answer, the room number is not occupied — which is exactly what guest map §4 decision 15 already allows for.
- B03 is at the **end of the body**, not in the fixed action bar: it must be reached deliberately by scrolling past the room number, the items and the payment block, and it can never be hit by the thumb that was aiming at the primary button.
- B03 opens SM-01 and sends nothing itself. SM-01 owns the reason, its presets, its bound and the request (SM-01 §5.2, §7.1).
- **What comes back from SM-01** — exactly three outcomes:
  1. **Cancelled** — SM-01 closes; S-02 renders the Cancelled state: C05 "ملغى / Cancelled", B02 and B03 gone, B04 in the action bar, and **C13** showing "ألغاه {staff name}: {reason} / Cancelled by {staff name}: {reason}".
  2. **Refused** because the order is already Delivered, or already Cancelled by someone else — SM-01 closes; S-02 renders the real status, with C03 for a delivery and C02 variant G or H for a cancellation.
  3. **Failed** — SM-01 stays open with its own error line and its own two buttons; S-02 is unchanged beneath it.
- **C13, the cancellation record**, is the only place on the staff side that says who cancelled: "ألغى الضيف هذا الطلب / The guest cancelled this order" when the guest did, and "ألغاه {staff name}: {reason} / Cancelled by {staff name}: {reason}" when the hotel did. **The staff name is shown here only and is never part of the reason string sent to the guest** (SM-01 §7.1), because G-06 §5.3 prints that string verbatim to the guest.
- **There is no un-cancel.** A cancelled order is final for both sides (locked decision 5), and the guest's path back is to place a new order.

### 5.9 Delivering (B02 in status On the way → SM-02)

- Tapping B02 in status On the way sends nothing. It opens **SM-02**, which restates the room number, the method, the amount to collect and the change, and whose confirm button is what sends the delivered request (map §4 decision 16).
- **What comes back from SM-02** — exactly three outcomes:
  1. **Delivered** — SM-02 closes; S-02 renders the Delivered state: C05 "تم التوصيل / Delivered", **C14** "تم تسجيل التوصيل والدفع / Delivery and payment recorded", B02 and B03 gone, B04 in the action bar, refreshing stopped.
  2. **Refused** because the order was cancelled meanwhile — SM-02 closes; S-02 renders the Cancelled state with C02 variant G or H (§5.7 rule 5).
  3. **Failed** — SM-02 stays open with its own error line; S-02 is unchanged beneath it and the order is still On the way.
- **S-02 never navigates by itself after a delivery.** The staff member is standing at a door; the screen states what was recorded and waits for the tap on B04. No auto-return, no countdown, no "next order" jump.

### 5.10 What is deliberately not on this screen

- **No editing of any kind** (map §4 decision 18): no items, no quantities, no room number, no notes, no payment method, no staff note attached to the order.
- **No undo of any status change**, and no way back to a previous status. Forward or cancelled, nothing else.
- **No partial delivery, no partial refund, no "item substituted"**: version 1 has one order with one outcome.
- **No contact control**: no call button, no message to the guest, no `tel:` link. The hotel's channel to a room is the room phone, which the staff member is about to stand next to.
- **No guest identity of any kind** (locked decision 4).
- **No unit prices, no images, no checkboxes** (§5.3).
- **No print, no receipt, no share.**
- **No toast, and no modal other than SM-01 and SM-02.**

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

Rendered **only** on a browser reload or a direct URL open, where no board response exists to draw from. On screen: S01 header complete; in the body, gray placeholder blocks in the shape of C04, C05, C07 and three item rows, pulsing in opacity between 100 % and 60 % every 1.5 seconds; the action bar showing B04 only, with no primary action until the status is known. It ends when the first order response arrives, and never lasts longer than the 10-second timeout; a failure then produces §6.3.

Entering from S-01 there is **no loading state**: the board response already carries everything C04, C05, C07, C09, C10, C11 and C12 need, and the first order request runs behind the drawn screen.

### 6.2 Empty

**Unreachable.** Every order carries a number, a time, a room number, at least one line, a payment method, a total and a status. An order missing any of them is treated as unreadable and renders the error state (§6.3), because a staff member must never act on a half-read order.

### 6.3 Error

One rendered error state: **"لم نعثر على هذا الطلب / We could not find this order"**, with exactly three triggers:

1. The order request answers that the server holds no order with this number.
2. The response is unreadable, or is missing any of the values listed in §6.2.
3. The URL carries an order number the server does not recognise (a reload of a stale tab, a mistyped URL).

On screen: S01 header with B01 and C01; C18 centered with its icon and three lines; the action bar containing B04 only. No part of the order body is rendered, no further request is sent, and no action can be taken.

Copy, exactly:
- Line 1 (20 px, bold): "لم نعثر على هذا الطلب" / "We could not find this order"
- Line 2 (16 px): "قد يكون رقم الطلب غير صحيح، أو لم يعد الطلب موجودًا لدى الفندق" / "The order number may be wrong, or the order is no longer in the hotel's system"
- Line 3 (14 px): "ارجع إلى اللوحة وافتحه من هناك" / "Go back to the board and open it from there"

**Failures that are not this state:** a failed refresh when the order is already on screen is a line (C06), not a state; a failed action is a line (C15), not a state; a refused action renders the real status, not an error.

### 6.4 Success

The order is readable. This is the state for all five statuses; they differ only in C05's label, C06's possibility, C12's lines (unchanged by status), C13 and C14's presence, C16's presence, and which of B02/B03/B04 is rendered:

| Status | Action bar | B03 "Cancel order" | Extra blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| New | C16 + B02 "قبول الطلب / Accept order" | Shown | — |
| Accepted & preparing | B02 "خرجت بالطلب / Heading out with the order" | Shown | C03 if the change came from elsewhere |
| On the way | B02 "تم التوصيل والدفع / Delivered and paid" | Shown | — |
| Delivered | B04 | Hidden | C14 |
| Cancelled | B04 | Hidden | C13, and C02 when this visit discovered the cancellation |

### 6.5 Session expired

When the session reaches its 12-hour limit (map §4 decision 3), S-02 keeps rendering the order in full — the room number, the items, the notes and the payment block stay readable, because reading harms nothing — and the action bar's contents are replaced by **C17** "انتهت مدة الجلسة — سجّل الدخول لتنفيذ أي إجراء / The session has expired — sign in to take any action" with **B05**. B02, B03 and B04 are all hidden while C17 is shown; the back arrow B01 still works. Signing in on S-03 returns to this order with the action bar restored.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on S-02 and no field element exists. The rules below govern the values it displays, the requests it sends, and the taps it accepts.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Order number (C04) | Server | Exactly as stored, identical to what the guest sees |
| Elapsed time (C04) | The order time against the device clock | The wording of S-01 §7.2; recomputed every 30 seconds with no request |
| Status (C05, B02, B03, C13, C14) | The most recent successful response, otherwise the value S-01 handed over | Always one of the five canonical labels of G-01 §5.2; a sixth value makes the order unreadable (§6.3) |
| Room number (C07) | Server, the room number string | Byte for byte, leading zeros kept, 1–5 Western digits, 64 px bold, never wrapped, never shrunk, never re-validated, alone on its line |
| Quantity (C09) | Server, line quantity | 1–10 per line (G-01 §7.1), printed as "×{N}" at 22 px bold |
| Product name (C09) | Server, the stored name in the current interface language | Wrapped over as many lines as needed, **never truncated**. If the name is missing in the current language, the other language's name is shown (G-01 §5.4 rule) |
| Item count (C08, C10) | Server, the sum of line quantities | The wording of G-01 §7.3, verbatim |
| Total (C10, C12) | Server, the order's stored total | Printed as stored, two decimals, currency per G-01 §7.2. **Never recomputed** from the lines |
| Payment, amount, change (C12) | Server, payment method and the guest's stated amount | The four cases of §5.5; change = stated amount − total, two decimals, half up |
| Notes (C11) | Server, notes | Shown in full, exactly as the guest typed them, up to G-04's 200-character bound, never truncated, never translated; hidden when empty |
| Cancellation record (C13) | Server: who cancelled, the reason, the staff name | The reason exactly as stored, cut at 120 characters with "…" (G-06 §5.3); the staff name shown on this screen only |

### 7.2 Request bounds

- **Order refresh:** one on open, then one every **10 seconds** from the end of the previous attempt, timeout **10 seconds**, paused while the tab is hidden, while a modal is open and while an action is in flight, stopped at Delivered, at Cancelled and in the error state.
- **Action requests** (accept, on-the-way, delivered, cancel): timeout **15 seconds**; idempotent by target status; never queued, never retried automatically, never sent optimistically.
- Every request carries the order number and the signed-in staff identity; none of them carries anything about the guest (locked decision 4).

### 7.3 Tap handling

- B01, B02, B03, B04 and B05 each respond to a single tap; the 300 ms debounce convention of G-01 §7.1 applies, and B02 additionally stays disabled for the whole life of its request, so a double-tap can never send two actions.
- Nothing else on S-02 is tappable: not the item rows, not the notes, not the payment block, not the room number, not the banners. A tap on any of them does nothing at all.
- There is no long-press, no swipe and no pull-to-refresh.

### 7.4 Language

- Every fixed string renders in the language saved on the device. Switching happens on S-01 or S-03; returning re-renders every label, status label, count wording and currency position.
- Values that are not interface copy — the room number, the order number, amounts, the guest's notes, and a stored cancellation reason — are never translated and never reformatted by a language switch.

### 7.5 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: the room number **64 px bold**; C05 22 px bold; the quantity in C09 and "المبلغ المطلوب / Amount to collect" 22 px bold; product names and notes 18 px; B02 line 1 18 px bold; C01 18 px bold; headings, C10 and B03/B04 labels 16 px; every other line 14 px. **Nothing on S-02 is smaller than 14 px.**
- Tap areas: B01 44 × 44 px; B02 the inner width × 64 px; B03, B04 the inner width × 56 px; B05 × 48 px.
- Contrast: the room number at least **7:1**; every other text at least 4.5:1, including the dark-filled banner. Nothing is carried by colour alone — the banners carry words, the primary button is distinguished by fill *and* by its two-line label.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled. With enlarged text the body scrolls under the fixed header and action bar; the room number never renders below 64 px and product names are never truncated.
- Screen reader: on arrival the title is announced, then C02 when present, then C04, C05, C06 when present, then C07 as "غرفة / Room" followed by the digits read **one at a time** ("zero three zero five"), then the items as a list of "{quantity} × {name}", then the notes, then the payment block, then the buttons. When a refresh or an action changes the status, the new status is announced once as "{order number}: {status label}". C15 is announced when it appears.
- Motion: no animation anywhere except the loading skeleton's opacity pulse.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Opening S-02 from an S-01 card draws the header and the full body on the first painted frame with no skeleton and no spinner, and sends one order request behind the drawn screen.
2. Opening S-02 by a browser reload or a direct URL shows the loading state until the first order response, and the error state if that request fails or the order is unknown.
3. The room number is rendered at 64 px bold, alone on its line under the word "غرفة / Room", with leading zeros kept, in Western digits, left-to-right in both languages, never wrapped and never shrunk; no other text appears on the same line as it.
4. At a 390 × 664 px viewport with a 34 px safe-area inset, the room number and the primary action button are both fully visible on the first painted frame without scrolling, in every status that has a primary action, including when C02 and C06 are both shown.
5. The room number is the largest text on the screen, and the status headline at 22 px is the second largest.
6. C05 shows the current status using one of the five canonical labels of G-01 §5.2 verbatim, in the current language.
7. The items section lists every order line as "×{N}" at 22 px bold followed by the product name at 18 px; a long name wraps and is never truncated or ellipsised.
8. No unit price and no per-line total appears anywhere on S-02; the only amounts on the screen are the order total in C10 and the amount to collect in C12.
9. C11 is rendered when and only when the order carries non-whitespace notes, and shows them in full without truncation.
10. C12 shows the Card variant with "أحضر جهاز الدفع / bring the card terminal", or one of the three Cash variants of §5.5, with "المبلغ المطلوب / Amount to collect" at 22 px bold and the change equal to the stated amount minus the total at two decimals.
11. A cash order with no stated amount shows "لم يحدّد الضيف المبلغ — احمل باقيًا كافيًا / The guest did not state an amount — carry enough change".
12. An order carrying "card" together with a stated amount shows the Card variant and ignores the amount.
13. In status New, B02 reads "قبول الطلب / Accept order" on line 1 and "الحالة بعد الضغط: تم القبول وجارٍ التحضير / Status after tapping: Accepted & preparing" on line 2, with C16 "بقبولك لن يستطيع الضيف إلغاء الطلب / Once you accept, the guest can no longer cancel it" directly above it.
14. No confirmation modal appears when B02 is tapped in status New, and no undo control appears anywhere after a successful accept.
15. In status Accepted & preparing, B02 reads "خرجت بالطلب / Heading out with the order" with "الحالة بعد الضغط: في الطريق / Status after tapping: On the way" on line 2.
16. In status On the way, B02 reads "تم التوصيل والدفع / Delivered and paid" with "يفتح تأكيد الدفع / Opens the payment confirmation" on line 2, and tapping it opens SM-02 and sends no request.
17. In statuses Delivered and Cancelled, B02 and B03 are not rendered and B04 "العودة إلى اللوحة / Back to the board" is the only button in the action bar.
18. While an action request is in flight, B02 is disabled and reads "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; two taps within 300 ms send exactly one request.
19. An action that times out after 15 seconds restores B02's label, shows C15 "لم يصل التحديث إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The update did not reach the system — try again", changes no status on screen, and can be retried by tapping again.
20. An action sent twice for the same target status leaves the order in that status with no second effect.
21. When an accept is refused because the guest cancelled first, S-02 renders the Cancelled state and shows C02 variant G with both ruled lines, B02 and B03 disappear, and no modal appears.
22. When an action is refused because the order was cancelled on another device, S-02 shows C02 variant H with both ruled lines.
23. C02 has no close control, persists for the rest of the visit, and is not shown again after leaving S-02 and returning.
24. When a refresh or a refusal reveals a status change this device did not make, C03 "تغيّرت حالة هذا الطلب من جهاز آخر / This order's status was changed on another device" is shown and persists for the rest of the visit.
25. One order request is sent on open and the next 10 seconds after the previous attempt ended; two are never in flight at once; an attempt unanswered after 10 seconds counts as failed.
26. Refreshing pauses while the tab is hidden, while SM-01 or SM-02 is open and while an action is in flight, and resumes with one immediate request; it stops permanently at Delivered, at Cancelled and in the error state.
27. When the most recent refresh failed, C06 "تعذّر تحديث هذا الطلب — هذه آخر حالة معروفة / Could not refresh this order — this is the last known status" is shown and disappears on the first success; no staleness duration is printed on S-02.
28. B03 "إلغاء الطلب / Cancel order" is rendered in statuses New, Accepted & preparing and On the way, is placed at the end of the body and never in the fixed action bar, and opens SM-01 without sending anything.
29. When SM-01 reports a successful cancellation, S-02 shows status Cancelled, hides B02 and B03, shows B04, and shows C13 "ألغاه {staff name}: {reason} / Cancelled by {staff name}: {reason}".
30. When the order was cancelled by the guest, C13 reads "ألغى الضيف هذا الطلب / The guest cancelled this order" and carries no staff name and no reason.
31. The staff name appears only in C13 on this screen and is never part of the reason string stored on the order.
32. When SM-02 reports a successful delivery, S-02 shows status Delivered, C14 "تم تسجيل التوصيل والدفع / Delivery and payment recorded", B04 only, and stops refreshing; the screen does not navigate by itself.
33. When SM-01 or SM-02 reports a failure, S-02 is unchanged and the modal stays open.
34. No status on S-02 offers any way to edit the order: no item, quantity, room number, note or payment control exists in any state.
35. No guest name, phone number or guest identifier appears anywhere on S-02, and no call, message or chat control exists.
36. The error state shows C18's three lines with an action bar containing only B04, renders no part of the order body, and sends no further request.
37. A failed refresh never produces the error state, and a failed action never produces it.
38. While the session has expired, the order is still fully rendered, B02, B03 and B04 are hidden, and C17 with B05 "تسجيل الدخول للمتابعة / Sign in to continue" occupies the action bar; signing in returns to this order with the action bar restored.
39. Tapping B01 or B04 returns to S-01 at the tab and scroll position it was left at, or to a fresh Active tab when S-02 was opened by reload or direct URL.
40. Nothing on S-02 is tappable except B01, B02, B03, B04 and B05; taps on item rows, notes, the payment block, the banners and the room number do nothing.
41. All numbers — room number, order number, quantities, totals, change, elapsed time — use Western digits 0–9 in both languages, and in Arabic the currency label follows the amount while in English it precedes it.
42. No text on S-02 is smaller than 14 px; the room number's contrast is at least 7:1 and every other text's at least 4.5:1; the screen reads correctly in grayscale.
43. Pinch-zoom and browser text scaling work in every state; with enlarged text the body scrolls under the fixed header and action bar, the room number stays at 64 px, and no product name is truncated.
44. A screen reader announces the room number digit by digit, the items as a list of "{quantity} × {name}", and a status change once per change.
45. No toast is displayed on S-02, and no modal other than SM-01 and SM-02 can open from it.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **One primary button, labelled by its result.** Line 1 is a verb, line 2 prints the canonical status label the tap will write, verbatim (§5.4; map §4 decision 5).
2. **Accepting has no confirmation modal and no undo** (§5.4; map §4 decision 7). Its weight is carried by its location — the order's own screen, under a 64 px room number and the full item list — and by C16's one-line statement that the guest's cancellation window closes. A mistaken accept is repaired by cancelling with a reason.
3. **The room number is 64 px, alone on its line, and nothing is ever printed beside it** (§5.2). It matches the size the guest read on M-01, so both people who can catch a wrong room see it at the same size.
4. **The first-screenful guarantee is proved by arithmetic, counting the safe-area inset at 34 px and both banners** (§5.2), and nothing may be shrunk or collapsed to make the sum work.
5. **Quantity leads every item row at 22 px bold, and product names are never truncated** (§5.3): a truncated name here is a wrong item on a tray.
6. **No unit prices and no images in the item list** (§5.3): the only two numbers that matter at a door are the room and the amount to collect, and every extra number near them is a misreading risk.
7. **The payment block states the method, the amount to collect at 22 px, and the change to bring, in four exhaustive cases** (§5.5), including the case where the guest stated nothing.
8. **"Cancel order" lives at the end of the body, not in the fixed bar** (§5.8), so it is reached deliberately and can never be hit by the thumb aiming at the primary action.
9. **Delivery goes through SM-02 because that is where money changes hands** (§5.9; map §4 decision 16), and S-02 never navigates away by itself afterwards.
10. **A refused action is answered by rendering the truth, not by an error**: the real status plus C03, or the Cancelled state plus C02 (§5.7).
11. **The cancellation race is shown as a dark-filled banner with an instruction — "do not prepare it and do not go to the room" — not as a modal** (§5.7 rule 5), matching the guest's own banner treatment of the same race.
12. **A status change made elsewhere is always disclosed** (C03, §5.6 rule 8), so a second staff member never believes they performed it.
13. **There is no offline queue and no optimistic status change** (§5.7 rule 6): an action that did not reach the server did not happen, and the screen says so with C15.
14. **Refresh is 10 seconds here against the board's 15**, because this is the screen where an action is about to be sent (§5.6).
15. **S-02 prints no staleness duration** (§5.6 rule 7) where the board does: here the doubt is resolved by sending the action, not by reading a clock.
16. **The cancellation record names the staff member on the staff side only**; the reason string the guest reads never contains it (§5.8; SM-01 §7.1).
17. **An expired session leaves the whole order readable and removes only the actions** (§6.5), because reading harms nothing and a staff member standing at a door must still be able to see the room number.
18. **One error state, three triggers, no order body rendered** (§6.3): a staff member must never act on a half-read order.

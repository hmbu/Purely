# S-03 — Staff sign-in (دخول الموظف)

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/staff/staff-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decision 7; `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` §2.1 (S-03 row), §2.3 ("End of shift" row), §3.2 (S-03 row), §3.3 rules 1, 4, 5, §4 decisions 2, 3, 13, 14, 21, 22; `/spec/staff/screens/S-01.md` §3.1, §5.6 (the chime rules this screen reuses, and rule 6, which points here for how audio is armed), §6.6; `/spec/staff/screens/S-02.md` §3.1 (S-03 row), §6.5; `/spec/screens/G-04.md` §7.1 (digit conversion, reused for the PIN).

Conventions reused verbatim from the guest specification and from S-01, not re-decided here: Western digits 0–9 in both languages; Arabic-Indic (٠–٩) and Extended Arabic-Indic (۰–۹) digits are converted to 0–9 as they are typed (G-04 §7.1); every tappable element has a tap area of at least 44 × 44 px; browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled; no toast anywhere; the 300 ms debounce of G-01 §7.1 on every tap.

**Why this screen exists at all.** The guest is anonymous; a staff member is not. Accepting closes the guest's cancellation window, cancelling puts a sentence in front of a waiting guest, and "Delivered and paid" says money was taken at a door. A hotel must be able to say who did each of those (map §4 decision 2). S-03 is the lightest thing that achieves it: four digits, one field, no button to find. It is also the screen a device falls back to between shifts, so it keeps counting the queue and keeps chiming while nobody is signed in (map §2.3).

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** S-03
- **Name:** دخول الموظف / Staff sign-in
- **Type:** Full staff screen with its own URL (example: `…/staff/sign-in`). The staff URL on a device with no live session opens it; the same URL opened on a device with a live session goes to S-01 instead, so a live session is never interrupted by a bookmark.
- **Variants:** **A — the device is linked to the hotel** (the PIN field, the queue line and the chime); **B — the device is not linked** (one explanatory block; no field, no request, no way forward from this interface; map §4 decision 22).
- **Language:** Arabic (RTL) on first open regardless of browser language; the header toggle switches to English (LTR) in place, and the choice is saved on the device and shared with S-01 (map §4 decision 21).
- **Viewport:** specified at 390 px wide. At 768 px and above the body is a single centered column 390 px wide; nothing else changes.

---

## 2. Purpose

Name the staff member who is about to act, with a 4-digit PIN, and keep announcing waiting orders while nobody is signed in.

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What S-03 reads, writes, and never touches

S-03 **reads**: the hotel link held on the device (a credential written when the hotel links the device, outside this interface); the saved language; the saved sound setting; the return target, when one was handed over (§3.1); and, from the server, the queue summary (§5.5) and the answer to a sign-in request.

S-03 **writes** exactly one thing to the device: on a correct PIN, the **session** — the staff member's identity, the staff member's name, and the device-clock time at which the successful response arrived. It sends exactly two kinds of request: the sign-in request (§5.2) and the queue-summary request (§5.5).

S-03 **never**: shows a list of staff names or lets anyone pick a name (the PIN alone names the person); shows a room number, an item, an amount or any order content; shows a guest identifier of any kind (map §4 decision 19); stores the PIN anywhere, on the device or in the page, after the request is sent; offers a way to link, unlink or register the device.

### 5.1 Header (S01)

- Title at the start edge, language toggle at the end edge. There is **no back arrow** (map §3.3 rule 1) and **no sound toggle**: the sound is switched on S-01 only (S-01-B02), and S-03 states it in words (C07) instead.
- The toggle works in both variants, so a manager who reads only English can read variant B.

### 5.2 The PIN, and signing in (F01, C04, C05)

1. **On arrival** in variant A, F01 is empty and focused, so on a phone the numeric keypad is already open. On a device with a physical keyboard, digits typed anywhere on the page go into F01.
2. **Typing.** Each digit fills the next cell as a dot. Backspace removes the last digit. There is no "show PIN" control and the last digit is never revealed, not even for a moment.
3. **The 4th digit sends the request** at once, with no button. In the same step F01 is disabled, the keypad closes, C04 is hidden and C05 "جارٍ التحقق… / Checking…" is shown. The four digits are not kept anywhere after the request is sent.
4. **Timeout: 15 seconds**, the action timeout of map §4 decision 20 — a staff member is standing still waiting on this request exactly as on an action.
5. **Outcomes** — exactly five, and no sixth:

| Response | What S-03 does |
|---|---|
| **Correct PIN** — the server returns the staff member's identity and name | Writes the session (§5.0), then navigates to the return target or to S-01, Active tab, top (§3.2). Nothing is drawn on S-03 in between: no "welcome", no checkmark, no name flash. The name appears in the S-01 header (S-01-C01) |
| **Wrong PIN** | F01 is cleared and re-enabled, focus returns to it, C05 is hidden, C04 shows "رمز الدخول غير صحيح — أعد إدخاله / Wrong PIN — enter it again" |
| **Locked out** — the server refuses because 5 consecutive wrong PINs were entered on this device (§7.1) | F01 is cleared and **disabled for 60 seconds** from this response, C04 shows "محاولات خاطئة كثيرة — انتظر دقيقة ثم حاول مرة أخرى / Too many wrong attempts — wait one minute, then try again". At 60 seconds F01 is re-enabled and focused and C04 is hidden. No countdown is shown |
| **Device not linked** — the server answers, readably, that this device's hotel link is missing or no longer valid | S-03 switches to **variant B** (§5.6) for the rest of this page load |
| **Failed** — not sent, connection dropped, no response in 15 seconds, any other error, or a response the device could not read | F01 is cleared and re-enabled, focus returns to it, C04 shows "تعذّر الوصول إلى النظام — تحقّق من الشبكة ثم أعد إدخال الرمز / Could not reach the system — check the network, then enter the PIN again" |

6. **Lockout is the server's count, not the device's.** The device keeps no counter and shows none. It only obeys the refusal for 60 seconds. A correct PIN resets the server's count.
7. **Clearing the field after a failure is deliberate**: re-typing four digits costs two seconds, whereas a field that keeps a masked, unverifiable value leaves the staff member unsure what is in it.

### 5.3 Where a correct PIN leads

- **No return target** (fresh load, reload, "End shift"): S-01, Active tab, top of the list. S-01's first board response is its chime baseline (S-01 §3.1).
- **Return target S-01** (from S-01-B06): S-01 at the tab and scroll position it was left at, with the session-expired bar gone and B03 "إنهاء الوردية / End shift" back in the header.
- **Return target S-02** (from S-02-B05): S-02 for that order, with its action bar restored (S-02 §3.1, §6.5). Browser back from there reaches S-01.
- The return target is held **in the page's memory only**. A reload on S-03 drops it, and a correct PIN after a reload goes to S-01, Active tab, top.
- A **different** staff member may sign in at a return target; the work continues under the new name, which S-01-C01 then shows. Nothing asks whether the name changed.
- **The session** lasts until "End shift" (S-01-B03) or until **12 hours** after the successful sign-in response, on the device clock (map §4 decision 3). It survives reloads and closing the tab. There is no idle lock.

### 5.4 Arming audio — the sign-in touch (referenced by S-01 §5.6 rule 6)

Browsers refuse to play sound on a page that has received no touch or key press since it loaded. The chime is the whole of the new-order alert (map §4 decision 14), so S-03 is where it is made to work:

1. **Armed** means: this page has received at least one touch, click or key press since it loaded, and the browser has not refused a playback since then.
2. **Any** touch, click or key press anywhere on S-03 arms audio — a tap on F01 to open the keypad, the first digit typed, a tap on the language toggle, or a tap on empty space. No chime is played when it is armed.
3. Because signing in requires typing four digits, **every successful sign-in arms audio**, and S-03 → S-01 is an in-page navigation, so S-01 starts with audio armed. This is the "tap that signs in" S-01 §5.6 rule 6 refers to.
4. While the saved sound setting is on and audio is not yet armed — a page freshly loaded on S-03 that nobody has touched — **C07 variant BLOCKED** says so in words. It disappears on the first touch.
5. While the saved sound setting is off (set with S-01-B02), **C07 variant OFF** says so on S-03 too. S-03 cannot change the setting; the line says where it is changed.
6. A page that arrives on S-03 from S-01 or S-02 has already been touched, so it is already armed and C07 shows only when the sound setting is off.

### 5.5 The queue line and the chime while nobody is signed in (C06)

Map §2.3 ("End of shift" row) rests on this: the cost of a mis-tapped "End shift" is four digits *because* S-03 keeps counting and keeps chiming. So, in variant A:

1. **The queue-summary request** is sent once on arrival, then every **15 seconds** measured from the end of the previous attempt, with a **10-second** timeout; it pauses while the browser tab is hidden and sends one immediate request when the tab returns — the cadence of the board (S-01 §5.5). It is authorised by the **device's hotel link**, not by a staff session, because there is none. It returns exactly: the number of orders in the three active statuses, and for each order in status New its order number and order time. **Nothing else** — no room number, no item, no amount, no note, because nobody is signed in to read them.
2. **C06** shows "طلبات نشطة الآن: {N} — جديدة: {M} / Active orders now: {N} — new: {M}" from the most recent success, or "لا توجد طلبات نشطة الآن / No active orders right now" when N is 0. On a failure it is replaced by "تعذّر تحديث عدد الطلبات / Could not update the order count" until the next success. There is no retry button and no duration: the request keeps running every 15 seconds.
3. **The chime** follows S-01 §5.6 rules 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 without change: one 2-second chime per response that contains a New order number not in the previous successful response; a repeat every **60 seconds** while any New order is older than **5 minutes** (map §4 decision 13, computed from the order times); nothing while the saved sound setting is off; nothing until audio is armed. **The baseline**: when S-03 is entered from S-01 or S-02, the set of New order numbers from S-01's most recent successful board response is the baseline, so the first queue response already chimes for an order that arrived during the hand-over; on a fresh load or reload, the first successful queue response is the baseline and plays no chime (S-01 §5.6 rule 3).
4. **On a correct PIN** the queue request stops and S-01 takes over with its own baseline (S-01 §3.1).
5. If the queue request answers that the device is not linked, S-03 switches to variant B, exactly as the sign-in request does (§5.2 outcome 4).

### 5.6 Variant B — the device is not linked (C08)

- **When:** the device holds no hotel link (checked on the device, before any request, so variant B is drawn on the first frame); or a sign-in or queue-summary request answers, readably, that the link is missing or no longer valid. A request that merely failed is **not** a reason for variant B.
- **What is on screen:** the header (C01 "دخول الموظف / Staff sign-in" and B01) and C08's icon and three lines. No field, no queue line, no sound line, no helper line, no button.
- **What happens:** nothing. No request is sent, no chime plays, and nothing on the screen leads anywhere. The link held on the device is **not deleted** by S-03; a reload checks again.
- **Why there is no way forward:** there is deliberately no self-service way to turn a random phone into a staff terminal (map §4 decision 22). Linking a device is done by the hotel outside this interface, which is out of scope.

### 5.7 What is deliberately not on this screen

- **No "Sign in" button**: the 4th digit is the submit, so a staff member with a tray in one hand never looks for a second target.
- **No name picker, no staff list, no user name, no password, no e-mail.**
- **No "remember me", no "stay signed in" option**: the session already lasts until "End shift" or 12 hours.
- **No PIN reset or change** on the device: C09 names the person who handles it.
- **No order content** of any kind before sign-in (§5.5 rule 1).
- **No sound toggle**; the state is stated in C07, and the toggle is on S-01.
- **No back arrow, no toast, no modal.**

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

- **On arrival:** there is no loading layout. Variant A or B is decided from the device on the first frame. In variant A, C06 stays hidden until the first queue response or failure, but its one-line height (22 px) is reserved from the first frame with nothing drawn in it, so C07, C03 and F01 do not move when it appears.
- **While the sign-in request is in flight:** F01 disabled with its four dots, C05 "جارٍ التحقق… / Checking…" in the message slot, keypad closed. It ends at the first of a response or the 15-second timeout. B01 still works.

### 6.2 Empty

- **Empty field:** the arrival state of variant A; not an error, no message.
- **Empty queue:** C06 reads "لا توجد طلبات نشطة الآن / No active orders right now". The queue request keeps running.

### 6.3 Error

Four rendered error cases, all in C04 under the field, plus one for the queue line:

| Case | Where | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong PIN | C04 | رمز الدخول غير صحيح — أعد إدخاله / Wrong PIN — enter it again |
| Locked out (60 s) | C04, F01 disabled | محاولات خاطئة كثيرة — انتظر دقيقة ثم حاول مرة أخرى / Too many wrong attempts — wait one minute, then try again |
| Sign-in request failed | C04 | تعذّر الوصول إلى النظام — تحقّق من الشبكة ثم أعد إدخال الرمز / Could not reach the system — check the network, then enter the PIN again |
| Enter pressed with fewer than 4 digits (physical keyboard or the keypad's "Go" key) | C04 | رمز الدخول 4 أرقام — أكمل إدخاله / The PIN has 4 digits — finish entering it |
| Queue request failed | C06 | تعذّر تحديث عدد الطلبات / Could not update the order count |

The fifth field message (a paste containing a non-digit) is in §7.2. Variant B is not an error state of this screen; it is the second variant (§5.6).

### 6.4 Success

A correct PIN. Nothing is drawn on S-03: the session is written and the next screen is shown within 100 ms of the response (§5.3).

### 6.5 Variant B

Defined in §5.6. It is the only content on the screen, it sends nothing, and it lasts until the page is reloaded.

## 7. Field rules

### 7.1 F01 — PIN (رمز الدخول / PIN)

| Rule | Decision |
|---|---|
| Required | **Yes.** Exactly 4 digits. |
| Input type / keyboard | One single-line input showing the phone's **numeric keypad with digits 0–9 only**; drawn as 4 cells (§4). Auto-complete, auto-correct and the browser's password saving are **off**: the browser never offers to save or fill it. |
| Accepted characters | Western digits 0–9. Arabic-Indic (٠–٩) and Extended Arabic-Indic (۰–۹) digits are converted to 0–9 as they are typed (G-04 §7.1). Any other typed character is **not entered** and shows no message. |
| Length | Exactly **4**. A 5th typed digit is impossible: the 4th sends the request and disables the field. |
| Leading zeros | Kept. "0042" is a valid PIN of four digits. |
| Paste | Allowed. After trimming spaces at both ends and converting digits, a paste of 1–4 digits fills the cells from the start (a paste of exactly 4 sends the request); a paste of more than 4 digits enters the first 4 and sends; a paste containing any other character enters **nothing** and shows the paste message of §7.2. |
| Masking | Always masked as dots. No reveal control, no momentary display of the last digit. |
| Stored | **Never.** The PIN exists only in the field until the request is sent, and the field is emptied on every outcome. |
| Sent | The 4-digit string, together with the device's hotel link. Nothing about any guest. |
| Uniqueness | Each PIN identifies exactly one staff member of the hotel, which is why no name is asked for. Issuing and changing PINs is done outside this interface. |
| Lockout | After **5 consecutive wrong PINs** on this device the server refuses sign-in for **60 seconds**; the device obeys the refusal (§5.2 step 6) and keeps no counter of its own. |
| Validation fires | On the 4th digit (sent to the server); on Enter with fewer than 4 digits (short message); on paste (paste message). There is no validation on blur. |

| Sign-in request failed | تعذّر الوصول إلى النظام — تحقّق من الشبكة ثم أعد إدخال الرمز | Could not reach the system — check the network, then enter the PIN again | The next digit is typed |

### 7.3 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| {N} in C06 | Queue-summary response, count of orders in New, Accepted & preparing and On the way | Western digits, no padding |
| {M} in C06 | Queue-summary response, count of orders in New | Western digits; printed even when 0 ("جديدة: 0 / new: 0") as long as N ≥ 1 |
| Sound line (C07) | The saved sound setting and the page's armed state (§5.4) | Two variants, or hidden |

### 7.4 Request bounds

- **Sign-in:** one request per 4th digit; timeout **15 seconds**; never retried automatically; never two in flight (F01 is disabled while one is).
- **Queue summary:** one on arrival, then every **15 seconds** from the end of the previous attempt; timeout **10 seconds**; paused while the tab is hidden; stopped on a correct PIN and in variant B.
- Both carry the device's hotel link; the sign-in request also carries the PIN. Neither carries anything about a guest.

### 7.5 Tap handling

- B01 and C03 respond to a single tap with the 300 ms debounce. A tap on C03 or on any cell focuses F01.
- Any touch, click or key press anywhere on S-03 arms audio (§5.4); it has no other effect outside B01, C03 and F01.
- There is no long-press, no swipe and no pull-to-refresh.

### 7.6 Language

- Every fixed string renders in the saved language; B01 is one of the two places it is changed (the other is S-01-B01), and the setting is shared.
- Digits typed in F01, the counts in C06, and the lockout are unaffected by a switch.

### 7.7 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: C01 18 px bold; C02, C03 and C06 16 px bold; C07, C04, C05 and C09 14 px; C08 line 1 20 px bold, line 2 16 px, line 3 14 px. **Nothing on S-03 is smaller than 14 px.**
- Tap areas: B01 44 × 44 px; F01 the full row of four cells (260 × 64 px).
- Contrast: every text at least 4.5:1, including the disabled cells at 50 % opacity. Nothing is signalled by colour alone — errors carry a "!" icon and words, and the next cell is marked by border weight.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled. With enlarged text the lines wrap and the body scrolls; the cells keep their 56 × 64 px size.
- Screen reader: F01 is announced with its label C03 and its fill state as "{n} من 4 أرقام / {n} of 4 digits" — never the digits themselves. C04 and C05 are announced when they appear. C06 is announced once on arrival and again only when {M} changes. C07 is announced once when shown. In variant B the three lines of C08 are read in order.
- Motion: none. No spinner, no shake on a wrong PIN, no transition to the next screen.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each statement is pass/fail.

1. Opening the staff URL on a device with no live session, or with a session older than 12 hours, shows S-03; opening the sign-in URL on a device with a live session shows S-01 instead.
2. On a linked device, S-03 opens with F01 empty and focused, and on a phone the numeric keypad is open without a tap.
3. S-03 has no back arrow, no sound toggle and no "Sign in" button.
4. F01 is drawn as four cells; each entered digit is shown as a dot and no digit is ever displayed, including the last one typed.
5. Typing Arabic-Indic or Extended Arabic-Indic digits enters the matching Western digits; typing any non-digit character enters nothing.
6. Entering the 4th digit sends exactly one sign-in request at once, disables F01, closes the keypad and shows "جارٍ التحقق… / Checking…".
7. After a correct PIN with no return target, S-01 opens on its Active tab at the top of the list within 100 ms of the response, and its header shows the name returned for that PIN.
8. After a correct PIN reached from S-01-B06, S-01 returns to the tab and scroll position it was left at; reached from S-02-B05, S-02 returns for that order with its action bar restored.
9. After a reload on S-03, a correct PIN leads to S-01, Active tab, top, whatever return target existed before the reload.
10. After a wrong PIN, F01 is empty, enabled and focused, and C04 shows "رمز الدخول غير صحيح — أعد إدخاله / Wrong PIN — enter it again"; the message disappears when the next digit is typed.
11. When the server refuses sign-in after 5 consecutive wrong PINs, F01 is disabled for 60 seconds with C04 "محاولات خاطئة كثيرة — انتظر دقيقة ثم حاول مرة أخرى / Too many wrong attempts — wait one minute, then try again", no countdown is shown, and at 60 seconds F01 is enabled and focused and the message is gone.

14. Pasting a value containing any non-digit enters nothing and shows "استخدم الأرقام فقط (0–9) / Use digits only (0–9)"; pasting exactly 4 digits sends the request.
15. The PIN is not present in device storage or in the page after the request is sent, and the browser never offers to save or autofill it.
16. No list of staff names, no name field and no password field exists on S-03.

20. ## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **The 4th digit is the submit; there is no "Sign in" button** (§5.2). One target for one hand, and nothing to look for after typing.
2. **The PIN field is drawn as four masked cells, never revealed, never stored** (§4, §7.1), and it is emptied on every outcome so its content is never in doubt.
3. **Five sign-in outcomes, no sixth**: correct, wrong, locked out, device not linked, failed (§5.2).
4. **Lockout is 60 seconds after 5 consecutive wrong PINs, counted by the server**; the device shows words and no countdown (§5.2 step 6, §7.1).
5. **Sign-in timeout is 15 seconds**, the action timeout of map §4 decision 20, because a staff member waits on it exactly as on an action (§5.2 step 4).
6. **Any touch on S-03 arms audio, and S-03 → S-01 is an in-page navigation**, so every successful sign-in hands the board a working chime (§5.4). An untouched S-03 says in words that sound is not yet enabled.
7. **The queue summary carries counts and New order numbers and times only — no room, no items, no amounts** — because nobody is signed in to read them (§5.5 rule 1). It is authorised by the device link.
8. **S-03 chimes with the board's exact rules**, and when entered from the board it inherits the board's last set of New orders as its baseline, so an order that arrives during the hand-over still chimes (§5.5 rule 3).
9. **The queue line and the sound line sit above the field**, where the numeric keypad cannot cover them (§4 layout paragraph).
10. **The return target lives in page memory only** (§5.3): a reload on S-03 goes to the board's Active tab.
11. **A different person may sign in at a return target**; the header name is the record of who continued (§5.3).
12. **Variant B is decided from the device on the first frame, or from an explicit server answer — never from a failed request** — and it neither deletes the link nor offers any way forward (§5.6).
13. **C09 names the duty manager for a forgotten PIN**; there is no reset on the device (§4, §5.7).
14. **Recorded difference between the map and S-01, followed in favour of S-01:** the map's S-03 row (§3.2) says a correct PIN leads to "S-01, Active tab, top of the list (or back to the S-02 …)", which reads as if a sign-in reached from the S-01 session-expired bar also lands at the top. S-01 §3.2 and its acceptance criterion 39 say B06 returns to the same tab and scroll position. This file follows S-01 (§3.1, §5.3). The map is not edited here.

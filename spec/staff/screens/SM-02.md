Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft** (status is changed only by the orchestrator in `/spec/staff/staff-map.md`)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 3, 5, 7; `/spec/staff/staff-map.md` §2.2 (SM-02 row), §3.2 (SM-02 row), §3.3 rule 2, §4 decisions 4, 5, 16, 17, 20; `/spec/staff/screens/S-02.md` §5.4 (the On-the-way row of B02), §5.5 (the four payment cases this sheet restates at the door), §5.6 rule 5, §5.7 rule 5, **§5.9 (the contract this file honours: what S-02 hands over and the three outcomes)**, §6.5; `/spec/staff/screens/SM-01.md` preamble (the staff modal conventions, reused here unchanged); `/spec/screens/G-01.md` §5.2 (canonical status labels) and §7.2 (currency and digits).

Conventions reused, not re-decided here: the **staff modal conventions of the SM-01 preamble** — bottom sheet over a 50 % black backdrop covering S-02 including S-02-S04, 85 % maximum height, 14 px text floor, pinned footer with "رجوع / Back" (outlined, 56 px) above the dark two-line confirm button (64 px), 16 px between them, 300 ms arming delay, not dismissable while its request is in flight; Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label after the amount in Arabic and before it in English, two decimals always (G-01 §7.2); change = stated amount − total, two decimals, half up (S-02 §5.5); the in-flight label "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; the action-failure sentence of S-02-C15, word for word; no toast.

**Why this sheet exists.** "Delivered" is the only status that says money changed hands (map §4 decision 16). S-02's payment block is read at the desk, before the walk; this sheet is read at the threshold, with the guest in front of the staff member and the tray still in one hand. So it says four things and nothing else — which room, how the guest pays, how much, and what change to give — and its confirm button is the one tap that writes تم التوصيل / Delivered.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** SM-02
- **Name:** تأكيد التوصيل والدفع / Confirm delivery and payment
- **Type:** Modal (bottom sheet) on top of **S-02 Order detail**. No URL, no history entry (map §3.3 rule 2). It is the only place in the staff interface where an order becomes Delivered.
- **Language:** the staff interface language saved on the device. No language toggle.

---

## 2. Purpose

At the door, state the room, the payment method, the amount to collect and the change to give, and record the order as Delivered on one confirmed tap.

## 5. Content

### 5.0 What SM-02 reads, writes, and never touches

SM-02 **reads** the five values S-02 hands it (§3.1) and the signed-in staff identity from the session.

SM-02 **writes** nothing to the device. It sends **one** request per confirmed tap: this order is Delivered.

SM-02 **never**: records which method was actually used or how much was actually handed over (map §4 decision 16 — the order's stored payment choice is what the hotel reconciles); lets the method or the amount be changed (map §4 decision 18); recomputes the total; shows items, notes or unit prices; shows a guest identifier (map §4 decision 19).

### 5.1 Sheet layout

Top to bottom: the title; the room at 40 px; the method; the amount to collect at 28 px; the one-line instruction about change; the consequence line; then the pinned footer.

- **The room number is the largest text on the sheet (40 px), and the amount to collect is the second (28 px)**, each alone on its own line, the room above the amount (map §4 decision 4). No other number shares a line with either.
- Height at a 390 × 664 px viewport: about 400 px of content plus a footer of about 172 px, near the 85 % ceiling (564 px); when C07 appears or text is enlarged, the scrolling area scrolls and the footer stays pinned.

### 5.2 The room block (C02)

- The room is restated here because the sheet covers S-02's 64 px room block, and the moment a staff member faces a door is the last moment a wrong room can be caught (map §1 property 2).
- The value is the room number S-02 shows, byte for byte, with leading zeros kept ("0305" stays "0305"), Western digits, laid out left-to-right in both languages, contrast at least 7:1. It is never re-validated.

### 5.3 The money lines (C03, C04, C05) — the four cases of S-02 §5.5, stated for the door

The case split is S-02 §5.5's, unchanged. The values are S-02's, unchanged. What changes is the tense: S-02 tells the staff member what to **carry** before the walk; SM-02 tells them what to **do** with the guest in front of them.

| Order | C03 (Arabic / English) | C04 amount | C05 (Arabic) | C05 (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Card** | الطريقة: **بطاقة** / Method: **Card** | {total} | خذ المبلغ على جهاز الدفع | Take the payment on the card terminal |
| **Cash**, stated amount **greater** than the total | الطريقة: **نقدًا** / Method: **Cash** | {total} | سيدفع بـ **{amount} ر.س** — أعطِه باقيًا **{change} ر.س** | Paying with **SAR {amount}** — give back **SAR {change}** change |
| **Cash**, stated amount **equal** to the total | الطريقة: **نقدًا** / Method: **Cash** | {total} | سيدفع بالمبلغ المطابق — لا يوجد باقٍ | Paying the exact amount — no change |
| **Cash**, no amount stated, or an amount **not greater** than the total | الطريقة: **نقدًا** / Method: **Cash** | {total} | لم يحدّد الضيف المبلغ مسبقًا — أعطِه الباقي مما يدفعه | The guest did not state an amount in advance — give change from what they pay |

- {change} = {amount} − {total}, two decimals, half up — the same figure S-02-C12 printed at the desk.
- An order carrying "card" together with an amount shows the Card row and ignores the amount (S-02 §5.5).
- The exact-amount wording is S-02's, word for word, because it is true at both moments.
- In the no-amount case there is no computed figure to print: the guest told the hotel nothing, and version 1 records no amount handed over (map §4 decision 16), so the sheet says what the situation is and asks for nothing to be typed.

### 5.4 The two buttons (B01, B02) and the consequence line (C06)

- **B02 "سلّمتُ الطلب واستلمتُ المبلغ / Handed over and payment taken"** is a verb in the first person that names both halves of the act, and its line 2 prints the canonical label it writes: "الحالة بعد الضغط: تم التوصيل / Status after tapping: Delivered" (map §4 decision 5). It is deliberately a different sentence from S-02-B02 "تم التوصيل والدفع / Delivered and paid", which only opens this sheet, so the tap that writes the status never reads the same as the tap that did not.
- **C06** stands directly above the footer: "اضغط فقط بعد أن يستلم الضيف الطلب وتستلم المبلغ / Tap only after the guest has the order and you have the payment". Delivered is final (locked decision 5) and there is no undo, so the order of events at the door is written down once.
- **B01 "رجوع / Back"** closes the sheet with the order still On the way. It is the path for everything that is not a completed handover — the door does not open, the card is refused, the guest disputes the order. What happens next uses the existing tools: B02 again later, or "إلغاء الطلب / Cancel order" on S-02 with a reason (SM-01).
- **Both buttons ignore taps for the first 300 ms** after the sheet starts to appear. That matters most here: the sheet slides up under the same thumb that just tapped S-02-B02 in S-02's bottom bar, and B02 sits in the same place.

### 5.5 The request, and the three outcomes S-02 expects

The request carries: the order number, the target status **Delivered**, and the signed-in staff identity. It carries no amount, no method and nothing about the guest. **Timeout: 15 seconds** (map §4 decision 20). It is idempotent by target status.

The response is classified into **exactly the three outcomes of S-02 §5.9**, and never into a fourth:

| What the device observed | Outcome handed to S-02 | What SM-02 does |
|---|---|---|
| The order is now **Delivered** (by this request, by an earlier attempt whose response was lost, or by another device — the server holds one Delivered and it is final) | **delivered** | Closes within 100 ms, with no message of its own. S-02 renders C05 "تم التوصيل / Delivered", C14 "تم تسجيل التوصيل والدفع / Delivery and payment recorded", B04 only, refresh stopped (S-02 §5.9 outcome 1) |
| The order is **Cancelled** | **refused — cancelled** | Closes. S-02 renders the Cancelled state with C02 variant G when the guest cancelled, variant H when the hotel did (S-02 §5.7 rule 5) |
| Anything else: not sent, connection dropped, no response in 15 seconds, an error response of any kind (including one saying the order does not exist or the session is not valid), an unreadable response, or a readable response carrying any status other than Delivered or Cancelled | **failed** | **Stays open** (§5.6). S-02 is unchanged beneath it and the order is still On the way |
| A response arriving after the 15-second timeout | Nothing | Discarded. The sheet is already in the failed state and does not change by itself |

- "Order does not exist" and a rejected session are *failed* for the reasons recorded in SM-01 §5.5: S-02 fixes exactly three outcomes, and S-02's own refresh and session rules report both cases once the sheet is closed.

### 5.6 The failure case

1. The sheet stays open, re-rendered in place with no second slide-up.
2. **C07** appears in the footer: "لم يصل التحديث إلى النظام — حاول مرة أخرى / The update did not reach the system — try again" — S-02-C15's sentence, because it is the same failure of the same kind of request.
3. B02 returns to its two-line label and is the retry; B01 still closes with the order On the way.
4. Retrying is safe: the request is idempotent by target status, so a delivery that did land is reported as *delivered* on the retry.
5. There is no attempt counter, no retry cap, no automatic retry, and no offline queue: a delivery that did not reach the server did not happen (S-02 §5.7 rule 6).

### 5.7 What is deliberately not on this modal

- **No items and no item count**: the tray is in the staff member's hand and S-02's list was read at the desk.
- **No order number**: the room is what is checked at a door, and every extra number near the room and the amount is a misreading risk (S-02 §9 item 6).
- **No "amount received" field, no method switch, no receipt** (map §4 decisions 16, 18).
- **No notes**: they were read in full on S-02 before the walk.
- **No close "×"**: B01, the backdrop, browser back, Escape and the swipe are the dismissals.
- **No toast and no confirmation after closing**: S-02's Delivered state is the report.

## 6. States

### 6.0 Ready (the state on open)

Backdrop; sheet with C01, C02, C03, C04, one variant of C05, C06; footer with B01 and B02. C07 hidden. Focus on C01; the sheet is announced as a dialog. Both buttons ignore taps for the first 300 ms.

### 6.1 Loading — the delivery in flight

Triggered by a tap on B02. Entered **synchronously on the tap**, so a second tap cannot find B02 active.

On screen: every text unchanged; C07 hidden if it was shown; B02 disabled and reading "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…"; B01 at 50 % opacity and inert; the backdrop, browser back, Escape and the swipe ignored. No spinner, no countdown.

It ends at the first of a response or the 15-second timeout. The sheet cannot be dismissed during those at most 15 seconds, for the reason given in SM-01 §9 item 9.

### 6.2 Empty

Not reachable. S-02 holds every value SM-02 shows for every readable order (S-02 §6.2), and SM-02 opens only from a readable order in status On the way.

### 6.3 Error

One error case: the *failed* outcome, shown as C07 in the footer with the copy of §5.6. Nothing is typed on SM-02, so there is no field error message.

### 6.4 Success

The *delivered* outcome. SM-02 and the backdrop are removed within 100 ms, with no checkmark, no message and no toast. S-02 reports it (§5.5). The *refused* outcome closes the sheet the same way and is reported by S-02.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on SM-02 and no field element exists. The rules below govern the values it displays, the request it sends and the taps it accepts.

### 7.1 Values shown, and their source

| Value | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Room number (C02) | S-02, the value it shows in C07 | Byte for byte, leading zeros kept, 1–5 Western digits, 40 px bold, alone on its line, never wrapped, never shrunk, never re-validated |
| Method (C03) | S-02, the order's stored payment method | "card" → بطاقة / Card; "cash" → نقدًا / Cash |
| Total (C04) | S-02, the order's stored total | Printed as stored, two decimals, currency per G-01 §7.2, 28 px bold. **Never recomputed** |
| Stated amount, change (C05) | S-02, the guest's stated amount | The four cases of §5.3; change = amount − total, two decimals, half up; ignored for a card order |

### 7.2 Request bounds

- One delivered request per tap of B02; timeout **15 seconds**; idempotent by target status; never queued, never retried automatically, never sent optimistically.
- It carries the order number, the target status and the staff identity; nothing about the guest, no amount and no method.

### 7.3 Tap handling

- B01 and B02 ignore taps for 300 ms after the sheet starts to appear; after that each responds to a single tap with the 300 ms debounce.
- B02 is disabled for the whole life of its request, so a double tap sends one request.
- After B01, the backdrop, browser back, Escape or the swipe, SM-02 ignores further taps until it is removed, so a double tap cannot reach S-02 beneath.
- Nothing else on the sheet is tappable: not the room number, not the amount, not the lines.

### 7.4 Language

- Fixed strings render in the staff language saved on the device; there is no toggle on SM-02.
- The room number and the money values are never translated or reformatted by a language switch.

### 7.5 Sizes, touch targets, text, and accessibility

- Text sizes: the room number **40 px bold**; the total **28 px bold**; C01 and B02 line 1 18 px bold; "غرفة / Room", C03, the "المبلغ المطلوب / Amount to collect" label, C05 and B01 16 px; C06, C07 and B02 line 2 14 px. **Nothing on SM-02 is smaller than 14 px.**
- Tap areas: B01 the sheet width × 56 px; B02 × 64 px.
- Contrast: the room number at least **7:1**; every other text at least 4.5:1, including B01 at 50 % opacity. Nothing is carried by colour alone.
- Browser zoom and text scaling are never disabled; the scrolling area grows and scrolls, the footer stays pinned, the room number never renders below 40 px.
- Screen reader: on open, the dialog is announced, then C01, then C02 as "غرفة / Room" followed by the digits read **one at a time** ("zero three zero five"), then C03, C04, C05 and C06 in order, then the two buttons. C07 is announced when it appears; "جارٍ الإرسال… / Sending…" is announced once on entering the in-flight state. S-02 behind the backdrop is not reachable by focus.
- Motion: the 200 ms slide-up is the only movement.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **Four values and one instruction, nothing else**: room, method, amount to collect, change (§5.1, §5.7). No items, no item count, no order number, no notes.
2. **Room 40 px, amount 28 px, each alone on its line, the room above** (§5.1, map §4 decision 4).

# A-02 — نظرة عامة / Overview

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft**
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decision 5; `/spec/admin/admin-map.md` §4 (global frame), §5.6 (reporting definitions, fixed once), §6 flag 5.
Conventions reused, not re-decided here: Western digits 0–9 in both languages; currency label from A-07, after the amount in Arabic ("45.00 ر.س") and before it in English ("SAR 45.00"), two decimals always (G-01 §7.2); the five status labels of G-01 §5.2 verbatim; the hotel time zone from A-07 governs every date and period boundary.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** A-02
- **Name:** نظرة عامة / Overview
- **Type:** Full admin screen inside the global frame. The default landing after sign-in.
- **Language:** the dashboard language (`AF-B06`).

---

## 2. Purpose

Tell the manager, in one screen and in this order, what is broken right now and what the store earned.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 Entry

| From | Trigger | What A-02 shows |
|---|---|---|
| A-01 | A successful sign-in with no remembered address | Loading state (§6.1), then the success state, period = last 7 days |
| Any admin screen | Sidebar "نظرة عامة / Overview" (`AF-B01`) | Same as above; the period always starts again at last 7 days (§7.1) |
| A-02 | "تحديث / Refresh" (`B07`) | Loading state, then the success state with the same period |
| A-02 | A period button (`B04`) | Only the period section and the top-products section reload (§6.1); the attention band is not re-fetched |
| Browser reload or a bookmarked A-02 URL | Browser load | Same as a sidebar entry. The period is **not** in the URL (§7.1) |

### 3.2 Exit

| Action | Destination |
|---|---|
| `B01` "لا توجد منتجات للبيع / Nothing to sell" | A-03 with no filter |
| `B02` "طلبات جديدة لم تُقبل / New orders not accepted" | A-06 with status = New and the date filter set to the last 30 days (A-06's default) |
| `B03` "منتجات غير متوفرة / Out-of-stock products" | A-03 with the stock filter set to "غير متوفر / Out of stock" |
| `B05` a top-product row | A-04 in edit mode for that product |
| Any sidebar item | That screen |
| `B04` period, `B07` refresh | Stays on A-02 |

Nothing on A-02 changes any data. It is entirely read-only: no switch, no save, no status change (`admin-map` §1).

## 5. Content

### 5.1 Reading order, and why it is this order

The page is three bands, top to bottom: **what is broken → what the store earned → what sold**. A manager who opens this screen once a week must see an empty catalog or an order nobody accepted before any revenue figure, because those two cost money silently and the revenue figure does not need acting on.

### 5.2 Attention band (`S01`)

Rows appear only when their condition is true, always in this order: `B01`, then `B02`, then `B03`. Each row is one line with a chevron at the end edge and opens the screen where the problem is fixed.

- **`B01` — nothing on sale.** True when the number of products that are both active (not removed, A-03 §5.3) and in stock is **zero**. This is the state in which every guest who scans the QR code sees G-01's empty state, so it is first and it is worded as a fact, not a count.
- **`B02` — new orders nobody accepted.** The number of orders whose status is `جديد / New` and whose submission time is **more than 10 minutes ago**. Ten minutes is the threshold because an in-room order that no one has even accepted after ten minutes is a staffing problem, not a busy kitchen. The row states the threshold in its own text so the number is never ambiguous. Clicking it opens A-06 filtered to New; A-02 offers **no** way to accept, cancel or change the order (`admin-map` §1).
- **`B03` — out-of-stock products.** The number of active products whose stock switch is off. Out of stock is a normal state, not a fault, which is why it is the last row and why its wording carries no alarm.
- Plural forms, all four buckets, for `B02` and `B03`:

| N | `B02` Arabic | `B02` English | `B03` Arabic | `B03` English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | طلب جديد واحد لم يُقبل منذ أكثر من 10 دقائق | 1 new order not accepted for more than 10 minutes | منتج واحد غير متوفر | 1 product out of stock |
| 2 | طلبان جديدان لم يُقبلا منذ أكثر من 10 دقائق | 2 new orders not accepted for more than 10 minutes | منتجان غير متوفرين | 2 products out of stock |
| 3–10 | {N} طلبات جديدة لم تُقبل منذ أكثر من 10 دقائق | {N} new orders not accepted for more than 10 minutes | {N} منتجات غير متوفرة | {N} products out of stock |
| 11 and above | {N} طلبًا جديدًا لم يُقبل منذ أكثر من 10 دقائق | {N} new orders not accepted for more than 10 minutes | {N} منتجًا غير متوفر | {N} products out of stock |

- **Freshness.** The band is fetched once, when the screen opens or when `B07` is clicked. It does **not** poll and it does not refresh itself. `C13` prints the time of that fetch, so a figure on screen is never taken for a live one.

### 5.3 Period band (`S02`)

- Three periods only: `اليوم / Today`, `آخر 7 أيام / Last 7 days` (the default), `آخر 30 يومًا / Last 30 days`. There is no custom range here — custom ranges live on A-06, which is the screen for looking something specific up.
- `C03` prints the exact boundaries, so "Today" and "Last 7 days" are never guesses: for Today, the same date twice; for the others, the first and the last day inclusive.
- The four figures, all computed over the selected period with the definitions in `admin-map` §5.6:
  1. `C04` **الطلبات المسلَّمة / Delivered orders** — count of orders whose status is Delivered.
  2. `C05` **الإيراد / Revenue** — the sum of the stored totals of those delivered orders.
  3. `C06` **متوسط قيمة الطلب / Average order value** — revenue ÷ delivered orders, two decimals.
  4. `C07` **الطلبات الملغاة / Cancelled orders** — count of cancelled orders in the period and, in brackets, that count as a percentage of **all** orders in the period.
- `C08` states the delivered-only rule on screen, every time, so the revenue figure is never read as "what guests ordered".
- An order belongs to a period by the moment it was **submitted**, not by the moment it was delivered, so an order never moves between periods after the fact and the same order is never counted twice.
- **No charts.** Four numbers and a list (`admin-map` §1).

### 5.4 Top products (`S03`)

- At most **5** rows, the best sellers by **units sold in delivered orders** in the selected period.
- Each row, start edge to end edge: rank number (1–5), product name in the dashboard language, units sold, revenue from that product (sum of `quantity × unit price` as stored on the delivered order lines).
- Ordering is deterministic: units sold descending; ties broken by revenue descending; ties still level broken by the product's Arabic name, alphabetically ascending.
- A product that has since been removed from the store still appears, with the suffix `(مُزال / removed)` after its name; its row opens A-04 in edit mode where it can be restored. Sales history is never hidden because a product was removed.
- Fewer than 5 sellers in the period → fewer rows. No filler.

### 5.5 What is deliberately not on this screen

- No live order board, no auto-refresh, no sound, no notification.
- No button that changes an order's status, cancels an order, or contacts a guest.
- No chart, sparkline, gauge or progress ring.
- No "orders today by hour", no occupancy, no guest counts — the product has no source for any of them.
- No comparison with the previous period ("+12 % vs last week"): two periods on one screen doubles every definition in `admin-map` §5.6 and the manager can select the other period.
- No stock value, cost price or margin — the catalog has no cost field, and adding one is a version-2 decision.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

- On a full load (`entry`, `B07`): `C12` skeletons replace the three bands' contents. The frame, the title `C01` and `C09` stay. No text where a figure will be.
- On a period change (`B04`): only `S02`'s four figures and `S03`'s rows are replaced by skeletons; the attention band keeps its values and `C13` keeps its time, because nothing about it changed.
- Timeout **10 seconds** per section, then §6.3.

### 6.2 Empty

Each band has its own empty answer, and none of them is a blank screen:

- Attention band with nothing wrong → `C02` all-clear line.
- Period band with no orders at all → `C04` and `C07` show `0`, `C05` shows `0.00`, `C06` shows `—`, and the cancellation percentage shows `—`.
- Top products with no delivered units → `C10`.

### 6.3 Error

The three bands are fetched independently, so one failure never blanks the screen.

- A band that fails or times out shows `C11` "تعذّر تحميل هذا القسم / This section could not be loaded" and `B06` "إعادة المحاولة / Try again" in place of its contents. The other bands are unaffected.
- `B06` re-fetches that band only.
- A failed attention band never shows an all-clear line: silence is not the same as "everything is running", and `C02` must never appear because a request failed.
- An expired session during any fetch opens A-01 per `admin-map` §4.3.

### 6.4 Success

All three bands loaded: the attention rows whose conditions are true (or `C02`), the four figures, the caption, up to five product rows, `C13` with the fetch time.

## 7. Field rules

Nothing is typed on A-02. The rules below govern the values it displays.

### 7.1 Period selection

- Exactly three values; the default is **آخر 7 أيام / Last 7 days** on every entry, including a browser reload. The choice is not saved and is not in the URL: a weekly opener should always see the same week without checking which period is selected.
- `اليوم / Today` = 00:00:00 to the present moment, hotel time zone. `آخر 7 أيام` = the 7 calendar days ending today, inclusive. `آخر 30 يومًا` = the 30 calendar days ending today, inclusive.

### 7.2 Dates and times

- Date format in both languages: `DD/MM/YYYY`, Western digits (example `22/09/2026`).
- Time format: 24-hour `HH:MM`, Western digits.
- Every date, time and boundary is in the hotel time zone set in A-07. The browser's time zone is never used.

### 7.3 Figures

- Counts: whole numbers, no thousands separator below 1000, a comma separator at 1000 and above in both languages (`1,240`).
- Amounts: two decimals always, currency label from A-07, position per G-01 §7.2.
- Average order value with zero delivered orders: `—`. Never `0.00`, which would read as "we delivered orders worth nothing".
- Cancellation percentage with zero orders in the period: `—`. Otherwise a whole number, rounded half up, followed by `%` in both languages.
- Units sold: whole numbers.

### 7.4 Text limits

- Product name in a top-product row: one line, truncated with an ellipsis at the width of its column; the full name is on A-04.
- Attention rows never wrap: each is one line at 15 px in both languages at 1024 px width.

### 7.5 Sizes

Figure cards 100 px tall in a row of four with 16 px gutters; attention rows 52 px; top-product rows 48 px; every clickable element at least 36 px tall and at least 44 px wide. Minimum text size 13 px. Browser zoom is never disabled.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

1. Signing in with no remembered address opens A-02 with the period set to "آخر 7 أيام / Last 7 days".
2. The page shows three bands in this order: attention, period figures, top products.
3. When the catalog has no product that is both active and in stock, the first attention row reads "لا توجد منتجات معروضة للبيع الآن / Nothing is on sale right now" and opens A-03 unfiltered.
4. When at least one product is active and in stock, that row is not rendered.
5. When N orders have been in status New for more than 10 minutes, the second attention row shows N with the exact plural form of §5.2 and opens A-06 filtered to status New.
6. An order that is in status New for less than 10 minutes is not counted in that row.
7. When N active products are out of stock, the third attention row shows N with the exact plural form of §5.2 and opens A-03 filtered to out of stock.
8. When all three conditions are false, the band shows exactly "كل شيء يعمل: هناك منتجات معروضة، ولا طلبات متأخرة عن القبول / Everything is running: products are on sale and no order is waiting to be accepted" and no rows.
9. When the attention band fails to load, it shows the error line and "إعادة المحاولة / Try again", never the all-clear line, and the other two bands still render.
10. "تحديث / Refresh" re-fetches all three bands and updates the "آخر تحديث {HH:MM} / Last updated {HH:MM}" line to the new fetch time.
11. A-02 sends no request by itself after the initial load: leaving the screen open for 30 minutes changes nothing on it.
12. Clicking a period button re-fetches only the figures and the top products; the attention band's values and its last-updated time do not change.
13. The period caption prints the first and last day of the selected period in `DD/MM/YYYY`, and for "اليوم / Today" prints today's date twice.
14. "الطلبات المسلَّمة / Delivered orders" equals the number of orders in the period whose status is Delivered.
15. "الإيراد / Revenue" equals the sum of the stored totals of exactly those orders, with two decimals and the currency label positioned per G-01 §7.2, and the footnote "يشمل الإيراد الطلبات المسلَّمة فقط… / Revenue counts delivered orders only…" is visible under the figures.
16. An order in status New, Accepted & preparing, On the way or Cancelled contributes nothing to Revenue.
17. "متوسط قيمة الطلب / Average order value" equals revenue ÷ delivered orders to two decimals, and prints "—" when no order was delivered in the period.
18. "الطلبات الملغاة / Cancelled orders" shows the count and, in brackets, that count as a whole-number percentage of all orders submitted in the period; with no orders in the period the percentage prints "—".
19. An order is counted in the period that contains its submission time, in the hotel time zone from A-07, regardless of when its status last changed.
20. Top products lists at most 5 rows, ordered by units sold in delivered orders descending, ties broken by revenue and then by Arabic name, each row showing rank, name, units and revenue.
21. A removed product that sold in the period still appears, marked "(مُزال / removed)", and its row opens A-04 in edit mode for it.
22. With no delivered units in the period, the section shows "لم تُسلَّم أي طلبات في هذه الفترة / No orders were delivered in this period" and no rows.
23. Clicking a top-product row opens A-04 in edit mode for that product.
24. While any band is loading, its contents are replaced by gray skeletons with no text, and the frame and band titles stay visible.
25. A band whose request exceeds 10 seconds shows the error line with "إعادة المحاولة / Try again", and that button re-fetches only that band.
26. No element on A-02 changes any data: there is no switch, no save, no status change and no cancel anywhere on the screen.
27. Every date is `DD/MM/YYYY` and every time `HH:MM`, both with Western digits, in the hotel time zone, in both languages.
28. Switching the dashboard language re-renders every label, plural form and footnote on A-02 in the other language and direction, with the same figures.
29. No chart, sparkline or graphic of any kind is rendered on A-02.
30. The screen fits its three bands with no horizontal scrolling at 1024 px width.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **Breakage before revenue.** The attention band is first, and its first row is "nothing is on sale", the only state that makes the whole store useless to a guest.
2. **Ten minutes is the unaccepted-order threshold**, stated in the row's own text, because an unaccepted in-room order after ten minutes is a staffing problem.
3. **A-02 never acts on an order.** It links to A-06; the five statuses of locked decision 5 are moved by the staff interface only.
4. **Revenue is delivered-only**, defined once in `admin-map` §5.6 and repeated on screen in `C08`.
5. **Orders belong to the period of their submission**, so figures for a past period never change afterwards.
6. **Three fixed periods, defaulting to 7 days, never remembered.** Custom ranges belong on A-06.
7. **No comparison with a previous period**, no charts, no cost or margin figures.
8. **Fetch once, say when.** No polling anywhere in the dashboard; `C13` prints the fetch time so nothing is mistaken for live.
9. **Three independently fetched bands**, so one failure never blanks the screen — and a failed attention band never shows an all-clear.
10. **Zero cases print `—`, not `0.00`**, for the average and the cancellation rate.
11. **Removed products stay in the best-seller list**, marked, because sales history is a fact about the past.

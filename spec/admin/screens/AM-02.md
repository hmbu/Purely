# AM-02 — تأكيد الإزالة / Confirm removal

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft**
Governing documents: `/spec/admin/admin-map.md` §2.2 (AM-02 row), §3.2 (AM-02 row), §3.3 rule 5, §5.3 (when a change reaches a guest), §5.4 (immediate saves and how they fail); A-03 §3.1, §5.4, §5.6, `C06`, `C07`; A-04 §3.2, §7.8, `B03`; A-05 §5.4, §5.6, §6.3, `B05`; A-02 §5.2 (`B01` condition); G-01 §5.7, §6.2; G-03 §5.2; M-04.
Conventions reused, not re-decided here: Western digits 0–9; Arabic (RTL) dashboard by default; body copy never smaller than 13 px.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** AM-02
- **Name:** تأكيد الإزالة / Confirm removal
- **Type:** Modal, no URL, two variants that share one layout:
  - **Variant P — product.** Opens on A-03 (`A-03-B05` "إزالة / Remove") and on A-04 in edit mode (`A-04-B03` "إزالة من المتجر / Remove from the store").
  - **Variant C — category.** Opens on A-05 (`A-05-B05` "حذف / Delete"), only for an empty category.
- **Language:** the dashboard language.

---

## 2. Purpose

Confirm removing a product from the store, or deleting an empty category, after stating exactly what the guest will and will not see.

---

## 3. Entry and exit points

### 3.1 Entry

| Variant | From | Trigger | Opens for |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | A-03 | "إزالة / Remove" on an active product's row | That product |
| P | A-04, edit mode, active product | "إزالة من المتجر / Remove from the store" | The product being edited, with or without unsaved edits on the form |
| C | A-05 | "حذف / Delete" on a category that holds zero products, active or removed (A-05 §5.4) | That category |

On open, one request asks for the current number of active in-stock products (variant P only, §5.3). Nothing else is fetched; the names shown come from the screen underneath.

### 3.2 Exit

| Action | Variant P from A-03 | Variant P from A-04 | Variant C from A-05 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `B01` confirm | AM-02 closes at once; the row leaves the list within 100 ms; `A-03-C06` "تمت إزالة "{name}" من المتجر / "{name}" was removed from the store"; `A-03-C02` recounts | AM-02 closes at once; A-03 opens without that product and with the same `A-03-C06` line. Any unsaved edit on A-04 is dropped, as `C05` stated | AM-02 closes at once; the row leaves the list within 100 ms; `A-05-C05` "تم حذف الفئة "{name}" / The category "{name}" was deleted" |
| The removal then fails (no connection, server error, 5-second timeout) | The row returns to its place, `A-03-C06` is replaced by `A-03-C07` (`admin-map` §5.4) | The product returns to its place on A-03 and `A-03-C07` is shown; the dropped A-04 edits are **not** restored | The row returns to its place, `A-05-C06` is shown; if the server said the category is no longer empty, A-05 §6.3 case 6 applies |
| `B02` "إلغاء / Cancel", Esc, or a backdrop click | AM-02 closes; nothing changed | AM-02 closes; A-04 exactly as it was, every typed value kept | AM-02 closes; nothing changed |
| Browser back while AM-02 is open | Same as `B02` | Same as `B02` | Same as `B02` |

AM-02 never opens AM-01 and never opens on top of it (AM-01 §3.1).

## 5. Content

### 5.1 Layout

Top to bottom: `C01` title, `C02` the two names, `C04` when shown, `C03` the consequences, `C05` when shown, then the button row: `B02` "إلغاء / Cancel" at the start edge and `B01` at the end edge. On open, keyboard focus is on `B02`, so Enter and Esc both cancel; confirming takes a deliberate click or Tab then Enter.

### 5.2 Variant P — consequence list (`C03`), exact copy

| # | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | لن يظهر للنزلاء في المتجر عندما يفتحونه من جديد. | Guests will no longer see it when they open the store again. |
| 2 | النزيل الذي في سلته هذا المنتج لن يستطيع إرسال طلب يتضمنه. | A guest who has it in their cart cannot send an order that includes it. |
| 3 | الطلبات المرسلة لا تتغيّر، ويبقى المنتج في سجل الطلبات والتقارير. | Orders already sent do not change, and the product stays in order history and reports. |
| 4 | يمكنك استعادته لاحقًا من «المنتجات» باختيار «مُزال»، ويعود غير متوفر. | You can restore it later from Products by choosing Removed; it comes back out of stock. |

Each line is a fact from the approved specifications, stated once:

1. A removed product has no card on G-01 (G-01 §5.7 last bullet); a guest who already has the store open sees it until their next catalog fetch (`admin-map` §5.3), which "when they open the store again" states without a technical term.
2. The guest's existing cart line survives, priced from its own snapshot and treated as out of stock (G-03 §5.2 item 2), which disables their Checkout on G-03; if the guest submits before their cart re-check notices, M-04 rejects the order at submission. Either way, no order containing the product can be sent.
3. Removal is archiving, never deletion (A-03 §5.4, decision 7).
4. A restored product returns out of stock (A-03 §5.6, decision 6).

### 5.3 Variant P — the last product on sale (`C04`)

- On open, AM-02 sends one request for the number of products that are active and in stock, and whether this product is one of them. It is the same count that decides A-02's "لا توجد منتجات معروضة للبيع الآن / Nothing is on sale right now" row (A-02 §5.2).
- `C04` is shown when that count is **1** and the one product is this product: removing it leaves every guest who scans the QR code facing G-01's empty state (G-01 §6.2). It is placed above the list so it is read first.
- The buttons are usable from the moment AM-02 opens; `C04` appears when the answer arrives (timeout **5 seconds**). If the request fails or times out, `C04` is not shown and nothing else changes: the warning is information, and the removal remains exactly as reversible as §5.2 line 4 says.
- `C04` is never shown for an out-of-stock product: removing one that is already out of stock changes nothing about what is on sale.

### 5.4 Variant C — consequence list (`C03`), exact copy

| # | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | الفئة فارغة ولا يراها النزلاء الآن، فلن يتغيّر شيء في المتجر. | The category is empty and guests do not see it now, so nothing changes in the store. |
| 2 | سيُحذف اسمها بالعربية والإنجليزية نهائيًا، ولا يمكن التراجع عن ذلك. | Its Arabic and English names will be deleted permanently. This cannot be undone. |

Line 1 is true by construction: AM-02 variant C opens only for a category with zero products (A-05 §5.4), and G-01 shows no chip for an empty category (G-01 §5.3). Line 2 is the only irreversible act in the dashboard, and it is stated as such.

### 5.5 What AM-02 does not do

- It never deletes a product permanently; there is no such action anywhere (A-03 decision 7).
- It never deletes a non-empty category; that case cannot open it.
- It asks for no typed confirmation ("type the name to confirm"): a removal is restorable in one click, and a category deletion loses two names of an empty category.
- It has no "don't ask again" option.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

AM-02 draws complete on open. The only request it sends is the variant-P count of §5.3, which never blocks a button and has no spinner; `C04` simply appears when its answer arrives.

The removal or deletion itself is **not** a loading state of AM-02: AM-02 closes the moment `B01` is clicked, and the save follows `admin-map` §5.4 on the screen underneath.

### 6.2 Empty

Not reachable: AM-02 always opens for one named product or category.

### 6.3 Error

AM-02 shows no error of its own. A failed removal or deletion is shown on the screen underneath, after AM-02 has closed, as `admin-map` §5.4 requires: the row returns and that screen's save-failure line appears (§3.2). A failed count request hides `C04` only (§5.3).

### 6.4 Success

`B01` clicked: AM-02 closes within 100 ms, the list updates optimistically, and the confirmation line of the screen underneath appears (A-03 §5.6, A-05 §5.6).

---

## 7. Field rules

AM-02 has no field.

- **Names in `C02`**: both names exactly as saved, never truncated; a name is at most 60 characters for a product (A-04 §7.1) and 24 for a category (A-05 §7.1), which fit two lines of the 432 px inner width at 15 px.
- **Confirmation lines** after the action use the name in the dashboard's current language (A-03 §5.6, A-05 §5.6).
- **Timeouts**: the removal or deletion 5 seconds (`admin-map` §5.4); the variant-P count 5 seconds.
- **Sizes**: card 480 px; buttons 44 px tall; title 18 px; names and `C04` 15 px; list and `C05` 13 px. Minimum text size 13 px. Browser zoom is never disabled.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

1. "إزالة / Remove" on an A-03 row opens AM-02 variant P titled "إزالة المنتج من المتجر؟ / Remove this product from the store?" and changes nothing until confirmed.
2. "إزالة من المتجر / Remove from the store" on A-04 opens the same variant P for the product being edited.
3. "حذف / Delete" on an empty category on A-05 opens variant C titled "حذف الفئة؟ / Delete this category?".
4. Both variants show the item's Arabic and English names on two lines, whichever language the dashboard is in.
5. Variant P shows the four consequence lines of §5.2 and variant C the two lines of §5.4, exactly.
6. The confirm button reads "نعم، أزل / Yes, remove" in variant P and "نعم، احذف / Yes, delete" in variant C; the cancel button reads "إلغاء / Cancel" in both.
7. On open, focus is on "إلغاء"; Enter, Esc, a backdrop click and browser back each close AM-02 with nothing changed.
8. A click on the confirm button within 300 ms of AM-02 opening does nothing.
9. When the product being removed is the only product that is active and in stock, variant P shows "هذا آخر منتج معروض للبيع. بعد إزالته لن يجد النزلاء أي منتج في المتجر. / This is the last product on sale. After you remove it, guests will find no products in the store." above the consequence list; for any other product, or when the count request fails, it is not shown.
10. The confirm button is usable before the count answer arrives.
11. Opened from A-04 with an unsaved edit, variant P shows "ستُفقد التعديلات غير المحفوظة في هذه الصفحة. / Unsaved edits on this page will be lost."; opened from A-04 with no unsaved edit, or from A-03, it does not.
12. Confirming variant P from A-03 closes AM-02, removes the row within 100 ms, recounts the header counts, and shows "تمت إزالة "{name}" من المتجر / "{name}" was removed from the store".
13. Confirming variant P from A-04 closes AM-02 and opens A-03 without the product and with the same confirmation line; AM-01 is not shown.
14. After a confirmed removal, the product has no card on G-01 after a catalog fetch, still appears in A-06, AM-03 and A-02's best sellers, and can be restored from A-03's "مُزال / Removed" filter, returning out of stock.
15. A guest whose cart holds the removed product cannot send an order that includes it.
16. Confirming variant C closes AM-02, removes the category row within 100 ms, and shows "تم حذف الفئة "{name}" / The category "{name}" was deleted"; the deletion cannot be undone from any screen.
17. When a confirmed removal or deletion fails or exceeds 5 seconds, the row returns to its place and the underlying screen shows "تعذّر حفظ التغيير — تحقّق من الاتصال وحاول مرة أخرى / The change could not be saved — check your connection and try again".
18. AM-02 never opens for a category that holds any product, active or removed, and offers no permanent product deletion.
19. Every string on AM-02 exists in Arabic and English, with Arabic first in the specification and in the Arabic interface.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **One modal, two variants, one layout.** The consequences differ; the shape, the button order and the dismissal rules do not.
2. **Both names are shown**, so the manager confirms the right item whichever dashboard language is active.
3. **The consequence list is written from the approved guest specs, one fact per line**, including the one a manager is most likely to wrongly assume: that orders already sent are affected.
4. **The last-product warning is shown only when it is true**, fetched on open, never blocking.
5. **AM-02 closes on confirm and the save follows `admin-map` §5.4 underneath**: optimistic, reverted on failure with the screen's own error line.
6. **From A-04, unsaved edits are dropped with a stated line (`C05`) instead of stacking AM-01 on top of AM-02.** Removing a product and saving edits to it in the same breath are contradictory intentions; the line makes the loss explicit.
7. **Cancel is the default focus, and confirm ignores clicks for 300 ms after opening.**
8. **Variant C's confirm reads "نعم، احذف / Yes, delete".** **Recorded difference from the map:** `admin-map` §3.2 names a single confirm label, "نعم، أزل / Yes, remove", for AM-02. A category deletion is permanent while a product removal is not, and the button that opens variant C reads "حذف / Delete" (A-05 `B05`); using "remove" for the permanent act would blur exactly the difference the modal exists to state. Variant P keeps the map's label unchanged.

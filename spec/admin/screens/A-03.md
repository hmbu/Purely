# A-03 — المنتجات / Products

Author: Thinker (Agent 1)
Status: **Draft**
Governing documents: `/spec/admin/admin-map.md` §4 (global frame), §5.1, §5.3 (when a change reaches a guest), §5.4 (immediate saves and how they fail); G-01 §5.3, §5.4, §5.7; G-03 §5.2, §5.6.

---

## 1. ID and name

- **ID:** A-03
- **Name:** المنتجات / Products
- **Type:** Full admin screen inside the global frame. The catalog as one table. It is the only screen where stock is switched.
- **Language:** the dashboard language. Product names are shown in **both** languages on every row, whichever language the dashboard is in (`admin-map` §4.2).

---

## 2. Purpose

Show the whole catalog exactly as the guest will meet it — in category order, with price and stock — and let the manager switch stock, reorder, open, add or remove a product.

## 5. Content

### 5.1 What the manager is looking at

The list is the catalog in the guest's own order: categories in the order set on A-05, products in the order set here. Reading A-03 top to bottom is reading G-01 top to bottom. That is the point of grouping it this way rather than sorting by name — the manager can see that "مياه / Water" is the first card a guest meets.

### 5.2 Two shapes of the list

- **Grouped (no filter active).** One `C03` heading per category, in the category order from A-05, then that category's product rows in their own order, with `B06`/`B07` on each row. A category with no products still shows its heading with `— 0 منتجات / — 0 products`, so the manager can see that it is empty and will therefore show **no chip at all** to the guest (G-01 §5.3). Removed products are not shown unless `F03` is set to "مُزال / Removed".
- **Flat (any filter active).** No headings, no `B06`/`B07`, one row per match, sorted by category order and then by the product's own order. Reordering a filtered list is meaningless, so the controls are hidden rather than disabled.

### 5.3 A product row (`C04`), start edge to end edge

| Column | Width | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Image | 48 px | 40 × 40 px thumbnail of the product image. No image → a gray 40 × 40 px box with a 16 px generic product icon, the same fallback the guest sees (G-01 §5.4) |
| Name | flexible | Two lines: the Arabic name at 15 px on line 1, the English name at 13 px gray on line 2. Each truncated with an ellipsis at the column width. A removed product's name is followed by the tag `مُزال / Removed` in a gray pill |
| Category | 160 px | The category name in the dashboard language, 13 px |
| Price | 120 px | The unit price exactly as the guest will see it in the dashboard's current language: `12.00 ر.س` in Arabic, `SAR 12.00` in English |
| Stock | 160 px | `B04` switch and its state word |
| Order | 80 px | `B06` ↑ and `B07` ↓, grouped, hidden while filtering |
| Actions | 100 px | `B05` "إزالة / Remove" or "استعادة / Restore" |

Both names are always shown because the most common catalog fault is an English name that was never written, and that fault is invisible on a screen that shows one language at a time.

### 5.4 What the stock switch means, exactly

Switching a product **off** (غير متوفر / Out of stock):

- The guest's card keeps its place in G-01 and stays openable, with the badge `غير متوفر / Out of stock`, its image at 50 % opacity and no Add button (G-01 §5.4, C07).
- A cart line that already holds it is **not** removed: the guest sees the badge with the quantity and must remove the line themselves on G-03, which is also what disables their Checkout (G-03 §5.4, map §4 decision 9).
- A guest who tries to submit anyway is stopped by M-04 at submission.
- **No order that has already been submitted is affected in any way.** Switching stock never cancels, changes or flags an order.

Switching a product **on** again reaches a guest only at their next catalog fetch: G-03 never clears an out-of-stock mark (`admin-map` §5.3).

Removing a product (`B05` → AM-02 variant P) is heavier and is why it has a modal: the product disappears from G-01 entirely (no card at all), and an existing cart line for it falls back to the line's own stored snapshot and is treated as out of stock (G-03 §5.2 item 2). A removal is **never** a deletion: the product keeps its sales history, still appears in A-06 and AM-03 and in A-02's best sellers, and `B05` becomes "استعادة / Restore". Nothing in this dashboard deletes a product permanently.

### 5.5 Counts in `C02`

`T` = every product including removed ones; `A` = active and in stock; `O` = active and out of stock; `R` = removed. `A + O + R = T`. The line is recomputed within 100 ms of any switch, removal or restore, with no page reload.

### 5.6 Confirmation lines (`C06`) — exact copy

| After | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| A save on A-04 (edit) | تم حفظ التغييرات على "{product name}" | Changes to "{product name}" were saved |
| A save on A-04 (create) | تمت إضافة "{product name}" إلى المتجر | "{product name}" was added to the store |
| A removal (AM-02 P) | تمت إزالة "{product name}" من المتجر | "{product name}" was removed from the store |
| A restore (`B05`) | تمت استعادة "{product name}" — لا يزال غير متوفر حتى تشغّل التوفر | "{product name}" was restored — it stays out of stock until you switch it on |

`{product name}` is the name in the dashboard's current language. A restored product always comes back **out of stock**, so an item that was removed because it ran out never returns to the store as available by accident; the confirmation line says so.

### 5.7 What is deliberately not on this screen

- **No permanent delete.** Order history must keep naming what was sold.
- **No bulk actions**: no select-all, no "switch all off", no bulk category move, no CSV import. A hotel catalog is at most 200 rows and bulk operations are the fastest way to empty a store by accident.
- **No duplicate-product button**, no drag-and-drop ordering (keyboard-usable ↑/↓ buttons instead), no inline price editing (price is edited on A-04, next to its live guest preview).
- **No stock quantity.** Stock is yes/no in version 1, exactly as the guest's availability check expects (G-03 §5.6: "there is no 'only 2 left'").
- **No cost, margin, supplier or barcode field.**
- **No pagination.** 200 products at 64 px is a scroll, not a paging problem.

## 6. States

### 6.1 Loading

`C10` skeleton in the list area; `S01`, `S02` and `C05` stay visible and usable; `C02` hidden. Timeout **10 seconds**, then §6.3.

### 6.2 Empty

- **No products at all** (and no filter active): `C08`, two lines, with `B01` in the header as the only path forward. `C02` hidden. This is the exact state in which the guest sees G-01's empty state, and `C08`'s second line says so in effect by telling the manager what to do.
- **No match for the active filters**: `C09` plus `B02` "مسح عوامل التصفية / Clear filters". The filter row stays populated so the manager can see what they asked for.

### 6.3 Error

1. **The list fails to load or times out**: the list area shows `تعذّر تحميل المنتجات / Products could not be loaded` with a "إعادة المحاولة / Try again" text button beneath it, which re-fetches. The header and filters stay.
2. **An immediate save fails** (`B04`, `B06`, `B07`, restore): the control reverts to its previous value on screen and `C07` appears (`admin-map` §5.4). The rest of the list is untouched.
3. **A product image fails to load**: the gray 40 × 40 px placeholder with the icon. No error text.
4. **Expired session**: A-01 per `admin-map` §4.3.

### 6.4 Success

The list rendered in its grouped or flat shape, `C02` counting correctly, `C05` always visible, `C06` for 5 seconds after an action that changed something.

## 7. Field rules

### 7.1 `F01` Search

- Filters on **both** product names at once: a row matches when the typed text appears anywhere in the Arabic name or anywhere in the English name.
- Case-insensitive. Arabic-Indic digits typed here are matched against Western digits. Leading and trailing spaces are ignored.
- Length 0 to 60 characters. Filtering runs **250 ms after the last keystroke**, not on every character.
- No search by price, category name, description or identifier. The category filter covers categories.

### 7.2 `F02` and `F03` Filters

- `F02` lists `كل الفئات / All categories` first, then every category in the A-05 order, then `بلا فئة / No category` **only if** at least one product has none (which can happen only when a category was deleted — A-05 forbids deleting a non-empty category, so this option is a safety net, never a normal state).
- `F03` options: `الكل / All` (active products only, in stock and out of stock), `معروض / On sale`, `غير متوفر / Out of stock`, `مُزال / Removed`. **`الكل` does not include removed products**; removed products appear only under their own option, so a removed product can never be mistaken for a live one.
- The three filters combine with AND.

### 7.3 Ordering

- Category order comes from A-05 and cannot be changed here.
- Within a category, `B06`/`B07` move a product exactly one place. The move applies on screen within 100 ms and is saved immediately; on failure the row moves back and `C07` appears.
- A new product is appended **last in its category**. Changing a product's category on A-04 appends it last in the new category.
- Removed products keep their position and return to it when restored.

### 7.4 Limits

- Maximum **200 products** in the catalog, counting removed ones. The reason is the guest side: G-01 loads the entire catalog in **one request with no pagination** (G-01 §5.4), and every product carries two names, two descriptions and an image.
- At 200, `B01` is disabled and `C11` states the limit and the remedy.

### 7.5 URL

The three filters are carried in the A-03 URL, so a filtered view can be reloaded, bookmarked and linked to from A-02. Nothing else about the screen is in the URL: scroll position and the 3-second row outline are not restored by a reload.

### 7.6 Sizes

Rows 64 px; headings 36 px; switch 44 × 24 px with a click area of at least 44 × 44 px; ↑/↓ 32 × 32 px with a click area of at least 40 × 40 px; every text button at least 32 px tall. Minimum text size 13 px. Browser zoom is never disabled. The table needs no horizontal scrolling at 1024 px width.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

1. Opening A-03 with no filter shows one heading per category in the A-05 order, each followed by its products in their own order, matching the order a guest sees on G-01.
2. A category with no products shows its heading with a count of 0, and the guest sees no chip for it.
3. Every row shows a 40 × 40 px image or the gray placeholder, the Arabic name on the first line and the English name on the second, the category, the price, the stock switch and the actions.
4. The price on a row is rendered exactly as the guest sees it in the dashboard's current language: `12.00 ر.س` in Arabic, `SAR 12.00` in English, always two decimals.
5. A product with no English name shows a visibly empty second line rather than repeating the Arabic name.
6. Clicking anywhere on a row except the switch, ↑, ↓ or the actions column opens A-04 in edit mode for that product.
7. Clicking the stock switch flips it within 100 ms, updates the state word and the counts in `C02`, and sends exactly one save request; the row does not open A-04.
8. When that save fails or times out after 5 seconds, the switch returns to its previous state and "تعذّر حفظ التغيير — تحقّق من الاتصال وحاول مرة أخرى / The change could not be saved — check your connection and try again" is shown.
9. Switching a product out of stock changes nothing about any order that was already submitted.
10. ↑ and ↓ move a product exactly one place within its own category, are disabled at the first and last row of that category, and save the new order immediately; the guest's product order on G-01 matches the new order after a catalog fetch.
11. With any filter active, headings, ↑ and ↓ are not rendered and the list is flat.
12. Typing in the search field filters on both names at once, 250 ms after the last keystroke, case-insensitively.
13. Setting the stock filter to "غير متوفر / Out of stock" shows exactly the active products whose switch is off, and A-02's out-of-stock row opens this view.
14. The option "الكل / All" in the stock filter excludes removed products; removed products appear only under "مُزال / Removed".
15. "مسح عوامل التصفية / Clear filters" is shown only while a filter is active and returns the screen to the grouped list.
16. The three filters are carried in the URL and survive a reload.
17. "+ منتج جديد / New product" opens A-04 in create mode; at 200 products it is disabled and the limit note states the maximum and the remedy.
18. `C02` counts T, A, O and R such that A + O + R = T, and recounts within 100 ms of any switch, removal or restore.
19. "إزالة / Remove" opens AM-02 variant P and changes nothing until it is confirmed there.
20. After a confirmed removal the product leaves the list, `C06` confirms it by name, and the product still appears in A-06, AM-03 and A-02's best sellers.
21. A removed row's action reads "استعادة / Restore", its stock switch is disabled, and restoring it returns it to its previous position **out of stock**, with `C06` saying so.
22. No control anywhere on A-03 deletes a product permanently, and there is no bulk action, no select-all and no import.
23. Returning from a successful save on A-04 scrolls to that product's row, outlines it for 3 seconds and shows the matching confirmation line from §5.6.
24. The propagation note "تظهر أي تغييرات هنا للنزيل عند فتحه المتجر من جديد / Changes here reach a guest the next time they open the store" is visible in every non-loading state.
25. With zero products and no filter, the screen shows the two-line empty state and no count line.
26. With a filter that matches nothing, the screen shows "لا توجد منتجات تطابق البحث / No products match your search" and the clear-filters button, and keeps the filter values visible.
27. While loading, the list area shows gray skeleton bars with no text, and the header, filters and note remain usable.
28. A list request that exceeds 10 seconds shows the load error with a "إعادة المحاولة / Try again" button that re-fetches.
29. Switching the dashboard language re-renders every label, state word and price format on A-03 in the other language and direction, and both product names still appear on every row.
30. No price is editable on this screen and no stock quantity appears anywhere on it.

## 9. Decisions settled on this screen

1. **The list is the guest's order, not an alphabetical table**, because both the category order and the product order inside a category are guest-visible (G-01 §5.3, §5.4).
2. **Product order inside a category is editable here**, with ↑/↓ buttons rather than drag-and-drop: they are precise, keyboard-usable, and trivially testable.
3. **Ordering controls are hidden while filtering**, because a position in a filtered list has no meaning.
4. **Both names on every row.** A missing English name is the most common catalog fault and must be visible without switching language.
5. **Stock is a yes/no switch saved immediately**, with an optimistic update and a revert on failure. It is the most frequent edit in the product and must cost one click.
6. **A restored product always comes back out of stock**, so a product removed because it ran out never silently reappears on sale.
7. **Removal is archiving, never deletion.** Order history, exports and best-seller lists must keep naming what was sold.
8. **"الكل / All" excludes removed products**, so a removed product is never mistaken for a live one.
9. **No bulk actions and no import in version 1.** A 200-row catalog does not need them, and they are the fastest way to empty a store by mistake.
10. **Hard limit of 200 products and 20 categories** (the second is enforced on A-05), because the guest loads the entire catalog in one request with no pagination.
11. **No stock quantity**, matching the guest's yes/no availability check (G-03 §5.6).
12. **Price is not editable inline**, because a price must be typed next to its live guest-facing preview (A-04 `C04`).

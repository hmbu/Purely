## 3. Ruling on the one finding that requires a change

**F5 — [Medium, cross-cutting] The trim rule can silently delete a still-active order.**
**Verdict: Essential.** Deleting a still-trackable record takes away the guest's only route to the cancel control that locked decision 6 grants them — the same failure as the G-08 gap, and an operational error on the hotel's side when an uncancellable order arrives at a door nobody expects.

The spec calls the case unrealistic and it is; that is not a reason to leave a gap in a rule, and the cure is three sentences with no new element, no new request and no guest decision.

**Ruling R1 (binding on the Thinker):**

1. **§5.4, trim rule — replace rules 1–3 with:**
   1. A record whose last known status is **New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way is never deleted by the trim**, whatever its age and whatever the length of the list.
   2. While the device holds more than 20 records **and at least one of them is Delivered or Cancelled**, delete the oldest such record by order time; repeat.
   3. The trim stops when 20 or fewer records remain, **or** when every remaining record is active. In the second case the list is drawn with **more than 20 rows**: nothing is deleted to reach the cap and nothing is hidden.
   4. State the consequence in one line: the cap limits how many **finished** orders the device keeps; it is never a reason to lose a trackable one.
2. **The draft's rule 2** ("delete the oldest record regardless of status") is **deleted in full**, together with its parenthetical justification.
3. **§3.1, the "Trim on entry" paragraph** gains the same guarantee in one clause: the trim runs before the first frame and never deletes an order whose last known status is New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way.
4. **C08 changes condition and copy**, because the list can now hold more than 20 rows and the old string would be false when it does:
   - Rendered when the list shows **20 rows or more** (was: exactly 20).
   - Copy (replaces the current string in the elements table, §5.4 and §6.4):
     `يحتفظ هذا الجهاز بآخر 20 طلبًا؛ الطلبات الجارية لا تُحذف`
     `This device keeps your last 20 orders; orders in progress are never removed`
   - Everything else about C08 is unchanged: 14 px, gray, centered, under the last row, not tappable, one line that may wrap to two.
5. **No new element, no warning, no confirmation, no "an old order was removed" notice, and no undo.** The guest is still never asked about a deletion and never sees a row disappear.
6. **Acceptance criteria:** rewrite 21 and 22 and add one criterion, exactly as set out in §7 R1.

**R1 — The trim never deletes an order that is still New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way.**

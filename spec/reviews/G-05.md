## 3. Rulings on the Medium findings that change the screen

G-05's test produced **no Critical finding**. Two Mediums are nevertheless ruled Essential, for the reasons the product manager gave: one is the framing defect shared with M-01 and G-06, and one is a proof that does not hold on its own terms.

### R1 — G-05-05 (persona 7): C05 frames cancellation as the remedy for a wrong *room number* only. **Essential.**

**Reason:** locked decision 6 lets the guest cancel a New order for any reason, but M-01, G-05 and G-06 all advertise that right as a room-number remedy, so a guest with a wrong *item*, a wrong payment method or a wrong note reads the sentence, concludes it is not about them, and believes version 1 offers no repair — a repair that exists and is one tap away is lost to wording alone.

**Ruling — wording only. No new element, no new control, no change to C05's type, size, placement or visibility.**

1. **C05's copy is replaced, in both languages:**
   - Arabic: `هل هناك خطأ في الطلب — رقم الغرفة أو المنتجات أو طريقة الدفع؟ يمكنك إلغاء الطلب من صفحة المتابعة ما دام الفندق لم يقبله بعد، ثم إرسال طلب جديد.`
   - English: `Something wrong in the order — the room number, the items, or the payment method? You can cancel it from the tracking page while the hotel has not accepted it yet, then place a new order.`
2. **What the new copy must keep and does keep:** it still names the room number first (it is the highest-stakes value and sits directly above); it still names the exact remedy (cancel from the tracking page); it still names the exact deadline (before the hotel accepts, locked decision 6); and it now names the outcome the guest actually wants (place a new order), which is the half the old sentence left out even for the room-number case.
3. **C05 remains a sentence, not a link, not a button.** The action it names is B02, two elements below. No `tel:` link, no contact control, no "edit order" path is added by this ruling.
4. **C05's visibility rule is unchanged and is not this review's to set:** it is hidden when the record's status is past New, exactly as ruled in `/spec/reviews/M-01.md` R1 and already written into §4 and §5.2 of this file. The wider wording makes that rule *more* correct, not less: a sentence that promises a cancellation is wrong in every status where cancellation is gone, whatever the mistake was.
5. **Line allowance.** The new sentence is longer and must be allowed to wrap to **3 lines** at 14 px in the 358 px inner width (the old rule allowed 2). §4's C05 row and §7.5 are updated, and R2's arithmetic below already counts 3 lines.
6. **Cross-screen:** G-06 carries the same reframing in the New status, in the matching element ruled in `/spec/reviews/G-06.md` R1. The two sentences are one family and must not drift apart; §7.1 below routes it.

### R2 — G-05-03 (persona 3): the fit rule leaves 6 px of slack and omits the safe-area inset this file states elsewhere. **Essential.**

**Reason:** a spec written so a technical team never has to ask a question cannot contain a proof that fails on the first real device. §7.5 sums to 658 px against a 664 px viewport while §4's S03 row itself requires "16 px bottom padding **plus the device's safe-area inset**"; on any notched phone the inset alone (~34 px) consumes the slack, so acceptance criterion 19 is false as written. R1 makes it worse by one line of copy. A false guarantee is a defect whether or not the screen still works.

**Ruling — the arithmetic is redone with the inset and the new copy, and the guarantee is honestly reduced rather than the screen redesigned.**

1. **Fixed measurement basis, stated once in §7.5:** viewport 390 × 664 px; **device safe-area inset 34 px** at the bottom, so the usable height for content that must be seen without scrolling is **630 px**; C05 at its worst case of 3 lines (60 px); C06 at 2 lines; C09 not shown (the ordinary case).
2. **The redone sum, to the bottom of B02:** 56 header + 32 top padding + 64 C02 + 16 + 26 C01 + 24 + 20 C03 + 8 + 88 C04 + 12 + **60 C05 (3 lines)** + 24 + 24 C06 + 8 + 20 C07 + 4 + 20 C08 + 32 + 56 B02 = **594 px**, which is 36 px inside the 630 px usable height.
3. **The guarantee, ranked, replacing the present one:** at 390 × 664 px with a 34 px safe-area inset and default text size, everything from S01 down to and including **B02 "متابعة الطلب / Track order"** is visible without scrolling — the success mark, the title, the room number, the repair sentence, the order number, the total, the payment line and the primary action.
4. **What gives:** **B03 "العودة إلى المتجر / Back to store" may fall below the fold and is reached by a short scroll.** This is accepted and is the right thing to sacrifice: B03 is the secondary exit and is duplicated by B01 in the fixed header, which never leaves the viewport, while B02 is the route to the only repair the guest has. B03 is **not** removed, not moved into the header, and not made smaller.
5. **When C09 is shown** (the already-existing case of `/spec/reviews/M-01.md` R1) the reduced guarantee is: S01, C02, C01, C03, C04 and the **first line of C09** are visible without scrolling; everything below C09, including B02, is reached by scrolling. This is correct rather than regrettable — C09 is the one thing that guest must read, and it sits above the buttons.
6. **Nothing may be shrunk, deleted or collapsed to make the original claim true.** In particular C04 stays at 48 px, C05 keeps its full new copy and its 3-line allowance, and no element is moved into a collapsed or expandable region.
7. **Acceptance criterion 19 is rewritten** to assert exactly 3, 4 and 5 above, naming the 34 px inset and the 3-line C05. The old "the entire content including B03 is visible without scrolling" is deleted.
8. The existing sentence about enlarged text (the body scrolls, the room number never shrinks below 48 px) stays and is now the same kind of statement as the rest of §7.5: a stated consequence, not a hidden failure.

## 4. Tester finding verdicts (all 14)

Finding IDs are the tester's own (F1–F14).

### 4.1 Critical

**F11 — [Critical, Persona 7] A guest with an active order who taps "Add" on G-02 never sees the "new, separate order" notice, because it lives only on G-01's banner.**
**Verdict: Essential.**

This is my own earlier ruling completed, not a new feature. In G-01's cycle I ruled the second-order problem Essential and fixed it in the banner's wording (G-01 review §3.1, F-00). That fix was incomplete: the **Add control also lives on G-02**, one tap past the banner, so a guest who scrolled past the banner and opened a product is warned nowhere at the moment they act. Two parties believing there is one order when two exist is an operational error for staff — the Essential bar — and the hole is closed the same cheap way it was closed on G-01: one line of copy, identical wording, no new behaviour.

**Ruling (binding on the Thinker — copy and placement only, nothing else):**

1. **One new element, `G-02-C13`.** Type: content block, plain text, one line, **not tappable**, no icon, no border, no fill, no color change, no animation.
2. **Copy — exactly the sentence already approved on G-01 (G-01 §5.2 line 2), reused verbatim in both languages:**
   - Arabic: `أي منتج تضيفه الآن سيُرسل كطلب جديد منفصل`
   - English: `Anything you add now is sent as a new, separate order`
3. **Placement — with the action row, never in the header and never at the bottom.** C13 sits inside the scrolling body S02, **8 px below S03**; when C06 ("الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10") or C08 ("لإزالته من السلة افتح السلة / To remove it, open the cart") is shown, C13 sits 8 px below that helper instead. The description section S04 then sits 24 px below C13 rather than below S03/C06/C08. C13 is not sticky and does not scroll independently. The reason for this placement is the whole point of the finding: the sentence must be on screen at the same time as the button it is about, and §5.3 already guarantees that the image, name, price and action row fit within the first 480 px of the viewport.
4. **Size and style.** 14 px, regular weight, centered on the full content width (358 px), wrapping onto a maximum of two lines and never truncated. 14 px — not G-01's 12 px — because on G-01 the sentence is line 2 inside a titled banner, while here it is a standalone line, and because F4 makes 14 px this screen's minimum text size.
5. **When it is shown.** Whenever the device holds at least one order whose **last known saved status** is New, Accepted & preparing, or On the way (the statuses of G-01 §5.2), in the **success state only**. It is shown regardless of the order's status among those three, regardless of whether the cart has items, and regardless of whether the action row currently shows Add, the stepper, or the C07 badge — one condition, no exceptions. It is **hidden** in the loading state and in the error state, and hidden when the device holds no such order.
6. **Where the state comes from — no request.** C13 reads the last known active-order state **already stored on the device**. G-02 **must not** send a status request of any kind: §5.6 and §6.1 rule 1 stand unchanged, and G-01 §5.2 already fetched that status once per load (map §2.3 rule 5). On the reload / direct-URL path C13 uses the saved status with no fetch and no error text; if the device has never stored an order, the line simply never appears.
7. **Explicitly rejected, exactly as on G-01:**
   - **Any banner with a status and a link.** No order number, no status label, no "متابعة الطلب › / Track order ›", no chevron, nothing tappable. That banner is G-01's job (map §1.3 places it at the top of G-01's product list), and a link from here would give G-02 a second purpose — order tracking — on a screen whose purpose is one product.
   - **Any disabling or gating of Add / + / −.** B02, B03 and B04 behave exactly as they do with no active order.
   - **Any modal, any confirmation, any "are you sure this is a new order?" step.**
   - **Any order-editing path**, any "add to my existing order", any cancel control. Editing a submitted order stays out of version 1: the answer remains cancel-while-New (M-02) then reorder (G-06 → G-03), and it is told on G-06.
8. **Two acceptance criteria are added** (wording in §7).

In the **loading** state and the **error** state it shows the fixed fallback copy `تفاصيل المنتج` / `Product details` (there is no name to show yet, or no product). **D1 is overturned.** No new element, no new ID, no layout change, no sticky behaviour anywhere.

The reachability half of F6 is answered by an ordinary scroll upward, with the fixed cart bar still offering a way forward at all times; the identity half cannot be answered by scrolling at all, which is why the header wins.

## 7. Required check 3 — does G-02 serve one clear purpose?

**Result: yes.** The stated purpose — "show one product in full … and let the guest add it or change its quantity without leaving the screen" — matches what the screen does, and every element in §2 serves either showing this product or acting on this product's quantity.

Three things were examined as possible second purposes; all three are cleared, two of them by rulings in this review:

- **The new C13 notice (F11).** Order-state content on a product screen is exactly what would give G-02 a second purpose — which is why the ruling makes it a sentence and nothing else: no order number, no status, no link to G-06, not tappable, no request.

# Review — G-01 Store (المتجر)

Author: Reviewer (Agent 3)
Cycle: 1
Documents reviewed: `/spec/screens/G-01.md` (Draft), `/spec/tests/G-01.md` (16 findings)
Governing documents: `/CLAUDE.md` locked decisions 1–7; `/spec/screens-map.md` §4 decisions 1–16 (both sets are locked and approved).

The single question applied to every item below: **do we need this in version 1?**

- **Essential** — without it the journey breaks or an operational error occurs.
- **Later** — useful, but can wait for version 2. Moved to `/spec/backlog.md`.
- **Remove** — complicates the experience or does not serve the screen's purpose.

The Thinker acts only on this file. Anything not listed here is unchanged.

## 3. Tester finding verdicts (all 16)

Finding IDs are assigned here for the Thinker's convenience; the tester's own wording is quoted in short form.

### 3.1 Critical

**F-00 — [Critical, Persona 7] A guest with an order already in flight can silently build a second, separate order.**
**Verdict: Essential.** Two parties believing there is one order when two exist is exactly an operational error for staff, which is the Essential bar.

**Ruling (binding on the Thinker — keep it to wording, nothing else):**

1. The fix lives entirely in **C03**, the active-order banner. Nothing on the product grid changes: **no** disabling of B04/B05/B06, **no** gating, **no** warning modal, **no** confirmation before adding, **no** change to the cart or to G-03/G-04.
2. C03 becomes three lines and grows from 64 px to **84 px**; it stays non-sticky and the whole block stays tappable to G-06:
   - Line 1 (14 px, bold, unchanged): `طلبك رقم {order number} — {status label}` / `Your order {order number} — {status label}`
   - **Line 2 (new, 12 px):** `أي منتج تضيفه الآن سيُرسل كطلب جديد منفصل` / `Anything you add now is sent as a new, separate order`
   - Line 3 (12 px, unchanged text): `متابعة الطلب ›` / `Track order ›`
3. Line 2 is shown whenever the banner is shown, regardless of the order's status and regardless of whether the cart currently has items. It is a statement of fact, not a warning state.
4. **Editing a submitted order stays out of version 1.** The map already rules that cancel-while-New (M-02) then reorder is the v1 answer, and that answer is told on G-06 — G-01 does not mention cancelling, does not link to it separately, and does not grow an "add to my existing order" path. Any attempt to widen this into order editing is rejected in advance.
5. Add one acceptance criterion: when the banner is visible, the sentence in line 2 is visible with it; adding a product while the banner is visible changes only the card and the cart bar, exactly as it does with no active order.

**Ruling on F-07 — 44 × 44 px wins.** The convention line and §7.6 are the rule; the elements table is the defect. The Thinker keeps the *visible* sizes exactly as they are (B04 40 px tall, B05/B06 40 × 40 px, so the 171 px card and the 40 px action row are unchanged) and states explicitly in the elements table — using the same wording already used for B03 — that each of B04, B05 and B06 has its **tap area extended to at least 44 × 44 px** beyond the visible shape. Add one acceptance criterion naming B04, B05 and B06 by ID. No layout, no card size and no grid change.

**Ruling on F-08 — one line only.** Add to §7.6: the page never disables browser zoom or text scaling (no `user-scalable=no`, no fixed maximum scale), and the guest can always pinch-zoom. Nothing further. A tested, reflowing large-text layout at 200 % is **Later** and is in the backlog; do not spec it now.

**Ruling on F-10 — extend the badge, do not add an element.** When a product is marked out of stock **and** the device cart already holds a quantity of it, C07 shows `غير متوفر — في سلتك {N}` / `Out of stock — {N} in your cart`. When the cart holds none of it, C07 keeps the current text. No stepper returns, no new element ID, no removal from the cart here (removal stays on G-03 per map §4 decision 9). Update §5.4 item 4 and acceptance criterion 22 accordingly.

**Ruling on F-11 — one rule, no new fetch.** G-01 still never re-fetches the catalog by itself. When G-03 (or M-04) discovers that a product is out of stock, that mark is written to the catalog copy held on the device for this session, so returning to G-01 shows that card with C07 (and, under F-10, the quantity still in the cart). Delete or rewrite the clause in §3.1 that promises steppers "reflect any change made on G-03" so it agrees with this rule, and add an acceptance criterion for the return path.

## 6. Required check 3 — does G-01 serve one clear purpose?

**Result: yes.** The stated purpose — "see everything the hotel sells, find a category fast, and add products to the cart from the card itself, without leaving this screen" — matches what the screen actually does, and every element in §2 serves either browsing, adding, or the header's two permanent entry points.

Two things were examined as possible second purposes and both are cleared:

- **The active-order banner (C03)** is order-*status* content on a browsing screen. It stays, because map §1.3 places it here and it is a one-tap pointer to G-06, not order management. The F-00 ruling deliberately keeps it a sentence and a link: no cancel control, no editing, no second-order UI on G-01. Had the fix been gating or editing, G-01 would have acquired a second purpose — that is precisely why it was rejected.
- **The empty state's "Refresh" (B08)** is recovery, not browsing, but it is the only control in a state that would otherwise be a dead end, and it returns the guest to the one purpose.

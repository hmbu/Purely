### 4.1 Critical

**F19 — [Critical, Persona 7] The Reorder merge caps a line at 10 and drops the excess silently, so the guest can believe the previous order was recreated in full.**
**Verdict: Essential.**

A guest who believes the reorder succeeded, submits it, and is then delivered fewer units than they think they bought is a guest-versus-staff dispute about money at the room door. That is squarely the operational-error bar. The fix is a message, not machinery: the toast is already the one place the merge reports what it did not do, so it carries the capped case as well.

**Ruling (binding on the Thinker — copy and counting only, nothing else):**

1. **The cap itself is upheld.** Max 10 per line stays exactly as it is (G-01 §7.1, G-03 §7.1, D8). The excess is still dropped, and the resulting line still shows C04 "الحد الأقصى 10 / Max 10".
2. **Two counters are defined after the merge, both counting distinct products, both from the previous order only:**
   - **S** = distinct products **skipped** (out of stock, not found, or absent from the session catalog copy). Unchanged from the draft.
   - **C** = distinct products whose quantity **could not be added in full because the line reached the cap of 10** — including a product whose line was already at 10 before the merge, so that nothing was added at all. New.
3. **The toast C10 is shown when S ≥ 1 *or* C ≥ 1** (the draft shows it only when S ≥ 1). It is still shown once per "Reorder" tap, still the only toast in version 1.
4. **Case A — only skipped (S ≥ 1, C = 0).** The existing table in §5.7 is unchanged, word for word:

| S | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | لم تتم إضافة منتج واحد لأنه لم يعد متوفرًا | 1 item was not added because it is no longer available |
| 2 | لم تتم إضافة منتجين لأنهما لم يعودا متوفرين | 2 items were not added because they are no longer available |
| 3–10 | لم تتم إضافة {S} منتجات لأنها لم تعد متوفرة | {S} items were not added because they are no longer available |
| 11 and above | لم تتم إضافة {S} منتجًا لأنها لم تعد متوفرة | {S} items were not added because they are no longer available |

5. **Case B — only capped (C ≥ 1, S = 0).** New table, using the same four plural buckets G-03 already established (1 / 2 / 3–10 / 11+). The sentence is verb-first so the Arabic agreement is correct in every bucket, and note that the counted noun here is the **subject** (nominative dual `منتجان`), not the object as in case A (accusative dual `منتجين`) — the two tables must not be copied onto each other:

| C | Arabic | English |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | بلغ منتج واحد الحد الأقصى 10 ولم تُضف الكمية الزائدة | 1 item reached the maximum of 10, so the extra units were not added |
| 2 | بلغ منتجان الحد الأقصى 10 ولم تُضف الكمية الزائدة | 2 items reached the maximum of 10, so the extra units were not added |
| 3–10 | بلغت {C} منتجات الحد الأقصى 10 ولم تُضف الكمية الزائدة | {C} items reached the maximum of 10, so the extra units were not added |
| 11 and above | بلغ {C} منتجًا الحد الأقصى 10 ولم تُضف الكمية الزائدة | {C} items reached the maximum of 10, so the extra units were not added |

6. **Case C — both (S ≥ 1 and C ≥ 1).** One toast, two sentences, the skipped sentence first, each starting on its own line:
   - Line 1: the case A string for S.
   - Line 2: the case B string for C.
   No connecting word, no bullet, no icon, no "and". Example for S = 1, C = 2:
   - Arabic: `لم تتم إضافة منتج واحد لأنه لم يعد متوفرًا` ⏎ `بلغ منتجان الحد الأقصى 10 ولم تُضف الكمية الزائدة`
   - English: `1 item was not added because it is no longer available` ⏎ `2 items reached the maximum of 10, so the extra units were not added`
7. **Two numbers change in the toast's bounds, nothing else.** Maximum height rises from 2 lines to **4 lines** (2 per sentence), and the visible duration is **5 seconds for a one-sentence toast, 7 seconds when both sentences are shown**, still followed by the 200 ms fade and still dismissible by a tap. Position, width, fill, text size, single-toast rule and "disappears when the guest leaves G-03" are unchanged.
8. **Explicitly rejected — none of these may appear in the revision:**
   - **A separate modal** for the cap (a modal to report a quantity the guest can see on the line is a stop sign in front of a fact).
   - **A per-line badge or marker** saying a line was capped or merged (C04 "Max 10" already sits on that line; a second marker is clutter and it is the machinery F20 also asks for and is refused).
   - **A merge preview / "review the reorder" screen** (it would give G-03 a second purpose and add a screen the map does not have).
   - **Any change to the max-10 cap**, per product or per order, and any "order the rest separately" path.

**Ruling on F12 — honesty in the helper line, not a block.** Checkout is **not** disabled when the availability check fails; D4 stands unchanged, and a guest on a weak hotel connection must never be left unable to order at all. What changes is one string, which today ends in a reassurance ("it will be checked when you send the order") that makes a failed gate sound like a completed one:

- The C08 failure text becomes, in both languages:
  - Arabic: `تعذّر التحقق من التوفر — قد يكون أحد المنتجات غير متوفر`
  - English: `Availability not checked — an item may be unavailable`
- Both strings are **shorter** than the ones they replace, so §7.6's "the longest string fits in one line at 358 px" still holds, the reserved 20 px helper row is unchanged, and S03 stays 140 px tall. No layout change, no new element, no Retry control (F18 deferred), no change to B05's enabled state.
- The precedence order in §5.5 is unchanged: out-of-stock text first, then "checking", then this failure text.
- **The residual risk is recorded, not hidden.** D4 gains one sentence in §5.8: when the check fails, the first stock gate does nothing, the guest may reach M-04 after typing a room number and confirming at M-01, and this is **a deliberate, accepted risk for version 1** — accepted because disabling Checkout on a failed check would strand a guest with a working store and no way to order.

**Ruling on F10 (and F26) — the badge carries the quantity, exactly as on G-01.** The G-01 review ruled (F-10) that C07 reads `غير متوفر — في سلتك {N}` / `Out of stock — {N} in your cart` whenever the cart holds the product, and G-02's C07 reuses G-01's wording verbatim. G-03 hiding the quantity is therefore an inconsistency with two screens already approved, and it is the worst screen to hide it on: G-03 still prints the line total (quantity × unit price) right next to it, so the guest can see the money but not the number that produced it.

- **C06's text becomes** `غير متوفر — في سلتك {N}` / `Out of stock — {N} in your cart`, with {N} the quantity stored on the line. On G-03 every line has N ≥ 1, so the bare form `غير متوفر / Out of stock` never occurs here; state that explicitly rather than duplicating a condition that cannot be false.
- **No new element, no returning stepper, no editing.** The quantity is text inside the badge; the only control on the line stays B04 "إزالة / Remove" (map §4 decision 9).
- The pill grows to fit its text on one line; if it does not fit at the current text size it wraps to a maximum of two lines and the line's minimum height grows with it.
- Delete the sentence in §5.4 that says the chosen quantity "is no longer shown anywhere on the line"; the rest of that paragraph (the quantity stays stored so G-01's suffix stays correct) is right and stays.

**Ruling on F23 — G-01 adopts G-03's snapshot fallback.** G-03 §5.2 item 2 and D10 define what happens when a cart line's product has vanished from the session catalog copy: render from the line's own snapshot, treat the line as out of stock. G-01 §7.2 defines the cart-bar total as quantity × "unit price as currently loaded in the catalog", which has no value for such a product. The two screens can therefore print different totals for one cart, and the guest sees one amount on the bar and another on the cart. **G-03 is right and does not change**; the change belongs to G-01's file and is listed in §7.2 of this review for the orchestrator to send back to the Thinker.
